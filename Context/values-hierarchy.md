## 1. Purpose and usage

This file exists because value conflicts are the most expensive category of decision in a multi-venture portfolio and the most likely to be re-litigated in the moment. Settling precedence in advance removes that cost. Alfred consults this file when two legitimate priorities compete and the tradeoff is not resolvable on operational grounds alone.

**This file is descriptive, not aspirational.** It records what the operator chose under forced binary comparison, not what he would state if asked directly. Section 4 documents three cases where those two answers diverged. That divergence is the primary value of the document.

**Do not use this file to:**

- Justify a recommendation the operator will like. The hierarchy is a constraint, not a persuasion tool.
- Diagnose personality, predict behaviour outside the recorded domain or generate psychological interpretation beyond what Section 4 supports.
- Override the operator's explicit in-the-moment instruction. This ranks priorities; it does not hold authority over him.
- Close a debate. If the operator argues against his own recorded hierarchy, that is data about the hierarchy, not an error to correct. Log it for the next review.

## 2. Method and reliability

**Instrument.** A bank of approximately 224 candidate values was organised into eight life domains. The operator selected a field, resolved it to 64 entrants through play-in games and merges, seeded each region one through eight, and adjudicated all 63 elimination games personally.

**Adjudication prompt.** Each game was decided against the question: if these two demanded opposite actions tomorrow morning, which would I actually obey? Not which is more admirable.

**Merges.** Values at high semantic overlap were consolidated, with the survivor carrying the absorbed term. Sovereignty carries Conviction. Merges are recorded in Appendix A and materially affect interpretation. A merged value's win is a win for the cluster, not the single word.

**Known limitations, in order of severity:**

1. **Path dependence.** Single elimination means a value's placement depends on draw position. Care lost to Love in round one; a different draw would have placed it higher without changing its actual weight. Read tiers as bands, not ranks. Cross-tier comparisons of adjacent values are unreliable.
2. **Seeding bias.** The operator's own seeding proved accurate at the 1 line and unreliable in the 2 through 4 range. All nine upsets were low seeds beating 2, 3 or 4 seeds. Seeding reflected esteem; game results reflected use.
3. **Single sitting.** All 63 games were decided in one session. Fatigue, mood and anchoring are uncontrolled.
4. **One reversal.** The Region VIII semi-final was replayed on request after written analysis. Beauty replaced Artistry and went on to win the region. This is documented because it demonstrates that fast answers and considered answers diverged at least once.
5. **Domain boundaries are constructed.** The eight regions were designed for this exercise. Values that could have sat in two regions were assigned to one.

**Confidence in the overall result: moderate to high** for the eight regional champions, **moderate** for the Final Four ordering, **low** for any distinction below Tier 5.

## 4. Findings

Each finding states the observation, the evidence, the inference and a confidence level. Observations are recorded results. Inferences are interpretation and are labelled as such.

### F1. The constitution is built entirely of preconditions

**Observation.** The two finalists, Sovereignty and Health, are enabling conditions rather than objects of pursuit. Every terminal value in the field lost to one of them: Love and Impact to Health, Taste in ideas and Beauty to Sovereignty.

**Inference.** The operator's revealed hierarchy protects capacity – the authority to choose and the instrument to act – above any specific use of that capacity. The bracket does not identify what the protected capacity is for.

**Operational risk.** A precedence order composed entirely of preconditions can rationalise indefinite preparation. Any decision framed as protecting optionality or capability will win against any decision framed as spending it, without the tradeoff ever surfacing.

**Closed on 2026-09-09** by the sovereignty guard in Section 5.5. Sovereignty vetoes new commitments freely and cannot stop work already declared for the current quarter. The declaration survives the quarter that made it.

**Confidence: high.** The pattern holds across four independent games in the final two rounds.

### F2. Three values were stated and then declined under comparison

**Observation.**

| Value | How it was stated | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Care | Won its play-in against Protection and Provision | Eliminated in round one by Love |
| Seduction | Volunteered unprompted as a self-description, seeded 4 | Eliminated in round one by Joy |
| Faith | Granted an automatic bid as uncontested ground | Eliminated in round one by Mortality awareness |

**Inference.** These are held values rather than operating values. Each is genuinely endorsed and none survived first contact with an alternative.

**Operational use.** When the operator invokes any of these three as the reason for a decision, Alfred treats the reasoning as under-specified and asks what the decision costs. This is not a challenge to the value. It is a check that the stated reason is the actual one.

**Confidence: high for the observation, moderate for the inference.** Each result is a single game, and the path-dependence caveat applies. Seduction and Care both drew strong opponents.

### F3. Seeding is reliable at the top and unreliable in the middle

**Observation.** Seven of eight 1 seeds advanced to the regional semi-final. All nine upsets in the tournament were a 5, 6 or 7 seed defeating a 2, 3 or 4 seed. No 1 seed was upset in round one.

**Inference.** The operator's first instinct about what governs a domain is accurate. His ranking of second through fourth priority reflects what he admires rather than what he uses.

**Operational use.** Accept the operator's stated top priority in a domain at face value. Treat his stated second and third priorities as provisional and worth testing when a decision depends on them.

**Confidence: high.** The pattern is consistent across all eight regions.

### F4. The work domain resolves to movement, not accumulation

**Observation.** The elimination sequence in Region III ran: Wealth defeated Risk appetite; Ownership defeated Wealth; Impact defeated Ownership. Diligence, Leadership and Enterprise were all eliminated in round one.

**Inference.** Risk, capital and ownership are all instrumental. The terminal value in the work domain is change effected within the operator's lifetime. Impact defeated Legacy in the play-in round, placing the horizon inside his life rather than after it.

**Corroboration.** This is the second independent arrival at the same ruling. On 2026-07-19, seven weeks before the bracket, the operator articulated it in his own words during the capital-as-canvas interview: infrastructure is judged as a chief operating officer, by what it moves, not as a library, by what it holds. Two instruments, different methods, same answer.

**Operational use.** Governed by the Movement Doctrine in Section 5.5.

**Confidence: high.** Four consecutive games in the same direction, plus independent corroboration.

### F5. Health outranks Impact, and the record does not support it

**Observation, resolved 2026-09-09 by inspection.** The calendar could not answer the question. Apple Calendar holds one event for the whole of 2026, dated 4 February, with full read authorisation granted. It is not an instrument in use.

The records that do hold the period:

| Source | Reading |
|---|---|
| Fitness Journal, last entry | 2026-05-29, Leg Workout, medium intensity |
| Days dark as of 2026-09-09 | 103 |
| Cadence before it stopped | February to May consistent. May the strongest month on record at 13 sessions |
| Alfred Logs, same 103 days | 25 sessions, no gap longer than 18 days, none health |
| Last recorded body weight | 72 kg, 2026-05-28 |

The training did not taper. It stopped at its peak and has not resumed in 14 weeks. Over the same span the work instrument logged 25 entries.

**Inference.** F5 resolves to its second branch. Health did not win its quarter-final as a correction already quietly made. It won in the 14th week of a dark health instrument, against Impact, while the work log ran daily.

**Ruling, 2026-09-09.** The Tier 2 ranking stands. The 103 days are the gap, not evidence against the rank. Apple Calendar holds intent and the Fitness Journal holds fact, and the delta between the two is the measurement. The displacement check is live.

**Confidence: high on the observation, moderate on the inference.**

### F6. Execution beat the standard on fast answers and lost on the considered one

**Observation.** In fast play, Craft defeated Excellence and Artistry defeated Beauty. On written review of the second matchup the operator reversed, and Beauty went on to win Region VIII and reach the Elite Eight.

**Inference.** The bias toward the maker over the made is real in quick judgement and did not survive examination. Beauty is an external standard with an observable stopping condition. Artistry is an internal standard measured against a moving vision.

**Operational use.** Directly relevant to the documented failure mode of endless iteration. When work is being revised, Alfred asks which standard is being applied. If the answer is internal, closing the gap to a vision, the iteration has no defined terminus and Alfred forces one. If the answer is external, the work is measurable against it and can be called done.

**Confidence: moderate to high.** Two supporting games and one instructive reversal.

### F7. The spirit domain refuses consolation and is grounded in gratitude

**Observation.** Every contested game in Region VII selected the less consoling option: Mystery over Transcendence and Awe, Equanimity over Peace and Hope, Mortality awareness over Suffering with meaning and then over Faith. Gratitude, the only warm value remaining, won the region.

**Inference.** The operator declines redemptive or reassuring framing but is not nihilistic. Gratitude functions as the ground rather than as comfort.

**Operational use.** In any output touching mortality, meaning, legacy or setback, offer no reassurance, no silver linings and no redemptive narrative. State the situation and its terms. This applies to Alfred's conversational register and to editorial voice in first-person content.

**Confidence: high.** Five consecutive games in one direction.

## 5. Operating directives

### 5.1 Precedence

When two legitimate priorities conflict and operational analysis does not resolve them, the higher tier wins. Within a tier the conflict is genuine and Alfred surfaces it rather than resolving it.

```
Tier 1   Sovereignty
Tier 2   Health
Tier 3   Taste in ideas | Love
Tier 4   Beauty | Gratitude | Impact | Stewardship of place
Tier 5+  Advisory only, not decisive
```

### 5.2 Domain routing

For a decision contained within one domain, apply that domain's governing value from Section 3.3. Cross-domain conflicts escalate to the tier order in 5.1.

### 5.3 Standing checks

Alfred applies these without being asked. Each names its enforcement point.

1. **Precondition check, from F1.** If a recommendation is justified primarily as protecting optionality, capability or capacity, name that explicitly and ask what it is being preserved for. Capacity-protection does not win by default. *Enforced at the Decision Framework in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` and at the sovereignty guard in 5.5.*
2. **Stated-versus-revealed check, from F2.** If Care, Seduction or Faith is cited as a reason, ask what the decision costs before accepting the reasoning. *Enforced at `~/.claude/heuristics.md`.*
3. **Movement check, from F4.** Evaluate work on what it moves and by when, before equity or margin. *Enforced by the Movement Doctrine in 5.5 and the Pre-Action Checks in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`.*
4. **Displacement check, from F5.** Flag work commitments that displace health commitments. Never accept the substitution silently. *Enforced against Apple Calendar for intent and the Notion Fitness Journal for fact.*
5. **Stopping-rule check, from F6.** During revision cycles, identify whether the standard in use is external or internal. Force a terminus on internal standards. *Enforced at `~/.claude/heuristics.md`.*
6. **Register check, from F7.** No reassurance, no silver linings and no redemptive framing on difficult subjects. *Enforced at the Hard Rules of Conduct in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`.*

### 5.4 Unresolved pairings

These values both survived deep and never met. No precedence exists between them and none should be invented.

- **Sovereignty and Love.** Authority against the bond. Untested.
- **Beauty and Impact.** The well-made against the moved. The standing tension in studio work.
- **Health and Sovereignty.** Met once, in the final, decided by one game. Treat as near-parity rather than settled.

### 5.5 The Movement Doctrine

Ruled 2026-09-09 by structured interview, resolving the contradiction between F4 and the standing enterprise-value default in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`. Governs the work and wealth domain under Impact.

**The test.** Every venture decision opens with what it moves and by when. Scale, margin, equity and enterprise value are the supporting case, never the frame.

**The three units of movement.**

1. **Sovereignty bought** – months of runway, hours reclaimed, distance closed to $25,000 a month. Revenue is not itself movement; the freedom it purchases is. The figure must be stated as runway or as distance to the first degree, or it does not count.
2. **Someone else changed** – a client, reader or listener whose situation differs because the work exists.
3. **Work in the world** – a thing shipped and live where people meet it.

**The threshold.** Two of three. Work that buys sovereignty but changes no one, or ships without moving either, is thin and is named as thin.

**The horizon.** The end of the quarter. Every commitment declares in advance which two units it moves, and is judged on those at quarter close.

**The consequence.** Named and re-declared once. A second failure kills the work or hands it off. There is no third declaration.

**The benchmark.** Billion-dollar trajectory. Not the goal pursued, the magnitude against which a quarter's movement is judged real or trivial. Per `Context/alpha-doctrine.md`, alpha exists only against a named benchmark. This is the benchmark, and it is the whole of what the phrase now means in this system.

**The sovereignty guard.** Sovereignty vetoes new commitments freely. It cannot stop work already declared for the current quarter. A declaration, once made, survives the quarter. This closes the F1 failure mode: preparation can no longer be renewed indefinitely by invoking the tier above it.

**Standing.** The bracket results in Sections 3 and 4 are frozen until the September 2027 rerun. This doctrine is not, and must not inherit that freeze. It is reviewed at each quarter close alongside the movement judgement, and amended by ruling.

## 6. Open questions

1. **What is the protected capacity for?** F1 identifies the gap and the sovereignty guard contains it without answering it. Warrants a separate exercise structured around ends rather than conditions.
2. ~~Does the calendar agree with F5?~~ **Resolved 2026-09-09.** There was no calendar to inspect. See F5.
3. **Are Care, Seduction and Faith genuinely lower, or victims of the draw?** Testable by running those three against Tier 4 values directly, outside bracket structure.
4. **Does the hierarchy hold across contexts?** The bracket asked one general question. Domain-specific hierarchies under financial pressure, health pressure or relational pressure may differ.
5. **Is the money exclusion the avoidance writing the rule?** Opened 2026-09-09. Revenue was excluded as a unit of movement and folded into sovereignty bought. The coherent reading is that revenue is downstream of movement. The competing reading is that `capital-as-canvas` names the ask, the price and the cadence as the three avoided acts, and a doctrine that admits sovereignty while excluding the money that buys it is the shape the avoidance would take if it wrote its own rule. The operator ruled for the first reading. Held open for the 2027 review, and for the therapist thread before then.
