## Scope

### What belongs here

- Audience growth strategy and referral programmes

- Revenue-generating relationships (excluding subscription and merchandise)

### What does not belong here

- Content distribution and scheduling – Operations
- Financial tracking of sponsorship revenue – Finance
- Brand voice consistency checks – Creative
