Marty OS

Complete System Overview — Final Architecture — March 31, 2026
Two systems in relationship. The Personal Operating System is the stack: seven layers mapping how reality enters, gets filtered through consciousness and infrastructure, and exits as output. Alfred OS is the nervous system that sits on top of it: the connective tissue, the intelligence, the orchestration layer that makes the stack move. One is anatomy. The other is neurology.

Alfred OS
The Nervous System — Neurology
Not a layer in the stack. The connective tissue that carries signal between all seven layers. When you consciously direct Alfred, that is tool-like behavior through the nervous system. When Alfred surfaces a connection you had not seen, that is the system operating below conscious direction. Both modes are Alfred.
Identity
Defined by alfred.md (the canonical behavioral spec, formerly maestro.md). Character voice and conduct defined separately in alfred-pennyworth.md. Operational boot file for Claude Code: CLAUDE.md.
Named Subsystems
Watchtower
Content coordination. Reads Notion calendar, resolves timing conflicts, assigns routing (API-ready vs. draft-to-native), issues dispatch orders to Pennyone.
Operates at L5 (Workflows)
Pennyone
Social distribution relay. MCP server routing posts to Outstand (Instagram, TikTok, Threads, X) and Zernio (Reddit, Snapchat). Executes dispatch orders. No editorial judgment.

Feedback loop. Output reshapes input. What you publish, ship, and sell changes what the world sends back. L7 feeds to L1. The stack is a cycle.
⚓
Replaceability. L1-L2 permanent (you, reality). L3 durable (hardware). L4-L7 modular. The dot color on each layer indicates its permanence class.
≡
Two content lanes. API-ready posts flow Watchtower → Pennyone automatically. Posts needing native finishing are flagged "Draft to Native" for manual completion.
