# Strategy

Audience research, platform intelligence, cultural positioning and competitive analysis. The thinking behind the work – what Creative builds, what Growth pursues and what Production ships is informed here first.

## Structure

```
Strategy/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Audience/              -- Subscriber demographics, engagement behaviour, segment maps
  Platforms/             -- Channel performance, algorithm awareness, format fit
  Cultural/              -- Emerging movements, zeitgeist, trend timing
  Competitive/           -- Adjacent creators, reference brands, category shifts
  Agents/                -- Studio-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Audience research, segmentation and behaviour analysis
- Platform-specific intelligence – algorithm shifts, format performance, competitive positioning
- Cultural trend sensing – what is emerging, what is fading, timing of waves
- Competitive intelligence on adjacent creators and reference brands
- Strategic positioning decisions for the brand

### What does not belong here

- Editorial direction and content pillars – those go in Creative
- Partnership execution – that goes in Growth
- Production workflows – those go in Production

## Key Context

- **Target audience:** Cultural creatives, entrepreneurs, creators at the intersection of taste, systems thinking and ambition
- **Platform coverage:** Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Substack, Threads, X, Reddit
- **Competitive set:** To be mapped – cultural commentators and creator-led media brands

## Agent Instructions

- Strategy work is primarily Reconnaissance and Direction phases
- Explorer and Strategist crews operate here most frequently
- Research briefs are Tier 1 (full autonomy). Strategic recommendations are Tier 2.
- Positioning decisions that affect audience-facing work are Tier 3 (approval required).

### Token Budget Defaults

| Task | Tier |
|---|---|
| Quick data lookups, platform stat pulls | Light |
| Audience briefs, platform analyses, competitive scans | Standard |
| Full positioning frameworks, strategic territory plans | Heavy |
