# Finance

Revenue models, cost tracking, projections, tax and financial health of the media company. The numbers.

## Structure

```
Finance/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Revenue/               -- Revenue tracking by stream (subscriptions, sponsorships, merch, speaking, consulting)
  Expenses/              -- Operating costs, tool subscriptions, production costs
  Projections/           -- Forecasts, budgets, financial models
  Metrics/               -- MRR, subscriber revenue, sponsorship revenue, KPIs
  Tax/                   -- Tax preparation, filings, documentation
  Agents/                -- Studio-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Revenue stream definitions and tracking
- Cost management and budgets
- Financial projections and forecasting
- Tax preparation and compliance
- Metrics and KPIs

### What does not belong here

- Personal finances – those live in the personal Notion workspace
- Five Points financial data – that is a separate venture entirely
- Partnership negotiation terms – those go in Growth
