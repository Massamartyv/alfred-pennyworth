### Product Descriptions

### Epiphany Newsletter

Cultural commentary, curated ideas and perspective pieces delivered via Substack. The written voice of the Architect of Vibe. Subscribers receive a window into how culture, creativity and entrepreneurship intersect.

### Marty Gras Podcast

Long-form conversations and solo episodes exploring culture, creativity, personal development and the entrepreneurial journey. Produced with ElevenLabs for audio excellence. Video versions for YouTube.

### Social Content

Platform-native cultural curation -- visual storytelling on Instagram, thought leadership on LinkedIn, short-form commentary on TikTok and Threads. Each platform gets content shaped to its native language, not cross-posted identically.

### Current State

All products are in early development. Formats, cadences and editorial calendars to be established.
