# Human Resources -- Marty Gras

Team, contractors and collaborators. The people who contribute to the brand's output.

### What Belongs Here

- Team structure and roles
- Contractor and freelancer agreements
- Collaboration terms and expectations
- Hiring plans as the venture scales

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Podcast guest management -- that goes in Operations/Clientele
- Audience management -- that goes in Marketing & Sales
- Financial terms of contracts -- those go in Finances

### Current Structure

Solo operation. All creative direction, production and distribution handled personally with Alfred as the AI operating layer.

### Future Roles to Consider

| Role | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Podcast editor | When production cadence is weekly | Audio and video post-production |
| Social media assistant | When content volume exceeds solo capacity | Scheduling, engagement, community |
| Newsletter editor | When subscriber base justifies investment | Copy editing, formatting, QA |
| Graphic designer | When visual output demand exceeds personal capacity | Social assets, brand collateral |

### Current State

No team members or contractors. All work is solo. Revisit when revenue supports investment in people.
