# Human Resources – Paradigm

Team, contractors, advisors and collaborators. The people who contribute to the brand's growth and output. Human Resources is now its own department – it no longer sits nested under Administration.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Team structure and role definitions
- Contractor and freelancer agreements
- Advisory board and mentor relationships
- Hiring plans as the venture scales
- Culture and values documentation
- Onboarding documents for new contributors

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Customer accounts – those go in Operations/Clientele
- Influencer and ambassador programmes – those go in Business Development/Affiliates and Ambassadors
- Financial terms of contracts – those go in Finances (contract documents themselves live in Administration/Legal)
