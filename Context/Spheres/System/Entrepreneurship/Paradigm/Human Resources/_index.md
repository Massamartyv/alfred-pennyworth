# Human Resources -- Paradigm

Team, contractors, advisors and collaborators. The people who contribute to the brand's growth and output.

### What Belongs Here

- Team structure and roles
- Contractor and freelancer agreements
- Advisory board and mentor relationships
- Hiring plans as the venture scales
- Culture and values documentation

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Customer accounts -- those go in Operations/Clientele
- Influencer and ambassador management -- that goes in Business Development
- Financial terms of contracts -- those go in Finances

### Current Structure

Solo operation. All research, strategy and development handled personally with Alfred as the AI operating layer.

### Future Roles to Consider

| Role | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Formulation chemist | When product development begins | R&D and formulation expertise |
| Graphic designer | When brand identity and packaging are in development | Visual assets and packaging design |
| Operations manager | When fulfilment volume exceeds solo capacity | Supply chain and order management |
| Marketing lead | When products are ready for market | Brand marketing and growth strategy |

### Current State

No team members or contractors. All work is solo. Revisit when the venture moves beyond ideation.
