# Growth – Paradigm

Revenue generation and channel architecture. Sales, partnerships, wholesale, distribution and direct-to-consumer. Growth owns every path from a finished product to a paying customer.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Wholesale programme – retailer outreach, buyer relationships, trade show strategy
- Distribution – regional and national distributor relationships, logistics alignment
- DTC channel – e-commerce storefront, paid acquisition, email, retention, subscriptions
- Partnership development – aligned wellness brands, practitioner networks, co-branding
- Affiliate and ambassador programmes – influencers, practitioners, affiliates
- Sales pipeline and revenue-generating outreach
- Offer architecture – product bundles, launch offers, subscription tiers

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Product formulation and manufacturing – those go in Production
- Brand identity and creative direction – those go in Creative
- Wholesale account management after the deal closes – that transitions to Operations
- Revenue tracking and reporting – that goes in Finance
