# Product Development – Paradigm

The execution engine, and the venture's own products and builds. Where formulas are developed, products are manufactured, packaging is produced and content is made – alongside the offer architecture that packages those products for sale and the venture's own flagship builds. Product Development owns the build quality across everything the brand puts into the world.

Carries the whole of the retired Production department, plus the offer architecture ground of the retired Growth department.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Product formulation R&D – ingredient selection, formula iteration, stability, efficacy
- Manufacturing coordination – CMO selection, production runs, batch records
- Packaging production – sourcing, print, material specification
- Content production – photography, video, social assets, long-form
- Quality assurance workflows prior to release
- Production SOP source files (registry lives in Operations)
- Offer architecture – product bundles, launch offers, subscription tiers
- Eden Theory – the flagship seasonal guide series, the venture's own product

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Brand and aesthetic direction – that goes in Marketing & Sales
- Fulfilment and inventory operations – those go in Operations
- Regulatory compliance review of labels and claims – that goes in Administration
- Marketing and sales execution, DTC channel – those go in Marketing & Sales
- Wholesale and distribution channel building – that goes in Business Development
- Revenue tracking – that goes in Finances
