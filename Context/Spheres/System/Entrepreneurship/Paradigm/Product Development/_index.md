# Product Development -- Paradigm

Formulations, product lines, packaging, R&D and everything that defines what the brand creates and sells.

### What Belongs Here

- Product line definitions and roadmap
- Formulation research and ingredient profiles
- Packaging design direction and specifications
- Pricing strategy at the product level
- New product ideation and development pipeline
- Product iteration notes and version history

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Fulfilment and supply chain workflows -- those go in Operations
- Marketing campaigns -- those go in Marketing & Sales
- Revenue tracking -- that goes in Finances

### Product Lines

| Line | Category | Status |
|---|---|---|
| To be defined | To be defined | Ideation |

### Development Pipeline

Products will move through four stages:

1. **Ideation** -- concept, market research, ingredient exploration
2. **Formulation** -- development, testing, iteration
3. **Validation** -- regulatory review, consumer testing, cost analysis
4. **Launch** -- production, packaging, go-to-market

### Current State

Pre-development. Product category, formulations and initial product line to be defined during ideation phase.
