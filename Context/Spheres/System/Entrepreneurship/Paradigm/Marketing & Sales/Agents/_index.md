# Marketing & Sales – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Marketing & Sales department is the aesthetic standard-bearer for Paradigm, and the path from that voice to a sale. Brand identity, packaging direction, content aesthetics and the DTC channel are set here. Everything that carries the Paradigm name is held to the standard defined in `Criteria/` (to be populated). Carries the whole specialist roster of the retired Creative department, plus the DTC Lead from the retired Growth department.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Marketing & Sales role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Marketing & Sales** | Department head. Holds the standard across all creative and DTC output. | All | – |
| **Brand Strategist** | Brand positioning, identity evolution, category differentiation. Writes the brief. | Direction | Researcher |
| **Art Director** | Visual direction for packaging, web, social, print. Directs to the brief. | Execution | Creator |
| **Packaging Designer** | Structural and graphic packaging design, unboxing experience, shelf presence. | Execution | Creator |
| **Content Strategist** | Content calendar, platform strategy, editorial planning. | Direction | Researcher, Mediator |
| **Copywriter** | Product copy, web copy, label copy, social copy. | Execution | Creator |
| **DTC Lead** | E-commerce, landing pages, paid acquisition, retention, subscriptions. | Execution | Creator, Broadcaster |

### Role activation

Not every task activates every role. A social caption may only need the Copywriter. A full brand identity and packaging build activates the full creative roster; a DTC campaign engages only the DTC Lead.
