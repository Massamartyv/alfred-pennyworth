# Marketing & Sales – Paradigm

The aesthetic standard-bearer of the brand, and the path from that voice to a sale. Everything that carries the Paradigm name passes through here – visual identity, packaging, content, tone – alongside the direct-to-consumer channel and the sales process that turns a visitor into a customer. Marketing & Sales sets the look, feel and voice; every other department executes against it.

Carries the whole of the retired Creative department, plus the DTC channel ground of the retired Growth department.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Brand identity system – logo, colour, typography, visual language
- Packaging aesthetics – structural design direction, label design, unboxing experience
- Brand voice and tone documentation
- Visual direction for photography, video and social content
- Content aesthetic standards – editorial cadence, wellness education register
- Creative briefs and reference libraries
- Aesthetic alignment with `creative-director.md`
- DTC channel – e-commerce storefront, paid acquisition, email, retention, subscriptions
- Sales process, discovery, proposals and pricing communication to the customer

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Physical packaging production – that goes in Product Development
- Wholesale, distribution and partnership channel building – that goes in Business Development
- Regulatory label compliance review – that goes in Administration
- Ingredient science and formulation – that goes in Product Development
- Offer architecture and pricing structure – that goes in Product Development
- Revenue tracking – that goes in Finances
