# Marketing & Sales -- Paradigm

Brand marketing, sales channels, community building and customer engagement. How the brand reaches people and earns their trust.

### What Belongs Here

- Brand positioning and messaging strategy
- Platform-specific marketing strategies
- Content marketing and educational content
- Community management and engagement
- Sales channel management -- e-commerce, retail, subscriptions
- Email marketing and CRM
- Analytics and performance tracking by channel

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Product development and formulation -- that goes in Product Development
- Wholesale and distribution partnerships -- those go in Business Development
- Revenue tracking -- that goes in Finances
