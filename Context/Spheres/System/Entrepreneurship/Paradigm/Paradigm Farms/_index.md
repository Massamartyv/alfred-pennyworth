# Paradigm Farms – Sub-Brand Index

Nutrition pillar of Paradigm, structured as a sub-brand with its own visual identity and voice register – the Studio-under-Lululemon model. First product: a native iOS application for AR plant management and horticultural education, with visionOS as the premium tier from the same codebase.

This is the first formalized sub-brand in the portfolio. A sub-brand keeps a light structure inside its parent venture rather than the full seven-studio layout: Foundation for its fingerprint, Production for build documentation. It inherits parent-venture governance (Agents/, brand fingerprint authority) until its own Foundation matures.
