# Finances – Paradigm

Revenue, COGS, expenses, projections and the financial health of the venture. Finance tracks the numbers and surfaces the signal in them.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Revenue tracking by channel – DTC, wholesale, distribution, subscriptions
- COGS tracking – ingredients, packaging, manufacturing, fulfilment, freight
- Operating expenses by category
- Financial projections, scenario modelling and forecasts
- Unit economics – margin, LTV, CAC, payback period
- Tax preparation, sales tax by state, filings
- Pricing analysis at the venture level (product-level pricing strategy lives in Marketing & Sales)
- Monthly and quarterly P&L, cash flow and management reports

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Personal finances – those live in the personal Notion workspace
- Other venture financial data – each venture is isolated
- Supplier contracts and legal terms – those go in Administration
- Invoice operations for individual customers – those go in Operations
