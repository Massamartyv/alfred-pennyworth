# Business Development – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Business Development department owns revenue generation and channel architecture, plus positioning and consumer insight. Wholesale, distribution, partnerships, affiliates, sales pipeline and the point of view on who the customer is and where the brand sits against the category all live here. Carries the specialist roster of the retired Growth department's channel roles and the retired Strategy department's positioning and consumer-insight roles.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Business Development role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Business Development** | Department head. Owns revenue generation, channel strategy and positioning. | All | – |
| **Wholesale Lead** | Retailer outreach, wholesale programme, buyer relationships. | Reconnaissance, Execution | Researcher |
| **Distribution Lead** | Regional and national distributor relationships, channel terms. | Reconnaissance, Direction | Researcher, Mediator |
| **Partnership Scout** | Aligned wellness brands, practitioner networks, co-branding. | Reconnaissance | Researcher |
| **Affiliate and Ambassador Lead** | Influencer programmes, practitioner ambassadors, affiliate operations. | Execution | Broadcaster |
| **Consumer Insight Lead** | Target customer profiles, behavioural segmentation, purchase drivers. | Reconnaissance, Direction | Researcher |
| **Positioning Lead** | Brand positioning, differentiation frameworks, narrative architecture. | Direction | Researcher |

### Role activation

A routine retailer outreach push engages only the Wholesale Lead. A national distribution push activates Wholesale, Distribution and Partnership Scout together. A repositioning exercise activates Consumer Insight Lead and Positioning Lead.
