# Business Development – Paradigm

Revenue generation and channel architecture, plus the market thinking that points it. Wholesale, distribution, partnerships, affiliates and ambassadors, sales pipeline, positioning and consumer insight. Business Development owns every path from a finished product to a paying customer, and the point of view on who that customer is and where the brand sits against the category.

Carries the wholesale, distribution, partnership and pipeline ground of the retired Growth department, and the positioning and consumer-insight ground of the retired Strategy department.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Wholesale programme – retailer outreach, buyer relationships, trade show strategy
- Distribution – regional and national distributor relationships, logistics alignment
- Partnership development – aligned wellness brands, practitioner networks, co-branding
- Affiliate and ambassador programmes – influencers, practitioners, affiliates
- Sales pipeline and revenue-generating outreach
- Consumer behaviour research – purchase drivers, ritual patterns, segment mapping
- Positioning frameworks and differentiation architecture
- Strategic briefs on channel, partnership and positioning that feed the Direction phase across other departments

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Product formulation and manufacturing – those go in Product Development
- Offer architecture – product bundles, launch offers, subscription tiers – that goes in Product Development
- Brand identity and creative direction – those go in Marketing & Sales
- DTC channel, sales process, discovery, proposals and pricing communication – those go in Marketing & Sales
- Wholesale account management after the deal closes – that transitions to Operations
- Revenue tracking and reporting – that goes in Finances
- Industry research and competitive intelligence – that goes in Knowledge Base
