# Business Development -- Paradigm

Partnerships, wholesale distribution, strategic alliances and pipeline development. The engine that opens doors and expands reach beyond direct-to-consumer.

### What Belongs Here

- Retail and wholesale partnership pipeline
- Distributor relationships and negotiations
- Strategic brand partnerships and co-branding
- Influencer and ambassador programme development
- Industry event strategy and trade show presence
- Licensing opportunities

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Day-to-day marketing campaigns -- those go in Marketing & Sales
- Product formulation and development -- those go in Product Development
- Revenue tracking -- that goes in Finances

### Key Relationships to Develop

| Type | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Retail partners | Boutique wellness retailers, specialty stores | Pipeline to be built |
| Distributors | Regional and national distribution | Pipeline to be built |
| Brand partners | Aligned wellness, fitness and lifestyle brands | Pipeline to be built |
| Influencers | Wellness creators and practitioners | Pipeline to be built |

### Current State

No active business development pipeline. Focus remains on product development and brand identity during ideation.
