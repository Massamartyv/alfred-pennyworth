# Life Time Fitness

**Who** – Premium multi-amenity health and fitness destination chain operating at a scale and ambition level above the conventional gym category: large-format clubs with spa, childcare, café, pools, programming and membership communities designed as a complete lifestyle destination rather than a workout facility.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Paradigm fingerprint (Layer 1 scaffold) specifically for "the premium destination." Paradigm's Spaces pillar – signature premium spaces as the third of three forms – aspires to the destination register, not the studio or gym register. Life Time sets the benchmark for what a premium physical wellness destination looks and feels like at scale. The relevant extraction is scale of ambition and completeness of the in-space experience, not the suburban-format execution.

**The proposition** – Card pending enrichment. Core mapping: Life Time demonstrates that a wellness space can be a complete destination – one that commands a meaningfully higher commitment from members because the experience is categorically different from the gym model. Paradigm's Spaces pillar draws this ambition: a Paradigm space should feel like arrival, not access. The destination quality is the extraction. The format, scale, and mass-market footprint are not.
