# Alo Yoga

**Who** – Premium wellness lifestyle brand that operates as an ecosystem of activewear products, a method (Alo Moves digital content platform) and physical spaces (Alo Sanctuaries and retail flagships) – all under one brand and one aesthetic register.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Paradigm fingerprint (Layer 1 scaffold) specifically for "the product-method-space ecosystem." That three-part structure – owned physical product, a taught method distributed via media, and branded spaces for community and experience – is precisely the tri-form architecture Paradigm is building. Alo is the most direct structural analogue in the wellness category.

**The proposition** – Card pending enrichment. Core mapping: Alo demonstrates that a wellness brand can hold three distinct commercial forms (product, media, space) under one identity without any form diluting the others. Paradigm inherits the architecture, not the aesthetic – Alo's register skews California-cool and accessible-aspirational; Paradigm's register sits at the premium-integrated pole anchored by Equinox's sensuality and status. The ecosystem structure is the extraction; the look and feel is not.
