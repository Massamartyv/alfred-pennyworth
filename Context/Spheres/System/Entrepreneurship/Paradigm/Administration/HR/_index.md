# HR – Paradigm

Team, contractors, advisors and collaborators. The people who contribute to the brand's growth and output. HR sits under Administration as part of the governance structure.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Team structure and role definitions
- Contractor and freelancer agreements
- Advisory board and mentor relationships
- Hiring plans as the venture scales
- Culture and values documentation
- Onboarding documents for new contributors

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Customer accounts – those go in Operations/Clientele
- Influencer and ambassador programmes – those go in Growth/Affiliates and Ambassadors
- Financial terms of contracts – those go in Finance (contract documents themselves live in Administration/Legal)

## Current Structure

Solo operation. All research, strategy and early development handled personally with Alfred as the AI operating layer.

## Future Roles to Consider

| Role | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Formulation chemist | When product development begins in earnest | R&D and formulation expertise |
| Regulatory consultant | When first product enters validation | FDA labelling review, structure-function claim compliance |
| Graphic designer | When brand identity and packaging are in development | Visual assets and packaging design |
| Operations manager | When fulfilment volume exceeds solo capacity | Supply chain and order management |
| Growth lead | When products are ready for market | Channel strategy and acquisition |
