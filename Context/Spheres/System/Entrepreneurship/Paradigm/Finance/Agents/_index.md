# Finance – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Finance studio owns revenue, COGS, expenses, projections and the numbers that reveal the health of the venture. Finance tracks the signal in the numbers and surfaces what matters.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Finance role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Finance** | Studio head. Owns the numbers. | All | – |
| **Revenue Analyst** | Sales tracking by channel, cohort analysis. | Execution | Strategist, Creator |
| **Cost Analyst** | COGS tracking, margin analysis, supplier cost management. | Execution | Strategist |
| **Financial Reporter** | P&L, cash flow, monthly and quarterly reports. | Release | Creator |
| **Compliance Validator** | Tax readiness, platform compliance, audit trails, sales tax by state. | Critique | Validator |

### Role activation

A routine invoice reconciliation engages only the Revenue Analyst. Quarterly reporting pulls Revenue Analyst, Cost Analyst and Financial Reporter together.
