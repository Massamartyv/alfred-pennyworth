## Executive Summary

Eden Theory is the nourishment arm of Paradigm – the philosophy of return, peace as a state of being. Its first commercial expression is a series of four seasonal guides, each an integrated season of return pairing whole-food seasonal recipes with bodyweight movement rituals carrying weighted alternatives. Movement is medicine; the guides deliver recognition of a healthier state of being rather than a clinical protocol. The register is secular-poetic – Eden as a light philosophical note, seasonality, craft and science leading.

Volume one is *Of Harvest & Hearth* (autumn), shipping early October 2026. The cycle continues with *Stillness & Spice* (winter), *Bloom Ritual* (spring), *A Labor of Love* (summer 2027), then the box set *A Year of Return*. This series is the flagship form that proves the Paradigm standard – the fingerprint's three-year milestone made concrete.

## 1. Operating Design – Dinner as Shoot

The anthology, the October date and the 10-hour week coexist only if production fuses into the operator's existing life. The nightly dinner is the camera cook: four to five nights weekly, the operator cooks a longlisted recipe, styles and shoots before eating. Twelve weeks at that rhythm yields 48 to 60 dishes – the anthology.

Division of labour:

| Line | Owner |
|---|---|
| Recipe longlist and development drafts from Notion history | Alfred (michelin-chef skill) |
| Test and camera cooks, styling, photography | Operator |
| Retouch pre-selects and processing pipeline | Alfred prepares, operator finishes |
| Seasonal essays and all prose | Alfred drafts, operator voices |
| Book design and layout system (150-plus pages) | Alfred |
| Waitlist site, channel scaffolding, launch assets | Alfred |
| Rails – LLC filing prep, Stripe, storefront | Alfred prepares, operator approves |
| Paradigm Farms v1 queue | Alfred, operator device-tests |

The shoot line doubles as the content engine: outtakes and process frames feed the Paradigm Instagram at three or more posts weekly without separate content production.

## 3. Economics – First Cycle, Engineered Floors

Warm audience seed: 2,000 to 10,000 across platforms. Planning band for the waitlist by pre-order with disciplined content: 400 to 1,000 – the floor to beat, not the ceiling. Launch conversion 8 to 15%.

- Volume one: 40 to 150 copies at ~$64 average → $2,600 to $9,600
- Year passes: 15 to 30 at $179 → $2,700 to $5,400
- Cycle one gross: roughly $5,000 to $15,000 against a $2,500 to $7,500 budget

Year-one arc across four drops plus the box set with a compounding list: $30,000 to $80,000 is the planning band for this series alone. The $83,333-per-month figure from early ideation is the full-product-family run rate – Paragon Guide, concierge, apparel, retreats – that this series exists to unlock. Cycle one's job is to prove the standard, forge the devoted core and compound the list that carries the house to that rate.
