# Production – Paradigm

The execution engine. Where formulas are developed, products are manufactured, packaging is produced and content is made. Production owns the build quality across everything the brand puts into the world.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Product formulation R&D – ingredient selection, formula iteration, stability, efficacy
- Manufacturing coordination – CMO selection, production runs, batch records
- Packaging production – sourcing, print, material specification
- Content production – photography, video, social assets, long-form
- Quality assurance workflows prior to release
- Production SOP source files (registry lives in Operations)

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Brand and aesthetic direction – that goes in Creative
- Fulfilment and inventory operations – those go in Operations
- Regulatory compliance review of labels and claims – that goes in Administration
- Marketing and sales execution – those go in Growth
