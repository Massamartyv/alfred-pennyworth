# Strategy – Paradigm

Market intelligence, competitive analysis, positioning and consumer behaviour. The thinking behind the work. What Creative builds, what Production formulates, what Growth sells and what Administration protects is informed here first.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Wellness industry landscape analysis
- Category intelligence – supplements, cosmetics, functional foods, wellness tech
- Competitive intelligence – incumbents, emerging brands, pricing maps
- Consumer behaviour research – purchase drivers, ritual patterns, segment mapping
- Positioning frameworks and differentiation architecture
- Whitespace analysis and category trend forecasting
- Strategic briefs that feed into Direction phase across the other studios

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Active formulation work – that goes in Production
- Active growth campaigns – those go in Growth
- Ingredient science reference library – that lives in Knowledge Base
- Regulatory and legal analysis – that goes in Administration

Pre-positioning. Strategy is the first studio to light up during ideation – competitive landscape, consumer behaviour and positioning must be understood before formulation and brand identity lock in.
