# Knowledge Base – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

Institutional memory for {Venture Name}. Industry research, competitive intelligence, consumer insight, case studies and methodology documentation. Knowledge Base is a department in its own right, and the Knowledge Curator role sits across departments.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Knowledge Base role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Knowledge Base** | Department head. Owns institutional memory. | All | – |
| **Knowledge Curator** | Captures research, indexes intelligence, maintains case study library and methodology documentation. | Release | Researcher, Creator |
| **Market Researcher** | Industry landscape, category trend detection, whitespace analysis. | Reconnaissance | Researcher |
| **Competitive Analyst** | Incumbent and emerging competitor monitoring, positioning maps, pricing intelligence. | Reconnaissance | Researcher |
| **Consumer Insight Lead** | Target customer profiles, behavioural segmentation, purchase drivers. | Reconnaissance, Direction | Researcher |

### Role activation

Research capture is ongoing. Case study additions trigger when industry moves or internal wins justify documentation. Methodology additions trigger when new frameworks prove themselves across multiple executions. Quick category lookups engage only the Market Researcher; full positioning support pulls Competitive Analyst and Consumer Insight Lead alongside.
