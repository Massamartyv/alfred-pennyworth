# Marketing & Sales – {Venture Name}

Brand identity and creative direction, visual and content standards, marketing, and the sales process once a lead is qualified – discovery, proposals, pricing. Marketing & Sales sets the look, feel and voice of the venture and carries the qualified lead through to the close.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Brand identity system – logo, colour, typography, visual language
- Brand voice and tone documentation
- Visual direction for photography, video and social content
- Content aesthetic standards – editorial cadence, platform register
- Creative briefs and reference libraries
- Aesthetic alignment with `creative-director.md`
- Sales process – discovery calls, proposals, pricing, deal management

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Physical production of assets – that goes in Product Development
- Prospecting, outreach and pipeline generation – those go in Business Development
- Partnership development and channel strategy – those go in Business Development
- Regulatory or compliance review – that goes in Administration
- Financial tracking – that goes in Finances
- Account management after the deal closes – that transitions to Operations
