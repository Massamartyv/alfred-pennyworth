# Marketing & Sales

Sales execution, proposal generation, pricing strategy, and the venture's own brand marketing. Everything from qualified lead to signed contract lives here.

## Agent Instructions

- Use `Proposal Templates/` as the skeleton when drafting any proposal. Never start from scratch.
- `pricing-strategy.md` defines the rules. Never discount -- add value instead.
- Load `objection-handling.md` before any sales-adjacent conversation.
- `content-marketing-strategy.md` guides the venture's own content (not client content -- that is in Operations/).
- All proposals require human approval before sending.

## What Belongs Here

- Sales process documentation
- Proposal and SOW templates
- Pricing and negotiation frameworks
- The venture's own brand and content strategy
- Objection handling and closing playbooks

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Lead generation and prospecting (Business Development/)
- Client content and deliverables (Operations/Clientele/)
- Offer definitions and tier structures (Product Development/)
