## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Creative** | Studio head. Holds the standard across all creative output. | All | – |
| **Brand Strategist** | Brand positioning, identity evolution, category differentiation. Writes the brief. | Direction | Researcher |
| **Art Director** | Visual direction for all design output across touchpoints. | Execution | Creator |
| **Content Strategist** | Content calendar, platform strategy, editorial planning. | Direction | Researcher, Creator |
| **Copywriter** | Product copy, web copy, social copy, platform-specific content. | Execution | Creator |

### Role activation

Not every task activates every role. A social caption may only need the Copywriter. A full brand identity build activates the full roster.
