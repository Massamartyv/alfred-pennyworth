# Creative – {Venture Name}

The aesthetic standard-bearer of the venture. Everything that carries the {Venture Name} name passes through here – visual identity, content, tone. Creative sets the look, feel and voice; every other studio executes against it.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Brand identity system – logo, colour, typography, visual language
- Brand voice and tone documentation
- Visual direction for photography, video and social content
- Content aesthetic standards – editorial cadence, platform register
- Creative briefs and reference libraries
- Aesthetic alignment with `creative-director.md`

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Physical production of assets – that goes in Production
- Marketing campaign execution – that goes in Growth
- Regulatory or compliance review – that goes in Administration
- Financial tracking – that goes in Finance
