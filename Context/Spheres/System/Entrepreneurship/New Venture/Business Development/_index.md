# Business Development – {Venture Name}

ICP, prospecting and outreach, partnerships, pipeline, channels, positioning and market strategy. Business Development owns the path from the market to the door – who the venture is for, where they are found and how the relationship opens.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Ideal customer profile and target segment definitions
- Sales pipeline – prospecting, outreach and pipeline generation
- Partnership development – aligned brands, networks, co-branding
- Channel strategy – direct, wholesale, distribution, digital acquisition
- Positioning frameworks and differentiation architecture
- Whitespace analysis and category trend forecasting that feeds market strategy
- Strategic briefs that direct positioning across the other departments

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Product development and manufacturing – those go in Product Development
- Brand identity and creative direction – those go in Marketing & Sales
- Sales process, discovery, proposals and pricing once a lead is qualified – those go in Marketing & Sales
- Account management after the deal closes – that transitions to Operations
- Revenue tracking and reporting – that goes in Finances
- Reference library and research archive – that lives in Knowledge Base
