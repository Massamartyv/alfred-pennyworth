# Product Development – {Venture Name}

The execution engine. Where products or services are developed, built and produced, and where the offer architecture that packages them lives – tiers, bundles, offer strategy and audits.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Core product or service development – specification, iteration, build
- Supplier and manufacturer coordination – sourcing, production runs, batch records
- Asset production – photography, video, social assets, long-form content
- Quality assurance workflows prior to release
- Production SOP source files (registry lives in Operations)
- Offer catalogue – bundles, tiers, subscription and launch offers
- Offer strategy and offer audits

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Brand and aesthetic direction – that goes in Marketing & Sales
- Fulfilment and delivery operations – those go in Operations
- Regulatory compliance review – that goes in Administration
- Prospecting, partnerships and channel strategy – those go in Business Development
- Discovery, proposals and pricing conversations – those go in Marketing & Sales
