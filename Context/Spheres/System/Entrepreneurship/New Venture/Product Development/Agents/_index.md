# Product Development – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Product Development department is the execution engine. Development, supplier coordination, content production, quality assurance and the offer architecture that packages what gets built live here. Everything the venture delivers, and how it is sold, is built through this department.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Product Development role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Product Development** | Department head. Owns build quality and the offer architecture. | All | – |
| **Development Lead** | Core product or service development, specification, iteration. | Execution | Creator |
| **Production Coordinator** | Supplier or manufacturer selection, production runs, quality. | Execution | Creator |
| **Content Producer** | Photography, video, short-form content production. | Execution | Creator |
| **Quality Assurance** | Pre-release review across products, assets and content. | Critique | Reviewer |

### Role activation

A single content shoot engages only the Content Producer and Quality Assurance. A new product or service launch, or a new offer built around it, activates the full roster.
