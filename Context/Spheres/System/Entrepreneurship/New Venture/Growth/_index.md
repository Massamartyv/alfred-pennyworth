# Growth – {Venture Name}

Revenue generation and channel architecture. Sales, partnerships, distribution and direct channels. Growth owns every path from a finished product or service to a paying customer or client.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Sales pipeline – outreach, proposals, deal management
- Partnership development – aligned brands, networks, co-branding
- Channel strategy – direct, wholesale, distribution, digital acquisition
- Affiliate and ambassador programmes
- Offer architecture – bundles, tiers, subscriptions, launch offers

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Product development and manufacturing – those go in Production
- Brand identity and creative direction – those go in Creative
- Account management after the deal closes – that transitions to Operations
- Revenue tracking and reporting – that goes in Finance
