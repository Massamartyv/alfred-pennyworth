## Governance – the Living Canon

The single source of truth for {Venture Name}. It has a rigid core and a free perimeter.

- **The invariant core** does not move without a named amendment and a re-derived chain. *List the three to six lines that, if changed, would make this a different brand. The non-negotiables.*
- **The flex layer** is everything else – devices, tactics, the reference set, the roadmap. It is expected to move.
- **Amendments are logged, never silent.** Each supersedes specific prior lines with a dated trace in the Amendment Log. Nothing is overwritten without memory.
- **Coherence cascades.** Layer 0's four questions are reverse-engineered, so a change upstream re-derives everything beneath it in the same pass, and any induced contradiction is surfaced, not absorbed.
- **The image is engraved, the platform rotates.** The oldest statement of this distinction in the trade is the instruction to decide what image the brand will hold, engrave it on a stone tablet and never change it, while the campaigns that carry it are expected to move. The invariant core is the tablet. The flex layer is the campaign. Confusing the two is how a brand redesigns its way out of its own recognition.

## Derivation – how the answers are arrived at

Layer 0 and Layer 1 are outputs, not opinions. This layer holds the instruments that produce them and the order they are run in. Nothing below this line may be filled from instinct alone.

**The order is fixed and it starts outside the brand.** Every instinct is to begin with what we want and what we are good at. Beginning there produces a brand that is about itself. The sequence below is inverted on purpose – the tension in the world is named and evidenced before the brand is allowed to speak about its own strengths.

**The trace rule.** Every answer in Layers 0, 1, 2 and 4 names the instrument that produced it and the date it was run, written as `[D3, 2026-09-10]`. An answer with no trace is an assertion, and an assertion cannot be audited when an amendment is proposed three years later.

**The five conditions.** An instrument has been run properly only when all five hold. They recur across every documented practice surveyed and each one is a failure mode in the negative.

1. Inputs are sourced from research, never invented in the room.
2. Elimination is forced, never optional.
3. One named owner breaks ties. Consensus is not a method.
4. The output is written as a checkable governing artefact, not a slide that gets archived.
5. The instrument matches the actual open question. Running a familiar instrument on the wrong question is itself the failure.

**The solo adaptation.** The published governance for these instruments assumes a room of stakeholders, and guards against consensus sanding the brand to the middle. A solo operator has the opposite exposure: every placement lands where the founder wishes the brand were rather than where it is. Self-flattery, unopposed. The correction is structural. The founder places; the agent is required to argue the opposing position on any placement the evidence does not support, before it is recorded. The sentence test is mandatory rather than a tiebreak.

---

### D1 – The Tension

*The state of the world this brand exists to stand against. Run first, always.*

Name one tension. Not a trend, not a complaint, not a market gap.

- **The trend test.** A trend passes and a tension persists. If it would not have been true five years ago and true in five years, it is a trend.
- **The evidence bar.** Three independent sources minimum, of which at least one is not commentary – behaviour data, a market fact, or a body of first-hand accounts. This bar is the standard's own addition; the source doctrine sets procedural safeguards but no evidentiary minimum, and a tension asserted without evidence is the most common way this whole layer becomes theatre.
- **The stress test.** State it to somebody outside the category. If it needs explaining, it is not yet a tension.

**Output** – one sentence naming the tension, with its three sources cited beneath.

---

### D2 – The Best Self

*The brand's strongest true claim. Run second, never first.*

Interrogate the brand until it confesses its strengths. This is an adversarial interview, not a list-making exercise – every claim is met with "prove it" and the ones that survive are the only ones that count. The claim must be true today, not aspirational.

**Output** – one sentence naming the brand at its best, with the proof that makes it credible.

---

### D3 – The Ideal

*The intersection of D1 and D2. The candidate spine sentence.*

Written in one fixed form:

> **{Brand} believes the world would be a better place if {X}.**

The form is borrowed deliberately. It forces the brand to hold a position about the world rather than a description of itself, and a brand that cannot complete the sentence honestly does not yet know what it is for.

**Output** – the Ideal sentence. It becomes the Layer 0 spine sentence unless a later instrument unseats it.

---

### D4 – The Word Sort

*The attribute set, and more importantly the negative space.*

Thirty to forty candidate words, sourced from the research rather than written in the room. Each is sorted individually, in silence, into one of three piles:

| Pile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **You Are** | True today, and defensible |
| **You Are Torn** | Wanted, not yet earned |
| **You Are Not** | Explicitly refused |

Then the sentence test on every survivor: write one concrete sentence in which the word does real work. A word that cannot produce that sentence is parked, which is what kills *innovative*, *authentic*, *premium* and *bold* before they ever reach the page.

**Output** – three to five attributes for Layer 1, and the You Are Not list, which becomes terminology governance in Layer 4. The refused pile carries more weight than the accepted one.

---

### D5 – The Spectra

*Design instructions, not descriptions.*

Four to six axes, each generated from a real competitive tension in this category rather than pulled from a standard set. Place a mark on each.

- **No midpoints.** A midpoint is a refusal to decide and it is recorded as an unresolved question, not as an answer.
- **Silent placement first**, before any discussion of the placement.
- **The opposing argument is mandatory** wherever the evidence does not support the mark. See the solo adaptation above.

A slider position is an instruction to whoever draws the type and picks the palette. It belongs to Layer 2, not to Layer 1.

**Output** – the axis set with marks and the one-line reasoning behind each.

---

### D6 – The Voice Dimensions

*Four axes, run last.*

Funny to serious. Formal to casual. Respectful to irreverent. Enthusiastic to matter-of-fact. This is the only instrument in the standard carrying published empirical validation behind it, which is why it is named specifically rather than left to taste.

**Output** – four placements feeding Layer 4, with one sentence of the brand's own copy demonstrating each.

---

### Instruments deliberately not in the standard

Named here so a future session does not add them by reflex.

- **Archetypes.** They check voice consistency; they do not generate attributes. Use as a review lens in Critique, never as a derivation instrument.
- **Brand pyramids and ladders.** Academic in origin and frequently misattributed. They restate what D3 and D4 already produce.
- **Market-strength indices.** The large strategy houses instrument the market rather than the room. Their diagnostics are licensable products built for portfolios of hundreds of brands and they do not scale down to a venture.
- **Identity-drift prisms.** Reach for one only when the open question is genuinely a gap between intended identity and perceived image, which requires an audience large enough to have a perception in the first place.

## Layer 0 – The Spine

### The spine sentence

*One sentence that governs all four answers below. The whole brand in a breath.*

### The Brand Journey

Reverse-engineered from the destination. Each answer derives from the one above it.

**One – what do I want to have happen.** *The destination. Measured first in the deepest success the venture reaches for, then in what that success affords. A nested staircase – lifetime, ten-year, three-year.*

**Two – what do I have to be known for.** *The one thing, stated so a stranger could repeat it. The negative space – what this is explicitly not.*

**Three – what do I have to do to be known for that.** *The flagship act. The proof. The cadence. The sacrifice. The accelerant.*

**Four – what do I have to learn.** *The load-bearing skills, ranked. What is irreducibly the founder's and what is systematised or dispatched.*

### The Brand Story

**The Catalyst.** *The gap in the world that is why this exists.*

**The Core Truth.** *The conviction underneath the work.*

**The Proof.** *The pattern that makes the claim credible – not a moment, a body of work.*

### Governing rules

*The three to five binding operating rules that emerged from the build. The lines that win when a tactical choice conflicts with them.*
