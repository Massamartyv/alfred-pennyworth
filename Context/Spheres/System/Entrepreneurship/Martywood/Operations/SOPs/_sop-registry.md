| MW-000 | Content Pipeline | Any idea entering the system, from any capture rail | Operations | `content-pipeline.md` |
| MG-001 | Epiphany Production | New Epiphany cycle begins | Production | To be created |

MW-000 is the master pipeline. Property-specific SOPs inherit its gates and do not restate them.

The remaining IDs are reserved. The MG prefix predates the Martywood renaming of 2026-09-02 and is retained until those SOPs are written, at which point they take the MW prefix.

## Agent Instructions

- Before executing a repeatable process, check this registry for an existing SOP.
- If no SOP exists and you execute a process three or more times, create one.
- Each SOP must include: trigger, steps, decision points, escalation rules, success criteria.
