# Marty V – Sub-Brand Index

The music – records and mixes shipped under the artist credit Marty V. Sub-brand of Martywood; the braille wordmark ⠍⠁⠗⠞⠽ ⠧ is its signature object. Night mixes in a lifestyle setting, the DJ-needle short form, the set overlaid behind the operator with the waveform visible. Ships at its own rhythm – the documentary cadence governs the canon layer, not the artist.

A sub-brand keeps a light structure inside its parent venture rather than the full nine-department layout: Foundation for its fingerprint, Product Development for the work itself. It inherits Martywood governance – Agents/, the master fingerprint, approval gates – until its own Foundation matures. Graduation criteria to the full nine-department layout live in `../../New Sub-Brand/_index.md`.
