# Administration – Martywood

Legal, licensing, brand protection and platform compliance. The structural backbone. HR has left Administration for its own standalone department, Human Resources.

## Structure

```
Administration/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Legal/                 -- Contracts, licensing, IP filings
  Compliance/            -- Platform terms, regulatory compliance
  Identity/               -- Entity documents, formation records
  Policies/               -- Internal policies, content guidelines, usage rights
```

### What Belongs Here

- Business entity documents and formation records
- Trademark and intellectual property filings
- Content licensing and usage rights
- Platform terms of service compliance
- Privacy policies and disclaimers
- Music licensing and rights clearance for the Conversation and the music

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Financial records – those go in Finances
- Team, contractors and hiring – those go in Human Resources
- Content production documents – those go in Product Development
- Content product definitions – those go in Marketing & Sales
- Partnership revenue terms – those go in Business Development
