# Human Resources – Martywood

Team, contractors and collaborators. The people who contribute to the brand output. Human Resources is a standalone department, folded out of the retired Administration/HR sub-folder.

### What Belongs Here

- Team structure and roles
- Contractor and freelancer agreements
- Collaboration terms and expectations
- Hiring plans as the venture scales

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Conversation guest management – that goes in Business Development
- Audience management – that goes in Operations
- Financial terms of contracts – those go in Finances
- Legal and compliance documents – those go in Administration

## Current Structure

Solo operation. All creative direction, production and distribution handled personally with Alfred as the AI operating layer.
