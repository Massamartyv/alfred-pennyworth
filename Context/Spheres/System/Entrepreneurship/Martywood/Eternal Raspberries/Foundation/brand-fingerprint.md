## Governance – the Living Canon

The single source of truth for Eternal Raspberries. It has a rigid core and a free perimeter. Until Layer 0 is derived, Martywood governance holds – `../../Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`.

- **The invariant core** does not move without a named amendment and a re-derived chain. *List the three to six lines that, if changed, would make this a different brand. The non-negotiables.*
- **The flex layer** is everything else – devices, tactics, the reference set, the roadmap. It is expected to move.
- **Amendments are logged, never silent.** Each supersedes specific prior lines with a dated trace in the Amendment Log. Nothing is overwritten without memory.
- **Coherence cascades.** Layer 0's four questions are reverse-engineered, so a change upstream re-derives everything beneath it in the same pass, and any induced contradiction is surfaced, not absorbed.
- **The image is engraved, the platform rotates.** The oldest statement of this distinction in the trade is the instruction to decide what image the brand will hold, engrave it on a stone tablet and never change it, while the campaigns that carry it are expected to move. The invariant core is the tablet. The flex layer is the campaign. Confusing the two is how a brand redesigns its way out of its own recognition.

## Derivation – how the answers are arrived at

Layer 0 and Layer 1 are outputs, not opinions. This layer holds the instruments that produce them and the order they are run in. Nothing below this line may be filled from instinct alone.

**The order is fixed and it starts outside the brand.** Every instinct is to begin with what we want and what we are good at. Beginning there produces a brand that is about itself. The sequence below is inverted on purpose – the tension in the world is named and evidenced before the brand is allowed to speak about its own strengths.

**The trace rule.** Every answer in Layers 0, 1, 2 and 4 names the instrument that produced it and the date it was run, written as `[D3, 2026-09-10]`. An answer with no trace is an assertion, and an assertion cannot be audited when an amendment is proposed three years later.

**The five conditions.** An instrument has been run properly only when all five hold. They recur across every documented practice surveyed and each one is a failure mode in the negative.

1. Inputs are sourced from research, never invented in the room.
2. Elimination is forced, never optional.
3. One named owner breaks ties. Consensus is not a method.
4. The output is written as a checkable governing artefact, not a slide that gets archived.
5. The instrument matches the actual open question. Running a familiar instrument on the wrong question is itself the failure.

**The solo adaptation.** The published governance for these instruments assumes a room of stakeholders, and guards against consensus sanding the brand to the middle. A solo operator has the opposite exposure: every placement lands where the founder wishes the brand were rather than where it is. Self-flattery, unopposed. The correction is structural. The founder places; the agent is required to argue the opposing position on any placement the evidence does not support, before it is recorded. The sentence test is mandatory rather than a tiebreak.

### D1 – The Tension

*The state of the world this brand exists to stand against. Run first, always.*

Name one tension. Not a trend, not a complaint, not a market gap.

- **The trend test.** A trend passes and a tension persists. If it would not have been true five years ago and true in five years, it is a trend.
- **The evidence bar.** Three independent sources minimum, of which at least one is not commentary – behaviour data, a market fact, or a body of first-hand accounts. This bar is the standard's own addition; the source doctrine sets procedural safeguards but no evidentiary minimum, and a tension asserted without evidence is the most common way this whole layer becomes theatre.
- **The stress test.** State it to somebody outside the category. If it needs explaining, it is not yet a tension.

**Output** – one sentence naming the tension, with its three sources cited beneath.

#### The tension `[D1, 2026-09-10]`

> **We treat digital as permanent. It is the most perishable material we own.**

**Evidence.** Three independent sources, two of them non-commentary.

1. Pew Research Center, 2024 – analysis of roughly one million webpages via Common Crawl plus half a million government pages, half a million news pages and five million posts. 38% of pages that existed in 2013 were gone by 2023; 54% of Wikipedia references point to a dead page; around one in five posts disappear within months. Measured behaviour, not commentary. `https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2024/05/17/when-online-content-disappears/`
2. Scholarly citation decay study, 2013 – 33% of cited URLs dead, decaying at roughly 3.7% a year. Independent academic corroboration a full decade earlier, which is what makes the condition structural rather than recent.
3. The digital preservation field itself – the digital dark age named as a concern in 1997, with format-migration practice operating at national archive level since 2002.

**The trend test – passes.** Two decades of documented history behind it and no plausible expiry. The cause is structural: commercial platforms have finite lifespans, formats go proprietary, subscriptions lapse. More personal working memory sits inside non-exportable software surfaces now than five years ago, so the exposure is widening rather than closing.

**The stress test – passes.** It requires no unpacking for somebody outside the category.

#### Rejected candidate `[D1, 2026-09-10]`

**"The tools outran the systems"** – the claim that people now hold capable assistants and have nothing structured for them to read. **Failed the bar.** Recorded so it is not proposed again without new evidence.

- The headline statistics commonly cited for it – some 80% of data being unstructured, and retrieval project failure rates in the seventies – dead-end in vendor blogs citing other vendor blogs with no traceable primary source. **These numbers are barred from all Eternal Raspberries copy.**
- It fails the trend test on its own best evidence. The condition is specific to 2023 onward and the market is visibly closing it: models now extract structured facts from raw unstructured text at above 90% accuracy in published clinical study, and structured-context standards have grown adoption by roughly three orders of magnitude in eighteen months. A gap the market is closing in real time is a trend.
- It fails the stress test. It reads as internal operations jargon outside the category.

**What survives of it.** Agent readability is not the tension. It is the mechanism, and it belongs to D2 and to the product specification. If digital is perishable, what makes a system durable is that it remains legible – named, described, structured – so that a person and a machine can both still read it in five years. Legibility is how preservation is actually achieved, which makes it the answer to the tension rather than a second tension standing beside it.

### D2 – The Best Self

*The brand's strongest true claim. Run second, never first.*

Interrogate the brand until it confesses its strengths. This is an adversarial interview, not a list-making exercise – every claim is met with "prove it" and the ones that survive are the only ones that count. The claim must be true today, not aspirational.

**Output** – one sentence naming the brand at its best, with the proof that makes it credible.

#### The best self `[D2, 2026-09-10]`

> **You never lose the thread of your own life.**

**What survived interrogation.** Three claims cleared present tense. The schemas were extracted from a workspace in genuine daily use rather than invented for sale. They are relational architecture, where figures compute rather than get typed, in a category that mostly sells decorated lists. And they have carried a heavy life – five ventures, forty-three spheres, a music catalogue, an essay line, a client roster – for four years without a migration, with the Projects database dating to 31 July 2022 and Resources to 28 September 2022. Both still in daily use.

**What failed present tense.** Taste in the artefact, and machine legibility. Neither had reached the products. Both are ruled claimable as forward promise, bound to a shipping gate: no product lists until it carries its descriptions and its Agent Brief.

**What the interrogation revealed about the product.** An agent already operates these schemas daily, but it does so partly because forty kilobytes of governance tell it how. A buyer's assistant arrives with none of that. Schema alone is not agent-operable; schema plus brief is. The Agent Brief is therefore not a bonus page, it is the missing half of the product.

**Buyer corroboration, found at D4.** Asked what finally made a system hold, one buyer answered unprompted: *the click never came from the system itself. It came when I realised all those systems relied on my own internal motivation, which is a resource that runs out. The only thing that ever worked long-term was adding an external force.* The assistant is that external force. It does not run out of motivation. This is why systems die, stated by a buyer before the brand had said a word about it.

**Seven passes.** The claim settled early and the sentence did not. Four failure modes were named and corrected in order: the audit register, which read as a curriculum vitae; the metaphor written into the copy, which read as folksy rather than warm; the object as grammatical subject, which described the artefact rather than the life; and the functional promise, which was true and unfelt. The sentence that survived puts the person first and says nothing about mechanism at all.

#### Voice register `[D2, 2026-09-10]`

Ruled during derivation and binding on all copy. Carried down to Layer 4.

- **The preserve lives in the image, never in the language.** Jars, cellars and seasons are the visual world. In words they read folksy and cartoonish.
- **Plain nouns, and the reader's own life.** Closer to how a phone describes itself than to how a studio describes its craft.
- **Technical language may appear where it helps, and is made fun rather than hidden.** The buyer is a non-technical person for whom leveraging this ought to feel as mundane as using the internet, so understanding is never a prerequisite of use – but the technical world is not a secret to be kept from them. *Amended 2026-09-11 to resolve the contradiction with D5 axis four. See the Amendment Log.*
- **The test.** Does what we produce nourish a better life for the person on the other end. If a line describes the object rather than the life, it fails.

### D3 – The Ideal

*The intersection of D1 and D2. The candidate spine sentence.*

Written in one fixed form:

> **{Brand} believes the world would be a better place if {X}.**

The form is borrowed deliberately. It forces the brand to hold a position about the world rather than a description of itself, and a brand that cannot complete the sentence honestly does not yet know what it is for.

**Output** – the Ideal sentence. It becomes the Layer 0 spine sentence unless a later instrument unseats it.

#### The Ideal `[D3, 2026-09-10]`

> **Eternal Raspberries believes the world would be a better place if the things we set up for ourselves stayed fresh.**

*Stayed fresh* is the answer to D1 and it belongs to both worlds at once – food goes stale, and so does a cache. A non-technical reader hears only the kitchen, which is the point. The word does its work as plain language first and the second meaning is a gift to whoever catches it.

### D4 – The Word Sort

*The attribute set, and more importantly the negative space.*

Thirty to forty candidate words, sourced from the research rather than written in the room. Each is sorted individually, in silence, into one of three piles:

| Pile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **You Are** | True today, and defensible |
| **You Are Torn** | Wanted, not yet earned |
| **You Are Not** | Explicitly refused |

Then the sentence test on every survivor: write one concrete sentence in which the word does real work. A word that cannot produce that sentence is parked, which is what kills *innovative*, *authentic*, *premium* and *bold* before they ever reach the page.

**Output** – three to five attributes for Layer 1, and the You Are Not list, which becomes terminology governance in Layer 4. The refused pile carries more weight than the accepted one.

#### The sort `[D4, 2026-09-10]`

**Sources.** Thirty-eight candidates from three pools, none written in the room. The category's self-description across Notion's gallery and the prominent Gumroad sellers. First-hand buyer language – two Apify pulls of 120 Reddit posts and comments across r/Notion, r/productivity and r/ObsidianMD, plus Substack, dev.to and app-store accounts. And twenty words already documented in the house corpus. Evidence at `.working/the-preserve/word-set.md` and the two Reddit datasets beside it.

**The adversarial pass.** Nine house words failed the sentence test before the sort began. *Composed* describes a bearing, not a product. *Rooted* is Martywood's fusion doctrine and does no work here. *Undeniable* produced no concrete sentence. *Credible* is hygiene rather than character. *Intentional* duplicates *Deliberate*. *Effortless* is the seamless family in disguise. *Exacting* cannot be claimed in the same session a formula was found wrong. *Fresh* belongs to the spine. *Considered* is design-speak the category already uses.

**You Are** – four words, each with its sentence.

| Word | The sentence that proves it does work |
|---|---|
| **Quiet** | It never asks you to look after it. |
| **Warm** | The instructions read like a note from a friend. |
| **Tended** | Somebody still looks after this, so you do not have to. |
| **Durable** | It still works when you come back to it a year later. |

*Tended* and *Durable* sit in deliberate tension – one says somebody looks after this, the other says it lasts without anybody looking after it. Both are true. Care is paid up front so that none is needed later, which is the brand.

**You Are Torn** – wanted, not yet earned.

- **Deliberate** – true of the operator's workspace, not of a catalogue still carrying an empty shell and twenty untested sprints. Earned when the reshaped pattern ships across the line.
- **Honest** – telling the buyer what the product cannot do. Cheap to become and nearly absent in the category. Earned when every product page carries its limits.

**Cut for overlap, not refused.** *Unhurried*, which *Quiet* carries. *Generous*, which *Warm* and *Tended* carry between them. *Returnable*, carried whole by the best self at D2.

**You Are Not.**

- **Restrained** – the most saturated tag in the operator's own library and the defining register of Five Points. Refusing it separates this property from the bone-and-ink restraint of the Quintessence. Eternal Raspberries is not austere. It is warm, and warmth sometimes means abundance.
- **Layered** – the buyers ask for less, not for depth held in reserve.
- **Clean** – contested, since it is also a house Design Principle. The category owns it.
- **The category's ruined words** – simple, powerful, beautiful, seamless, aesthetic, all-in-one, ultimate, minimal, productive, streamlined, complete.

*Aesthetic* carries particular weight. Among buyers it is a pejorative – the name they give to the trap. *I had to stop trying to get it to aesthetic perfection and just use it for functionality.* The brand delivers beauty and never claims it.

**The buyer's vocabulary of failure** – not attributes, and not refused words either. These are what the brand stands against, in the buyer's own mouth: overwhelmed, complicated, messy, abandoned, guilt, and the line that says it all – *the fancier your system, the faster you will abandon it.*

### D5 – The Spectra

*Design instructions, not descriptions.*

Four to six axes, each generated from a real competitive tension in this category rather than pulled from a standard set. Place a mark on each.

- **No midpoints.** A midpoint is a refusal to decide and it is recorded as an unresolved question, not as an answer.
- **Silent placement first**, before any discussion of the placement.
- **The opposing argument is mandatory** wherever the evidence does not support the mark. See the solo adaptation above.

A slider position is an instruction to whoever draws the type and picks the palette. It belongs to Layer 2, not to Layer 1.

**Output** – the axis set with marks and the one-line reasoning behind each.

#### The spectra `[D5, 2026-09-10]`

Six axes, each drawn from a tension present in this category's evidence. Placed silently by the operator first, no midpoint available by design. The opposing argument was made on every mark the evidence did not support, and is recorded beside the mark so that a future amendment can see it was weighed rather than missed.

| Axis | Mark | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Made to be admired ↔ made to be used | **Leaning used** | Supported. Matches the buyers, the refusal of *aesthetic* and the attribute *Quiet*. |
| Everything included ↔ a skeleton that grows | **Leaning everything** | Held over the opposing argument. See below. |
| The expert's method ↔ a friend's notes | **Leaning friend** | Supported. The D2 voice and the attribute *Warm*. |
| Speaks the machine ↔ speaks the kitchen | **Leaning machine** | Held over the opposing argument, with no governing rule. Contradiction with D2 resolved 2026-09-11 by amending D2. |
| Serious ↔ playful | **Leaning playful** | Light by default, serious where the work demands it. Consistent with the wit ruled load-bearing, and short of the cartoonish register refused twice during D2. |
| Premium ↔ within reach | **Strongly premium** | Held over the evidence. See below. |

**Axis two – the opposing argument.** Every source gathered pointed toward the skeleton. *The fancier your system, the faster you will abandon it. Complexity kills consistency.* The one buyer who described success described a skeleton. Demo data was ruled a minimal skeleton, *Layered* was refused and *Quiet* chosen. The proposed reconciliation – splitting scope from depth, so a product covers its whole domain on day one but starts shallow – was offered and declined. The mark stands as comprehensive.

**Axis four – the opposing argument and the open contradiction.** The mark allows the technical world to show. The D2 voice register, ruled binding on all copy, says no schema talk, no agent talk, and that understanding is not a prerequisite of use. A proposed rule – technical vocabulary may show, technical concepts may not – was offered and declined. The contradiction was resolved on 2026-09-11 in favour of this mark: the D2 register was amended so that technical language may appear where it helps.

**Axis six – the opposing argument.** A buyer ceiling of $20 to $40 was found for a comparable product, and Life OS – the most comprehensive and most expensive product on the shelf – is named by a buyer as the category they always abandon. Axis two and axis six together describe that exact profile. The mark stands; the price-ladder review inherits this as its first problem to answer.

**For Layer 2.** A slider position is a design instruction. Leaning used means nothing on a product page exists only to be looked at. Leaning friend means the instructions are written in first person by someone who has done it. Leaning playful means the dry beat is permitted and expected. Strongly premium means the finish must justify the price on first sight, since the category's buyers will not otherwise pay it.

### D6 – The Voice Dimensions

*Four axes, run last.*

Funny to serious. Formal to casual. Respectful to irreverent. Enthusiastic to matter-of-fact. This is the only instrument in the standard carrying published empirical validation behind it, which is why it is named specifically rather than left to taste.

**Output** – four placements feeding Layer 4, with one sentence of the brand's own copy demonstrating each.

#### The voice `[D6, 2026-09-11]`

| Dimension | Mark | The brand, demonstrating it |
|---|---|---|
| Funny ↔ serious | **Leaning funny** – carried from D5 axis five | Your system has been waiting patiently. It does not hold grudges. |
| Formal ↔ casual | **Leaning formal** | Everything you need to begin is on the first page. |
| Respectful ↔ irreverent | **Leaning irreverent** | Most templates are made to be photographed. This one is made to be used on a Tuesday. |
| Enthusiastic ↔ matter-of-fact | **Leaning enthusiastic** | Come back after six months away and everything is exactly where you left it. We never tire of that. |

**The irreverence has one target.** Productivity culture, the category and the aesthetic trap – never the buyer. The buyer has usually already blamed themselves for a system that failed them; the brand's job is to take that blame off them, not to add to it.

**Quiet and enthusiastic are not in conflict.** *Quiet* at D4 describes what the product asks of the buyer, which is almost nothing. *Enthusiastic* describes the energy of the voice when it does speak. A friend who never makes demands of you and is plainly glad to see you is both at once.

### Instruments deliberately not in the standard

Named here so a future session does not add them by reflex.

- **Archetypes.** They check voice consistency; they do not generate attributes. Use as a review lens in Critique, never as a derivation instrument.
- **Brand pyramids and ladders.** Academic in origin and frequently misattributed. They restate what D3 and D4 already produce.
- **Market-strength indices.** The large strategy houses instrument the market rather than the room. Their diagnostics are licensable products built for portfolios of hundreds of brands and they do not scale down to a venture.
- **Identity-drift prisms.** Reach for one only when the open question is genuinely a gap between intended identity and perceived image, which requires an audience large enough to have a perception in the first place.

## Layer 2 – Expression

### The concept `[2026-09-10]`

**Kitchen and Kernel** – the world built from the words that belong to both the kitchen and the computer. Feed, batch, harvest, seed, root, branch, fork, prune, graft, field, yield, store, cache, kernel, bloom. And the ones that land on the tension directly: stale, fresh, expire, preserve, shelf life. Two worlds sharing one language, warm and technical in the same breath.

**Governing rule – plain language first.** Every word must work as ordinary English before it works as a double. The second meaning is a gift, never the point, and any line that only works once the reader notices the joke is cut.

**The concept governs; the device is rationed.** The idea generates the identity. The visible wordplay appears rarely and never announces itself, on the same discipline that governs wit everywhere in the house – frequency is rare, and forced is worse than absent.

**Concepts considered and not chosen.** The Still-Room, the Herbarium and Machine Legible, each developed with its own scholarship in `direction-ground.md`. The Still-Room may still supply image, material and type where the vocabulary supplies the words; Machine Legible was ruled out for contradicting the derivation, since legibility sits in the second clause of the Ideal rather than at the spine.

## Layer 3 – Standards

### The shipping gate

**No product lists until it carries a description on every database and every property, and its Agent Brief.** `[D2]` This is what makes the forward promise of legibility honest rather than aspirational. The gate is binary and it is checked, not assumed.

### The product

- **Schema plus brief is the product.** An assistant can operate a schema only when it is told how. The Agent Brief is not a bonus page, it is the missing half. `[D2]`
- **Construction standards** live in the sub-brand index: databases inline, a primary view for the data plus a Main list view carrying the database icon, no headings, a framing callout and a collapsed Start here toggle, a minimal skeleton of demo data, and a residue sweep before publication.
- **Coverage, not depth.** Each product covers its whole domain on day one. `[D5 axis two]`
- **Delivery that survives the duplication failure.** Notion's own template duplication fails for a share of buyers who are told they are logged out. Every delivery page carries a written fallback before any listing goes live.

### Copy conventions

- House grammar: en dashes, no Oxford comma, no parentheses in body copy, no contractions.
- Plain nouns and the reader's own life. Technical language may appear where it helps, made fun rather than hidden, and never a prerequisite of use. `[D2, amended]`
- **The wordplay is rationed.** Kitchen and Kernel doubles appear rarely. Any line that works only once the reader notices the joke is cut. `[Layer 2 concept]`
- **Barred statistics.** The figure of some 80% of data being unstructured, and retrieval project failure rates in the seventies percent, trace to no primary source and never appear in Eternal Raspberries copy. `[D1]`

### Channels

- **Gumroad** – the rail. Cover, gallery, description, delivery, tax.
- **Pinterest** – the traffic engine. Vertical pins, problem-led rather than product-led, in volume. Validated by a seller in the category: pins rank on Google and bring traffic for months.
- **Facebook** – a Page and two or three high-intent Groups, organic, pointing at Gumroad. Marketplace prohibits digital goods and is not a channel.

Cadence per channel is set with the channel plan, not here.

## Layer 4 – Behavioural

### Voice `[D6]`

Leaning funny, leaning formal, leaning irreverent, leaning enthusiastic. Composed sentences, a light touch, warm energy, and a raised eyebrow aimed in exactly one direction.

**The irreverence has one target.** Productivity culture, the category and the aesthetic trap – never the buyer. The buyer has usually already blamed themselves for a system that failed them. The brand takes that blame off them; it never adds to it.

### Terminology governance

- **Words used.** The Kitchen and Kernel vocabulary, sparingly – feed, batch, harvest, seed, root, branch, store, cache, kernel, bloom, fresh, stale, expire, preserve, shelf life, thread.
- **Words never used.** Simple, powerful, beautiful, seamless, aesthetic, all-in-one, ultimate, minimal, clean, productive, streamlined, complete. And the refused attributes: restrained, layered.
- **The buyer's vocabulary of failure** – overwhelmed, complicated, messy, abandoned, guilt – may be named as what the brand stands against, and is never used to describe the buyer.

### Decision defaults

When two good choices conflict, these break the tie.

1. **The life over the object.** A line about what the buyer's life becomes beats a line about what the product contains.
2. **The person as the subject.** If the product is the grammatical subject of a sentence, try the sentence again with the buyer as the subject.
3. **Used over admired.** Nothing on a page exists only to be looked at.
4. **Evidence over eloquence.** A claim that cannot be sourced does not ship, however well it reads.

### The quality gate

Every buyer-facing output passes all six before it ships.

1. Does it describe the life rather than the object.
2. Does every word work as plain language first.
3. Is every claim true today, or bound explicitly to the shipping gate.
4. Is it free of every word on the never-used list and every barred statistic.
5. Could a non-technical buyer use it without understanding anything first.
6. Does it help nourish a better life for the person on the other end.
