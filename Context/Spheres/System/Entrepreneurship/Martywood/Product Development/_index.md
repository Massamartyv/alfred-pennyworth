# Product Development – Martywood

The execution engine that turns editorial direction into finished work the audience receives, plus the venture's own physical products and merchandise. Product Development covers the Conversation, Epiphany, the music, social and video production, and the product line – e.g. the Marty Bag. Folded from the retired Production department, and from the physical-product ground the retired Creative department once held.

## Structure

```
Product Development/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Products/               -- Physical product and merchandise design briefs, e.g. the Marty Bag
  Conversation/            -- Production workflows, Conversation templates, audio specs
  Epiphany/                 -- Draft workflows, formatting, Substack publishing specs
  Masters/                   -- Music masters and mixdowns, the music property
  Social/                     -- Platform-native production specs and templates
  Video/                       -- YouTube and video production workflows
```

### What Belongs Here

- Production workflows and SOPs for every content format
- Conversation, Epiphany and post templates
- Production stack documentation
- Quality assurance specifications
- Asset preparation and rendering specs
- Physical product and merchandise design briefs and the product line itself

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Content product definitions and editorial direction – those go in Marketing & Sales
- Distribution scheduling and publishing platform management – those go in Operations
- Client and content delivery workflow to an external audience – Operations
- Audience growth, partnerships and positioning – Business Development

## Agent Instructions

- Build work is primarily Execution and Critique phases.
- Creator and Reviewer crews operate here most frequently.
- Template-based production is Tier 1. Draft production to finished work is Tier 2.
- Anything that ships to the audience is Tier 3 (approval required before publish).
- Load `brand-fingerprint.md` before any visual or audio production to confirm specs.
