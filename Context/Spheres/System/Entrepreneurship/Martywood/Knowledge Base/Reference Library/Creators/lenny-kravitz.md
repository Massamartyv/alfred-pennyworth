# Lenny Kravitz

**Who** – First member of the Martywood reference quintet, the five artists whose careers and positions anchor the brand architecture. In Layer 1 Kravitz is named for the artist-as-lifestyle dimension – the home and the destinations as part of the art.

**Why aligned** – Marty is the master brand, life as expression, where the person is the brand and the works are properties. Kravitz is the proof that the life itself is a property. The home, the design practice, the destinations are not adjacent to the work, they are the work. This is the integration Marty aspires to – the creative life answering to nothing but the standard, designed end to end.

**The formula** – The dimension Kravitz governs is artist-as-lifestyle. Treat the whole life as a single authored object, not a career with a personal life beside it. The home and the destinations are part of the art, so every surface a life touches is composed to the same standard as the released work. What to metabolise: the total integration – the refusal to let any square foot fall outside the aesthetic, intention per square foot held across living and making alike. What not to: the source carries no caution line for Kravitz – hold the integration as principle, not as a mandate to perform the lifestyle.

**Canon** – No specific works are named in the fingerprint. The reference is the position, not a discography – the artist whose life and work are one designed body.

**Pull for** – world-building, materiality, spatial-direction, hospitality, ritual, artist-as-life framing.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. The fingerprint carries the dimension Kravitz anchors but no current-cycle signal.
