# Knowledge Base – Martywood

Cultural references, audience insights, platform intelligence, competitive analysis, content research and the intellectual fuel that powers the brand. Folded in the audience, platform, cultural and competitive research ground of the retired Strategy department – the thinking that informs what Marketing & Sales builds, what Business Development pursues and what Product Development ships.

### What Belongs Here

- Cultural research and reference material, cultural trend sensing – what is emerging, what is fading, timing of waves
- Audience insights, demographics, behaviour patterns, segmentation
- Platform-specific intelligence – algorithm shifts, format performance
- Content performance analysis and learnings
- Competitive intelligence – adjacent creators, reference brands, category shifts
- Methodology documentation for content creation
- AI learnings specific to media production

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Personal sphere research – that lives in the personal Notion workspace under Sphere Manager
- Five Points research – that is a separate venture entirely
- Active content drafts – those go in Operations or Product Development
- Positioning decisions and market strategy calls themselves – those go in Business Development, informed by the research held here

## Key Context

- **Target audience:** Cultural creatives, entrepreneurs, creators at the intersection of taste, systems thinking and ambition
- **Platform coverage:** Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Substack, Threads, X, Reddit
- **Competitive set:** To be mapped – cultural commentators and creator-led media brands

## Agent Instructions

- Research work is primarily Reconnaissance and Direction phases.
- Researcher crews operate here most frequently.
- Research briefs are Tier 1 (full autonomy). Strategic recommendations drawn from research are Tier 2, and route to Business Development for positioning decisions that are Tier 3.
