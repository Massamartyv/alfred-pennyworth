# Creative – Atlas

The aesthetic standard-bearer of the venture. Brand identity, voice, visual system and the clinical communication standards that govern every artefact a doctor or patient sees from Atlas.

> **Pre-identity.** Atlas is the working code name. The final brand identity awaits an art-direction pass through the `art-director` skill. Until that pass lands, this studio operates on a placeholder voice register: clean, clinical, professional, direct.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Brand identity system – name, logo, colour, typography, visual language (pending art direction)
- Brand voice and tone documentation – how Atlas speaks to clinicians, patients, partners
- Clinical communication standards – the tone of every AI-generated artefact a doctor or patient sees
- Product UI direction – the visual register of the in-product experience
- Visual direction for marketing collateral, sales decks, web presence
- Creative briefs and reference libraries
- The Atlas equivalent of `personal-brand-identity.md` and `creative-director.md` once authored

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Physical or digital production of assets – that goes in Production
- Marketing campaign execution – that goes in Growth
- Regulatory review of patient-facing copy – that goes in Administration
- Financial tracking – that goes in Finance

## Placeholder Voice Register

Until the art-direction pass produces the proper brand voice documentation, Atlas product copy and external communications use this register:

- Clean and clinical. No marketing puffery. Doctors are sceptical of AI in healthcare; bombast amplifies scepticism.
- Direct. Active voice. Short sentences in product copy. Longer sentences in explanatory writing.
- Confident without overreach. The product is good, not magical.
- Plain language wherever possible. Technical vocabulary only when accuracy demands it.
- The voice of a competent senior colleague, not a vendor.

This is the floor, not the ceiling. The art-direction pass replaces it.
