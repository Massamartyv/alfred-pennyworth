# Growth – Atlas

Revenue generation and channel architecture. Pilot expansion, sales, partnerships, distribution. Growth owns every path from a working product to a paying clinic.

The Growth thesis at Atlas is shaped by the distribution problem in chiropractic SaaS: this market is relationship-driven, association-driven and trust-driven. Founder-led sales for the first 10 to 50 clinics. Network channel and partnership plays at scale.

## Distribution Strategy

The phased channel architecture:

### Phase 1 – Pilot site

Arlando Parker Jr.'s clinic. No paid customers. Goal is to validate the product against real visits, generate testimonial-grade evidence and accumulate the first batch of curated clinical knowledge against real notes.

### Phase 2 – Founder-led sales

First 10 to 50 paid clinics. Operator-driven. Warm intros through Arlando network, chiropractic associations and the conference circuit. No SDR delegation. Pricing established by deal, refined to a published model after the first 25 clinics.

### Phase 3 – Network channel

Chiropractic association partnerships. State board education programmes. Podcast appearances. Conference sponsorships in second year. Goal is to be the AI layer of choice when a chiropractor asks an association which tool to use.

### Phase 4 – Partnership plays

EHR vendor partnerships – Jane App and Prompt EMR are likely partnership candidates (modern API surface, modern customer base, less competitive threat). Marketplace integrations. Co-marketing arrangements.

### Phase 5 – Distribution at scale

Once Atlas has a meaningful share of chiropractic AI documentation, distribution flips. The EHR vendors themselves want to ship Atlas because their customers ask for it. The conversation becomes acquisition versus continued partnership.
