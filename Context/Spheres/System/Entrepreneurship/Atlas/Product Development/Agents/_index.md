# Product Development – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Product Development department is the execution engine. Development, build quality and offer architecture live here. Everything the venture ships is built through this department.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Product Development role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Product Development** | Department head. Owns build quality across all output. | All | – |
| **Development Lead** | Core product or service development, specification, iteration. | Execution | Creator |
| **Production Coordinator** | Supplier or vendor selection, production runs, quality. | Execution | Mediator |
| **Content Producer** | Photography, video, short-form content production. | Execution | Creator |
| **Quality Assurance** | Pre-release review across products, assets and content. | Critique | Reviewer:Scrutiny |

### Role activation

A single content shoot engages only the Content Producer and Quality Assurance. A new product or service launch activates the full roster.
