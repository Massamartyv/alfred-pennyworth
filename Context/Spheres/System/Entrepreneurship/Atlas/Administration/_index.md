# Administration – Atlas

Legal, compliance, brand protection, entity formation, IP and HR. The structural backbone that keeps Atlas legally sound and operationally clean in a regulatory environment that does not forgive sloppy work.

In healthcare AI, Administration is not a back-office function. HIPAA exposure, state chiropractic board interactions and BAA infrastructure are load-bearing for the entire venture.

## HIPAA Posture (Phase 1 priority)

Before any patient data enters any pipeline:

1. Entity formation complete and HIPAA-eligible
2. BAAs signed with: model provider (Anthropic), infrastructure host (AWS / GCP / Azure healthcare tier), observability provider, transcription provider, EHR API integration
3. Encryption documentation complete – TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, key management documented
4. Audit log architecture deployed and tested – immutable, append-only, tokenised identifiers
5. Breach response runbook authored, tested, signed off
6. Cyber liability insurance in force
7. Privacy policy and terms of service published

No exceptions to this list. The HIPAA boundary is binary.

## Regulatory Posture

Chiropractic is regulated at the state level by individual state chiropractic boards. Documentation generated by Atlas must satisfy the documentation requirements of the pilot jurisdiction's board. Phase 1 work:

1. Identify Arlando Parker Jr.'s state chiropractic board
2. Pull current documentation requirements for that board
3. Map requirements to validation contract assertions
4. Obtain regulatory counsel opinion on whether Atlas-generated notes satisfy the requirements
5. Document the opinion in the validation contract evidence dossier

Expansion to additional pilot states requires repeating this process per jurisdiction.

## Entity Formation

Recommended structure: Delaware C-Corporation. Rationale:

- Healthcare SaaS at billion-dollar trajectory requires a structure that supports institutional capital and clean exit mechanics
- C-Corp is the default for venture investors
- Delaware law is the most predictable for governance disputes

Alternative: LLC for initial pilot phase, conversion to C-Corp at first institutional funding. Decision pending operator review.

## Arlando Parker Jr. Advisor Agreement

Pending Direction-gate approval of commercial terms. Three structures under consideration:

1. **Retainer only** – Monthly retainer in exchange for clinical advisor duties (Reviewer:Behavioural authority during Phase 1, pilot site for product validation, brand association). Clean. Easy to exit on either side.
2. **Revenue share** – Percentage of net revenue attributable to clinics referred through Arlando or to features informed by his clinical knowledge curation. Aligns long-term but introduces tracking complexity.
3. **Hybrid** – Lower retainer plus revenue share. Most common structure for clinical advisors at venture-stage healthcare companies.

Operator decision required at Direction gate. Counsel review required before any term sheet is presented to Arlando.
