# Business Development – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Business Development department owns positioning, channel architecture, partnerships and the pilot pipeline. The thinking behind the work and every path from a working product to a paying clinic that is not a direct sale.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Business Development role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Business Development** | Department head. Owns the strategic point of view and channel strategy. | All | – |
| **Positioning Lead** | Brand positioning, differentiation frameworks, narrative architecture. | Direction | Researcher |
| **Consumer Insight Lead** | Target customer profiles, behavioural segmentation, purchase drivers. | Reconnaissance, Direction | Researcher |
| **Partnership Scout** | Aligned brands, networks and co-branding opportunities. | Reconnaissance | Researcher |
| **Channel Lead** | Distribution and channel strategy depending on venture model. | Direction, Execution | Researcher, Broadcaster |
| **Affiliate and Ambassador Lead** | Influencer programmes, referral networks, affiliate operations. | Execution | Broadcaster |

### Role activation

A quick positioning lookup engages only the Positioning Lead. A full channel launch or partnership push activates the entire roster.
