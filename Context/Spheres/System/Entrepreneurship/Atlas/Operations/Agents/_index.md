# Operations – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Operations department is the engine room. Delivery, quality control, customer service, clientele management and SOPs. Operations makes sure what gets built actually reaches the client or customer cleanly.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Operations role definition and full specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Operations** | Department head. Owns the engine room. | All | – |
| **Delivery Coordinator** | Order fulfilment, logistics, returns, partner management. | Execution | Broadcaster |
| **Quality Control Lead** | Incoming quality inspection, compliance checks, standards enforcement. | Critique | Reviewer:Scrutiny |
| **Customer Service Lead** | Client inquiries, issue resolution, service SOPs, feedback capture. | Execution | Creator, Broadcaster |
| **Clientele Coordinator** | Account management for active clients and partners post-close. | Execution | Broadcaster |

### Role activation

Routine delivery coordination engages only the Delivery Coordinator. A new client onboarding pulls Delivery Coordinator, Quality Control and Clientele Coordinator together.
