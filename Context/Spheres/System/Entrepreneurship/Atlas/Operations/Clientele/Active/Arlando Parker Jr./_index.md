## Commercial Terms (Pending)

Three structures under consideration. Operator decision required at Direction gate. Counsel review before any term sheet is presented.

### Option 1 – Retainer only

Monthly retainer in exchange for advisor duties. Clean. Easy to exit on either side. Minimum complexity.

### Option 2 – Revenue share

Percentage of net revenue attributable to clinics referred through Arlando or to features informed by his clinical knowledge curation. Aligns long-term but introduces tracking complexity.

### Option 3 – Hybrid

Lower retainer plus revenue share. Most common structure for clinical advisors at venture-stage healthcare companies.

The Hybrid structure is the most common for a reason – it gives the advisor a baseline that does not disappear if the venture fails to monetise quickly, and gives them upside that scales with the venture's success. Recommend Hybrid as the default unless Arlando's preferences or counsel guidance suggest otherwise.
