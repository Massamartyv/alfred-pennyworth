# Foundation – Atlas

Community initiatives, education and contribution to the chiropractic profession. Where Atlas invests in the field it serves.

> **Pre-launch.** No active Foundation initiatives at venture genesis. Foundation activates once revenue allows meaningful investment in profession-level contribution.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Education contribution – chiropractic college partnerships, continuing education programmes, free resources
- Open clinical knowledge – portions of the curated knowledge that Atlas releases to the profession as a contribution
- Mentorship and pro-bono pilots for under-resourced clinics
- Research partnerships – contributing anonymised aggregate data to chiropractic research where consented and ethically sound
- Conference sponsorships with educational focus
- Profession-level advocacy on AI standards in chiropractic

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Revenue-generating partnerships – those go in Growth
- Marketing campaigns with a charitable angle – those go in Growth
- Personal charitable giving – that lives in personal scope, not Atlas

## Strategic Posture

Atlas operates in a profession that is often under-resourced relative to its medical peers. The Foundation thesis is that contributing back to the profession is both ethically correct and strategically aligned: clinics that see Atlas as a profession partner adopt faster than clinics that see Atlas as a vendor.

Phase-by-phase Foundation activation:

| Phase | Foundation activity |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 (Pilot) | None – capital is in product build |
| Phase 2 (Founder sales) | None – capital is in sales motion |
| Phase 3 (Network channel) | Conference sponsorship with educational focus; chiropractic college partnership exploration |
| Phase 4 (Scale) | Open knowledge release; mentorship pilots for under-resourced clinics; chiropractic research partnership |
| Phase 5 (Distribution at scale) | Profession-level standards work; advocacy on AI in chiropractic |
