# Marketing & Sales – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Marketing & Sales department is the aesthetic standard-bearer for {Venture Name} and owns the sales pipeline. Brand identity, visual direction and content aesthetics are set here; outreach, proposals and deal management for paid expansion close here. Everything that carries the {Venture Name} name is held to the standard defined in `Criteria/` (to be populated).

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Marketing & Sales role definition and full specialist seats.

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Marketing & Sales** | Department head. Holds the standard across all creative output and owns the deals that close. | All | – |
| **Brand Strategist** | Brand positioning, identity evolution, category differentiation. Writes the brief. | Direction | Creator |
| **Art Director** | Visual direction for all design output across touchpoints. | Execution | Creator |
| **Content Strategist** | Content calendar, platform strategy, editorial planning. | Direction | Creator, Broadcaster |
| **Copywriter** | Product copy, web copy, social copy, platform-specific content. | Execution | Creator |
| **Sales Lead** | Direct sales pipeline, outreach, proposals, deal management. | Reconnaissance, Execution | Researcher |

### Role activation

Not every task activates every role. A social caption may only need the Copywriter. A full brand identity build or sales push activates the full roster.
