# Foundation – {Sub-Brand Name}

Home of the sub-brand fingerprint. Unlike a venture Foundation, this folder carries no community or philanthropy remit – in the light structure it exists for identity alone.

Until `brand-fingerprint.md` is authored here, the sub-brand inherits the parent fingerprint and all parent governance – voice, visual system and approval gates read from `../../Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md` and `../../Agents/`.

When the sub-brand voice diverges enough to need its own record, author the fingerprint from the blank standard at `New Venture/Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md` – Governance, Layer 0 Spine, Layers 1 through 4, Amendment Log. An authored fingerprint governs the sub-brand only; the parent fingerprint remains the house authority.
