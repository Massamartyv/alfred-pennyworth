# {Sub-Brand Name} – Sub-Brand Index

{Description}. Sub-brand of {Parent Venture} – its own visual identity and voice register inside the parent house, the Studio-under-Lululemon model.

A sub-brand keeps a light structure inside its parent venture rather than the full nine-department layout: Foundation for its fingerprint, Product Development for build documentation. It inherits parent-venture governance – Agents/, brand fingerprint authority, approval gates – until its own Foundation matures.

## Structure

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Foundation/ | Sub-brand fingerprint – to be authored; inherits {Parent Venture} governance until then |
| Product Development/ | Build plans and product documentation |

## Graduation

The light structure is the default for as long as it holds. Add a single department folder from the `New Venture/` template when work in that domain becomes real and recurring – one department at a time, never speculatively. Graduate to the full nine-department layout when two or more of the following are true:

1. **Own revenue line** – pricing, billing or a P&L that can no longer be read as a line item of the parent
2. **Own roster** – clients, channels or partnerships managed separately from the parent Operations and Business Development departments
3. **Own governance** – an authored brand fingerprint that has diverged from the parent, plus agent guidelines or integrations of its own
4. **Three or more active domains** – sustained work that Foundation and Product Development can no longer hold without misfiling

Graduation copies the missing departments from `New Venture/`, rewrites this index to the venture-index shape and keeps the sub-brand registered in the parent index. Whether a graduated sub-brand later leaves the parent to stand as a sovereign peer venture is a separate operator ruling through the specificity gate – never automatic.
