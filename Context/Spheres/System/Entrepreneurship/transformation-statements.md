# Transformation Statements

One candidate transformation statement per brand in the portfolio, derived from each venture Layer 0 spine. Candidates only. Nothing here is canon until the operator ratifies a line and it is written into the owning fingerprint under a dated amendment.

A transformation statement is not a spine sentence and not a tagline. The spine sentence says what the brand *is*, from the inside. The transformation statement says what the brand *does to a person*, from the outside – the before-state named plainly, the after-state named plainly, the mechanism suppressed. It is the sentence that sells, and the sentence that tells you when a venture has drifted: the day the after-state stops being true, the brand is broken, whatever the marketing says.

## The form

Three constraints, applied to every line below.

1. **One subject.** One person, named as they are before the brand reaches them. Never a market, never a segment.
2. **A real before-state.** Concrete enough that the subject would recognise themselves in it and wince.
3. **No mechanism.** No product, no method, no stack. The moment the mechanism enters, the statement becomes a description.

Each statement is held against the negative space in its own fingerprint. A line that survives the positive claim but breaks the negative space is rejected.

## Derivation

### Five Points Digital Studio

**Before** – the business runs on the attention of the founder and on rented hours; every engagement depreciates on delivery.
**After** – the founder owns infrastructure that compounds without them in the loop.

Traces to the master filter, does this compound or does it consume, and to the Core Truth that infrastructure outlives the engagement while a deliverable dies on delivery. Holds the negative space by naming no retainer, no service and no deliverable.

Note for reconciliation: `Growth/Prospecting/cold-call-script.md` already runs a transformation line in the field – "the business that runs without living in their head". That line is the same shape, pitched one altitude lower and aimed at the operational buyer rather than the singular mind. Both should not run unreconciled. Either the cold-call line becomes the compressed spoken form of this one, or the two audiences are formally separated and each gets its own.

### Marty V

**Before** – two worlds sit in silos and the culture keeps them there.
**After** – the connection is made, and it cannot be unmade.

Traces to the reference line: every connection must be non-obvious and, once made, undeniable. Deliberately not a service promise. An artist brand that promises a customer outcome collapses into the commentator-and-explainer category the fingerprint exists to escape, so the transformation is stated as something that happens *to* the audience rather than something offered to them.

Property-level line for Marty Gras, should one be wanted beneath the master brand: *a guest you would never have put in the room, and by the end you cannot imagine the room without them.*

### Paradigm

**Before** – optimised in pieces, a pill here, an app there, a studio down the road, none of them speaking.
**After** – treated as one whole, to a single standard, inside a room worth belonging to.

Traces to the Catalyst and to the Core Truth that belonging to something held to that standard is itself part of the medicine. The final clause carries the accelerant – belonging as the growth engine – and holds the negative space against wellness-as-austerity.

Weakness on record: this is the softest of the seven, because the tri-form house has no single subject. Product buyer, method student and space member are three different people arriving with three different before-states. The line papers over that with an abstraction, "a person". Either the house names its primary subject, or Paradigm carries three statements under one spine.

### Lillie and Lynette

**Before** – braced for service, having met only fussy heritage pastiche or sleek five-star cold.
**After** – genuinely received, and unable to tell owned property from kept property.

Traces to the invariant core: genuine welcome is the product, one name and one standard across every distance. The second clause does real work – it is the monolith stated as a felt outcome, and it is the hardest promise in the portfolio to keep, because Stewardship leads the sequence and the standard has to travel onto property the house does not own.

### Atlas

**Before** – drowning in documentation, handed general tools that hallucinate on the basics of the craft.
**After** – the dreaded work has disappeared, and going back is unthinkable.

Traces to the spine directly – work they dread turned into work they never have to think about – and to the Proof, which is the refusal to go back. The closing clause is the proof written into the promise, which is why it earns its length.

### Athena

**Before** – overlooked, walked past by the gatekeepers, unseen rather than lesser.
**After** – undeniable, with a career built rather than a booking taken.

Traces to the Core Truth and to governing rule three, build do not broker. Chooses the talent as the subject rather than the client. The client-side transformation exists – a brand that cast from the same narrow pool now casts from range – but it is secondary, and a house that leads with the client transformation is a booker describing itself as a house.

### Paradigm Farms

**Before** – guessing at what a plant needs, and buying food that was meant to be medicine.
**After** – seeing what the plant needs, and growing it.

Lowest confidence of the seven. The sub-brand Foundation fingerprint is not yet authored, so this is derived from the sub-brand index and `Production/build-plan-v1.md` rather than from a spine. Holds the confirmed positioning – home gardener leads, food-as-medicine carried in pro-real-food language rather than anti-establishment language. Treat as a placeholder until the sub-brand fingerprint exists.

## Recommendation

Add **the transformation statement** as a standing field in the fingerprint standard, placed in Layer 0 immediately beneath the spine sentence, and add it to `New Venture/Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md` so every future venture inherits it.

The rationale is coherence cascade. The spine sentence governs the four Journey answers, but nothing in the current standard forces a venture to state its promise in the language of the person receiving it. That gap is where drift starts – a venture can hold a perfect spine and still sell an outcome nobody recognises. Making the statement a required field means the next amendment to any spine must re-derive the promise in the same pass, and a promise that will not re-derive is a spine that has quietly moved.
