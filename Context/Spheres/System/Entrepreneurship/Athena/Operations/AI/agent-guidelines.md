# Agent Guidelines – Athena

Rules for AI agent behaviour within Athena. Read this before executing any task scoped to this venture.

---

## Execution Tiers

### Tier 1 – Full Autonomy

Execute without approval:
- Drafting internal documents – talent briefs, casting summaries, research
- Generating templates and variations
- Extracting and summarising data from Notion
- Updating registries and indexes
- Compiling talent portfolios from existing assets

### Tier 2 – Execute Then Notify

Act within defined parameters, then notify for review:
- Populating talent or client folder templates
- Generating booking proposals from templates – must be reviewed before sending
- Creating reports from talent or booking data
- Updating SOPs with improvements

### Tier 3 – Approval Required Before Execution

Do not proceed without explicit approval:
- Sending anything client-facing or talent-facing
- Committing to booking dates, rates or exclusivity terms
- Modifying talent agreements or commission structures
- Creating or modifying legal documents
- Making financial decisions or commitments
- Publishing content externally on behalf of the agency or its talent

---

## Red Lines

1. Never commit to a booking date or rate without verifying talent availability and approval.
2. Never share talent personal information outside authorised channels.
3. Never send anything client-facing or talent-facing without human review.
4. Never modify source documents in Product Development/ without approval.
5. Never negotiate exclusivity terms without explicit direction.
6. Never guess at rates – always reference the rate card and commission structure.

---

## File Interaction Rules

- **Read freely.** Any file in this venture is accessible for context.
- **Write to drafts.** Create new files in appropriate locations freely.
- **Update registries.** Keep `_clients-registry.md` and `_sop-registry.md` current.
- **Never delete.** Move to Archive, never delete. If something is wrong, flag it.
- **Always use frontmatter.** Every new file gets the standard YAML frontmatter.

---

## Context Loading

When starting a task:
1. Read `_index.md` at the venture root – orient to the venture
2. Read this file – know the rules
3. Read the relevant department `_index.md` – find the right files
4. Read the specific files needed for the task
5. Do not load everything. Be surgical.

---

## Escalation

When uncertain, ask rather than improvise. The cost of a question is always lower than the cost of a wrong action taken with confidence.
