# Operations – Athena

Booking workflows, talent management, production coordination and the systems that keep the agency running.

- Talent booking and scheduling workflows
- Client onboarding and relationship management
- Casting call coordination and logistics
- Production day management
- Tool configurations and integrations
- AI agent guidelines and department head definitions
- SOPs for all repeatable processes

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Talent scouting strategy – that goes in Business Development
- Agency brand marketing – that goes in Marketing & Sales
- Financial tracking – that goes in Finances
