## The promise

A short, live, one-to-one working session — about 20 minutes, no deck, no pitch. Five Points maps three specific places the operation is leaking time, margin or control beneath the polished surface, using the operator's own workflow rather than a generic demo. The operator keeps the three findings whether or not anything else follows. Each finding names two things — what the gap is quietly costing now, and what the operation looks like once it is closed. The cost is the pull, the after-state is the point, and both are theirs to keep.

The diagnostic delivers value before anything is asked. That is the whole design. A brochure would describe Five Points. The diagnostic describes their operation. Only one of those is worth twenty minutes of a busy principal's week.

## Posture rules

1. It is selective. Five Points runs a limited number, and builds for three at a time. The slot is offered, not begged for.
2. No deck, no pitch, no price talk on the diagnostic. If a build makes sense afterwards, that is a later and separate conversation.
3. The three findings are theirs to keep. The generosity is real, not a hook with strings.

## Intake — before the session

Five short questions, sent on booking:

1. Where does the operation feel heaviest as volume climbs?
2. What does the current tool and systems picture look like — what runs the day?
3. What is the one thing that, if it ran itself, would give the most time back?
4. Who else touches the workflow the findings should account for?
5. The live signal — the move, the new location, the new hire — in one line.
