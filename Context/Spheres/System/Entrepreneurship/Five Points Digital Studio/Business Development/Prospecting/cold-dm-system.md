# Cold DM System — Instagram and LinkedIn

**CTA:** The Operational Intelligence Diagnostic (see `operational-intelligence-diagnostic.md`)
**Channels:** Instagram DM and LinkedIn (operator-selected).
**Drawn from:** the Sales Conversation Framework, Sam McKenna's Show Me You Know Me, the Hormozi value equation. Chris Do and Myron Golden underneath.
**Send rule:** No sends against a real list without operator approval per Touchpoint G.

---

## The bearing

We do not sell. We serve. This is working principle 7 — their win first: find the win of the person across the table, ask what they need and become the shortest path to it. A DM sequence is four touches with air between them, and the first one does not mention Five Points at all.

1. **Touch 1 is pure curiosity about them.** Show you know their world, ask one real question, and stop. No pitch, no link, no mention of what you do. The goal is a reply, not a booking. This is the change that kills the salesy read.
2. **Let the cost do the persuading, not your opinion.** When you go deeper, you ask about the gap and what it costs — you never assert that they have a problem.
3. **Offer the after-state, not the toolset.** When you finally name the reason you wrote, name the transformation — the business that runs without living in their head — and make finding out cost them nothing.
4. **Reluctance is the posture.** You build for three at a time. You leave cleanly. The door-open close pulls more replies than any chase.

## The sequence — both platforms

**Touch 0 — Warm (24 to 48 hours before Touch 1).** Follow or connect. Engage with one recent post in a specific, genuine way — a real remark, never "great post."

**Touch 1 — Show you know them.** A specific, true observation about their world plus one genuine question. Not a word about Five Points. Goal: a reply.

**If they reply:** talk like a human, one or two exchanges, follow their energy. Then, lightly, the take-away invitation. Move to booking.

**Touch 2 — The cost (3 to 4 days later, if no reply).** A question or quiet observation about what the gap costs. Still no pitch. You are showing you see them, not selling them.

**Touch 3 — The invitation (4 to 5 days later).** Name the after-state, then the de-risked offer. One ask, framed as theirs to take or leave.

**Touch 4 — The close (5 to 7 days later).** Leave it cleanly. Then 90 days before re-entry.

## Instagram track

**Register.** Spare, observational, human. The owner often runs the account, so it is personal — keep it cool, not familiar. No emoji. Keep Touch 1 short enough to survive the message-request preview.

**Anti-template rule (operator ruling, 2026-07-24).** Never open with the "[First name] — observation" pattern; it reads as a mail merge. The templates below define structure and intent only. Every sent message is drafted fresh as flowing, natural prose — the name woven into a sentence where it belongs, sentence shapes varied across a batch so no two messages share a skeleton. Each message should read as the genuine start of a conversation that could become a partnership.

### Touch 1 — show you know them, by vertical

- **Real Estate:** "[First name] — I have been watching [signal]. A team at your level does not change houses lightly. Out of genuine curiosity, no agenda: when a move like that lands, what is the part that has to get rebuilt underneath before the brand out front catches up?"
- **Med Spa:** "[First name] — saw [signal]. Opening a second place is the moment most owners feel the ground shift, even when it looks effortless from outside. Curious what stretched first for you — the scheduling, the follow-up, something else?"
- **Golf / Club:** "[First name] — [signal] reads as a real statement about where the club is headed. The part I always wonder about from outside: did the systems underneath keep pace, or is it a new set of booking and billing rules sitting beside the old ones?"
- **Homebuilder:** "[First name] — [signal] is serious throughput. However good the finished homes look, that pace tends to test the seams. Genuinely curious: how much of the coordination still lives with a few key people?"
- **Aviation:** "[First name] — [signal] is a real step. In this business the quiet pressure is that everything rides on coordination staying flawless as you grow. Curious how that feels from where you sit." *(Patience by design.)*

### Touch 2 — the cost (no pitch)

> "[First name] — one thing I keep noticing with [vertical] mid-[growth or move]: the cost never shows up where you look for it. It is not the visible thing, it is the deal or the hour that slips while the machinery is half-rebuilt. Curious whether you are feeling any of that, or whether it has been smoother than that."

### Touch 3 — the after-state, then the de-risked offer

> "[First name] — the real reason I wrote. The ones who get the layer underneath right stop living inside the machine; the standard holds without them holding it up. If it is ever useful, I spend twenty minutes showing three places the gap is costing you now — your numbers, kept either way, nothing to buy. Only if you want it."

### Touch 4 — close

> "[First name] — I will leave it here so I am not crowding you. If the timing ever turns, the twenty minutes stands and the findings are yours regardless. Either way, genuinely, good luck with [signal]."

## LinkedIn track

**Register.** Professional, composed, a touch fuller. Substance over warmth, but the same serve-not-sell bearing.

### Touch 0 — connection note (show you know them, no pitch)

> "[First name] — [signal] caught my attention. A [team / practice / club] at your level does not make that move lightly. No agenda — would value the connection."

### Touch 1 — after the connection is accepted

- **Real Estate:** "Thanks for connecting, [First name]. I have been following [signal] — a move like that re-opens every system at once underneath the brand. Genuinely curious, no pitch: which part of the rebuild is proving the heaviest? The modern buyer turns up already holding the data, so I always wonder where the strain lands first."
- **Med Spa:** "Thanks for connecting, [First name]. [Signal] is the kind of step where inventory, follow-up and compliance all stretch at once across two sites. Curious which one stretched first for you — I find it is rarely the one owners expect."
- **Golf / Club:** "Thanks for connecting, [First name]. [Signal] reads as a real investment in the member experience. The question I always find interesting: did the booking, billing and scheduling keep pace, or quietly fragment further? Curious how it feels from the inside."
- **Homebuilder:** "Thanks for connecting, [First name]. [Signal] is the kind of throughput that tests the seams — the coordination across dozens of stakeholders and months, and the cost precision that protects the margin. Curious how much of that still lives with a few key people."
- **Aviation:** "Thanks for connecting, [First name]. [Signal] is a real step. Everything in this business rides on coordination staying flawless as you scale. Curious how you are holding discretion and precision at the new size — no agenda, it is simply what I find myself thinking about." *(Long game — slow the cadence.)*

### Touch 2 — the cost

> "[First name] — a thought since my last note. The cost of a stretched operation rarely shows up where people look for it. It is not the visible thing, it is the margin or the hour that leaks quietly while attention is elsewhere. Curious whether any of that is familiar, or whether it has held together better than most."

### Touch 3 — the after-state, then the offer

> "[First name] — the actual reason I wrote. The [vertical] who get the layer underneath right stop living inside the machine; the standard scales without thinning, and without the founder holding it up. If it is ever useful, I run a short diagnostic — about twenty minutes, no deck — that names three places the gap is costing you now, using your real workflow, and you keep the findings whether or not anything follows. Only if it is worth your time."

### Touch 4 — close

> "[First name] — I will leave it there rather than crowd your inbox. If the timing changes, the twenty minutes stands and the findings are yours regardless. Good luck with [signal]."

## Merge fields

- `[First name]` — the decision-maker.
- `[signal]` — the specific live tell. Load-bearing. Never send without it.
- `[vertical]` — real estate team, premium spa, club, builder, aviation operator.

## Volume without a blast

Touch 1 and Touch 3 are written by hand, never automated — they are the human ones. The hundred is tiered, not uniform: Tier 1 names get a call plus a personal DM, Tier 2 names get this sequence personalised by signal. See `outreach-sprint-playbook.md`.
