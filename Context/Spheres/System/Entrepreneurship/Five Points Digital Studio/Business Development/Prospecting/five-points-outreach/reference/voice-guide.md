# Voice guide — Marty's outreach

Provisional. There is no saved writing-style profile yet, so this is a
starting frame built from how he writes elsewhere. It gets corrected by
his edits, not by guesswork.

## The register

Atlanta, considered, dry. Someone who reads widely and does not need you
to know it. Direct without being blunt. Warm without being familiar.

He is a creative director writing to a business owner. Not a salesperson,
not a peer pretending to be a peer. The confidence comes from having
actually looked at their business, which he has.

## Do

- Open with the specific thing. No runway.
- Short sentences next to long ones. Vary the rhythm.
- Say the ask plainly, once.
- Let a dry observation carry the warmth. Understatement travels further
  than enthusiasm.
- Sound like the message took four minutes to write, because it did.

## Do not

- No "I hope this finds you well". No "I came across your profile".
- No compliments as runway. "Love what you're building" is a tell.
- No credentials in message one.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji in the first message.
- No "quick question" when it is not quick.
- No jargon he would not say aloud.
- No em dashes.

## The test

Read it aloud. If it sounds like a message, send it. If it sounds like
copy, rewrite it.
