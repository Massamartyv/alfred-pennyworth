# Message drafting

Read `queue/<slug>/research.md` and `reference/voice-guide.md`.
Write `queue/<slug>/draft.md`.

## The move

Do not offer help. **Ask them to help.**

The opening message asks the recipient to critique a diagnostic Marty is
building for people like them. This is honest — he genuinely wants their
input, and it genuinely improves the instrument. It also inverts the
transaction: he is asking for expertise rather than offering unsolicited
assistance, which is the difference between a message that gets answered
and one that gets archived.

Cohort-specific framing is in `reference/cohort-briefs.md`.

## Constraints

- **Under 120 words.** Instagram DMs and LinkedIn messages get skimmed.
- **Open with the hook.** Something specific and true from the research.
  Never "I came across your profile" or "I hope this finds you well".
- **One ask.** Never two.
- **No compliments before substance.** Praise as a runway signals a pitch.
- **No credentials in the first message.** He has not earned the right
  to a CV yet, and it inverts the frame he is trying to set.
- **Nothing from the research that reveals close reading.** Post from
  last week, fine. Something from three years ago, unsettling.
- **No urgency, no scarcity, no fake deadline.**
- **Read it aloud.** If it does not sound like something a person says,
  rewrite it.

## Output format

    # Draft — <Name>
    slug: <slug>
    channel: <linkedin|instagram>
    hook source: <where the opening line came from>
    drafted: <date>

    ## Message
    <the text, exactly as it should be sent>

    ## Word count
    <n>

    ## Why this opening
    <two sentences>

    ## Risk check
    - Reads as a pitch?          no / why not
    - Reveals close reading?     no / why not
    - Anything unverified?       no / what
    - Would Marty say this?      yes / flag it

    ## If they reply
    Three likely responses and how to handle each. No scripts — Marty
    is having the conversation, not reading from a card.

## Learning his voice

There is no saved writing-style profile yet. When Marty edits a draft,
read the diff and append what changed to `reference/voice-guide.md` under
**Observed corrections**. After roughly ten edits the drafts should need
very little work. That accumulation is the point of the system.
