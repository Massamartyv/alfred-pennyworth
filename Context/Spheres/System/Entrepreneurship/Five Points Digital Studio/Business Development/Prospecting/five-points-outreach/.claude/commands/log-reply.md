description: Record a reply and suggest a response
---
Arguments: <slug> then the reply text.

Set status `replied` (or `booked` if a call is agreed, or `passed` if
they declined), set replied_date, save the text in queue/<slug>/thread.md.

Then read the research brief and suggest how to respond — as notes for a
conversation Marty is having, not a script. If they raised a problem the
brief missed, say so; that is a correction to the research method, and
worth more than the single reply.
