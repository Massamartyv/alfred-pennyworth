# Lead Qualification

The second stage of the client flywheel – Qualify. Buying-power appraisal and ICP scoring, run through the prospect-appraiser skill, filed to the CRM as a star rating. The lightest copy load in Business Development – nothing here is written for a prospect to see; the output is a judgment call for the Sales Lead, never a client-facing artefact.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Researcher

- Confirm a CRM card exists for the prospect – dedupe before create, per the Notion Contacts protocol
- Load whatever capture already exists – Discovery Kit forms, transcripts, prior notes, a prior appraisal if one is on file
- Determine the invocation depth the moment calls for – a snapshot for quick triage, a full appraisal ahead of a proposal, a sweep across a pipeline batch
- Confirm ICP criteria from `Business Development/_index.md` before scoring begins

**Output:** Qualification intake brief – CRM state, capture on hand, invocation depth.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Researcher

- Confirm the provenance ceiling realistically available for this prospect before research time is spent – what public evidence exists, what falls to benchmark
- For a sweep, announce scope before spending – count, names, snapshot depth – and hold for the go
- Confirm which ladder doors in Product Development are plausible before the appraisal maps capacity to a rung

**Output:** Appraisal scope – invocation confirmed, provenance ceiling, plausible door range.

**Note:** This is a scope check, not a Manor Protocol hard gate. Qualification output never reaches the prospect in any form and is Tier 2 under `Agents/agent-guidelines.md` – execute, then notify.

---

## Execution

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Researcher

- Dispatch to the prospect-appraiser skill at the confirmed invocation – appraise, snapshot, sweep or reconcile
- Work Lens One – their economics: customer lifetime value, revenue triangulated from at least two methods, every figure rung-tagged
- Work Lens Two – Five Points economics: marketing capacity, retainer capacity, ladder mapping, Client LTV
- Assign the star rating by judgment against the anchors, citing the anchor phrase – never by formula

**Output:** Completed appraisal – star rating, revenue band, recommended door, Client LTV, confidence level.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Offer Steward
**Crew:** Reviewer:Scrutiny

- Confirm every figure carries its provenance rung – Disclosed, Direct, Inferred or Benchmark – and that none has borrowed a rung it did not earn
- Confirm no source is fabricated – an unsourced figure drops down the ladder rather than acquiring a citation
- Confirm any divergence between a disclosed figure and the public-evidence estimate is flagged, not averaged away
- Confirm the star rating cites its anchor and states confidence separately from capacity

**Output:** Appraisal critique – pass, or return to Execution with specific notes.

**Note:** Internal instrument, Tier 2. This Critique compresses rather than gates – consistent with `Agents/agent-guidelines.md` treatment of internal or draft content.

---

## Release

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Broadcaster

- Write the four CRM properties and the body section per the appraisal
- Present the summary in chat before writing, per the skill's Tier 2 protocol
- Route a cleared prospect to the Sales Lead for the Working Call; route a sub-scale or off-ICP prospect back to nurture rather than forward to Discover

**Output:** CRM updated, prospect routed to Discover or held at nurture.
