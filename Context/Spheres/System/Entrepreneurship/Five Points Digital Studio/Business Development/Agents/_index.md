# Business Development – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Business Development department owns ICP, prospecting and outreach, partnerships, pipeline, channels, and positioning and market strategy. Everything that fills the pipeline and holds the bar on who we sell to. Carries forward the Attract and Qualify ground of the former Growth department and the positioning and market-strategy ground of the former Strategy department.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Business Development role definition and specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Business Development** | Department head. Owns pipeline health and positioning integrity. | All | – |
| **Business Development Lead** | Outbound sequences, partnership development, pipeline generation. | Reconnaissance, Direction | Researcher |
| **Positioning Lead** | Client positioning, differentiation frameworks, narrative architecture, audience and buyer-behaviour research. Joins complex or Platinum-track discovery calls for a market-positioning read. | Reconnaissance, Direction | Researcher |

### Role activation

Single follow-up emails and CRM updates engage only the Business Development Lead. Full outbound pushes activate the Business Development Lead across all three workflows. Positioning frameworks and market-strategy questions activate the Positioning Lead.
