# Business Development – Five Points Digital Studio

ICP, prospecting and outreach, partnerships, pipeline, channels, positioning and market strategy. Business Development owns who we sell to and how we fill the pipeline – the ground that used to sit inside Growth's Attract and Qualify stages and inside Strategy's positioning and audience work.

## Structure

```
Business Development/
  _index.md                       – This file
  Prospecting/                    – ICP sourcing, seed lists, outreach runbooks, Clay audience builds
  Call Scripts/                   – Foundation and vertical-specific cold-call scripts
  Government Contracting/         – SAM registration dossier and fillsheet
  positioning-prospecting-directive.md – Positioning and ICP ruling (from Strategy)
  Agents/                         – Department-specific agents, workflows, criteria
    _index.md                    – Agent roster and workflow registry
    Workflows/                   – Named Manor Protocol sequences
```

## Scope

### What belongs here
- Offer-agnostic prospecting and outbound sequences
- Lead generation, ICP definition and lead qualification
- Business development and partnerships
- Pipeline management and channel strategy
- Market research and competitive intelligence gathered for a live prospecting push
- Client positioning and strategic recommendations
- Audience analysis and buyer behaviour research
- Industry trend analysis and pattern detection that feeds a positioning call

### What does not belong here
- Brand marketing, content creation and sales process (Marketing & Sales)
- Offer architecture and pricing (Product Development)
- Client delivery post-sale (Operations)
- Institutional case studies and standing methodology archives (Knowledge Base)
