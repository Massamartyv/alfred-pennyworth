# Rolls-Royce

**Who** – The English luxury motor car manufacturer founded 1904, operating today as Rolls-Royce Motor Cars from Goodwood, West Sussex. The Bespoke programme allows each Phantom, Ghost or Spectre commission to be individually specified: exterior colour matching, interior leather sourcing, marquetry veneers, monogrammed headrests, illuminated fascias. No two Bespoke commissions are identical. The Goodwood facility documents every commission in detail before delivery.

**Why aligned** – Rolls-Royce is the primary referent of the Marque Principle, the cross-layer governing philosophy of Five Points Digital Studio: "Every Five Points offer is treated the way Rolls-Royce treats a Phantom commission." Not a service. Not a subscription. A marque-grade commission. The Marque Principle is the second of the two invariant operating rules – the Asset Principle is what Five Points makes; the Marque Principle is how Five Points treats what it makes. Rolls-Royce surfaces across all four layers of the fingerprint. It is the single most consequential external reference in the entire document.

**The proposition** – That bespoke is not a price tier, it is an orientation. A Rolls-Royce Bespoke commission begins with a conversation about the owner's life: where they travel, who rides with them, what materials they have handled before, what the car must feel like at rest in a specific light. The spec emerges from the person. The object is configured to one life, then documented in a commissioning record that travels with it. The craft standard is set before the commission begins, not negotiated during it.

**Canon** – Rolls-Royce Bespoke programme. The Phantom commission process and documentation. Rolls-Royce Goodwood commissioning documentation photography: bespoke colour configurations, leather matching spreads, marquetry veneer grain selections, hand-painted coachlines. The Spectre electric grand tourer (video grading reference for the "open modern" register – named alongside Ferrari Roma in Layer 3 of the fingerprint).

**Pull for** – positioning, proposition, offer-design, commissioning-document language, photography, art-direction, video-grading, Marque Principle applications, client-relationship framing, quality-gate decisions, style-arbiter questions.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. The Marque Principle is structural rather than trend-sensitive. The Spectre electric platform is current and positions Rolls-Royce in a contemporary conversation without compromising the bespoke stance – a relevant proof point for the Five Points thesis that premium infrastructure can be modern without being generic.
