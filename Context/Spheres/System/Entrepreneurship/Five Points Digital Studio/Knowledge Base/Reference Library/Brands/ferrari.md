# Ferrari

**Who** – The Italian automotive manufacturer founded 1939 in Maranello, Emilia-Romagna. Ferrari Special Projects (XP, one-off commissions and personalisation orders) operates at the highest tier of bespoke automotive production. The Maranello factory has been documented in archival photography since the 1950s – craftsmen finishing bodywork by hand, engineers leaning over technical drawings, painted metal in progress under workshop light.

**Why aligned** – Ferrari is the co-primary referent of the Marque Principle alongside Rolls-Royce: "Every Five Points offer is treated the way Ferrari treats a Special Projects build." Decision Default 5 in the fingerprint reads: "When in doubt about style, ask what Rolls-Royce Bespoke or Ferrari Special Projects would do." Ferrari also provides two distinct visual references: (1) the canonical photography source – "Ferrari Maranello archival photography – factory floor, craftsmen at work, hand-finished components" – one of nine canonical references in the Five Points image library; (2) the video colour register – the "open modern" grading regime is defined as "the Ferrari Roma and Rolls-Royce Spectre register."

**The proposition** – That craft is legible in process as much as in product. The Ferrari Maranello archive does not photograph the car. It photographs the hand holding the tool, the painter steadying a coachline, the engineer annotating a blueprint. The object earns authority through proximity to its making. Special Projects extends this to an individual commission: each is a recorded act of design, not a production variant.

**Canon** – Ferrari Special Projects programme and SP series commissions. Ferrari Maranello archival photography (factory floor documentation, 1960s through present). The Ferrari Roma (video grading and colour register reference: bright, controlled, intentional light – the "open modern" benchmark). The Ferrari F40 and 250 GTO as historical reference points for objects that accrue in value through the specificity of their making.

**Pull for** – positioning, proposition, offer-design, photography, art-direction, video-grading, Marque Principle applications, commissioning-document language, quality-gate decisions, style-arbiter questions.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. The Special Projects programme is ongoing. The Roma's visual register remains current and is aligned with the broader shift in luxury automotive marketing away from velocity-and-track toward precision-and-life.
