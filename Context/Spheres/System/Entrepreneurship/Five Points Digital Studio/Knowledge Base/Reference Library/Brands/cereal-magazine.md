# Cereal Magazine

**Who** – UK-based biannual travel and lifestyle publication founded 2012. Distinguished by photography that centres material texture and surface quality: the grain of a stone wall, the weave of a linen sheet, the patina on a brass tap. The editorial point of view is that a place is understood through its materials, not its panoramas.

**Why aligned** – Cereal Magazine is one of nine canonical photography references in the Five Points image library. Its specific contribution is named as "material honesty" – the discipline of showing things as they are: their grain, their weight, their marks of use or making. The Five Points photography standard explicitly names "natural materials in close-up: leather grain, paper texture, wood finish, brass patina, wool weave" and "hand-built detail shots: stitching on leather, wood grain on a dashboard, brass on a dial." These are Cereal's native registers applied to the Five Points context.

Material honesty in this context also carries an anti-airbrushing implication: the thing photographed is not idealised or smoothed, it is lit to reveal its actual character. This aligns with the broader Five Points commitment to specificity – every spec named, no vague scopes, the bespoke is bespoke.

**The proposition** – That material surfaces carry cultural and craft information that architecture and landscape do not. To photograph a woven fabric or a thrown ceramic correctly is to communicate something about the quality of attention that produced it. In the Five Points context, this is the photograph as proof of standard: the leather commissioning document is photographed at the grain level because the grain is where the argument lives.

**Canon** – Cereal magazine editorial spreads (volume 1 through present). The characteristic close-up of a textured wall, a worn step, a poured concrete surface. Travel features that find an entire city in the material quality of its building stock.

**Pull for** – photography, art-direction, visual-direction, collateral-photography, material-documentation, case-study-photography.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. The material-honesty aesthetic remains current and continues to gain ground against the flat, evenly-lit product photography standard in direct-to-consumer and professional services. Its uptake in the wider market increases rather than decreases its utility as a reference – material photography is no longer a niche signal; executed at Cereal's standard, it remains distinctive.
