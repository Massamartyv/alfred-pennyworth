# Pangram Pangram

**Who** – Montreal-based independent type foundry founded 2017. Known for contemporary typefaces with editorial versatility across display, body and mono applications. The foundry produces type at a professional level with a pricing model accessible to independent studios: one-time licensing per face rather than subscription, in the $200 to $400 range per face for production use.

**Why aligned** – Pangram Pangram is the exclusive type foundry for all three Five Points typefaces. This is not a reference for inspiration – it is the operational type supplier for the entire brand system. Three faces:

- **PP Editorial New** – primary display face. Weights: Thin, Ultralight, Light, Regular, Light Italic, Medium, Semi-Bold. Used for Headline Large, Headline Small, Subheading, Pull Quotes and the wordmark. The wordmark weight is Medium 500; display headlines run at Light 300; pull quotes run at Light Italic 300i.
- **PP Neue Montreal** – body face. Weights: Book 350, Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700. Used for all body copy, captions, eyebrow text, Alt Subheading and the email signature.
- **PP Supply Mono** – mono face. Used exclusively for hex values, filenames, version numbers, timestamps and technical labels. Never body copy.

Open-source fallbacks are documented in the fingerprint for each face: Fraunces for Editorial New, Space Grotesk for Neue Montreal, Space Mono for Supply Mono.

**The proposition** – A type foundry that produces editorial typefaces at the quality expected by design studios without the enterprise licensing costs that typically accompany that quality tier. The Pangram Pangram model allows Five Points to hold a proprietary typographic identity – one that reads as commissioned and specific rather than sourced from a free font library – at a licensing cost that does not require enterprise budget. The open-source fallback stack means the system degrades gracefully in environments where the paid licence cannot be embedded.

**Canon** – PP Editorial New: editorial display, serif with warmth, legible at headline scale and holding character at display scale. PP Neue Montreal: humanist grotesque, versatile across weight range, confident body text face. PP Supply Mono: workhorse mono, clean and functional without character-as-feature. Full Pangram Pangram catalogue.

The editorial serif display face + geometric grotesque body pairing is a stable contemporary typographic system with a long runway before it reads as dated.
