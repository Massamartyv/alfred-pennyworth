# Alex Hormozi

**Who** – American entrepreneur and author, founder of Acquisition.com. Known for the Grand Slam Offer framework, systematised in *$100M Offers* (2021). Built and sold gym chains, software businesses and coaching companies before transitioning to investing and teaching business operators how to construct offers that close at premium price points. The central thesis: price resistance dissolves when perceived value is engineered correctly.

**Why aligned** – The Hormozi Grand Slam Offer framework governs the entire Human Construct offer architecture – the flagship strategic advisory product at the top of the Five Points ascending transaction model. It is named directly in the frontmatter of the human-construct-base-offer document: "Framework: Hormozi Grand Slam Offer (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood / Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice)."

Hormozi also governs the pricing posture of the studio at the fingerprint level. Decision Default 3 reads: "When pricing, lead with the value equation, never with the hourly rate – The Hormozi framework." This is a named invariant decision rule, not an optional reference. Every Five Points pricing conversation begins with value, not time.

The value equation – Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement divided by Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice – is the operational lens through which every tier of the offer ladder is positioned. The equation does not replace judgement; it structures it. Each variable is a design lever: raise the dream outcome by naming the specific asset that will be built; raise perceived likelihood by providing proof; lower time delay by naming the delivery horizon; lower effort and sacrifice by removing the friction the principal would otherwise carry.

**The formula** – Construct the offer by maximising numerator variables and minimising denominator variables, in that order. Name the asset, prove it works, compress the timeline, remove the friction. Price after the value is built, not before. Never open a pricing conversation with a number without first anchoring the equation in the principal's mind.

**Canon** – *$100M Offers* (2021). The Grand Slam Offer framework. The value equation: (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort and Sacrifice). The "core offer" and "offer stack" architecture (what is included, why each element is included, how each element reduces a variable in the equation).

**Pull for** – offer-design, positioning, proposition, pricing decisions, sales-page copy, commissioning-document structure, Human Construct tier positioning, ascending-transaction-model structure.
