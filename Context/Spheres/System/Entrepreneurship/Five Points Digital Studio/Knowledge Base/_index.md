# Knowledge Base – Five Points Digital Studio

Institutional memory for Five Points. Case studies, research, methodologies and AI agent learnings. Knowledge Base absorbs the standing research and competitive-intelligence ground of the former Strategy department – industry trend analysis and pattern detection that outlives a single prospecting or positioning push belongs here, not in a live workflow folder.

## Structure

```
Knowledge Base/
├── Case Studies/              – Client results documented
├── Research/                  – Industry trends, competitive intel
├── Methodologies/             – Proprietary frameworks
├── AI Learnings/              – Proven prompts, tool evaluations
├── Reference Library/         – Creators, brands, works and curators for inspiration and creative direction
└── Agents/                    – Department-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here
- Client case studies and documented results
- Standing market and industry research
- Competitive intelligence gathered for institutional reference
- Proprietary methodologies and frameworks
- AI agent learnings – proven prompts, tool evaluations

### What does not belong here
- Live prospecting research scoped to a single push (Business Development)
- Positioning decisions for a live client engagement (Business Development)
- Discovery methodology applied in a live call (Marketing & Sales)
- The Reference Library's own governance – see its `_index.md`

## Agent Instructions

- When pitching a prospect, search Case Studies/ for relevant proof points.
- When drafting thought leadership, check Research/ for current data.
- When discovering an effective prompt or technique, add it to AI Learnings/.
- This is where the business gets smarter over time. Contribute back.
