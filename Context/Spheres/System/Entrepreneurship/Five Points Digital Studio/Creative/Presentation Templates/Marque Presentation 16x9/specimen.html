Five Points Marque presentation 2026 pg 4
A document system, not a document.
The work begins with one question – why is it still done this way?

Most operations run on systems built for the company they used to be. The gap is the most expensive line on a ledger no one keeps.

The Letterpress cut the client journey into 16 instruments, and this deck is the first – the framework every presentation the studio gives descends from. An instance is a fill-and-render exercise, never a fresh design engagement.

Assets, not services. Infrastructure, not deliverables. Built once, owned for life, compounding every quarter.

12 components govern 10 sheets. One voice speaks per document. Two registers – bone and ink – carry the rhythm, and the serif, the grotesque and the mono each hold their own office.

The grammar
Assembled, never composed.

Every sheet is assembled from named components – the running head, the ghost line, the action card – so no instance begins from a blank page.

The running head carries the house, the document and the folio. The ghost line withholds the second thought at a whisper. The action card closes every chapter with an invitation rather than a summary.

Change the copy and the components hold their stations; the grid does not renegotiate itself per instance.

Five Points Marque presentation 2026 pg 9
A template that believes its own argument does not re-argue it from scratch.
The house · proposal doctrine

The studio argument is the same for every singular mind it serves; only the evidence changes per principal. So the canon stays fixed in the framework and the slots carry what varies.

The gates hold the rest – overflow, punctuation, runts and serif discipline are checked on every render, and nothing ships with a slot left unfilled.

Marque presentation 2026 pg 10
The last word.

Every commission leaves the owner holding the keys – the system, the book of it and the leverage it compounds.

The framework ships gated. A sheet that overflows refuses to render; a document that breaks the conventions refuses to send.

Nothing leaves the house below the marque standard, and nothing asks the operator to re-litigate design to hold that standard.
