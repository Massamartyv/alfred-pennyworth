# Content Creation

Blog posts, social content, newsletters, long-form writing, captions. The most frequent workflow in Creative – scales from a single caption to a full content series.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Researcher
**Crew:** Explorer

- Review content calendar and editorial direction
- Research the topic: trends, existing coverage, audience interest
- Gather reference material: competitor content, industry data, cultural touchpoints
- Identify the audience for this specific piece

**Output:** Topic brief with references, angle and audience context.

**Note:** For lightweight content (single captions, routine posts), Reconnaissance may be a 30-second scan rather than a full research phase. Scale to complexity.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Brand Strategist (or Copywriter for lightweight tasks)
**Crew:** Strategist

- Define the angle: what is the point of view?
- Set the format: blog, social carousel, newsletter, long-form
- Establish tone within the brand voice range (casual to formal)
- Outline the structure: sections, hook, body, close

**Output:** Content brief with angle, format, tone and structure.

**Hard gate for substantial content (blog posts, newsletters, series). Lightweight content (captions, single posts) may compress Direction into Execution.**

---

## Execution

**Role:** Copywriter
**Crew:** Creator

- Write to the brief
- Maintain brand voice throughout
- Optimise for the platform (character limits, formatting conventions)
- Include calls to action where appropriate

**Output:** Draft content, formatted for the target platform.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Editor
**Crew:** Evaluator

- Run the grammar audit (invoke grammar-nazi skill)
- Evaluate against the four criteria:
  1. **Brand alignment** – Does it sound like us (or the client)?
  2. **Originality** – Is there a genuine point of view, or is this content anyone could have written?
  3. **Craft and finish** – Is the prose clean? Is the rhythm intentional?
  4. **Emotional resonance** – Does it earn the reader's attention?

**Output:** Edit notes or approval.

**Hard gate for client-facing content. Internal or draft content may compress Critique.**

---

## Release

**Crew:** Maestro

- Format for publishing platform
- Schedule via Buffer (if Five Points or personal brand content)
- Hand off to Operations (if client content)

**Output:** Published or delivered content.
