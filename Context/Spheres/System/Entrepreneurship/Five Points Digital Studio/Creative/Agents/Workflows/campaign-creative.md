# Campaign Creative

Visual and copy direction for marketing campaigns, ad creative and landing pages. Cross-studio workflow -- Creative sets the direction, Production builds the assets, Growth defines the objectives.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Researcher
**Crew:** Explorer

- Review campaign objectives from Growth (target audience, conversion goal, budget)
- Research competitor campaigns in the space
- Gather visual and tonal references for the campaign mood
- Identify cultural moments, trends or timing opportunities
- Review past campaign performance data if available

**Output:** Campaign reconnaissance brief -- objectives, references, opportunities.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Brand Strategist
**Crew:** Strategist

- Define the campaign concept: the central idea that holds everything together
- Set visual direction for campaign assets (colour, imagery, typography treatment)
- Establish copy direction: headline approach, tone, messaging hierarchy
- Define asset list: what needs to be created (ads, landing page, email, social)
- Map the campaign narrative: how does the story unfold across touchpoints?

**Output:** Campaign creative brief.

**Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding.**

---

## Execution

**Roles:** Art Director, Copywriter
**Crew:** Creator

### Art Director
- Create visual concepts for campaign assets
- Design ad layouts, landing page wireframes, social templates
- Ensure visual consistency across all campaign touchpoints

### Copywriter
- Write headlines, body copy, CTAs for each asset
- Adapt messaging for different formats and platforms
- Write email sequences if the campaign includes outbound

**Output:** Campaign asset suite -- all creative elements, formatted per platform.

**Note:** Production studio handles the technical build of landing pages and complex assets. Creative provides the direction and raw creative; Production engineers the final product.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Editor
**Crew:** Evaluator

- Evaluate the campaign as a system -- do all pieces feel like one story?
- Apply the four criteria to each asset:
  1. **Brand alignment** -- Is the campaign on-brand for the client?
  2. **Originality** -- Does it stand out in the client's market?
  3. **Craft and finish** -- Are the details sharp across every touchpoint?
  4. **Emotional resonance** -- Does the campaign make the audience feel something?

**Output:** Campaign critique report.

**Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding.**

---

## Release

**Crew:** Maestro

- Hand off approved assets to Production for build (landing pages, complex layouts)
- Hand off approved copy to Growth for deployment (email sequences, ad platforms)
- Archive campaign creative in Operations/Clientele/{Client}/Deliverables/

**Output:** Campaign creative package, handed off to cross-studio partners.
