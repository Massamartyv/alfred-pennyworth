# Client Brand Direction

Ongoing creative direction for existing clients – ensuring their output stays on-brand across all touchpoints. This is a recurring workflow, not a one-time project. It runs continuously for the duration of the client engagement.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Researcher
**Crew:** Researcher

- Review recent client output across all channels
- Scan for brand drift: inconsistencies in voice, visual, or messaging
- Monitor competitor brand evolution
- Surface cultural shifts that may affect the client's positioning

**Output:** Brand health check – observations, drift flags, opportunities.

**Cadence:** Monthly for retainer clients. Ad hoc when triggered by new output review.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Brand Strategist
**Crew:** Creator

- Assess whether drift flags require a course correction
- If correction needed: write a brand direction memo with specific guidance
- If no correction needed: confirm current direction holds
- Update client brand guidelines if positioning has evolved

**Output:** Brand direction memo or confirmation.

**Hard gate only if a course correction is recommended. Confirmation of current direction flows through without a gate.**

---

## Execution

**Roles:** Copywriter, Art Director
**Crew:** Creator

- Apply direction memo to upcoming client deliverables
- Create brand-aligned templates for recurring content types
- Update style guides, voice docs, or visual references as needed

**Output:** Updated client creative assets and templates.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Editor
**Crew:** Reviewer:Scrutiny

- Review updated assets against the four criteria
- Specifically check: does the updated direction feel like a natural evolution, not a jarring shift?
- Verify consistency across all touchpoints

**Output:** Critique notes or approval.

**Hard gate: human approval before releasing updated brand direction to the client.**

---

## Release

**Crew:** Broadcaster

- Deliver updated brand assets to Operations/Clientele/{Client}/Brand Assets/
- Notify client of any direction changes (via Operations)
- Update the client brief if positioning has shifted

**Output:** Updated brand direction, delivered and documented.
