# Five Points Digital Studio – Primary Mark Commission

Working title: **the Quintessence** – the fifth essence, the element beyond the four.

---

## The commission

Design the primary geometric mark for Five Points Digital Studio: a five-fold faceted snowflake. The mark lives alongside the "FIVE POINTS" wordmark set in PP Editorial New and carries the studio across every surface where the wordmark is absent or too long to work – business card fronts, envelope flaps, email signatures, social avatars, ceremonial seals and favicons.

This brief supersedes the five-circle cluster prompt in the brand fingerprint. The golden ratio discipline of that brief is retained in full; only the form changes. The change is logged as a fingerprint amendment.

## Context

Five Points builds the personal infrastructure of singular minds – owned operating systems that compound, commissioned to marque standard, built around one mind and one working life. The register is editorial luxury: Pentagram rigour, Aesop restraint, Bottega Veneta and Hermes hardware, Rolls-Royce Bespoke and Ferrari Special Projects as the lens when in doubt. The studio is precise, composed, systemic, bespoke, understated and declarative. The mark must be able to sit on a commissioning document beside a fountain pen and belong there.

## The concept

Nature builds every snowflake six-fold. A five-fold snowflake is a crystal that cannot occur on its own – it can only exist by design. That is the studio thesis in one object: infrastructure engineered to specification, never grown by default.

The lead idea is **one of one**. No two snowflakes are alike; no two commissions are alike. The mark is therefore a generative system, not a single drawing – one geometric law capable of producing an infinite family of unique crystals, with a single canonical crystal standing for the studio itself.

## The geometry law

These are construction constants. Every one is binary and checkable against the drawn artefact.

1. **Five-fold symmetry.** Five identical arms at 72 degrees. No six-fold read may survive at any scale.
2. **One morpheme.** Every closed facet is a golden rhombus – long diagonal to short diagonal in the ratio phi, 1.618. No other closed shape appears except the core pentagon and the spine.
3. **Fibonacci descent.** The rhombi step down the Fibonacci ladder. Canonical long-diagonal lengths in construction units: base kite 34, first branch pair 21, second branch pair 13, tip mote 13. Short diagonals follow as 21, 13, 8 and 8. The self-similar family is the ornament; nothing else is added.
4. **The core is a regular pentagon**, circumradius 21 units, vertex aligned to each arm. The pentagon carries phi natively – its diagonal-to-side ratio is the golden ratio. Five-ness and phi are the same fact in this shape.
5. **The spine** is an elongated four-point shard of total length 47 units, its segments descending the Fibonacci ladder – 21 to the widest point, 13 more to the second branch station, 13 more to the tip. It tapers to a sharp point.
6. **Branches spring at Fibonacci stations.** Two branch pairs per arm at approximately 58 degrees from the arm axis, sweeping outward and upward. The first springs beside the spine's widest point at 21 units along its 47-unit length; the second springs at 34 units. Each station is a rung down the descent.
7. **The tip mote is detached.** The smallest rhombus floats beyond each spine tip, separated by the standard seam. It is the crystal's terminal punctuation and the smallest member of the self-similar descent.
8. **Uniform negative space.** Every seam between adjacent facets is one constant channel width across the entire mark. No exceptions. Non-uniform seams are the single named failure mode of this commission.
9. **Softened joins, held points.** Every corner rounds at one small fixed radius, identical on every shape. The radius must be small enough that arm tips and mote tips still read as points. Sharpness of character survives; brittleness does not.
10. **Flat colour only.** Single colour per rendering. No strokes as visible outlines, no gradients, no shading, no shadows, no dimensional effects. The facets are carried entirely by the seams.

## The generative system

The mark is a law, not a logo. Deliver it as such.

- **Constants that never vary:** five-fold symmetry, the golden-rhombus morpheme, Fibonacci descent, uniform seam width, the corner radius, the detached mote, flat single colour.
- **Parameters that may vary per issue:** branch angle within a stated range, branch position along the upper spine within a stated range, relative rhombus proportions stepping to adjacent Fibonacci pairs, mote count from one to two per arm. The designer defines the exact parameter ranges such that every possible output is unmistakably of the family and no two issues are identical.
- **The canonical crystal** is one fixed parameter set, chosen at refinement, reserved for the studio itself – the favicon, the seal, the avatar, the letterhead mark.
- **Issued crystals** are generated per commission and belong to the principal's engagement – printed on the commissioning document cover, the owner's book and the certificate of authenticity. Each issue is recorded with its parameter set, like a chassis number.

## Scale discipline

There is one form. The seamed crystal is the mark at every size; no separate reduced drawing exists. At small scale the seam channels close optically and the crystal reads as a solid five-armed silhouette on its own – the simplification is performed by distance, not by a second construction.

- Verification renders at 400, 120, 40, 16 and 8 pixels
- At 8 pixels the silhouette must read as a five-armed crystal, never as a blot or an asterisk
- Optical-size compensation may be presented at refinement – a measured widening of the seam channel at small rendering sizes, in the manner of optical sizes in type design. Any compensation preserves the single-form principle: same geometry, same law, seam width as the only optical variable

## Forbidden

- Any six-fold or asterisk read
- Any literal five-pointed star or interlaced pentagram figure
- The register of crypto, corporate memphis, SaaS landing pages, playful bubbles, stock geometric logos or anything at home on Product Hunt
- Gradients, shadows, glows, bevels, 3D, outline-only versions
- Rotation other than zero degrees
- Non-uniform seams or mixed corner radii
- Snowflake-as-winter cliche: no frost textures, no blue, no seasonal framing anywhere in presentation

## Deliverables

2. PNG renders at 400, 120, 40, 16 and 8 pixels for scale verification
3. Horizontal lockup beside "FIVE POINTS" in PP Editorial New Medium 500 UPPERCASE, mark height equal to wordmark cap height, baseline-aligned
4. Vertical lockup, mark above wordmark, one mark-height gap
5. Favicon at 64 by 64 pixels rendered from the canonical crystal, carrying any approved optical-size compensation
6. The generative specification sheet: constants, parameter ranges, issue-numbering convention and three sample issued crystals demonstrating family coherence
7. Clear space and minimum size notation matching fingerprint conventions – 1x mark-height clear space minimum, 1.5x preferred

## Process

Present three candidates of the canonical crystal, each obeying every construction constant, differing only in the generative parameters – branch angle, branch position, proportion pairing. The founder selects one for refinement. Refinement iterates until the crystal is correct. Then produce the deliverables and the generative specification.
