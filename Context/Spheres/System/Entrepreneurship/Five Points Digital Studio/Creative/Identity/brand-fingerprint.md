# Five Points Digital Studio -- Brand Fingerprint

The governing reference for every studio-facing and client-facing output. Four layers -- Identity, Expression, Production, Behavioural -- plus cross-layer principles that are load-bearing across all four. Every field traces to a line of loaded context. Every decision was walked through a Direction gate, a Placeholder walk and a Critique gate under the Manor Protocol.

---

## Cross-Layer Principles

### The Asset Principle

Five Points manufactures durable infrastructure -- operating systems the client owns, operates and compounds returns from. Every engagement is architected to produce an asset, not a deliverable. Every decision is measured against a single question: does this compound or does it consume?

### The Marque Principle

Every Five Points offer is treated the way Rolls-Royce treats a Phantom commission or Ferrari treats a Special Projects build. Not a service. Not a subscription. A marque-grade commission. The Asset Principle is what Five Points makes; the Marque Principle is how Five Points treats what it makes.

Where it surfaces across layers:

| Layer | How it surfaces |
|---|---|
| Layer 1 | Below the Asset Principle as a cross-cutting principle. Resolves the tagline tension -- "passion" is the word of the heritage automobiliers, not motivational speak |
| Layer 2 | Ferrari and Rolls-Royce archival material in the canonical photography references. Hand-built detail shots, craftsmen at work, technical drawings, archival treatments |
| Layer 3 | Proposals reframed as commissioning documents. Case studies as marque histories. System Operations Manuals as owner's books. "A Five Points Commission" as the marque eyebrow on every cover page |
| Layer 4 | When in doubt, ask what Rolls-Royce Bespoke or Ferrari Special Projects would do. Treat every engagement as a commission, every client as a principal, every deliverable as a marque-grade artefact |

### The A-Series Paper Standard

All Five Points documents are produced in ISO 216 A-series dimensions. A4 for proposals, client letters, statements of work, invoices and case studies. A3 for presentation spreads. A5 for with-compliments slips and intimate collateral. The full A0 to A10 range is available for specific applications.

Rationale: European standard. Places Five Points in the editorial-luxury conversation. The square root of two proportional folding produces identical proportion at every fold. International signal that lands correctly in London, Lagos, Singapore, Medellin and New York.

### The AFOM Execution Principles

Seven principles extracted from the A Friend Of Mine Bayport case study, applied to Five Points within the locked framework:

1. **Total colour commitment per piece.** Each artefact floods one dominant colour. Obsidian is the commissioning register. Parchment is the working register. Emerald is the continuation register. Burnt Orange is kept strictly for eyebrows and marque detail.
2. **A mark that scales from 8px to 400mm.** The commissioned five-circle mark must hold identity at every rendering size.
3. **The wordmark lives on covers, not on letterhead.** Letterhead carries only the mark and a contact cluster. The wordmark appears when the document is announcing itself, not when it is working.
4. **Asymmetric editorial spreads.** Full-bleed image on one page, narrow text column on the other. Never fifty-fifty. Never centred.
5. **Corner-anchored contact typography.** Contact clusters live in one corner. Nothing runs the full width as a header or footer.
6. **Physical craft as signature.** Edge painting in the opposite colour to the face on every printed piece.
7. **Photography is either full-colour document or total monotone.** Never half-treatment.

#### Colour hierarchy

- Tier 1 signature duo: Obsidian and Parchment -- 80% of surface area
- Tier 2 supporting: Emerald and Burnt Orange
- Tier 3 reserved: Shadow Emerald and Aged Brass
- No fifth accent enters the brand without a rewrite of this document

#### Organic shape cycling rotation

Emerald, then Burnt Orange, then Obsidian. Fixed order, three-colour maximum. Parchment or Obsidian surface. 15% to 40% opacity on overlap.

#### Forbidden pairs

- Burnt Orange on Emerald -- competing saturation
- Graphite on Obsidian -- dark-on-dark fails contrast
- Stone on Parchment -- light-on-light fails contrast
- Aged Brass on Burnt Orange -- metamerism

#### Approved combinations

|---|---|
| Parchment on Obsidian | Signature reversal, body copy at scale |
| Obsidian on Parchment | Standard body on light |

| Emerald on Parchment | Secondary headlines, pillar labels |

### Typography

#### Primary display typeface

**PP Editorial New** by Pangram Pangram. Weights: Thin, Ultralight, Light, Regular, Light Italic, Medium, Semi-Bold. Open-source fallback: Fraunces from Google Fonts. Licensing: $200 to $400 per face one-time from Pangram Pangram for production use.

**Layer 2 amendment:** The default display weight shifts from Light 300 to Medium 500 for the wordmark. Semi-Bold 600 is added for the interim mark. Light 300 is retained for display headlines, pull quotes and editorial italic at hero scale.

#### Secondary body typeface

**PP Neue Montreal** by Pangram Pangram. Weights: Book 350, Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700. Open-source fallback: Space Grotesk from Google Fonts.

#### Mono typeface

**PP Supply Mono** by Pangram Pangram. Open-source fallback: Space Mono from Google Fonts. Used only for hex values, filenames, version numbers, timestamps and technical labels. Never body copy.

#### Digital hierarchy rules

- Modular scale 1.25 ratio -- major third
- Minimum body: 16px desktop, 15px mobile
- Display mobile scaling: approximately 30% reduction

#### Paragraph width rule

Approximately 65 characters per line, roughly 10 words at body size. Maximum measure: 680px at desktop. Lines exceeding this measure are a brand violation.

### Voice and Tone

**Language:** British English spelling, internationally legible phrasing. No regional idioms. No contractions. No em dashes.

#### Voice attributes -- fixed, never shift

| Attribute | Manifestation |
|---|---|
| Architectural | Speaks in systems, structures and phases. Never tasks, to-do lists or checkboxes. Process equals phases. Outcome equals asset |
| Composed | No manufactured urgency. No hype vocabulary. Urgency stated once, quietly, only when real |
| Declarative | Statements not hedges. "Builds" not "helps build." "Delivers" not "strives to deliver." No softening adverbs. No conditional qualifiers |
| Withholding | Reveals on proximity, not from across the room. Headlines imply; body copy names |
| Specific | Every number, spec, timeline, price and guarantee is named. No ranges where a number will do |

#### Tone calibration

| Context | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Client proposal | Exacting, measured, specific. Blueprint handed across a table. The composure sells |
| Sales page | Editorial, not corporate. Magazine, not landing page. Rewards a reader who is willing to read, not a reader who skims |
| Social media | Coolest register. Single observations. Single sentences. Never agency-posting-tips voice. When in doubt, post less |
| Internal documentation | Functional and clean. Voice steps back, the work speaks directly. No wit or flourish in body |
| Crisis communication | Slower, not faster. Composed, not reassuring. No apology theatre. The composure is the reassurance |
| Thought leadership | Highest-stakes register. Sage and Pioneer archetypes allowed to surface. Long-form essays, newsletter editions, keynote manuscripts |

### Photography and Imagery

**Style essence:** Low-key, warm, architectural, editorial. Interiors over exteriors. Objects over people. When people appear they are in the context of their work, never centred, never eye-contact. The image library feels like a private archive from a quiet workshop, not a stock library.

**Canonical references:**

- Kinfolk -- architectural restraint
- Cereal Magazine -- material honesty
- Monocle long-form features -- people-at-work register
- Pentagram case study photography -- object-centric discipline
- Ferrari Maranello archival photography -- factory floor, craftsmen at work, hand-finished components
- Rolls-Royce Goodwood commissioning documentation -- bespoke configuration, leather matching, marquetry
- Road and Track, Automobile Quarterly, Car Magazine pre-1990 editorial
- Concours d'Elegance photography -- object in its most composed moment
- Aman resort editorial -- low-key luxurious warmth

**Do:**

- Interiors: workshops, studios, libraries, drafting rooms, archives
- Objects of making: fountain pens, notebooks, drafting tools, paper, machined brass, tanned leather, aged wood, ceramic, stone
- Instruments of work: books, laptops, keyboards, sketched diagrams, blueprints
- People in context of work: hands at a desk, figure reading by a lamp, silhouette at a drafting table
- Natural materials in close-up: leather grain, paper texture, wood finish, brass patina, wool weave
- Architectural details: rooflines, doorways, corridors, cast shadows
- Hand-built detail shots: stitching on leather, wood grain on a dashboard, brass on a dial
- Craftsmen at work: hands holding instruments, a figure leaning over a drafting table
- Technical drawings and blueprints as compositional elements
- Heritage archival treatments -- the feeling of an image shot in 1964 and filed in an archive
- Monochrome when the subject earns it -- deliberate, not filter

**Do not:**

- Stock photography of any kind
- Bright evenly-lit office shots
- People looking directly at the lens
- Ring-light modernity or influencer aesthetic
- Desk shots with three devices and a succulent
- City skyline aerial shots
- Handshake-across-a-table shots
- Team photos lined up against a brand wall
- Overcomposed product shots with white sweeps
- Flat lays of branded merchandise
- Visible logos from other tools

**Lighting:**

- Single source, directional, warm
- Colour temperature 2700K to 3200K indoor, golden hour outdoor
- Shadows welcomed as compositional elements
- Cool light only as deliberate editorial choice
- Workshop lighting permitted -- shop light from a work lamp
- Forbidden: flat even lighting, overhead fluorescent, ring light, on-camera flash

**Colour in imagery:**

- Graded to match the brand palette
- Shadows: warm blacks, never crushed to pure black
- Midtones: earth-warm, slight orange-green bias
- Highlights: creamed whites, resolve toward Parchment not pure page
- Saturation: moderate, desaturated blues and greens, preserved warmth in reds, oranges and browns
- Contrast: medium-high, shadow detail retained
- Forbidden: bright-and-airy grading, pastel shifts, heavy teal-and-orange, Instagram aesthetic filters

**Composition:**

- Architectural -- frame built with floor-plan discipline
- Rule of thirds respected but not slavish; dead-centre permitted for singular objects
- Negative space: 50% to 70% of frame
- Leading lines, depth, framing within framing
- Human scale: people small within frame, architecture and objects carry equal weight
- Crop discipline: shoot for the frame, not shoot and crop

**Photography sourcing strategy:**

Imagery-minimum as the structural default -- the type and colour system carry most of the brand's weight. When imagery is required, it is generated via AI against tight prompt discipline referencing the Ferrari and Rolls-Royce canonical stack, and curated into a tightly-held library. Commissioned original photography is the long-term goal, budgeted for Q3 2026 or later once MRR clears sustained $15,000.

## Layer 3 -- Production

### Document Standards

#### Copy conventions

Inherited rules:

- No em dashes. En dashes only
- No contractions
- British English spelling
- Romance language possessive structures
- Numerals for 10 and above, write out under 10, percentages over 10% in numerals
- No Oxford comma
- No parentheses. Use en dashes or rewrite
- No plus sign as a conjunction
- No double hyphens
- No filler phrases

Studio-specific additions:

- No ampersand as a conjunction in running text. "And" written out. Ampersands permitted only in legal entity names and as graphic rule dividers
- Curly quotes always, never straight
- Dates: "11 April 2026" format
- Times: 24-hour in internal documents, 12-hour with lowercase am/pm in client-facing copy

#### Interim mark

The numeral 5 in PP Editorial New Semi-Bold 600. Ships immediately on all mark surfaces. Applied with the same discipline, lockup rules, clear space, colour applications and file format standards as the commissioned mark.

The interim mark is scale-invariant. A single character holds its identity at 8px where the commissioned five-circle mark would fragment. It steps aside cleanly when the commissioned mark arrives.

#### Commissioned geometric mark -- creative prompt

The geometric mark is deferred to commissioned execution. The prompt below is the governing brief.

> Design the primary geometric mark for Five Points Digital Studio. The mark lives alongside the "FIVE POINTS" wordmark set in PP Editorial New, and it carries the studio across the surfaces where the wordmark is absent or too long to work: business card fronts, envelope flaps, email signatures, social avatars, ceremonial seals and favicons.
>
> Honour the DNA of the existing Five Points mark. The current mark is a composition of five circles of descending sizes arranged as a cluster, with diameters that nod to the golden ratio. Preserve the five-circle form. Do not replace the circles with a polygon, a star, a radial, a letterform or a monogram.
>
> Apply the golden ratio as a strict mathematical discipline rather than a loose visual suggestion. Five circles in proportional descent of 1 to 1/phi to 1/phi squared to 1/phi cubed to 1/phi to the fourth, where phi is 1.618. The arrangement is a cluster rather than a chain or a pentagon. The feeling is grape-cluster organic, not engineered line.
>
> Solve the scale-down problem. The existing mark collapses below 40px because the smallest circles fall below pixel viability. The new mark must hold its identity at 8px digital. Either through a composition that naturally preserves all five circles at small scale, or through a tiered simplification rule where the mark reduces progressively while preserving the golden ratio between the remaining elements. The designer chooses the strategy.
>
> The register is editorial luxury. Think Bottega Veneta Intrecciato, the carriage mark of Hermes, the Aesop wordmark, the Aman monogram, Pentagram Design rigour, Vitsoe discipline, Aman resort editorial, Kinfolk architectural restraint and A Friend Of Mine collateral execution. Reference Rolls-Royce Bespoke and Ferrari Special Projects as the Marque Principle lens when in doubt.
>
> Forbidden register: anything that reads as crypto, corporate memphis, tech startup SaaS landing page, playful bubbles, stock geometric logos, generic flat illustration or anything that would look at home on Product Hunt.
>
> Render in a single colour only. No strokes on filled circles. No gradients. No shading. No drop shadows. No filters. Primary application is Obsidian on Parchment. Reversed application is Parchment on Obsidian. Additional approved applications are Burnt Orange on Parchment and Emerald on Parchment.
>
> Deliver a master SVG vector, PNG renders at 400px, 120px, 40px, 16px and 8px for scale verification, a horizontal lockup alongside the "FIVE POINTS" wordmark in PP Editorial New Medium 500 UPPERCASE, a vertical lockup, a favicon at 64 by 64px, and clear space and minimum size notation matching the conventions in this document.
>
> Process: present three concept directions before committing to one. Each direction carries the five-circle golden-ratio DNA but explores a different arrangement and a different scale-down strategy. The founder selects one for refinement. Refinement iterates until the mark is correct.

#### Motion and colour discipline

**Transitions.** Cuts for scene changes. Fades for emphasis. Cross-dissolves permitted only for the ending slate transition. No wipes, no spins, no 3D flips, no lens distortions.

**Type animation.** Fade-in or character-by-character reveal. 400 to 600ms for title cards, 200 to 300ms for lower thirds. No bounces, no overshoots, no elastic.

**Colour treatment.** Two approved grading registers:

| Register | Description | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm editorial | Shadows warm, highlights resolving toward Parchment, moderate saturation, medium-high contrast with retained shadow detail | Case study films, workshop footage, behind-the-build content, anything where the interior and material world is the subject |
| Open modern | Bright, controlled, intentional light. Clean whites, open shadows, warm midtones, high key without overexposure. The Ferrari Roma and Rolls-Royce Spectre register | Lifestyle content, social content, founder-facing thought leadership, anything where confidence and forward-facing energy is the subject |

Forbidden: Instagram-aesthetic filters, pastel shifts, crushed blacks, heavy teal-and-orange, flat contrast with no shadow shape, ring-light evenness, aggressive LUTs.

**Captions and subtitles.** Burned in at the bottom of the frame, centred, inside the title-safe area. PP Neue Montreal Regular 400, 32px at 1080p, Parchment on Obsidian background strip at 70% opacity. Maximum two lines visible at a time.
