# Administration – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Administration studio is the structural backbone. Legal, compliance, policies, HR, team structure, governance. Administration absorbs the former Human Resources department.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Administration role definition and specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Administration** | Studio head. Owns structural integrity and compliance posture. | All | – |
| **Legal Coordinator** | Contract templates, service agreements, NDAs, SOWs, contractor agreements. | Execution, Critique | Creator, Validator |
| **Compliance Officer** | Business registration, insurance, data protection, regulatory filings. | Critique, Release | Validator |
| **People Lead** | Team structure, contractor onboarding, culture, partner hiring criteria. | Direction, Execution | Strategist, Creator |

### Role activation

Routine policy updates engage minimal roles. Contract drafting activates Legal Coordinator + Compliance Officer. Organisational design activates People Lead + Head of Administration.
