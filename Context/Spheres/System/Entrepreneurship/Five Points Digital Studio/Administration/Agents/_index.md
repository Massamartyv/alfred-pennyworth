# Administration – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Administration department is the structural backbone. Legal, compliance, policies, brand protection, governance. Team structure, contractor management, hiring and culture route to Human Resources.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Administration role definition and specialist seats.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Administration** | Department head. Owns structural integrity and compliance posture. | All | – |
| **Legal Coordinator** | Contract templates, service agreements, NDAs, SOWs, contractor agreements. | Execution, Critique | Creator, Reviewer:Scrutiny |
| **Compliance Officer** | Business registration, insurance, data protection, regulatory filings. | Critique, Release | Reviewer:Scrutiny |

### Role activation

Routine policy updates engage minimal roles. Contract drafting activates Legal Coordinator + Compliance Officer. Team structure and hiring questions route to Human Resources.
