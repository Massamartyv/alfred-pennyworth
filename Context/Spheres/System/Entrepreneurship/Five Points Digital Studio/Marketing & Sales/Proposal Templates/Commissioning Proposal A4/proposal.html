Every practice that arrives at this point arrived the same way. Someone decided the work would be done properly, then did it properly for long enough that the market noticed. None of that is luck and none of it is an accident.

«PRINCIPAL» built «PRACTICE» on «THE FOUNDING PROPOSITION», and it worked exactly as intended. «THE EVIDENCE OF DEMAND». The work spoke, the right people listened and the calendar filled itself.

Then comes the wall, and it arrives the same way every time. Somewhere near one million in revenue the founder-led model that built the practice becomes the constraint upon it. Habits that were craft at 10 clients become risk at 100. The excellence stays intact, because the principal keeps holding it up by hand, and the cost of that holding is never billed to anyone but the principal.

Underneath the account of it sits a single sentence: this practice is oversubscribed and underleveraged at once. More demand would make the week worse. Control over the demand already arriving would change the business.

That is a systems problem, not a marketing problem, and it is the only kind of problem this studio takes. What follows is that conversation, continued on paper.
