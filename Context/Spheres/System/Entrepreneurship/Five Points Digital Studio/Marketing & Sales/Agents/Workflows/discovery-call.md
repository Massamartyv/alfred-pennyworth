# Discovery Call

The third stage of the client flywheel – Discover. The Working Call, run against the Discovery Kit, converted into the full client dossier by the discovery-architect skill. Jointly owned with Business Development. This is the stage the outer flywheel itself flags with a Manor gate – nothing advances to Propose without the Direction gate clearing here.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Researcher

- Complete `pre-call-card.md` five minutes before dialling
- Run the call against `call-spine.md` – perspective first, desire second, the willingness to walk away stated plainly and only if true; investigate, do not sell
- Complete `post-call-capture.md` within thirty minutes of hanging up, while the register is still fresh
- Run `qualification-scorecard.md` against the capture – fit, problem, budget and register
- Pull the prospect's existing appraisal from Qualify rather than re-deriving buying power from nothing

**Output:** Completed Discovery Kit capture – pre-call card, call notes, post-call capture, qualification scorecard.

**Cross-department input:** Business Development's Positioning Lead may join complex or Platinum-track calls for a market-positioning read; prior Business Development research feeds the capture where it exists.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Creator

- Hand the completed capture to the discovery-architect skill
- discovery-architect runs Reconnaissance and Direction in full – prospect economics via prospect-appraiser, competitive landscape, then the client brief, brand positioning, goal framework, engagement terms draft, ICP qualification, offer recommendation, Manor Protocol routing plan and risk register
- discovery-architect runs its own internal quality bar against the four criteria and a grammar-nazi pass on the narrative sections before it will present the gate
- Present the gate summary to the operator – ICP fit, recommended offer and tier, the staged artefacts

**Output:** Complete client dossier, staged at `Operations/Clientele/Active/{Client}/` and in the Five Points CRM.

**Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding.** This is the Direction gate the flywheel flags at this stage. discovery-architect is scoped to stop here – it does not draft proposals, send anything client-facing, modify offer pricing or commit to a timeline. Approve to proceed to Execution, revise with specific notes, or reject and return to Reconnaissance.

---

## Execution

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Creator

- On approval, file the dossier as canonical and update `_clients-registry.md`
- Create the Five Points Projects and Engagements record – departments activated, start date, status: Direction approved
- If the recommendation surfaces a bespoke offer not yet in the catalogue, dispatch to offer-creator before Propose opens
- Activate the Manor Protocol routing plan named in the dossier

**Output:** Filed dossier, live Notion engagement record, routing plan activated.

Execution never begins on a dossier that has not cleared the gate. On revise, discovery-architect re-runs Direction against the operator's specific notes. On reject, the workflow returns to Reconnaissance.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Offer Steward
**Crew:** Reviewer:Scrutiny

- Confirm no placeholder text remains across the ten filed artefacts
- Confirm the recommended offer's pricing matches the current Product Development file, not the figure quoted at the gate
- Confirm the ICP rationale is specific to this prospect rather than boilerplate

**Output:** Filing integrity check – pass, or return to Execution with specific notes.

This Critique is mechanical and does not reopen the Direction gate. The strategic call was already made and approved; this phase confirms what was filed matches what was approved.

---

## Release

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Broadcaster

- Notify the Proposal Architect that Propose is open for this client
- Notify Operations that an engagement now exists, ahead of Onboard
- Update the client's stage in the Five Points CRM

**Output:** Client moved to Propose, downstream departments notified.
