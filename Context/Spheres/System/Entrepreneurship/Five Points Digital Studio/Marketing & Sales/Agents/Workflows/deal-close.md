# Deal Close

The fifth stage of the client flywheel – Close. Final terms, the Master Service Agreement and Statement of Work, and Stripe registration. Jointly owned with Administration. No copy specialist dispatch here – the work is negotiation and paperwork, not persuasion.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Researcher

- Pull the sent proposal and any client response – questions, objections, redlines
- Confirm the decision-maker and the client's internal approval path
- Confirm the payment method and cadence the client expects, against what Five Points supports through Stripe

**Output:** Close-readiness brief – open items, decision path, payment expectations.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Mediator

- Resolve any tension between the client's ask and the proposal's terms – scope, timeline or payment structure
- Hold the line per Five Points pricing doctrine – never discount, add value instead
- Decide what, if anything, moves – scope additions, payment cadence, start date – without touching the underlying offer price

**Output:** Final terms – the negotiated position, with what was held and what was added.

**Note:** Where the tradeoff reaches into pricing itself rather than scheduling or scope framing, this phase escalates to the operator before Execution, per `Agents/agent-guidelines.md` Tier 3.

---

## Execution

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Creator

- Coordinate with Administration's Legal Coordinator to draft the Master Service Agreement and Statement of Work from the master templates in `Operations/Templates/Standardized Document Library/` – `05a-master-services-agreement.md` and `05b-statement-of-work.md` – populated with the final terms
- Register the engagement in Stripe (via the `stripe-fivepoints` connection) – product, price and payment link matched to the closed offer, or confirm the existing catalogue link if no bespoke terms apply
- Populate the client's `Legal/legal-status.md` – executed agreements, IP ownership, compliance notes

**Output:** MSA and SOW drafted, Stripe registration complete, legal status filed.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Offer Steward
**Crew:** Reviewer:Scrutiny

- Confirm the MSA and SOW match the negotiated terms exactly – no silent scope drift from what the proposal promised
- Confirm the Stripe product, price and payment link are correct and live
- Coordinate with Administration's Compliance Officer to confirm compliance notes are complete for any regulated industry the client operates in

**Output:** Close critique report – pass, or revision notes.

**Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding.** Client-facing and financially binding, per `Agents/agent-guidelines.md` Tier 3 – committing to scope or pricing, modifying legal documents, making financial commitments.

---

## Release

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Broadcaster

- Send the MSA and SOW for signature
- On signature, confirm the Stripe payment link is used and the first payment lands
- Update the client's stage in the Five Points CRM
- Hand off to Operations – client folder, signed agreements, Stripe confirmation, for Onboard

**Output:** Signed agreements, live Stripe registration, client handed to Operations.
