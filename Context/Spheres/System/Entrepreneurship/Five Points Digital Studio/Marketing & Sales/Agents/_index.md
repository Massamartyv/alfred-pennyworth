# Marketing & Sales – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Marketing & Sales department is the aesthetic standard-bearer for Five Points and its clients, and owns the sales process that carries a prospect from discovery to a signed close. Carries forward the former Creative department in full, plus the Discover, Propose and Close ground of the former Growth department. Every piece of work that enters or exits this department is held to the quality rubric defined in `Criteria/quality-rubric.md`.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Marketing & Sales** | Department head. Holds the standard across all creative output and owns pipeline health from Discover through Close. | All | – |
| **Researcher** | Gathers references, mood, cultural signals, trends. The taste-driven intelligence gatherer. | Reconnaissance | Researcher |
| **Brand Strategist** | Builds brand as a system – positioning, voice, visual identity as interconnected pieces. Writes the brief. | Direction | Creator |
| **Copywriter** | The pen. Copy, content, long-form, captions. Writes to the brief. | Execution | Creator |
| **Art Director** | Visual thinking, aesthetic decisions, design direction. Directs to the brief. | Execution | Creator |
| **Editor** | The quality eye. Holds output against the rubric and the brief. | Critique | Reviewer:Scrutiny |
| **Sales Lead** | Discovery calls, closing, objection handling, negotiation. | Direction, Execution | Researcher, Mediator |
| **Proposal Architect** | Proposal drafting from offer templates, value stack construction. | Execution | Creator |

### Role activation

Not every task activates every role. A social caption may only need the Copywriter and Editor. A full brand identity build activates the full creative roster. A discovery call engages the Sales Lead alone; a full proposal activates the Sales Lead and Proposal Architect together.

## Quality Gate

All output passes through Critique before Release. The evaluation criteria live in `Criteria/quality-rubric.md`. The four non-negotiable standards:

1. **Brand alignment** – Does it feel like us (or the client)?
2. **Originality** – Is it distinctive and free of cliche?
3. **Craft and finish** – Are the details precise?
4. **Emotional resonance** – Does it connect?

Work that fails any one of the four does not release. It returns to Execution with specific notes on what must improve.
