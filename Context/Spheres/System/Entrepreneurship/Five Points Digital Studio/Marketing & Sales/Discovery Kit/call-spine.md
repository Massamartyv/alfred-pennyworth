# Call Spine

Glanceable during the call. The arc is fixed; the wording is yours. Capture the
starred fields verbatim – they are the ones that write the proposal.

## The Arc

**1. Their story.** "How did this business come to be?" Let them run. Origin
stories surface identity, pride and the era their habits were formed in.

**2. Their present.** "Walk me through a normal week right now." Listen for the
bottleneck they mention without naming.

**3. The fork.** *"Do you want more customers – or better control of the ones
already calling?"* This one question separates a growth engagement from an
operations engagement, and answering it wrong on a proposal is fatal. A busy
trades owner almost always wants control.

**4. The wand.** ★ "If you could wave a magic wand at one part of the business,
what changes tomorrow?" Capture the answer in their words, complete sentences.

**5. Success.** ★ "Say we talk a year from now and this went well – what does
well look like?" Their definition, verbatim, with any number attached.

**6. The scar tissue.** ★ "What have you already tried? What did it cost and
what came back?" Name of provider, amount, mechanism, why it failed in their
view. This decides what the proposal must not resemble.

**7. The numbers.** ★ Every number with its unit and referent: revenue band if
offered, average job value, current monthly spend, any competing quote – amount,
provider, what it covers. A bare number in the notes is a gap wearing a disguise.

**8. The next step.** ★ Agree it out loud and write it as agreed: what gets
sent, to which email address, by when, and what happens after.

## Never Leave Without

- ★ Direct email address, read back to them
- ★ How far out they are scheduled – the horizon with its unit
- ★ Headcount today, including part-time and subcontract help
- Whether they have ever claimed their Google Business Profile
- Who hosts the website, what platform runs it and what they refuse to change
  about it – a rebuild aversion discovered after the proposal is a rewrite;
  discovered on the call it is scope
- Anything they volunteer about identity or values – capture verbatim, never
  paraphrase. It matters to them or they would not have said it.

## The Register

Perspective first, desire second, serve if at all. Sell nothing. The call
investigates; the proposal sells.
