# Marketing & Sales – Five Points Digital Studio

Brand identity and creative direction, visual and content standards, marketing, sales process, discovery, proposals and pricing. Everything the world sees, and everything that carries a prospect from discovery through to a signed close, passes through this department's lens. Marketing & Sales absorbs the former Creative department in full, and the discovery-call, proposal-build and deal-close ground of the former Growth department.

## Scope

### What belongs here
- Brand identity and visual direction (Five Points and clients)
- Content creation and creative writing
- Design direction and art direction
- Brand voice and positioning
- Creative briefs and mood boards
- Content calendars and editorial direction
- Sales process and closing – discovery calls, proposals, deal close
- Discovery methodology and frameworks
- Pricing presentation and the Two Prices structure
- Proposals and pitch decks

### What does not belong here
- Deliverable production and technical builds (Operations)
- Prospecting, outbound sequencing and pipeline management (Business Development)
- Offer architecture and catalogue pricing (Product Development)
- Market research and competitive intelligence for institutional archive (Knowledge Base)

## Key Context

- **Pricing:** No retainer model. Three public doors – the Survey, the Commission, Evolution – priced from the validated ladder $197 / $1,970 / $19,700; Evolution quoted at commissioning as 20% of commission value annually, billed quarterly. Human Construct at Platinum is the reserved tier, principal-only, priced from a $35,000 floor. See `Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md` and `private-menu-quoting-discipline.md`
- **Never discount – add value instead**
- **Proposal to close target:** 50% within 14 days

## Agent Instructions

- Load `creative-director.md` and `personal-brand-identity.md` before any brand or content work
- All creative output follows The Manor Protocol lifecycle
- Direction phase requires human approval before moving to Execution
