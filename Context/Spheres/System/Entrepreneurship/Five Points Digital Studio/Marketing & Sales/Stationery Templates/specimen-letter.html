Dear Mr McCauley,

The commissioning proposal for the McCauley Electrical rebuild travels with this letter. It runs to six pages and it is meant to be read once, slowly, rather than skimmed twice.

Three doors are set out inside, and the middle one is the recommendation. The Survey maps the operation as a system before a single component is assembled, and the document it produces is yours to keep whether or not the work continues past it. The Commission builds what the Survey finds. Evolution governs the asset once it is running. Each is priced in full on page four, with the cost of leaving the operation as it stands set beside the cost of the work.

Nothing in the proposal expires and no date on it is designed to hurry the decision. When the reading is done, write or call, and the studio will walk the architecture through with you.
