## Voice and Tone

### Language fundamentals

From [`Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`](Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md):

> **Language:** British English spelling, internationally legible phrasing. No regional idioms. No contractions. No em dashes.

### Voice attributes – fixed, never shift

From [`Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`](Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md):

> | Attribute | Manifestation |
> |---|---|
> | Architectural | Speaks in systems, structures and phases. Never tasks, to-do lists or checkboxes. Process equals phases. Outcome equals asset |
> | Composed | No manufactured urgency. No hype vocabulary. Urgency stated once, quietly, only when real |
> | Declarative | Statements not hedges. "Builds" not "helps build." "Delivers" not "strives to deliver." No softening adverbs. No conditional qualifiers |
> | Withholding | Reveals on proximity, not from across the room. Headlines imply; body copy names |
> | Specific | Every number, spec, timeline, price and guarantee is named. No ranges where a number will do |

### Tone calibration by context

From [`Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`](Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md):

> | Context | Adjustment |
> |---|---|
> | Client proposal | Exacting, measured, specific. Blueprint handed across a table. The composure sells |
> | Sales page | Editorial, not corporate. Magazine, not landing page. Rewards a reader who is willing to read, not a reader who skims |
> | Social media | Coolest register. Single observations. Single sentences. Never agency-posting-tips voice. When in doubt, post less |
> | Internal documentation | Functional and clean. Voice steps back, the work speaks directly. No wit or flourish in body |
> | Crisis communication | Slower, not faster. Composed, not reassuring. No apology theatre. The composure is the reassurance |
> | Thought leadership | Highest-stakes register. Sage and Pioneer archetypes allowed to surface. Long-form essays, newsletter editions, keynote manuscripts |

### Always-use terminology

From [`Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`](Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md):

> | Term | Replaces | Rationale |
> |---|---|---|
> | Build | Deliver, create | The studio builds. Assets are manufactured, not delivered |
> | System | Product, solution, platform | A system is architectural, interconnected, owned |
> | Bespoke | Custom, customised, tailored | "Custom" is commodity language. "Bespoke" signals one-of-one |
> | Assemble | Create, make | Evokes the manufacturing metaphor. Components assembled into a whole |
> | Operating infrastructure | Tools, tech stack, software | Tools are disposable. Infrastructure is permanent, owned, load-bearing |
> | Construct | AI, chatbot, assistant, bot, agent | The named product. A Construct is an entity with an identity, not a feature |
> | Evolution | Retainer, maintenance, support | The companion service. "Evolution" implies compounding growth |
> | Commissioning | Onboarding, kickoff, intake | A commissioning is a deliberate act of patronage, not an administrative process |
> | Owner, principal, founder | Client, customer, user | The person who commissions a build owns the result |
> | Marque-grade | High-quality, premium, best-in-class | "Marque-grade" carries the automobile thesis |
> | Calibration | Testing, QA, debugging | The system is calibrated, not debugged |
> | Engagement | Contract, deal, project | An engagement is a relationship, not a transaction |
> | Architecture | Strategy, plan, roadmap | Architecture implies a built structure |

### Never-use terminology

From [`Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`](Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md):

> | Term | Rationale |
> |---|---|
> | Solution | Vendor-speak |
> | Partner, unless contractual | Dilutes the word |
> | Leverage, as a verb | Consultant jargon. Say "use" or rewrite |
> | ROI | Finance-speak that reduces the relationship to a spreadsheet |
> | Synergy | Dead on arrival |
> | Cutting-edge, next-gen, revolutionary, game-changing | Hype vocabulary |
> | Really, truly, genuinely, actually | Softening adverbs that undermine declarative voice |
> | Unlock | Implies the value was always there and just needed a key |
> | Empower | Implies the principal was powerless |
> | Transform | Use "rebuild" |
> | Scale, as a verb in brand copy | Startup-speak |
> | Disrupt | Silicon Valley vocabulary |
> | Innovate, innovation | Overused to meaninglessness |
> | Pain point | Say "friction" or name the specific problem |
> | Stakeholder | Say who they are |
> | Touch base, circle back, loop in | Corporate meeting-speak |
> | Reach out | Say "write to" or "call" |
> | Deliverable | The studio builds assets, not deliverables |
> | Content, as a mass noun | Name the form -- essay, case study, film, photograph |

### Copy convention rules

From [`Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`](Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md):

> Inherited rules:
> - No em dashes. En dashes only
> - No contractions
> - British English spelling
> - Romance language possessive structures
> - Numerals for 10 and above, write out under 10, percentages over 10% in numerals
> - No Oxford comma
> - No parentheses. Use en dashes or rewrite
> - No plus sign as a conjunction
> - No double hyphens
> - No filler phrases
>
> Studio-specific additions:
> - No ampersand as a conjunction in running text. "And" written out. Ampersands permitted only in legal entity names and as graphic rule dividers
> - Curly quotes always, never straight
> - Dates: "11 April 2026" format
> - Times: 24-hour in internal documents, 12-hour with lowercase am/pm in client-facing copy
> - Currency: "$50,000" in full for emphasis, "$50K" permitted in tables and financial summaries
> - Titles: sentence case by default, Title Case permitted for proposal and contract section headers only

### The Manor Protocol (governing methodology)

From [`Agents/_index.md`](Agents/_index.md):

> The Manor Protocol does not separate "creative" from "operational." It holds all work to the same standard of taste and precision. A proposal is craft. An onboarding experience is craft. A financial report is craft. The methodology treats every output as an expression of the standard, regardless of which studio produced it.

> **Lifecycle:** Reconnaissance > Direction [gate] > Execution > Critique [gate] > Release

### Manor Protocol phases verbatim

From [`Agents/_index.md`](Agents/_index.md):

> **1. Reconnaissance.** Wide scan. Gather raw material, references, signals, context. Instinct-led exploration. The output is not answers – it is a landscape of possibility.
>
> **2. Direction.** The creative call. A point of view crystallises from the reconnaissance. This is the brief – the decision that everything downstream is measured against. **Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding to Execution.**
>
> **3. Execution.** Build the thing. Write the copy. Design the system. Structure the proposal. The brief is the guardrail. Craft is the standard. Speed serves the work, not the other way round.
>
> **4. Critique.** Hold the output against the brief and the standard. Is it excellent? Is it honest? Does it meet the bar? This is not review for approval – it is review for quality. The evaluator's eye, not the manager's clipboard. **Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding to Release.**
>
> **5. Release.** Ship it. Hand it off. Deliver it to the world or to the next person in the chain. Clean, complete, with nothing left undone.

### Cross-studio website build flow

From [`Production/Agents/Workflows/website-development.md`](Production/Agents/Workflows/website-development.md):

> 1. **Growth** closes the deal. Defines engagement scope and tier (Rapid, Custom or Platform). Hands off to Operations.
> 2. **Operations** onboards the client. Creates the client folder from template. Populates `Onboarding/client-brief.md`. Hands off to Strategy and Creative in parallel.
> 3. **Strategy** runs competitive research and market positioning. Output goes to Creative (for brand direction) and Production (for technical reconnaissance).
> 4. **Creative** runs the brand-identity-build or client-brand-direction workflow. Produces brand direction – visual identity, voice, content. Hands off to Production.
> 5. **Production** receives all of the above. Runs this workflow.
> 6. During Critique, **Creative** receives the preview URL and runs its quality rubric evaluation. Returns visual critique report to Production.
> 7. **Production** runs its technical quality rubric evaluation. Both critique reports go to the human gate.
> 8. After Release, **Operations** receives the production URL and executes client delivery.

### AFOM Execution Principles

From [`Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md`](Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md):

> 1. **Total colour commitment per piece.** Each artefact floods one dominant colour. Obsidian is the commissioning register. Parchment is the working register. Emerald is the continuation register. Burnt Orange is kept strictly for eyebrows and marque detail.
> 2. **A mark that scales from 8px to 400mm.** The commissioned five-circle mark must hold identity at every rendering size.
> 3. **The wordmark lives on covers, not on letterhead.** Letterhead carries only the mark and a contact cluster. The wordmark appears when the document is announcing itself, not when it is working.
> 4. **Asymmetric editorial spreads.** Full-bleed image on one page, narrow text column on the other. Never fifty-fifty. Never centred.
> 5. **Corner-anchored contact typography.** Contact clusters live in one corner. Nothing runs the full width as a header or footer.
> 6. **Physical craft as signature.** Edge painting in the opposite colour to the face on every printed piece.
> 7. **Photography is either full-colour document or total monotone.** Never half-treatment.

## Contradictions and Open Questions

### 1. Webflow vs Next.js for the studio site

[`Agents/integrations.md`](Agents/integrations.md) lists Webflow under "Tool-Only – No MCP Connection" with the note `Five Points site pending rebuild`. Every client build in the repository is Next.js / Vercel and the brand fingerprint specifies vanilla CSS with strict design tokens. No file in the repository states which stack the Five Points site itself will rebuild on. **Open:** is the studio site going to be Webflow, Next.js, or a hybrid (e.g. marketing site on Webflow, utility/intake routes on Next.js)?

### 2. Tailwind v4 vs vanilla CSS

[`Production/Agents/Workflows/website-development.md`](Production/Agents/Workflows/website-development.md) names "Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4 (or vanilla CSS per project)" as the global stack.
[`Operations/Clientele/Active/Custom Window Decorators/Deliverables/Website/prd.md`](Operations/Clientele/Active/Custom%20Window%20Decorators/Deliverables/Website/prd.md) is emphatic: "Vanilla CSS with structured design tokens in CSS custom properties. No Tailwind. No utility classes."
The brand fingerprint frontend overrides do not name Tailwind in either direction. **Open:** is Tailwind v4 sanctioned for the studio site or is vanilla CSS the rule that should govern all Five Points-facing work?

### 3. Font system: PP Editorial New vs Cormorant Garamond vs Fraunces

The brand fingerprint specifies PP Editorial New as primary display (paid Pangram Pangram faces, $200–$400/face) with Fraunces as the open-source fallback. The CWD proposal uses Fraunces directly. Both CWD luxury and FCC PRDs designate **Cormorant Garamond** (a Google Font) as their client-facing display face, paired with DM Sans (CWD) and Inter / Crimson Pro (FCC). The client-facing builds are using a different display face than the studio's own brand specifies. **Open:** does the studio site itself use PP Editorial New (paid), Fraunces (free fallback), or another face, and should client builds inherit the studio's display system or each retain a bespoke face?

### 4. Buffer vs Pennyone

The global CLAUDE.md context (loaded as system context) states: "Pennyone replaces Buffer ecosystem-wide" and is live since 2026-04-23. The content-creation workflow in [`Creative/Agents/Workflows/content-creation.md`](Creative/Agents/Workflows/content-creation.md) still references Buffer: "Schedule via Buffer (if Five Points or personal brand content)." **Open:** the workflow doc is stale relative to the Pennyone migration. Likely a documentation lag, not a strategy contradiction.

### 5. Notion as headless CMS – aspirational across sites

CWD prd.md mentions "Structured for future Sanity CMS migration." FCC prd.md mentions "Future consideration: Notion as headless CMS." The website-development workflow says "data architecture (Notion as CMS where applicable)." There is no committed answer on whether the studio site itself runs static content, Notion-backed, or Sanity-backed. **Open:** which CMS strategy governs `fivepoints.studio`?
