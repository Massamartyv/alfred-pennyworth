# Offer Catalogue Sweep – Appendix to the August 2026 Architecture Audit

File-level sweep of all 62 markdown files under Growth/Product Development/ executed 2026-08-08 by a dedicated audit pass. Companion to offer-architecture-audit-2026-08.md. Headline results below; the parent dossier carries the findings.

## Headline counts

- 46 distinctly priced offers on disk against the canonical claim of 39 plus three bundles. The root _index.md is internally inconsistent: prose says 39, its own registry table sums to 41, disk holds 40 core-pillar offers plus 3 bundles, 2 sessions and the Evolution add-on.
- 59 of 62 files carry Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum public-tier language. Only the two Sessions offer files and the Camp welcome email are clear of it.
- Ten files price offers as monthly retainers, including the root _index.md doctrine line "Gold – Full-service, the retainer sweet spot ($5K-$8K/mo)".
- Zero occurrences of the Survey, the Commission or Evolution as door names anywhere in the tree. Zero occurrences of the 20% annual Evolution formula.
- Human Construct is documented as a four-tier public ladder with open-enrolment Bronze at $497 – against the canonical principal-only Platinum reserve.
- Evolution priced $3,000/month flat (with quarterly and annual discounts) in three files.
- Three credit windows coexist: 30 days (Automation Strategy Sprint), 60 days (both Sessions files – the standard), 90 days (five files).
- Stripe references exist only in the Camp Pennyworth offer file. Every other file carries a naming-convention placeholder, not a created product.
- Curriculum-vs-offer price mismatch inside Emerging Technology: Bronze fluency rung $250 in the curriculum file, $197 in the live offer file.
- The Command Session offer file (filed 8 Jul 2026) is the documented home of the enterprise session rungs: SMB $5,500 on-site / $3,500 remote; Enterprise Rung 1 $10,700, Rung 2 $16,700.

## Pillar judgments

Market Presence, Development, Creative and Strategic Advisory read as internal delivery menus – retainer math, scoped ranges, operator strategy notes embedded beside sales copy. Emerging Technology is mixed. Sessions is the only pillar operating inside the live three-door model – Stripe products, Decision Log ratifications, shipped copy, the 60-day credit standard. Bundles read public in tone but are defined entirely by old tier-coded components and cannot be reconciled to doors until components are renamed.
