### ICP for Outbound (from the Offer Suite)

**Primary target:** Professional services firms and healthcare organizations
- Annual revenue: $1M -- $10M

- Decision maker: Founder, CEO, or Marketing Director
- Digital maturity: low to moderate
- AI adoption: early stage

### Outbound Sequence (Five Emails)

**Email 1 -- The Pattern Interrupt:**
"I looked at [Company]'s digital presence and found three things that are likely costing you qualified leads. Would it be useful if I sent over what I found?"

**Email 2 -- The Value Drop (Day 3):**
One specific, genuine observation about their website, social presence, or competitor positioning. Not generic. Claude researches, you approve the insight before it sends.

**Email 3 -- The Workshop Invite (Day 7):**
"We are running a free AI workshop for [industry] businesses next month. 20 seats. Given what I saw on your site, this might be relevant: [link]." This bridges outbound into the workshop funnel.

**Email 4 -- The Case Study (Day 12):**
"We built an AI-powered content and automation system for a [similar company] that generated [result] in 90 days. Here is a two-minute breakdown: [link or summary]."

**Email 5 -- The Close (Day 18):**
"I have capacity for one new [Fractional CMO / Content Engine] client this quarter. If this is worth a 20-minute conversation, here is my calendar: [link]."

## Phase 3: Close and Onboard (April 14 -- May 31)

*"Stop selling at poverty prices. Price is a function of the transformation, not the transaction."* -- Myron Golden

### The Sales Conversation (Chris Do Blind Method, Adapted)

When calls come from either the pipeline or the workshop funnel:

1. **Diagnose (10 minutes).** Ask about their business, goals, frustrations, what they have tried. Listen. Do not pitch.
2. **Quantify the gap.** "So you are generating about 10 leads per month and need 50 to hit your growth target. That is a $200K annual pipeline gap."
3. **Present the offer stack.** Walk through the specific offer from the suite. Let them see the $18K+ in perceived value before naming the price.
4. **Name the price. Stop talking.** "The investment is $5,000 per month for the Fractional CMO engagement." Then silence. First person to speak after the price loses. -- Myron Golden
5. **Handle objections with questions.** "What would need to be true for this to feel like the right decision?"

## Phase 4: The Delivery Flip (June -- July)

*"You are the most expensive employee in your company."* -- Codie Sanchez

By June, with the pipeline producing and two to three new clients onboarded, MRR should be $12K -- $17K. The delivery load will break the model if you remain 80% hands-on.

### The Flip: 80/20 to 40/60

**From:** 80% delivery, 20% systems/BD
**To:** 40% delivery, 60% systems/BD/content

**How:**

1. **Hire a delivery contractor** ($1,500 -- $2,500/mo retainer). They handle: social content creation, ad management execution, reporting, email campaign deployment. You handle: strategy, QA, client calls, creative direction.

2. **Templatize by offer type.** Each Gold retainer has a repeatable monthly cycle:
   - Content Engine: content calendar → creation → approval → scheduling → reporting
   - Fractional CMO: strategy call → action items → execution oversight → performance review
   - Paid Media: campaign setup → optimization → creative refresh → reporting

   The contractor follows the cycle. You review the output. Claude handles the first draft of reports, content briefs, and strategy memos.

3. **Use Claude as a junior strategist.** At $200/mo for Max, Claude should be replacing $3K -- $5K/mo in contractor hours: content drafts, ad copy variations, client communication drafts, competitive analysis, reporting narratives.

**Play 1: Productized Bronze at Scale**
The AI Fluency Workshop runs monthly. The Automation Audit ($497) and Content Catalyst Kit ($297) are available 24/7 as digital products. Combined Bronze revenue target: $3K -- $5K/mo. This is the moat -- passive revenue that funds operations while you focus on Gold and Platinum.

**Play 2: Raise Prices Quarterly**
Every new client pays more than the last. The offer suite already supports this -- the value stacks are designed for $7.5K -- $10K Gold retainers. When the third case study lands, the new client rate goes up by $1,000 -- $2,000. Existing clients stay at their rate. This is not dishonest. It is the market rewarding your proof.

**Play 3: Upsell Existing Clients**
A Fractional CMO client ($5K/mo) who sees results in Q2 is a natural candidate for the Content Engine add-on ($4K/mo) or an Automation Build Out project ($5K -- $10K). The conversation: "Based on what we built in Q2, here is the next layer." Cross-pillar upsells are the highest-margin revenue in the entire suite.

**Play 4: Land One Platinum or Bundle**
The AI Powered Brand Ecosystem Bundle ($25K -- $35K) or an AI Operations Overhaul ($20K -- $35K) -- one of these per quarter transforms the revenue picture. At $30K as a project, that amortizes to $10K/mo over three months. One Platinum engagement per quarter plus the retainer base gets you past $25K comfortably.
