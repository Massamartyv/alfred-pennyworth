## The market bands – memorise these

| Band | Published range | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| DIY and template rebuilds | $500–$3,500 | The credibility floor sits above this band |
| Freelancer custom builds | $3,000–$8,000 | Where studio claims stop being believable |
| Agency custom, 10–20 pages | $5,000–$15,000 | The market's reference band; normal ~$9,000–$10,000 |
| Boutique studio commissions | $6,000–$35,000+ | The marque register; where the studio's path runs |

Five Points Custom Website Build: **$11,500** – upper half of the reference band, below the structural ceiling, priced as a commissioned asset.

## The three arguments – weave, do not recite

1. **Market position.** The commission sits where the market already prices serious custom work. Quote the reference band, then the number. The price reads as considered, not maximal.
2. **Capacity to pay.** Marketing budgets run seven to 9% of revenue (SBA, Gartner, Deloitte CMO surveys). For a $1M+ business, a commissioned build amortised over its three-to-four year life is one to 4% of a single year's marketing budget. The constraint is never budget; it is belief – which the demonstration gate and the guarantee answer.
3. **The build-cost floor.** Market labour rates (BLS web developer median ~$44/hour) put a four-to-eight week engagement at $5,000–$9,000 of labour value before margin. Prices below the reference band are not generous; they are structurally impossible to deliver at standard – a quiet answer to "why not cheaper."

## The two prices – always both

Every pricing conversation names the cost of yes and the cost of no. The cost of no for website engineering: the buyer's demand keeps routing to whoever the search shows instead, the template keeps signalling template-grade work, and every month of delay is rent paid to invisibility. Anchor the inaction ledger before the fee, per the house Two Prices rule.

## Founding-client doctrine

Strong Tower Christian Ministry and Hibachi With A Twist hold the commission at $7,500, locked, as founding clients (operator ruling 2026-07-24). The lock is named in their documents as appreciation – it is a relationship asset, never a discount precedent. No other client inherits it; new work prices at list.

Presentation rule (operator ruling 2026-07-24): founding-client documents never show the list price. Their price stands alone as their price – no anchor, no comparison, no "normally priced at" framing, in any document or presentation addressed to them.

## Provenance discipline

When any figure above enters a client-facing document, it carries its source and vintage on request. Tier the evidence per the house standard: official statistics first (BLS, SBA, Census), institutional surveys second (Gartner, Deloitte CMO), commercial price guides third – cited as market convergence, never as single-source truth.
