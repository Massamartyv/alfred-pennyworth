---

## The reconciliation — why 100 touches does not break the strategy

The directive caps Human Construct at three slots and forbids volume-driven prospecting. A hundred cold touches reads against that on its face. It reconciles in two moves:

1. **The hundred feed a free diagnostic, not the build.** Volume sits at the front door. The three slots stay protected behind it. You are filling a funnel, not selling a flagship cold.
2. **The hundred are tiered by signal, not sprayed uniformly.** The signal-hot names get a human call and a personal message. The rest get the personalised DM sequence. Every touch still carries the specific tell. Nothing generic leaves the building.

This is how the volume goal and the signal gate hold at the same time.

## Honest calibration on "meetings by Friday"

The directive ranks the verticals by deal speed. Weight the week accordingly:

- **Real Estate (cycle: weeks)** and **Medical Spa (owner decision, 4 to 8 weeks)** — the realistic source of *this week's* booked diagnostics. Lead here.
- **Golf and Country Clubs, Homebuilders** — slower, governance or capital drag. Seed them; do not expect a Friday meeting.
- **Private Aviation** — the long game by design. Plant the relationship, measure it in months, not days.

A booked diagnostic is the win, not a closed build. Five to ten diagnostics booked from this week would be a strong result and a full pipeline.

## The daily rhythm

- **Monday:** Prep Tier 1. Confirm each signal against its correction line. Warm Tier 1 DM targets — follow, one genuine engagement. Begin calls on the first eight.
- **Tuesday:** Finish Tier 1 calls and same-day DMs. Launch Tier 2 Touch 1 in batches of 15 to 20.
- **Wednesday:** Diagnostics from Monday's calls begin landing. Tier 2 Touch 1 continues. Reply to every responder fast.
- **Thursday:** Tier 2 Touch 2 to Monday's non-repliers. Hold Thursday and Friday open for booked diagnostics.
- **Friday:** Tier 2 Touch 3 invitations. Book into next week. Tally diagnostics booked.

A reply at any point jumps that name straight to the conversation and the soft invitation. The sequence is the floor, not a script to finish regardless.

## Guardrails

1. **Touchpoint G holds.** No call dialled and no DM sent against a real person without operator approval. Alfred drafts, queues and personalises; the operator confirms the send.
2. **The values filter is live.** Every name has passed it once. If anything surfaces mid-week — extractive model, oppression, environmental abuse at scale — it drops, no exceptions.
3. **Signal or silence.** No touch goes out without its specific signal merged. A generic send is worse than no send.
4. **Four touches, then 90 days.** No name is worked past Touch 4 in this window. Persistence past that reads as desperation and burns the brand.
5. **The diagnostic is the only ask.** No price, no build, no pitch in cold contact.
