# Deliverable #1 — Affluent-Neighborhood Seed List

**Granularity:** Top 5 neighborhoods per major metro
**ICP band:** $1M to $50M annual revenue
**Status:** Pending Touchpoint B review

---

## Executive Summary

This seed list covers 11 territories — 8 US states plus the United Kingdom, Australia, and Canada — spanning 32 metros and 160 named neighborhoods (5 per metro). Three cross-region patterns are worth noting at review. First, luxury real estate agents and medical spa owners register high-density vertical signals in nearly every enumerated metro, making them the most consistent entry verticals across all geographies. Second, private aviation density closely tracks urban HNW cluster size, with Sun Belt and coastal metros (South Florida, LA, NYC, London) forming the strongest clusters and inland or secondary metros trailing materially — a useful proxy for prospect travel behavior. Third, the golf and country club vertical shows disproportionate density in resort and Sun Belt markets (Naples, Palm Springs, Scottsdale, Coachella Valley) relative to their population size, signaling niche outreach opportunity in those metros beyond what gross market size would suggest.

#### Perth

1. **Peppermint Grove** — Australia's most expensive postcode by median land value; median home value $5M+; smallest LGA in WA by area with the highest average wealth; historic mining and pastoral dynasty estates.
2. **Cottesloe** — Beachfront enclave on the Indian Ocean; median home value $3M–$4M+; UHNW lifestyle concentration.
3. **Dalkeith** — River-facing suburb adjacent to Peppermint Grove; median home value $3.5M+; large-lot trophy homes; significant mining and resources-industry executive population.
4. **Claremont** — Prestige shopping and lifestyle corridor; median home value $2.5M+; private school adjacency (Christ Church Grammar, Scotch College); established wealth concentration.
5. **City Beach** — Ocean-facing suburb; median home value $2.8M+; large modern estates popular with resources-sector UHNW households; strong luxury homebuilder activity.

**Vertical signal:** Luxury Real Estate Agents (high), Medical Spa Owners (medium), Golf Courses & Country Clubs (medium), Homebuilders (high), Jet Rentals / Private Aviation (high).

**Values-filter note:** Perth's resources sector has historical associations with environmental impact in mining operations. This does not affect the service operators Five Points would engage in the Peppermint Grove / Dalkeith / Cottesloe corridor; noted for context only.

### Canada

#### Toronto (Greater Toronto Area)

1. **Bridle Path** — Median home values $5M–$20M+; Toronto's most exclusive enclave; dense with private estates on multi-acre lots; luxury brokerages including Sotheby's and Christie's International Real Estate.
2. **Rosedale** — Median home values $3M–$8M; historic upper-class district; ravine-bordered detached homes; old-money family wealth with high HNW household density.
3. **Forest Hill** — Median home values $3M–$7M; private school corridor anchored by Upper Canada College and Bishop Strachan; dense with wealth management offices and premium service providers.
4. **Lawrence Park** — Median home values $2.5M–$5M; quiet established residential enclave adjacent to Forest Hill; high concentration of professionals and HNW households.
5. **Yorkville / Annex (Summerhill corridor)** — Median condo and townhouse values $1.5M–$4M+; Toronto's luxury retail and hospitality core; dense concentration of medical spas, high-end clinics, and premium service operators.

**Vertical signal:** Luxury Real Estate Agents (high), Medical Spa Owners (high), Golf Courses & Country Clubs (medium), Homebuilders (medium), Jet Rentals / Private Aviation (medium).

#### Vancouver, British Columbia

1. **Shaughnessy** — Old-money enclave; median home values $4M–$8M+; dense concentration of heritage mansions on large lots.
2. **West Vancouver (British Properties / Chartwell / Dundarave)** — Highest median household income in Canada; hillside estates with ocean and city views; median detached home values $3M–$10M+.
3. **Kerrisdale** — Affluent residential enclave in Vancouver west side; median home values $3M–$5M; boutique high-end retail corridor.
4. **Point Grey / Kitsilano Upper** — Oceanfront and near-oceanfront estates at $4M–$15M+; home to senior academics, tech executives, and established professionals.
5. **Coal Harbour / Yaletown (West End waterfront)** — Luxury high-rise condos at $2M–$10M+; concentration of corporate executives, international HNW buyers, and wealth management clients.

**Vertical signal:** Luxury Real Estate Agents (high), Medical Spa Owners (high), Golf Courses & Country Clubs (medium), Homebuilders (medium), Jet Rentals / Private Aviation (medium).

**Values-filter note:** Vancouver's luxury real estate market has historically attracted significant capital from mainland China, some of which drew regulatory scrutiny (BC's foreign buyer tax, beneficial ownership registry). Operators in the luxury brokerage vertical may carry compliance complexity worth acknowledging at the outreach stage.
