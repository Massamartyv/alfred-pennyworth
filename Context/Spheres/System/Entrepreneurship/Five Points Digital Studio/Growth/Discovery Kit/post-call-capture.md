# Post-Call Capture

Fill within 30 minutes of hanging up, while the voice is still in your ear.
Complete sentences for the starred fields – a fragment tonight is a research
mission tomorrow. Then: "Alfred, run discovery on {name}" with this capture.

## The Form

```
LEAD
Company:
Person and role:
Direct email (read back on call):
Phone:

Website:
Lead source: cold call | referral | inbound | outbound reply

STORY

Headcount today (incl. part-time and subs):
Trade / niche and what they refuse to do:

PRESENT STATE
The week right now (their words):
Scheduled out: {number + unit}
The fork answer: MORE customers | CONTROL of existing | both
Bottleneck they named:
Bottleneck they did not name (your read):

★ THE WAND (verbatim, complete sentence):

★ NUMBERS (unit + referent on every one)

Current monthly spend: $      on

Revenue band (if offered):

IDENTITY / VALUES (verbatim, never paraphrase):

★ AGREED NEXT STEP

```

## The Standard

If a starred field is blank, the capture is not done – either recall it now or
mark it OPEN so Reconnaissance chases it deliberately instead of discovering the
hole mid-proposal. The McCauley engagement lost four fields this way; this form
is the reason it does not happen twice.
