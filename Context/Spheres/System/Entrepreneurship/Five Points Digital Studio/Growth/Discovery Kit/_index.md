# Discovery Kit

The standard instrument for cold-outreach discovery. Born from the McCauley
Electrical engagement of July 2026 – the first cold-call lead – where the call
was won on an investigative register and the notes still left six load-bearing
gaps that had to be recovered afterwards. This kit exists so the operator can be
fully present in the conversation and let the instrument hold the checklist.

## The Three Pieces

| Piece | When | Time |
|---|---|---|
| `pre-call-card.md` | Before dialling | Five minutes |
| `call-spine.md` | On the call – glanceable | Zero extra |
| `post-call-capture.md` | Within 30 minutes of hanging up | Ten minutes |

Then hand the capture to Alfred with "run discovery on {name}" and the
discovery-architect skill takes it from signal to Direction gate.

## The Register

The register that wins these calls, codified from the McCauley call:

1. **Perspective first.** Ask how they see their own business before offering any
   view of it.
2. **Desire second.** What do they actually want – not what the category is
   supposed to want. A trades owner drowning in calls does not want more calls.
3. **Serve if at all, third.** The stated willingness to conclude that no
   engagement is the right answer. Said plainly, early. It is the single
   strongest trust move available on a cold call and it is only available if it
   is true.

Sell nothing on the call. The proposal sells; the call investigates.

## Qualification

`qualification-scorecard.md` scores the lead after capture – fit, problem, budget
and register – and routes to an offer family. The scorecard feeds the dossier's
ICP section; it never overrides the Direction gate.
