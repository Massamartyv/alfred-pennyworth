# Pre-Call Card

Five minutes before dialling. The goal is one honest insight to open with and
zero illusions about who is answering.

## The Sweep

1. **Their website, 90 seconds.** What do they sell, in whose voice? Is there
   online booking or only phone and form? One thing done well, one thing
   obviously broken.
2. **Google their name plus city.** Who owns the results – them, directories or
   competitors? Do they appear in any round-up for their own trade and town?
3. **Reviews, the fast read.** Google count and stars if visible, Yelp count.
   Sentiment or volume – which is the weak side? A business with glowing sparse
   reviews has a capture problem, not a quality problem. That distinction shapes
   the whole conversation.
4. **One competitor, 60 seconds.** The biggest local operator in their trade.
   Note one thing that operator's website does that theirs does not – usually
   booking.
5. **The person.** LinkedIn or the About page. Owner-operator or a team? Years
   in. Anything they are visibly proud of – that is where the conversation lives.

## Write Down Three Lines Before Dialling

- **The opener insight:** one specific, true, non-flattering observation about
  their public footprint. Specific beats clever.
- **The likely friction:** your hypothesis for what actually hurts – to be tested
  on the call, never asserted.
- **The disqualifier:** what answer would make this a no-engagement call. Decide
  it before dialling so the serve-if-at-all posture is real.
