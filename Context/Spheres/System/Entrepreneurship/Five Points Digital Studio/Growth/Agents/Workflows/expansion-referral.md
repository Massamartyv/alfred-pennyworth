# Expansion and Referral

The ninth and final stage of the client flywheel – Ascend and Refer. The upsell to the next rung of the Priestley Ascending Transaction Model, the case study, and the referral ask. This is where the loop closes – a referral here feeds Attract at stage one, which is why the flywheel spirals rather than ending.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Researcher

- Review the client's engagement outcomes against the goal framework set at Discover
- Identify expansion signal – unused capacity in the current tier, ambitions beyond current scope, a natural next rung
- Identify referral-worthy relationships – who in the client's network fits the ICP, anyone the client has already mentioned
- Confirm the client's willingness to serve as a case study subject before building anything client-facing around their results

**Output:** Expansion and referral brief – signal, candidate next rung, referral candidates, case study consent.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Mediator

- Decide the right next rung to propose – weighing the client's appetite and budget against the value they have actually realised, not the rung that maximises revenue in isolation
- Decide the referral ask's framing and timing – tied to a genuine win, never generic
- Decide the case study's angle – which result, which arc, told in the client's own language

**Output:** Ascend and refer brief – recommended rung, referral approach, case study angle.

---

## Execution

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Creator

- Dispatch to the **sales-copy** skill for the case study – the client's problem, the engagement, the result, written for prospect-facing use
- Dispatch to the **email-copy** skill for the upsell proposal note and the referral ask
- Dispatch to the **social-copy** skill for the case study's social distribution assets

**Output:** Case study, upsell note, referral ask and social assets, drafted.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Offer Steward
**Crew:** Reviewer:Scrutiny

- Confirm every claim in the case study is client-approved and accurate – no inflated or unverified results
- Confirm the upsell pricing matches the current Product Development file for the recommended rung
- Evaluate against the four criteria: brand alignment, originality, craft and finish, emotional resonance (`Creative/Agents/Criteria/quality-rubric.md`)

**Output:** Ascend and refer critique report – pass, or revision notes.

**Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding.** Client-facing on two fronts – the case study publishes externally and the upsell reaches an existing client relationship – per `Agents/agent-guidelines.md` Tier 3 and Red Line 3.

---

## Release

**Role:** Sales Lead
**Crew:** Broadcaster

- Publish the case study – it feeds the Knowledge Base for the institutional archive and doubles as reference material at Attract
- Send the referral ask and the upsell proposal
- Log any resulting referral as a new prospect at Attract; log an accepted upsell as a new pass through Propose and Close at the higher rung

**Output:** Case study published, referral and upsell sent, the loop closed back to Attract.
