# Outbound Prospecting

The first stage of the client flywheel – Attract. ICP outbound sourced and enriched via Clay, inbound and referral signal captured alongside it, and the Gift front door (Bronze AI Fluency Workshop) held as the soft landing for anyone not ready for a call. The heaviest copy load of any Growth workflow – three specialist dispatches run at Execution.

---

## Reconnaissance

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Researcher

- Confirm ICP fit criteria from `Growth/_index.md` before sourcing anything – sector, revenue band, team size
- Source and enrich the target list via Clay – firmographic, technographic and decision-maker signal
- Scan referral and inbound channels for warm signal running alongside the cold list
- Identify trigger events per prospect or segment – expansion, leadership change, a visible pain, a category moment
- Gather reference material – the prospect's public presence, competitor positioning, the language the category uses about its own problem

**Output:** Prospecting brief – segment, enriched list, trigger events, reference material.

---

## Direction

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Creator

- Select the channel mix for this push – outbound email, organic or paid social, paid ads, or a blend
- Define a distinct angle per channel – the hook that earns attention without selling on first contact. Vary the core reframe across channels, so a prospect who meets the email, the post and the ad reads one campaign, not one idea in three outfits
- Set the sequence architecture – touch count, cadence, and where the Gift front door lands as the low-friction next step
- Confirm any offer surfaced in the sequence against Product Development – never quote pricing from memory

**Output:** Outbound brief – channel mix, angle, sequence architecture, offer alignment.

---

## Execution

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Creator

- Dispatch to the **email-copy** skill for the outbound sequence – cold open, follow-ups, the Gift front door placement
- Dispatch to the **social-copy** skill for organic and paid social prospecting assets – profile-visible hooks, DM openers, creative supporting the same trigger events
- Dispatch to the **ad-copy** skill for paid acquisition creative – headline, primary text and creative brief for any channel carrying spend
- Each specialist loads the brand fingerprint itself, carries its own craft for its medium and runs its own grammar-nazi pass before returning

**Output:** Assembled outbound asset set – email sequence, social assets, ad creative – ready for review.

---

## Critique

**Role:** Offer Steward
**Crew:** Reviewer:Scrutiny

- Confirm the three channels tell one coherent story from varied angles – neither three unrelated pitches, nor one reframe worn three times
- Confirm every offer or price named in the sequence matches the live Product Development file
- Confirm the list and messaging stay inside ICP – no scope creep toward off-ICP prospects for volume's sake
- Evaluate against the four criteria: brand alignment, originality, craft and finish, emotional resonance (`Creative/Agents/Criteria/quality-rubric.md`)

**Output:** Outbound critique report – pass, or revision notes.

**Hard gate: human approval required before proceeding.** Client-facing – nothing in this sequence reaches a prospect's inbox, feed or ad placement without review, per `Agents/agent-guidelines.md` Red Line 3.

---

## Release

**Role:** Business Development Lead
**Crew:** Broadcaster

- Schedule and launch the approved sequence across its channels
- Log the push to the Five Points CRM – segment, sequence, launch date
- Route any reply or engagement to Qualify for buying-power appraisal

**Output:** Live outbound sequence, pipeline entries logged, replies routed to Qualify.
