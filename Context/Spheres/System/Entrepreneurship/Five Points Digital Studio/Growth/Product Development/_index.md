## Tier Structure

- **Bronze** – Entry-level, self-serve or light-touch
- **Silver** – Mid-tier, strategic with guided execution
- **Gold** – Full-service, the retainer sweet spot ($5K-$8K/mo)
- **Platinum** – Enterprise or comprehensive transformation
