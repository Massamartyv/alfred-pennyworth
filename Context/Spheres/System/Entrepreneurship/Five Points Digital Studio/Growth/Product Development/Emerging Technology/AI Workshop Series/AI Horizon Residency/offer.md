## Pricing

**Price:** $15,000 (one time, up to 12 participants from one organization)

## Format

Two day on location intensive at the client's office or a curated venue

## What Is Included

- Pre retreat: comprehensive digital audit, stakeholder interviews, and workflow analysis (delivered two weeks before)
- Day 1: AI Fluency immersion for the full team. Framework training, live demos, collaborative workflow mapping across departments
- Day 2: Implementation day. Five Points team builds the top five to seven priority automations live with the client team. Hands on training so the team can maintain and extend what is built.
- Post retreat: 90 day support retainer including two virtual check in sessions, async troubleshooting, and one additional automation build
- Deliverable: Complete AI Integration Blueprint — a strategic document covering every department's AI roadmap for the next 12 months
- Professional documentation of the retreat (photos and summary) for the client's internal communications

## Bonus

Quarterly AI strategy call for one year (four calls total, valued at $3,000) to review progress, troubleshoot, and identify new opportunities as AI tools evolve.

## Total Value

The $15,000 engagement includes:
- $15,000 engagement fee
- $3,000 in strategy calls
- AI Integration Blueprint (valued at $5,000)
- Professional documentation (valued at $1,500)

Total stated value: $24,500 for $15,000

## Guarantee

If the implementations do not save a combined 20 hours per week of team time across the departments involved within 90 days, Five Points conducts the follow up session at no charge.

## Upsell Path

Ongoing consulting retainer, full brand ecosystem build (Strategic Advisory, Market Presence and Creative services).

## Note on Travel

Client covers travel and accommodation for on location engagements outside the Atlanta metro area. A travel fee schedule should be published.
