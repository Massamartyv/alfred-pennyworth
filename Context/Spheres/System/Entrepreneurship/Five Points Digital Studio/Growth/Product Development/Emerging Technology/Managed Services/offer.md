# 1.4 — Managed Services

**Pillar:** 01 Emerging Technology
**Offer Number:** 1.4
**Stripe Name Pattern:** 1.4 — [Tier Name] — [Tier]

**Description:** Post-build hosting, maintenance, performance monitoring, system health and ongoing optimization. The natural upsell destination for every Development engagement. Formalizing these services as a standalone offer converts what is currently given away as a bonus into a recurring revenue stream.

---

## Rationale

Every website and application Five Points builds requires ongoing care. Custom Website Build (5.2-G) currently includes "first three months of hosting and maintenance" as a bonus. Digital Platform Build (5.2-P) includes 90 day post launch support. Once those windows close, the client either manages their own infrastructure or comes back for help on an ad hoc basis. Neither outcome is good. The first leads to degraded performance and brand erosion. The second creates unpredictable support requests that disrupt other engagements.

Managed Services solves both problems. It gives the client continuity and gives Five Points predictable recurring revenue. Like the Automation Oversight Retainer (1.3) is to automation builds, Managed Services is to everything that ships from Pillar 5.

---

## Tier Structure

### Silver — Site Care Plan
**Price:** $500 per month (minimum six month commitment)
**Stripe Name:** 1.4 — Site Care Plan — Silver

**Ideal for:** Standard websites built at the Silver or Gold tier (Rapid Site Build, Custom Website Build)

**What Is Included:**

- Hosting management (server monitoring, uptime checks, SSL renewal)
- Monthly CMS and plugin updates with compatibility testing
- Monthly performance report (load times, uptime percentage, security status)
- Up to two minor content updates per month (text changes, image swaps, link updates)
- 48 hour response time for non-critical support requests
- Monthly automated backups with 30 day retention
- Basic security monitoring and malware scanning

**Not Included:** Feature development, design changes, SEO optimization, or anything requiring more than 30 minutes of development time per request.

---

### Gold — Platform Care Plan
**Price:** $1,500 per month (minimum six month commitment)
**Stripe Name:** 1.4 — Platform Care Plan — Gold

**Ideal for:** Custom websites and web applications built at the Gold or Platinum tier (Custom Website Build, Digital Platform Build, MVP Application Build)

**What Is Included:**

- Everything in Silver plus:
- Priority support (24 hour response time on business days)
- Up to five content or minor feature updates per month (up to two hours of development time each)
- Performance optimization (quarterly speed audit with implemented improvements)
- Advanced security monitoring with proactive patching
- Database maintenance and optimization (monthly)
- Weekly automated backups with 90 day retention
- Staging environment maintenance for testing updates before deployment
- Monthly 30 minute check in call to review system health and upcoming needs

---

### Platinum — Full Operations Plan
**Price:** $3,000 per month (minimum 12 month commitment)
**Stripe Name:** 1.4 — Full Operations Plan — Platinum

**Ideal for:** Complex digital platforms, applications with active users, and clients with mission critical digital infrastructure (Digital Platform Build, Full Application Build)

**What Is Included:**

- Everything in Gold plus:
- Dedicated support channel with four hour response time for critical issues
- Up to 10 hours of development time per month for feature enhancements, integrations, or design updates
- Real time monitoring and alerting (uptime, error rates, performance thresholds)
- Quarterly infrastructure review and scaling recommendations
- Daily automated backups with 180 day retention
- Load testing and capacity planning (quarterly)
- Third party integration monitoring and maintenance
- Monthly 60 minute strategy call to align platform roadmap with business objectives
- Annual architecture review with written recommendations

## Transition Protocol

When a Development engagement reaches its post launch support window (30 or 90 days depending on tier), the project manager initiates the Managed Services handoff:

1. Client receives a System Health Report summarizing the current state of the build
2. Five Points presents the appropriate Managed Services tier based on the complexity of what was built
3. If the client opts in, the transition is seamless — no gap between post launch support ending and managed services beginning
4. If the client declines, Five Points delivers a Self Maintenance Handoff Document with instructions for the client's team to manage the system independently

**Pricing note on Development bonuses:** The "first three months of hosting and maintenance included" bonus in the Custom Website Build (5.2-G) effectively gives the client three months of the Silver tier at no charge. This remains a valid bonus structure. When the three months end, the client is already experiencing the value and the conversation shifts from selling a new service to continuing one they already rely on. This is the intended conversion mechanism.

## Upsell Paths

- Silver clients who outgrow minor updates are natural Gold upgrades
- Gold clients whose platforms become mission critical are natural Platinum upgrades
- Any Managed Services client is a warm lead for Automation Oversight Retainer (1.3) if they have manual processes adjacent to their digital platform
- Quarterly strategy calls at the Platinum tier surface new Development and Market Presence opportunities

---

## Margin Analysis

All three tiers target a 75% gross margin floor at a $100 per hour labor rate:

- **Silver ($500/mo):** Estimated one to two hours of labor monthly. Hosting costs absorbed. Target margin: 75%+
- **Gold ($1,500/mo):** Estimated four to six hours of labor monthly. Target margin: 75%+
- **Platinum ($3,000/mo):** Estimated 12 to 15 hours of labor monthly (10 hours development + two to three hours monitoring and calls). Target margin: 70 to 75%

The Platinum tier operates closer to the margin floor because it includes meaningful development time. This is acceptable because Platinum clients represent the highest lifetime value and the development hours frequently surface upsell opportunities that exceed the margin concession.

---

## Guarantee

If system uptime falls below 99.5% in any calendar month due to a failure on the part of Five Points (excluding third party outages, client-initiated changes, or force majeure), the client receives a pro-rated credit for that month.

## Revenue Projection

Assuming a modest conversion rate from Development engagements:

- If 50% of Gold and Platinum Development clients convert to Managed Services at an average of $1,000 per month, and Five Points completes 20 Development engagements in year one, that is 10 Managed Services clients generating $120,000 in annual recurring revenue
- This compounds: unlike project revenue, Managed Services revenue carries forward. Year two doubles if the same conversion rate holds and no clients churn
- At scale, Managed Services becomes the stabilizing revenue layer underneath the project-based work — the recurring foundation that makes the business less dependent on new client acquisition every month
