# 4.1-S — Brand Identity Sprint

**Pillar:** 04 Creative
**Parent Offer:** Brand Identity
**Tier:** Silver (Done With You)
**Stripe Name:** 4.1 — Brand Identity Sprint — Silver

---

## Pricing

**Price:** $5,000 (one time)

## Format

Three week engagement, virtual

## What Is Included

- Week 1: Brand strategy session (90 minutes). Positioning, audience, voice, values, competitive landscape
- Week 2: Five Points develops two to three logo concepts and visual direction options. Client provides feedback.
- Week 3: Refinement and delivery of selected direction
- Deliverables: Primary logo, one alternate mark, color palette, typography selection, basic brand guidelines (10 to 15 pages)
- Two rounds of revisions included

## Bonus

Social media profile kit (optimized graphics for all major platforms) (valued at 500 to 750 dollars).

## Guarantee

If three people in your target audience, selected by you, do not respond positively to the new identity in a blind test, we go back to the drawing board at no charge.

## Upsell Path

Gold for comprehensive identity system.
