## Strategic Context

Human Construct is the core offer of Five Points Digital Studio. Not the most expensive offer and not the entry point, but the heart of the business. Every other service in the catalog either leads toward it or flows from it.

The downstream logic: if a prospect is not ready for a personal AI system, they encounter Five Points through more familiar, commoditized services — content, social media, web development, brand identity. These are recognizable entry points with established market expectations. They serve as proof of competence and relationship-building vehicles that eventually surface the core question: what if we built something like this for you personally?

The upstream logic: clients who complete a Human Construct build become natural candidates for premium, higher-investment engagements — full brand ecosystem builds, digital platform builds, ongoing strategic advisory. The Construct itself becomes the connective tissue. A founder who has a personal AI operating system is a founder who sees the value of systematizing everything else.

This positioning does not change external messaging. The five pillars remain the public-facing architecture. Human Construct is the internal focal point — the offer around which go-to-market strategy, content, sales infrastructure, and fulfillment systems are prioritized first.

## The Asset Model

Human Construct operates on the economics of manufactured durable goods, not the economics of professional services. The closest analogy is the automobile.

A personal agent is assembled from multiple components — wrapper identity, skill library, tool integrations, automation workflows, knowledge bases — the same way a vehicle is assembled from an engine, chassis, drivetrain, electronics, and interior. The assembly requires expertise. The personalization requires consultation. The delivery requires calibration. But the end product is an asset that the client owns and operates independently.

The client pays a flat rate for the build. They drive it off the lot. They do not pay Five Points again unless they need the system augmented, recalibrated, or expanded — the same way a car owner does not pay the manufacturer again unless they need service.

This positions Five Points as agent mechanics. The initial build is a manufacturing engagement. Evolution (the companion offer) is a service plan. Future augmentation requests are shop visits. The relationship is durable, low-friction, and scales with the client's needs rather than with arbitrary retainer cycles.

This framing has three structural advantages:

**Ownership psychology.** When someone buys a car, they feel ownership. When someone pays a monthly retainer, they feel subscription fatigue. Human Construct clients own their system. That ownership creates pride, investment, and evangelism — three things that a retainer relationship structurally discourages.

**Referral mechanics.** People recommend the car they drive. They recommend the mechanic they trust. They do not typically recommend their subscription services with the same conviction. A client who owns a Construct and loves it becomes a referral source in the same way someone who loves their vehicle becomes one — naturally, without being asked, in the context of daily life.

**Market timing.** Personal agents are on the same adoption curve as personal computers in the early 1980s and smartphones in the late 2000s. The market does not yet understand what a personal AI system is, how to evaluate one, or where to buy one. Five Points is positioning itself to be the place you go to get yours built — the same way you went to a dealer to get your first car configured and delivered. Early movers in this space will define the category. The familiar service offerings in the rest of the catalog keep the business funded while this market matures.

## Positioning

Human Construct is where Five Points stops building for businesses and starts building for the person behind them. This is bespoke AI system design for founders, portfolio entrepreneurs and high net worth individuals who want an AI operating system engineered around their brain, their ventures, their communication patterns and their life.

This is not a chatbot. This is not a prompt library. This is a personal AI ecosystem with a named identity, trained on the client's context, connected to their tools, and designed to think the way they think. The system gets a name. The system gets a personality. The system gets a wrapper identity that the client defines during a signature onboarding moment. From that point forward, the client is not talking to AI. They are talking to their Construct.

**Anchor:** A full-time chief of staff costs $80,000 to $150,000 per year. A senior executive assistant in a major metro runs $60,000 to $90,000. A technical AI consultant bills $200 to $400 per hour. Human Construct delivers elements of all three as a permanent, compounding asset — not a recurring expense — for a fraction of a single year of any one of them.

## The Naming Ceremony

This is the signature moment of every Human Construct engagement at Silver and above. It is not a form field. It is not a dropdown menu. It is a guided, consultative experience where the client defines the identity of the intelligence that will operate alongside them every day.

The session covers:

- **Name:** The client names their Construct. This is the name they will use in conversation, in commands, in reflection. It should feel natural, personal, and intentional.
- **Personality attributes:** The client defines the character. Warm or direct? Formal or casual? Challenging or supportive? The wrapper is calibrated to match.
- **Domain affinities:** Where does the Construct lean in? Strategy? Creative? Operations? Analytics? The skill library and response patterns are weighted accordingly.
- **Boundary protocols:** What does the client never want the Construct to do? What topics are off limits? What decisions should always be escalated to the human?
- **Relationship dynamic:** Is this a chief of staff? A thought partner? A creative collaborator? An operations manager? The framing shapes every interaction.

This moment matters because it transforms the relationship from "using a tool" to "working with an entity." The psychological shift is the product. When a client says "ask [Name] to pull the numbers on that" instead of "open the AI and run a query," the Construct has achieved what it was designed to achieve. It has become infrastructure, not software.

The prospect walks away having experienced the value. The close rate on the follow-up becomes a formality.

No pitch. No deck. Just proof.

**Conversion path:** AI Audit leads naturally to Bronze (the full architecture document) or directly to Silver/Gold for prospects who already know they want the build.

## Case Study Architecture

Every Human Construct build produces a case study by design, not by afterthought. This is the perceived likelihood multiplier in the Hormozi value equation.

**Measurement protocol:**

- At intake: document the client's baseline — hours per week spent on cognitive labor, number of tools in use, self-reported operational friction score (1 to 10), and specific workflows the Construct will address
- At 60 days post-handoff: document the same metrics. Hours saved, tools consolidated, friction score change, and specific outcomes the Construct delivered
- At 90 days (Platinum only): expanded measurement including impact across ventures, team adoption metrics, and automation performance data

**Output:** Each case study follows a structured before/after format. No client names without permission. Anonymous case studies are still powerful — "a portfolio entrepreneur running three ventures saved 18 hours per week within 60 days" carries weight even without a name attached.

**Usage:** Case studies feed content marketing (Pillar 3), sales conversations, the Bronze AI Blueprint (as proof of what the architecture can become when built), and the website. Three case studies in hand transforms the sales conversation from selling a concept to selling proof.

## Intake Protocol

Human Construct operates on quarterly intake windows to protect build quality and founder bandwidth.

- **Q1 intake:** January (applications open December 1)
- **Q2 intake:** April (applications open March 1)
- **Q3 intake:** July (applications open June 1)
- **Q4 intake:** October (applications open September 1)

Maximum three concurrent builds per quarter across Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Bronze is not capacity-constrained and can be purchased at any time.

**Waitlist mechanic:** The three-build cap is real scarcity. It should be visible. A public waitlist — even if it is a simple form that says "applications for Q3 2026 open June 1 — join the waitlist to be notified" — creates social proof and urgency without manufacturing either. When a prospect sees that the next available slot is three months away, the perceived value of the current window increases. The waitlist also builds a pipeline of pre-qualified prospects for the next quarter.

Applications are reviewed for fit. The anti-ICP characteristics that disqualify prospects across Five Points apply here with additional filters:

- The client must be willing to invest time in discovery. Human Construct requires genuine participation, not "just build me something."
- The client must have an existing operational infrastructure (ventures, tools, workflows) worth systematizing. If there is nothing to systematize, there is nothing to build.
- The client must be open to the consultative process. The Naming Ceremony, the identity design, the calibration period — these are not optional steps that can be skipped to "get to the build faster."

- **Gold ($15,000 to $25,000):** Estimated 50 to 70 hours of labor across six to eight weeks. Effective rate: $285 to $357/hour. Margin: 75%+

All tiers clear the 75% gross margin floor at a $100/hour labor rate. Bronze clears the floor but its strategic value is measured in conversion rate to Silver/Gold, not in standalone margin. The effective hourly rates at Silver through Platinum are among the highest in the Five Points catalog, which is appropriate for a bespoke, consultative, high-intimacy engagement.
