## Pricing

Pricing is buyer-based, per the pressure-test ruling: buyer, not format, sets the price. Teams inside larger organizations buy the enterprise lane regardless of headcount.

### SMB lane (route: /team)

- **On site:** $5,500 flat, up to 15 people, three hours, taught on the buyer's premises. The studio is based in Atlanta; the session travels. Travel terms beyond the metro are settled in booking correspondence, never on the page.
- **Remote:** $3,500 flat, up to 10 seats, live over video. Same instruments, same action plans.
- **Ascension:** either fee credits in full toward a Build ($19,700) within 60 days.

### Enterprise lane (route: /enterprise, ratified 8 July 2026)

- **Rung 1:** $10,700 flat, up to 20 people, three hours, on site. Operator floor of $10,600 cleared; price keeps the house ending-in-7 pattern.
- **Rung 2:** $16,700 flat, up to 50 people, delivered as two cohorts of up to 25 in one day. Every seat stays a hands-on seat; the per-person action plan survives at full headcount.
- **Includes the stack review:** a pre-session working review of the tools the organization sanctions. The session is built inside the approved stack.
- **Ascension:** fee credits in full toward a commissioned build quoted at enterprise scale (the $35K-floor tier) within 60 days. It does not credit toward the $19,700 Build.
- Remote delivery and recording policy for regulated buyers are settled in correspondence.
- Larger rooms than 50 are quoted, not listed.

## What Is Included

- The instruments: the working toolset for the operation, named, demonstrated, ranked by return.
- Live demonstrations built on the team's real workflows. Nothing canned.
- One written action plan per person, against their role: three moves for the fortnight after, in order, tools named. Begun in the room, delivered within two days.
- The full session recording.
- Taught by a partner of the firm. Never an associate, never a contracted trainer.

## Ideal Client

**SMB lane:** owner-led service businesses with 5 to 25 staff. Two tracks share one call list: the locked premium verticals (med spas, brokerages, clubs) and the live responder pool (AI-active Atlanta founders, home services, professional services). The buyer is always the owner.

**Enterprise lane:** teams and departments inside mid-market and enterprise organizations - pharmaceutical, corporate, regulated industries. The buyer is a department lead or executive with budget authority; the first conversations run warm through the operator's network rather than cold. A 10-person team inside an enterprise buys this lane, not the SMB lane.

## Ethos

Ratified by the operator on 8 July 2026. This is the governing idea of the offer; all collateral derives from it.

> Hand a room of capable people a case of fine instruments and you get noise. Teach each player their instrument and their part, and the same room makes music. The Command Session exists for the distance between those two rooms - and the owner conducts.

### Lineage

The ethos scales the brand essence from one player to the ensemble. The fingerprint essence reads "An instrument tuned to one mind. Drafted by hand. Calibrated until it sings." (Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md, Layer 1). The Command Session is that sentence in the plural: many instruments, tuned, until the room sings. It also resolves the tension between a team offer and the singular-minds spine - an ensemble is many players organized around one interpretation. The one mind remains the owner.

### The mappings (use only these; do not extend)

| Metaphor | Reality |
|---|---|
| The instruments | The AI toolset |
| The players | The team |
| The parts | The per-person action plans |
| The tuning | The session itself |
| The conductor | The owner - never Five Points |
| The tuner | The partner, who tunes and leaves |

Five Points is the instrument maker and tuner, never the conductor. The studio hands over the keys and leaves the loop (the Asset Principle). Any copy that places the studio at the podium is a violation.

### Usage rules

1. **The idea governs; the word stays offstage.** "Symphony" is spoken language (calls, the room), not printed language. On the page the idea surfaces through instruments, parts, tuning and music - the reader completes the thought (Withholding attribute; Decision Defaults 8 and 9).
2. **Two touches maximum per artifact.** One planted passage, one echo. The motif never colonizes a surface (quality gate X1).
3. **The noise half is stated universally, never about the prospect's team.** "Hand a room fine instruments and you get noise" - a fact about untrained hands anywhere, not an accusation (quality gate X2).
4. **Banned adjacent vocabulary:** orchestrate, harmony/harmonious, in sync, alignment as a noun of feeling, magic, chemistry. These drift the Architect register into motivational-poster territory (never hype-driven; the Magician half of the archetype stays structural).
5. **Native vocabulary:** instrument, part, tuned, calibrated (always-use term), plays, music, ensemble (sparingly).

### Spoken register (cold call)

The phone is where the image runs at full volume - spoken language is not governed by the written gates:

"Right now everybody's got these expensive instruments and they're all playing different songs. Nobody taught them their part. That's what the session fixes - three hours, and your team is playing the same piece of music."
