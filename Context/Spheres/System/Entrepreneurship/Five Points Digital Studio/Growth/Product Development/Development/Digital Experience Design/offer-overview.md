# Offer 5.1: Digital Experience Design

**Pillar:** 05 Development
**Offer Number:** 5.1
**Stripe Name Pattern:** 5.1 — [Tier Name] — [Tier]
**Relocated from:** Pillar 4 Creative (previously Offer 4.2: Website Design)

**Description:** Website UX and UI design from sprint through full information architecture and design system.

**Tiers Available:**
- Silver — Website Design Sprint
- Gold — Full Website Design

**Rationale for Relocation:** Development absorbs Digital Experience Design alongside Website Engineering and Application Engineering. This is where intent becomes artifact. Design and build live under one roof because agentic engineering is collapsing the gap between them. The separation is maintained at the offer level (design-only engagements can still be scoped) but the pillar acknowledges that these disciplines are converging.
