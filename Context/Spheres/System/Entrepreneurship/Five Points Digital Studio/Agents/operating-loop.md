# The Five Points Client Flywheel

The venture operating loop – the client journey that runs across every department, drawn as the outermost loop that contains all the others.

It is a flywheel, not a funnel. It closes, because a referral at the end feeds attention at the start. It climbs, because every completed turn moves the client up a rung of the Priestley Ascending Transaction Model. A funnel ends; this loop spirals.

## The Nine Stages

| # | Stage | Owner | What happens | Manor gate | Copy load |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attract | Business Development | ICP outbound via Clay, inbound, referral; the Gift front door (Bronze AI Fluency Workshop) | – | Heavy |
| 2 | Qualify | Business Development | Buying-power appraisal, ICP scoring, star-rating to the CRM | – | Low |
| 3 | Discover | Marketing & Sales + Business Development | The Working Call, discovery, dossier, offer recommendation | Direction | Medium |
| 4 | Propose | Marketing & Sales | Offer architecture, the two prices, proposal from templates | – | Heavy |
| 5 | Close | Marketing & Sales + Administration | Terms, MSA and SOW, Stripe registration | – | Medium |
| 6 | Onboard | Operations | New Client template, intake, kickoff, access | – | Medium |
| 7 | Deliver | Operations orchestrates and builds; Marketing & Sales directs | The work – sites, content, campaigns, SEO, per SOPs and playbooks | Critique | Heavy |
| 8 | Retain | Operations + Finances | Reporting, QBRs, retainer health, metrics | – | Medium |
| 9 | Ascend and Refer | Business Development | Upsell the next Priestley rung, case study, referral ask; feeds stage 1 | – | Heavy |

## The Nested Principle

The ring of nine is the biggest loop. Inside each stage runs its own five-phase Manor Protocol lifecycle – Reconnaissance, Direction, Execution, Critique, Release. The venture is therefore a loop of loops: the operating ring, and the five-phase cycle inside every stage. Named workflows are the third level down, living in each department's `Agents/Workflows/` folder.

## Department Ownership

- **Business Development** owns three of the nine stages – Attract, Qualify, Ascend and Refer – plus a joint hold on Discover. The lead-generation arc.
- **Marketing & Sales** owns Discover (jointly), Propose and Close (jointly), and directs the Deliver stage's brand and content dimension. The largest workflow set.
- **Operations** owns Onboard and Retain, and orchestrates and builds Deliver.
- **Administration** feeds Close. **Finances** feeds Retain.

## The Copy Thread

Copy is load-bearing in eight of the nine stages and the spine of four – Attract, Propose, Deliver, Ascend and Refer. It is the connective tissue of the flywheel, not a Marketing & Sales side-task. Each copy-bearing stage dispatches to a specialist in the copywriting skill layer:

| Stage | Copy specialist dispatched |
|---|---|
| Attract | email (outbound), social, ads |
| Discover | – (synthesis, internal) |
| Propose | sales page and long-form |
| Onboard | email (lifecycle) |
| Deliver | all client copy – content, social, email, sales pages, ads per engagement |
| Retain | email (newsletter, reporting) |
| Ascend and Refer | sales page (case study), email, social (referral) |

Each specialist is a complete, standalone skill. It loads the relevant brand fingerprint itself, carries its own craft in full for its medium, invokes grammar-nazi at its gate and holds itself to the four Critique criteria. No shared core skill – the six stand on their own.
