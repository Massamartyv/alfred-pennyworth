# The Five Points Client Flywheel

The venture operating loop – the client journey that runs across every studio, drawn as the outermost loop that contains all the others.

It is a flywheel, not a funnel. It closes, because a referral at the end feeds attention at the start. It climbs, because every completed turn moves the client up a rung of the Priestley Ascending Transaction Model. A funnel ends; this loop spirals.

## The Nine Stages

| # | Stage | Owner | What happens | Manor gate | Copy load |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attract | Growth | ICP outbound via Clay, inbound, referral; the Gift front door (Bronze AI Fluency Workshop) | – | Heavy |
| 2 | Qualify | Growth | Buying-power appraisal, ICP scoring, star-rating to the CRM | – | Low |
| 3 | Discover | Growth + Strategy | The Working Call, discovery, dossier, offer recommendation | Direction | Medium |
| 4 | Propose | Growth | Offer architecture, the two prices, proposal from templates | – | Heavy |
| 5 | Close | Growth + Administration | Terms, MSA and SOW, Stripe registration | – | Medium |
| 6 | Onboard | Operations | New Client template, intake, kickoff, access | – | Medium |
| 7 | Deliver | Operations orchestrates; Production and Creative build | The work – sites, content, campaigns, SEO, per SOPs and playbooks | Critique | Heavy |
| 8 | Retain | Operations + Finance | Reporting, QBRs, retainer health, metrics | – | Medium |
| 9 | Ascend and Refer | Growth | Upsell the next Priestley rung, case study, referral ask; feeds stage 1 | – | Heavy |

## The Nested Principle

The ring of nine is the biggest loop. Inside each stage runs its own five-phase Manor Protocol lifecycle – Reconnaissance, Direction, Execution, Critique, Release. The venture is therefore a loop of loops: the operating ring, and the five-phase cycle inside every stage. Named workflows are the third level down, living in each studio's `Agents/Workflows/` folder.

## Studio Ownership

- **Growth** owns six of the nine stages – Attract, Qualify, Discover, Propose, Close, Ascend and Refer. The revenue arc, and the largest workflow set.
- **Operations** owns Onboard and Retain, and orchestrates Deliver.
- **Production and Creative** build inside Deliver.
- **Strategy** feeds Discover. **Administration** feeds Close. **Finance** feeds Retain.

## The Copy Thread

Copy is load-bearing in eight of the nine stages and the spine of four – Attract, Propose, Deliver, Ascend and Refer. It is the connective tissue of the flywheel, not a Creative side-task. Each copy-bearing stage dispatches to a specialist in the copywriting skill layer:

| Stage | Copy specialist dispatched |
|---|---|
| Attract | email (outbound), social, ads |
| Discover | – (synthesis, internal) |
| Propose | sales page and long-form |
| Onboard | email (lifecycle) |
| Deliver | all client copy – content, social, email, sales pages, ads per engagement |
| Retain | email (newsletter, reporting) |
| Ascend and Refer | sales page (case study), email, social (referral) |

Each specialist is a complete, standalone skill. It loads the relevant brand fingerprint itself, carries its own craft in full for its medium, invokes grammar-nazi at its gate and holds itself to the four Critique criteria. No shared core skill – the six stand on their own.
