# Document 5A of 7: Five Points Master Services Agreement (MSA)

**Document type:** Foundational legal agreement governing the entire client relationship
**Purpose:** Establishes the terms, standards, and expectations that apply to every engagement between Five Points Digital Studio and the client. Signed once. Governs all current and future Statements of Work for the duration of the agreement. Never needs rewriting.
**Tone:** Professional and clear. Legal precision without unnecessary complexity. The client should be able to read this without an attorney and understand what they are agreeing to, even though legal review is always recommended.

**Used by:** Every client relationship across all tiers and pillars.

**Relationship to other documents:** The MSA governs the relationship. The Statement of Work (Document 5B) governs each specific engagement. The SOW references the MSA and inherits all of its terms. If there is a conflict between the MSA and a SOW, the SOW controls for that specific engagement only.

**Important note:** This document is a comprehensive framework built on standard agency and professional services agreement principles. It should be reviewed by a licensed attorney before use to ensure compliance with applicable state and federal law.

## ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

### 1.1 Purpose

This Agreement establishes the general terms and conditions under which Five Points will provide professional services to the Client. The specific scope, deliverables, timeline, and investment for each engagement will be defined in individual Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement.

### 1.2 Scope

This Agreement governs all services performed by Five Points for the Client during the Term, including but not limited to: AI education and training, automation consulting and implementation, digital marketing strategy and execution, brand identity design, website and application design and development, and any other professional services described in an executed SOW.

### 1.3 Relationship of Documents

Each SOW executed under this Agreement is incorporated by reference and subject to the terms of this Agreement. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, the terms of the SOW will control with respect to that specific engagement only. All other terms of this Agreement remain in full force.

### 1.4 No Obligation to Execute SOWs

This Agreement does not obligate either party to execute any SOW. Each SOW is entered into voluntarily by both parties.

## ARTICLE 2: TERM AND RENEWAL

### 2.1 Initial Term

This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and will remain in force for a period of five (5) years ("Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Article 10.

### 2.2 Renewal

At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive five (5) year periods ("Renewal Terms") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

### 2.3 Effect on Active SOWs

Expiration or non-renewal of this Agreement does not terminate any SOW that is active at the time of expiration. Active SOWs will continue under the terms of this Agreement until completed or terminated in accordance with their own terms.

## ARTICLE 3: STATEMENTS OF WORK

### 3.1 Format

Each SOW will include, at a minimum: a description of the services to be performed, the specific deliverables, the timeline and milestones, the investment and payment terms, and any engagement specific terms that supplement or modify the terms of this Agreement for that engagement.

### 3.2 Execution

SOWs may be executed in one of two ways:

**Formal execution:** For Silver, Gold, and Platinum tier engagements, the SOW must be signed by authorized representatives of both parties. Electronic signatures are acceptable.

**Email confirmation:** For Bronze tier engagements, the SOW may be accepted by the Client via email confirmation. Five Points will send the SOW to the Client's email address on record, and the Client's written reply confirming acceptance (including the words "I accept" or "approved" or substantially similar language) constitutes binding acceptance of the SOW.

### 3.3 Amendments to a SOW

Any changes to an executed SOW (scope, timeline, deliverables, or investment) must be agreed upon in writing by both parties. Verbal agreements to modify a SOW are not binding. Amendments may be documented via a formal amendment document or via email exchange where both parties explicitly confirm the change.

## ARTICLE 4: FEES AND PAYMENT

### 4.1 Fees

All fees for services are specified in the applicable SOW. Fees are exclusive of any applicable taxes, which are the responsibility of the Client.

### 4.2 Payment Terms

Payment terms are specified in the applicable SOW. Unless otherwise stated in a SOW, invoices are due within fifteen (15) business days of issuance.

### 4.3 Late Payment

Invoices not paid within the specified payment period will be subject to a late fee of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month on the outstanding balance, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

### 4.4 Deposit

Where a deposit is specified in a SOW, work will not commence until the deposit has been received. Five Points is under no obligation to begin any work, reserve any resources, or hold any timeline until the deposit clears.

### 4.5 Expense Reimbursement

Reasonable, pre-approved expenses incurred by Five Points on behalf of the Client (including but not limited to travel, accommodation, third party tool subscriptions, ad spend, production costs, and stock assets) will be billed at cost with no markup. Expenses exceeding five hundred dollars ($500) require prior written approval from the Client. Expenses are invoiced separately from service fees and are due within fifteen (15) business days.

### 4.6 Fee Adjustments

Five Points reserves the right to adjust its standard rates. Any rate changes will be communicated to the Client in writing at least sixty (60) days before the new rates take effect. Rate changes apply only to new SOWs executed after the effective date of the adjustment. Active SOWs are not affected by rate changes.

## ARTICLE 5: CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

### 5.1 Cooperation

The Client agrees to cooperate with Five Points in good faith and provide timely access to the information, materials, personnel, and systems reasonably necessary for Five Points to perform the services described in each SOW.

### 5.2 Timely Feedback

Where a SOW specifies deadlines for Client feedback or approvals, the Client will use reasonable efforts to meet those deadlines. Delays in Client feedback will shift subsequent milestones by an equivalent duration. Five Points is not responsible for delays caused by late Client feedback.

### 5.3 Feedback Responsiveness

If Client feedback on a deliverable is not received within fifteen (15) business days of delivery, Five Points will send a written reminder. If feedback is still not received within ten (10) additional business days (twenty-five business days total), Five Points reserves the right to pause the engagement. Paused engagements may be resumed at the Client's request, subject to Five Points availability. No refunds will be issued for delays caused by Client unresponsiveness.

### 5.4 Accurate Information

The Client warrants that all information provided to Five Points is accurate and complete to the best of the Client's knowledge. Five Points is not responsible for errors, delays, or additional costs resulting from inaccurate or incomplete information provided by the Client.

### 5.5 Decision Authority

The Client will designate a single primary point of contact with the authority to provide feedback, approve deliverables, and make binding decisions on behalf of the Client. Changes to the designated point of contact must be communicated to Five Points in writing.

## ARTICLE 6: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 6.1 Client Deliverables

Upon receipt of final payment in full for an applicable SOW, all deliverables created by Five Points specifically for the Client under that SOW ("Client Deliverables") become the exclusive property of the Client. Client Deliverables include but are not limited to: final design files, brand assets, automation workflows, website code, written content, and any other work product defined in the SOW.

### 6.2 Five Points IP

Five Points retains exclusive ownership of all proprietary frameworks, methodologies, templates, tools, processes, and pre-existing intellectual property ("Five Points IP") used in the delivery of services. Five Points IP is not transferred to the Client. This includes but is not limited to:

- The Five Points AI Fluency Framework
- The Five Points Digital Presence Audit methodology and scoring rubric
- Standardized intake forms, assessment templates, and process documentation
- Internal automation templates and workflow patterns
- Training curricula, presentation frameworks, and educational materials
- Any tools, scripts, or systems developed by Five Points prior to or independent of the Client engagement

The Client is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license to use any Five Points IP delivered as part of a SOW for the Client's internal business purposes only. This license is perpetual and survives termination of this Agreement, provided all fees under the applicable SOW have been paid in full.

### 6.3 Third Party Materials

Third party tools, platforms, software, stock assets, fonts, and licenses used in the delivery of services remain the property of their respective owners. The Client is responsible for maintaining its own subscriptions and licenses for any third party materials after the engagement ends. Five Points will identify any third party materials included in deliverables and their associated licensing requirements.

### 6.4 Portfolio Rights

Five Points retains the right to display, reference, and describe the work performed for the Client in its portfolio, case studies, marketing materials, and award submissions. The Client may request in writing that Five Points not use specific deliverables or not identify the Client by name. Five Points will honor such requests.

### 6.5 Pre-Payment IP

Until all fees under a SOW are paid in full, Five Points retains ownership of all work product created under that SOW. The Client receives a limited, non-exclusive license to use delivered work product in its business operations during the payment period, but ownership does not transfer until final payment is received.

## ARTICLE 7: CONFIDENTIALITY

### 7.1 Definition

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party ("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party") in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to: business strategies, financial information, client lists, pricing structures, proprietary processes, trade secrets, technical data, and any information explicitly identified as confidential.

### 7.2 Obligations

The Receiving Party agrees to: (a) protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; (b) use Confidential Information only for the purposes of performing or receiving services under this Agreement; and (c) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, except to employees, contractors, or agents who need access to perform services and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these.

### 7.3 Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was known to the Receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice to the extent permitted by law.

### 7.4 Duration

Confidentiality obligations under this Article survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years following the date of termination or expiration.

THE TOTAL LIABILITY OF FIVE POINTS TO THE CLIENT FOR ALL CLAIMS ARISING UNDER OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT OR ANY SOW WILL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY THE CLIENT TO FIVE POINTS UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

### 9.2 Exclusion of Consequential Damages

IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA, OR BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER SUCH DAMAGES WERE FORESEEABLE OR WHETHER THE PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

### 9.3 Exceptions

The limitations in this Article do not apply to: (a) breaches of confidentiality obligations under Article 7; (b) a party's indemnification obligations under Article 11; or (c) willful misconduct or gross negligence.

## ARTICLE 10: TERMINATION

### 10.1 Termination of the Agreement

Either party may terminate this Agreement: (a) upon ninety (90) days written notice without cause; (b) immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice of the breach; or (c) immediately upon written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases to operate as a going concern.

### 10.2 Termination of a SOW

Either party may terminate an individual SOW with fourteen (14) days written notice. The financial terms of termination for each SOW are specified in the SOW itself.

### 10.3 Effect of SOW Termination on the Agreement

Termination of a SOW does not terminate this Agreement. The Agreement remains in force and additional SOWs may be executed.

### 10.4 Effect of Agreement Termination on Active SOWs

If this Agreement is terminated, all active SOWs will continue under the terms of this Agreement until completed, unless a SOW is separately terminated in accordance with its own terms.

### 10.5 Survival

The following Articles survive termination or expiration of this Agreement: Article 6 (Intellectual Property), Article 7 (Confidentiality), Article 8 (Representations and Warranties), Article 9 (Limitation of Liability), Article 11 (Indemnification), and Article 13 (General Provisions).

## ARTICLE 13: GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 13.1 Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

### 13.2 Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement will first be addressed through good faith negotiation between the parties. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days, either party may pursue mediation administered by a mutually agreed upon mediator in Atlanta, Georgia. If mediation fails to resolve the dispute within sixty (60) days, either party may pursue binding arbitration or litigation in the courts of the State of Georgia, Fulton County.

### 13.3 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with all SOWs executed hereunder, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, proposals, and agreements, whether written or oral, relating to the subject matter hereof.

### 13.4 Amendments

This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

### 13.5 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect.

### 13.6 Waiver

The failure of either party to enforce any provision of this Agreement does not constitute a waiver of that provision or the right to enforce it in the future.

### 13.7 Notices

All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by email to the addresses specified above (or such other address as either party may designate in writing). Notices are deemed received on the date of confirmed delivery.

### 13.8 Force Majeure

Neither party will be liable for delays or failure to perform due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, pandemics, government actions, internet outages, or third party platform failures. The affected party will provide prompt notice and use reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact.

### 13.9 Assignment

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Five Points may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.

## ASSEMBLY GUIDE FOR THE FIVE POINTS TEAM

### When to present the MSA:

Present the MSA after the discovery call, at the same time as the first SOW. The MSA and SOW can be sent together in a single email. The client signs both. For all future engagements with the same client, only a new SOW is needed.

### How to present it:

Frame the MSA as a partnership agreement, not a legal hurdle. Suggested language for the email:

"Attached are two documents. The first is our Master Services Agreement, which covers the foundational terms of how we work together. You sign it once and it covers everything going forward. The second is the Statement of Work for [engagement name], which covers the specifics of this project. Both are designed to be clear and straightforward. If you have any questions about either, I am happy to walk through them."

### Returning clients:

When a returning client engages Five Points for a new project, check whether an active MSA is on file. If yes, only a new SOW is needed. If the MSA has expired or was terminated, a new MSA must be executed.

### Record keeping:

Maintain a registry of all executed MSAs with: client name, effective date, expiration date, and renewal status. Set a reminder ninety (90) days before each MSA expires to evaluate renewal.
