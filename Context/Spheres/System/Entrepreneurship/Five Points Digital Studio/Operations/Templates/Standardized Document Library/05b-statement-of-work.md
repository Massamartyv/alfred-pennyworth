# Document 5B of 7: Five Points Statement of Work (SOW) Template

**Document type:** Modular, plug and play engagement document executed under the Master Services Agreement
**Purpose:** Defines the specific scope, deliverables, timeline, investment, and engagement specific terms for each individual project or retainer. References the MSA for all foundational terms (IP, confidentiality, liability, termination conditions, etc.) so the SOW stays lean and focused on the work.
**Tone:** Professional and clear, with a warm engagement summary that grounds the document in the client's specific situation. The SOW should feel like a project plan with a signature line, not a second legal document.

**Used by:** Every Silver, Gold, and Platinum engagement across all five pillars. Bronze engagements accepted via email confirmation (no formal signature required per MSA Article 3.2).

**Relationship to other documents:** The SOW is subordinate to the MSA. All terms in the MSA apply to every SOW unless the SOW explicitly modifies a specific term for that engagement. The SOW does not restate MSA terms. It references them.

**Data dependency:** The SOW is generated after the discovery call using information from the unified prospect profile (Documents 1 through 4) and the outcomes of the discovery conversation. No new information is collected from the client.

Either party may terminate this SOW with fourteen (14) days written notice.

**If the Client terminates:**
- All work completed to date will be delivered to the Client
- Payments for completed milestones are non-refundable
- Payments for milestones not yet begun will be refunded in full
- Payments for milestones in progress will be prorated based on the percentage of work completed

**If Five Points terminates:**
- All work completed to date will be delivered to the Client
- Any payments for undelivered work will be refunded in full
- Five Points will provide a fourteen (14) day transition period

Termination of this SOW does not affect the MSA or any other active SOWs.

## ASSEMBLY GUIDE FOR THE FIVE POINTS TEAM

### Building a SOW: Step by Step

1. **Confirm active MSA.** Check the MSA registry to verify the client has a current, executed MSA. If not, prepare the MSA for simultaneous execution.

2. **Assign a SOW number.** Use the format SOW-YYYY-### (e.g. SOW-2026-001). Sequential numbering within each calendar year.

3. **Write the Engagement Summary (Section 1).** This is the human part of the document. Reference the discovery call and the client's specific situation. Pull language from the unified prospect profile (their biggest challenge, desired outcome, and magic wand response from Document 3 if applicable).

4. **Define the Scope of Work (Section 2).** Pull deliverables from the relevant offer tier in the offer suite document. Be specific. Include exclusions.

5. **Build the Timeline (Section 3).** Use the standard delivery timeline for the offer tier as a starting point. Adjust for client availability, start date, and any dependencies. Always include client responsibilities at each milestone.

6. **Set the Investment and Payment Terms (Section 4).** Use the standard pricing for the offer tier. Apply Community Circle discount if applicable. Include expense estimates if relevant. Standard payment splits:
   - Under $5,000: 50% deposit, 50% on completion
   - $5,000 to $15,000: 40% deposit, 30% midpoint, 30% completion
   - Over $15,000: 30% deposit, then milestone based payments
   - Retainers: First month at signing, subsequent months invoiced monthly

7. **Set revision allowances (Section 5).** Use the standard rounds for the tier.

8. **Set communication terms (Section 6).** Use the standard response times for the tier. Designate primary contacts and communication channel.

9. **Insert the guarantee (Section 7).** Pull verbatim from the offer suite document.

10. **Add engagement specific terms (Section 8) if needed.** Only include this section if the engagement has unique requirements not covered by the MSA.

11. **Include the appropriate acceptance section (Section 10).** Formal signature for Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Email confirmation for Bronze.

12. **Review the complete document** for consistency with the MSA and the offer suite. Send to the client with the MSA (if first engagement) or alone (if returning client with active MSA).

### Estimated assembly time: One to two Pomodoro sessions (25 to 50 minutes).

### Document format:

The SOW should be delivered as a branded PDF consistent with the Five Points visual identity. For formal signature engagements, use a digital signature tool (DocuSign, HelloSign, or similar). For Bronze email confirmation engagements, the SOW can be sent as a PDF attachment with instructions to reply with confirmation.
