# Document 6 of 7: Five Points Client Onboarding Packet

**Document type:** Client facing welcome document and orientation guide
**Location:** Delivered to the client via email as a branded PDF after the Project Scope and Agreement (Document 5) is signed and the deposit is received.
**Purpose:** Welcomes the client, sets expectations for the engagement, provides access to shared resources, introduces the communication cadence, and gives the client everything they need to know about working with Five Points. This is the first impression of the working relationship, not the sales relationship. It should feel organized, warm, and confidence building.
**Tone:** Warm, organized, and reassuring. The client just made a significant investment. This document should make them feel like they made the right decision. It should feel like being welcomed into a well run studio, not like receiving an HR orientation manual.

**Used by:** Every Silver, Gold, and Platinum engagement across all five pillars.

**Data dependency:** This document is generated after the Project Scope and Agreement is signed using information from the unified prospect profile (Documents 1 through 4 as applicable) and the signed agreement (Document 5). No new information is collected from the client.

**No Redundancy Rule:** The client has already provided all relevant information through the intake process and signed the scope agreement. The onboarding packet references this information but never asks the client to provide it again.

---

## DOCUMENT STRUCTURE

The Client Onboarding Packet is a branded PDF document with a consistent structure across all engagements. Variable content is populated based on the specific engagement tier, pillar, and scope.

---

## SECTION 1: WELCOME

**Purpose:** Warm, personal welcome that reinforces the client's decision and sets the tone for the engagement.

**Contents:**

```
Welcome to Five Points.

[Client First Name],

We are excited to get started. You shared something important with us
during our conversation — [reference a specific detail from the discovery
call or intake that shows Five Points was listening, e.g. "that your
team is spending 20 hours a week on manual follow ups" or "that your
brand has not kept pace with how much your business has grown"]. That
stuck with us, and it is shaping everything we are about to build
together.

This packet has everything you need to know about how we work, what
to expect, and how to reach us. Read it when you have a few minutes.
If anything is unclear, we are a message away.

Let us make something great.

Five Points Digital Studio
```

**Guidance for the Five Points team:**

This section should never be generic. The opening paragraph must reference something specific from the discovery call or intake responses. This is what separates Five Points from agencies that send a boilerplate welcome email. The client should read this and think "they were actually paying attention."

## SECTION 3: HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

**Purpose:** Sets clear expectations for the working relationship so there are no surprises.

**Contents:**

```
HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

COMMUNICATION
- Primary channel: [Email / Slack / project management tool]
- Response time: We respond within [X business hours] on business days
  (Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern)
- Scheduled check ins: [Frequency and format]
- For urgent matters: [Contact method]

Please keep all project related communication in [designated channel].
This keeps everything in one place and ensures nothing gets lost.

- When we deliver work for your review, we will let you know what
  kind of feedback is most helpful at that stage.
- Please consolidate your feedback into one written response. This
  helps us make changes efficiently and avoids conflicting direction.
- Your engagement includes [X] rounds of revisions at each milestone.
- We ask for your feedback within [X] business days of delivery so
  we can keep the project on schedule.

YOUR ROLE IN THIS
- We handle the heavy lifting, but great work is collaborative.
  Here is what we will need from you during this engagement:
  [List specific client responsibilities based on the engagement,
  e.g. "Timely feedback at each milestone," "Access to your Google
  Analytics by [date]," "Availability for a 60 minute session in
  Week 2," "Final approval on brand direction before we move to
  collateral design."]
- If anything comes up that might delay your availability, just let
  us know. We would rather adjust the timeline together than have
  the project stall.
```

**Guidance for the Five Points team:**

Create the client's Google Drive project folder before sending the onboarding packet. The folder structure should be:

```
[Client Name] — [Engagement Name]
├── 01 — Discovery and Intake
│   └── [Compiled intake responses, discovery call notes]
├── 02 — Work in Progress
│   └── [Internal working files, drafts]
├── 03 — Client Review
│   └── [Files sent to client for feedback]
├── 04 — Final Deliverables
│   └── [Approved final files]
├── 05 — Session Recordings
│   └── [Recorded calls and sessions]
└── 06 — Administrative
    └── [Signed agreement, invoices]
```

The client should have view access to folders 03, 04, and 05. Folders 01, 02, and 06 are internal.

## SECTION 5: WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

**Purpose:** Gives the client a clear, immediate next step so they know exactly what to do after reading this packet.

**Contents:**

```
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Here is what is coming up in the next [X] days:

1. [Immediate next step, e.g. "We will send you a calendar invite
   for our kickoff session on [date]." or "Our team will begin the
   pre-engagement audit this week. You do not need to do anything
   until we reach out."]

2. [Second step if applicable, e.g. "Please grant us view access
   to your Google Analytics by [date]. Here is how: [brief
   instructions or link to a guide]."]

3. [Third step if applicable]

If you have any questions before we get started, reach out to
[contact name] at [email]. We are here.
```

**Guidance for the Five Points team:**

Never end the onboarding packet without a specific, time bound next step. The client should close this document knowing exactly what happens tomorrow. Vague closings like "we will be in touch soon" create anxiety. Specific closings like "you will receive a calendar invite for our kickoff session on Thursday at 2 PM Eastern" create confidence.

## SECTION 6: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (Optional — include for Gold and Platinum)

**Purpose:** Preemptively answers the questions clients most commonly ask in the first week.

**Contents:**

```
COMMON QUESTIONS

What if I need to reschedule a session?
We understand things come up. Please give us at least 24 hours notice
and we will find a new time. Same week rescheduling is always our goal.

What if I change my mind about the creative direction?
That is what the revision process is for. At each milestone, you will
have the opportunity to provide feedback and redirect. If you have a
major shift in vision, let us know as early as possible so we can
adjust the scope together.

What happens after the engagement ends?
You own everything we build for you. We will make sure you have all
files, documentation, and access credentials before we close out.
If you want ongoing support, we will discuss options toward the end
of the engagement.

Can I refer someone to Five Points?
We would be honored. Referrals are the highest compliment we receive.
If someone you know could benefit from working with us, an email
introduction to [email] is the best way.

What if something is not working?
Tell us. Directly. We would rather hear about a concern early than
discover it late. Our goal is a great outcome, and honest feedback
is how we get there.
```

## ASSEMBLY GUIDE

When generating a Client Onboarding Packet for a specific engagement, the Five Points team should:

1. Create the client's Google Drive project folder using the standard structure.
2. Write the Welcome section (Section 1) with a specific, personal reference from the discovery call or intake.
3. Populate the Engagement Overview (Section 2) from the signed Project Scope and Agreement.
4. Customize the How We Work Together section (Section 3) with engagement specific client responsibilities, communication channel, and feedback timeline.
5. Populate the Project Resources section (Section 4) with the Google Drive link, communication channel link, and any relevant documents.
6. Write the What Happens Next section (Section 5) with specific, time bound next steps.
7. Include the FAQ section (Section 6) for Gold and Platinum engagements. Optional for Silver.
8. Generate the branded PDF and send to the client via email within 24 hours of receiving the signed agreement and deposit.

**Document format:** Branded PDF, consistent with the Five Points visual identity. Clean, generous whitespace, easy to read on screen and on mobile. Two to four pages maximum. This is a reference document, not a novel.
