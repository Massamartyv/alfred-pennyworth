## FEEDBACK AND REVISION STANDARDS

| Standard | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Standard feedback window | 5 business days from deliverable presentation |
| First follow up reminder | After 15 business days of no response |
| Engagement pause trigger | After 25 business days of no response (15 + 10 additional) |
| Session recording access | 90 days from recording date |

### Revision Rounds by Tier

| Tier | Rounds Included | Additional Round Cost |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| Silver | 2 rounds per deliverable | 5% of total engagement value |
| Gold | 3 rounds per milestone | 5% of total engagement value |
| Platinum | 3 rounds per milestone + mediated realignment session | 5% of total engagement value |

### Definition of a Revision Round

A single set of consolidated feedback delivered in writing (via Notion shared workspace or email) within 5 business days of deliverable presentation. Verbal feedback, feedback delivered across multiple messages over time, or contradictory feedback that reverses previously approved direction are treated as additional rounds.

### Travel Policy Notes

- All travel costs are billed at cost with no markup per MSA Article 4.5
- Travel expenses are invoiced separately from service fees
- Client provides written approval before Five Points books travel
- Cancellation of on-location engagements within 14 days of the scheduled date: client covers any non-refundable travel expenses already incurred
- Atlanta metro area engagements: no travel fee, no accommodation, no flight
