# McCauley Electrical Services – Engagement Terms

Superseded note: the original single-offer terms (Automation Audit, $497)
were replaced at the revised Direction gate of 2026-07-10 by the three-door
slate. Pricing source of truth: `offer-slate.md` in this folder.

## Offer

- **Offer type:** The Three Doors – client-scoped slate derived from Rapid
  Site Build 5.2-S, Automation Build 2.1-G and Site Care 1.4-S components
- **Structure:** Good, better, best menu. He commissions exactly one door.
- **Engagement model:** Project – one-time commission per door

## Financial

- **The Open Door:** $1,000 one time – five business days
- **The Capture Engine:** $2,000 one time – ten business days
- **The Full Calendar:** $3,000 one time – ten business days plus a 30-day
  care window
- **Billing:** Pay-at-demonstration, per operator ruling of 2026-07-10 (art
  direction session). Nothing is charged to commission: the door is chosen on
  the working call and confirmed in writing, the studio builds at its own
  risk, and payment collects in full at the handoff demonstration once the
  machinery is shown working. Stripe products registered upon his commitment.
- **Client-side platform cost, named transparently:** field-service platform
  subscription $59-79 monthly, his account, his keys.
- **Minimum commitment:** None beyond the chosen door.

## Timeline

- **Start:** On commissioning – the working call doubles as scoping
- **Delivery:** Five business days (Open Door) or ten (Capture Engine, Full
  Calendar); Full Calendar adds 30 days of care after launch
- **Renewal:** Nothing renews itself.

## Scope

- **Included per door:** As specified in `offer-slate.md` – booking layer,
  Google Business Profile claim and completion, NAP repair and licence
  display at every door; missed-call text-back, review-request automation and
  executed directory corrections from the Capture Engine up; care month,
  community layer (OUT Georgia enrolment, LGBTBE certification application,
  Nextdoor optimisation) and the site identity detail at the Full Calendar.
- **Not included:** Advertising, paid lead generation, directory lead-buying,
  ongoing retainers and – by his own expressed wish – any website rebuild.
  All work rides the existing site. Later chapters are priced in the proposal
  – Deeper Build from $5,000, Care Plan $500 monthly – and commissioned only
  by his separate decision.
- **Revision policy:** Make-good until demonstrated working; one adjustment
  pass within 14 days of handoff.

## Guarantees – suite doctrine compliant

All doors price above the $497 line, so guarantees are make-good service:
the machinery is demonstrated working at handoff – booking taking
appointments, missed calls answered by text within a minute, review asks
firing per completed job – or the building continues at no charge until it
does. Payment itself collects only at that demonstration, which closes the
open-ended-make-good concern raised at behavioural review: he holds his money
until he has watched the machinery work. The Full Calendar adds the 30-day
care window.
