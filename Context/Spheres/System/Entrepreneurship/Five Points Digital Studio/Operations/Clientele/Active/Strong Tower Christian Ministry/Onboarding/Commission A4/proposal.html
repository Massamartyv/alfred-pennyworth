About this document

What this is

This document sets out the work Five Points has built for Strong Tower Christian Ministry. It names what the new site holds, what comes with it, what it costs and how the last few steps run.

Most studios send a paper like this before any work begins. This one arrives late, and on purpose. You came to us knowing what the ministry needed, so the building started early and moved quickly. The site is nearly finished.

So read this as a map rather than a pitch. It puts the whole job in one place, in plain words, so nothing about the work or the cost has to be remembered from a phone call.

The first visit to a church now happens on a screen. A family looks the church up, reads a little and watches a service before anyone parks a car. What follows is the home built for that first visit.

The build

What was built

A website made for Strong Tower alone. The pages, the accounts and the keys all belong to the church. Nothing here is rented, and no outside company holds it.

The house
12
Pages, each built for this church
  • Home, and the welcome it opens with
  • About, and what we believe, holding the Confession of Faith and Sonship
  • Leadership, with full bios of both Goodsons
  • Watch, events, visit, get involved and ministries
  • Give, prayer and contact
The life
5
Ministries, each named on the site
  • SCORE, and Is There Not a Cause
  • GEMS and GENTS, and the Sonship School of the Firstborn
  • Prophetic Impartation
  • Your own photos and films throughout, and the crest at full size
The rails
4
Ways in, open every day
  • Realm, Cash App and text-to-give, one tap from any page
  • A prayer box on every page, sent straight to the ministry inbox
  • The live stream and past sermons, ready to switch on
  • Built to load fast, and set up to be found in search

Also done since the first draft

The photos and films of the church were lifted out of the old site and placed through the new one, both leadership portraits among them. The lettering was recut. The real crest now appears across every page. An accessibility pass ran clean over all 12 pages, on phones and on desktops. None of it changed the price.

The commission

What comes with it,
and what it costs

What comes with it

Two full rounds of changes. 30 days of support after the site goes live. A training session, so the ministry team can edit the site without calling anyone. The first three months of hosting and care, worth $1,500.

The commission

$7,500, once.

One price, set before the work began. There is no retainer, no renewal and nothing that bills quietly in the background.

Half begins the work, $3,750. The other half is due at handoff, once the church has seen the site working.

What the church owns

The site, the pages and the keys pass to the ministry at handoff. The code, the domain and every account sit in the name of the church. The studio keeps working at no extra cost until the site does what this document says it does.

Say the word

Nothing is needed to start but your go-ahead. The studio handles every part of it that does not need a pastor.

The last details and the keys

A few things still need your eyes: the history, the leadership list and the visit details, plus your approval of the belief pages. Then the keys for the live stream, the forms and the newsletter, and the editing tool so the team can keep the site current.

The move, then the handoff

The web address moves off Wix in whichever week suits the church, and the site goes live at strongtowercm.org. The handoff follows, and the last half is due then.
