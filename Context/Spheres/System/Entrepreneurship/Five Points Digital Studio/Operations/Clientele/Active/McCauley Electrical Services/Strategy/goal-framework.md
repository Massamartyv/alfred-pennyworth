# McCauley Electrical Services – Goal Framework

Reframed at the revised Direction gate: the engagement goal is booked
appointments – capturing and converting the demand he already has toward a
calendar full enough to hire again. Targets scale with the door he chooses.

## Primary Goal

**Statement:** The demand already calling McCauley Electrical gets captured
and booked instead of lost – online, after hours and on missed calls.
**Metric:** Online booking live and taking real appointments.
**Baseline:** No booking path of any kind; roughly a third of missed calls to
a busy trade are lost for good per the cited research.
**Target:** Booking live within five business days of commissioning any door.
**Deadline:** Five business days from commissioning.

## Secondary Goals

1. **Missed-call recovery – Capture Engine and above:** every missed call
   texted back within one minute, demonstrated at handoff. Metric: text-back
   live and firing.
2. **Review velocity – Capture Engine and above:** every completed job
   triggers a review ask; the flawless-but-thin reputation starts compounding.
   Metric: review requests firing per job; Google review count trending up
   by day 90.
3. **Found properly:** Google Business Profile claimed, verified and complete
   with booking attached; one phone number and one set of hours everywhere.
   Metric: profile live and consistent by handoff.
4. **Community visible – Full Calendar:** OUT Georgia enrolment live, LGBTBE
   certification application prepared, Nextdoor optimised, the stripes on the
   site. Metric: each item shipped within the engagement window.
