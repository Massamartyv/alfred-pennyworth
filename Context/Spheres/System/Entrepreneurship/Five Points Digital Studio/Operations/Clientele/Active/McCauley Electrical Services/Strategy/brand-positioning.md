# McCauley Electrical Services – Brand Positioning

This document positions the relationship and any future brand chapter. The current
commission is the Automation Audit; nothing here is in scope until he commissions it.

## Positioning Statement

For intown and north-metro Atlanta homeowners who want electrical work done right
by the person who quoted it, McCauley Electrical is the 27-year owner-run
electrician whose president still performs every estimate and every installation
himself – because at this house, quality does not delegate.

## Brand Voice

- **Personality:** Warm, direct, craftsman-personal, quietly witty – "We'll Keep You
  Grounded" – and proud.
- **Tone range:** Conversational-professional. Never corporate, never salesy. He
  speaks as a neighbour who happens to be the best at this.
- **Language to embrace:** Owner-run. Done right. On time, cleaned up after. Since
  1999. The person who quotes it does the work.
- **Language to avoid:** Fleet language – technicians, dispatch, crews. Franchise
  superlatives. Urgency theatre. Anything a conglomerate call centre would say.

## Competitive Differentiation

- **Primary differentiator:** The owner is the product. Every estimate and every
  job is Patrick. The PE-backed alternatives sell scale; he sells the absence of it.
- **Proof points:** Uniform five-star sentiment on every platform sampled. BBB A+
  with a file open 27 years. A customer calling him "a unicorn in the home service
  provider industry". Same-day availability and return-at-no-charge stories in
  reviews.
- **Competitors:**
  1. BriteBox Electrical – Wrench Group, PE-backed, 2,239 Google reviews. Positions
     on scale, availability and booking convenience.
  2. TE Certified – claims 20,000+ reviews. Positions on process polish and
     multi-trade breadth.
  3. Mister Sparky Atlanta – franchise. Positions on punctuality branding.
  All three run live online booking. None can say the owner does the work.

## Content Pillars

Future lanes – activated only in a later chapter, with his direction.

1. **The craft:** What good electrical work actually looks like – safety,
   code-quality, the details a homeowner never sees. Sample topics: what a proper
   panel upgrade includes, why the cheap quote costs more.
2. **The neighbourhood:** Intown Atlanta identity – Morningside, Virginia-Highland,
   Midtown. Sample topics: old-home wiring stories, neighbourhood project features.
3. **Pride in the work:** The rainbow-stripe identity he holds personally, expressed
   publicly and authentically – community ties, OUT Georgia Business Alliance
   membership, the flag on the van as fact rather than campaign. Only with his
   explicit blessing; it is his identity, not our angle.

## Messaging Hierarchy

- **Headline level:** The electrician your neighbours actually recommend.
- **Subhead level:** Owner-run since 1999. Every job done by the person who quoted it.
- **Body level:** Specifics – services, guarantee behaviour, response norms, licence
  and insurance made visible.
- **CTA level:** Book him – once booking exists. Until then: call or write, one
  number, one form.
