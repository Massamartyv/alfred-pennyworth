# Nomad Express -- Brand Positioning

## Positioning Statement

For event producers, logistics coordinators, and enterprises who need freight that cannot fail, NOMAD EXPRESS is the elite event logistics and specialty freight operation that delivers on the biggest stages in the world, because they have done it seven Super Bowls and counting.

## Tagline Candidates

- "Freight that shows up when it matters most."
- "Elite logistics. Proven on the biggest stages."
- "Where it matters. When it matters."
- "Move with confidence."

## Brand Voice

- **Personality:** Confident, proven, professional, grounded
- **Tone:** Understated authority -- let credentials persuade
- **Language to embrace:** Direct, clear, action-oriented, specific credentials
- **Language to avoid:** Commodity language ("reliable, safe, on-time"), jargon, hype

## Competitive Differentiation

- **Primary differentiator:** Elite event logistics resume (Super Bowl x7, WrestleMania, FIFA, RNC)
- **Proof points:** National event credentials, broadcast infrastructure, temperature-controlled, one-of-a-kind art
- **Competitors:** Generic freight carriers with commodity positioning
- **Moat:** No one else has this resume at this scale from a single operator

## Typography

- **Headlines:** Bold geometric sans-serif (Aktiv Grotesk, Inter). ALL CAPS for impact, title case for sections.
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif at 16-18px
- **Accents:** Monospace or condensed for data points, DOT/MC numbers
