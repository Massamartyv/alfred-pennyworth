# Hibachi With A Twist – A Commission
### Five Points Digital Studio · The Stage The Twist Deserves

**What this is for**

You cook in front of people. The chef's table is the show, the twist is the signature, and the proof is already in the room every time the grill lights. What has been missing is the stage before the show – the place where Harris Health found you, where the next corporate client decides you are the one, where a family planning a birthday sees the fire before they ever smell it.

You have seen the new site on the private review link. This document names what was built, what it costs and what remains before it carries your name in public.

**What was commissioned**

A website built for your business alone – not a template wearing your colours.

- A home built around your own photography and film – your tables, your service, your events. The Harris Health celebration and the Commissioner Rodney Ellis service stand as corporate proof, named and real.
- Six menu pages carrying the full roster, with prices living where you decided they live – in the booking estimator and the menu documents, never loose on a page.
- A booking estimator that carries your exact business rules: protein pricing per person, the children's rate, deposit timing, travel by distance and the specialty roster by invitation only.
- A corporate flow that runs on purchase orders, the way your corporate clients actually buy.
- Performance engineering and a search foundation, so Houston finds the twist without being told the address.

**The commission**

Your commission is **$7,500, locked**. You were one of the first to trust this studio with your name, and the studio does not forget the first tables it served – this is your price, and it holds.

Included with the commission: two full rounds of revisions, thirty days of post-launch support, a training session so your team manages the site without a developer and your first three months of hosting and care included – valued at $1,500.

**The guarantee**

If the site does not score 80 or better on Google PageSpeed on mobile and desktop at launch, Five Points optimises until it does at no charge.

**Payment**

Half commissions the work – $3,750. The final half falls due at the handoff demonstration, once you have watched it work. We keep building and refining at no charge until the site does what it promised, or that final half is never owed.

**What remains before launch**

- Your Stripe account, so deposits land in your name from day one
- The calendar connection and booking delivery
- A proper portrait session and grill footage – the site is ready for them
- The domain cutover from the old template site, and the menu codes reissued

**The other price**

Doing nothing has a price too. The old template stays generic while the calendar fills by referral alone, and the corporate proof you have already earned – the county, the hospital system – sits invisible to every buyer searching for exactly this. What this studio builds, you own outright: the site, the system and the keys.

**Where to start**

Say the word and the remaining pieces go live in order. If anything in this document reads wrong, tell me plainly – that is useful too.
