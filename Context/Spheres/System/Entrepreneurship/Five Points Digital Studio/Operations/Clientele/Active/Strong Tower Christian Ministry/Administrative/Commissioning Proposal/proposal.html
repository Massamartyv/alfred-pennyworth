What You Asked

The conversation,
continued

Every Sunday at 10:30 am the doors on National Cemetery Road open, and within minutes a visitor feels the thing your own words promise: He Loves You to Life, So We Love You to Life. That welcome is the ministry’s gift. Nobody who walks in misses it.

The trouble is where people look first. Before a first-time visitor ever drives to the building, they search. And what the search returns today is a page whose newest sermon is four years old – a quiet room standing in front of a living church. The gap is nobody’s failure. Churches are built to shepherd people, not websites. But the gap is real, and it works against the ministry every single week, silently, among the very people most in need of finding you.

You also said something that shaped this document. You said Strong Tower intends to pay what the work is worth. That request honours the labourer, and it deserves a precise answer in return. So this document names everything: what has been built, what each part of it is worth at open-market rates and what Strong Tower is asked to invest. Nothing is vague, nothing is hidden and no number appears without its reason.

This is that answer, on paper.

The Build

What now stands

The new digital home of Strong Tower Christian Ministry is built and standing – designed from your own identity, tested and ready for its congregation. Four things about it matter most.

01

A sanctuary online

Tower blue and gold as light, your taglines carried through every page, scripture set with reverence. Type runs large and contrast runs high, so the elders of the congregation read as comfortably as the youth. It does not look like a website with a church’s name on it. It looks like Strong Tower.

02

A pulpit that reaches

Each new sermon appears on the site automatically, pulled from your YouTube channel with no staff effort at all – the four-year silence can never happen again. When the church streams, the site shows a live indicator and carries the visitor straight into the service.

03

The prayer door

A private prayer-request form delivers straight to the pastors and to no one else – a door a prophetic house should have. Beside it stand the giving page, the contact form, the newsletter and a Plan Your Visit page written for the person deciding whether to come this Sunday.

04

Your keys

Strong Tower owns this home outright – the site, its content and the code beneath it. After one training session your staff edit service times, events and pages themselves. If this studio disappeared tomorrow, the house would still stand and it would still be yours.

Most people will meet Strong Tower online before they ever walk through its doors. The website is the ministry’s first handshake – and now it feels like the church.

The Two Prices

What yes costs,
and what no costs

The price of yes

$7,500, once.

Half the ledger. The other $7,500 of worth is the studio’s own investment in this ministry, named plainly and given gladly. This is not a discount, because nothing was marked up. It is patronage, and it runs one direction only.

Paid as three payments of $2,500 – at the commissioning word, at staff training and on the Sunday the site goes live. The money moves at the rhythm of the work.

The promise that travels with it

At launch the site scores 80 or better on Google’s own speed measure, on phone and desktop, or the studio tunes it at no charge until it does. Thirty days of care, the training session and three months of hosting sit inside the price. Nothing bills quietly afterward.

The price of no

Declining costs nothing today. It costs what it has cost since 2022: each week, people in Florence search for a church, find a quiet page and keep scrolling. No invoice is written for them – which is what makes them expensive.

After launch

Nothing else is proposed today – no plans, no packages, no fine print. The commission is the website, whole and complete. Whatever the church may need in seasons to come, the studio remains a call away, and that conversation happens if and when Strong Tower opens it.

How this proceeds

The commission, drawn as one line
1

The walkthrough

I will call you this week and walk this document page by page – and walk you through the new site itself, live on screen. Bring every question. Take whatever time the church needs to pray on it.

2

The word

When Strong Tower says yes, it is confirmed in writing and the first payment of $2,500 commissions the launch. From that day the schedule is set and shared.

3

Training, then the cutover Sunday

Your staff are trained on the editing system, the final content is confirmed with you, and then strongtowercm.org points at the new home – timed so it can be announced from the pulpit. The old site retires the same day.

Strong Tower loved people to life for years without a website’s help. A site will never do the ministry’s work – it only opens the door so more people find the room where that work happens. However the church decides – it has been an honour to build for this house.
