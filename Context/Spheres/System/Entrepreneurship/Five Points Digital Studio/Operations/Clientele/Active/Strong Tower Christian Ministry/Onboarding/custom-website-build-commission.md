# Strong Tower Christian Ministry – A Commission
### Five Points Digital Studio · A Home Worthy Of The House

**What this is for**

Pastor Goodson, Prophetess Goodson – the ministry's word is "He Loves You to Life, So We Love You to Life," and a congregation's first visit now happens on a screen more often than at a door. The old site carried the address. The new one carries the welcome.

You have seen the rebuilt site on the private link. This document names what was built, what it costs and what remains before it stands at strongtowercm.org.

**What was commissioned**

A website built for Strong Tower alone.

- Eleven pages and growing – home, beliefs with the Confession of Faith, leadership with your full bios, watch, events, visit, get involved, give, prayer and contact.
- The ministries restored by name: SCORE, Is There Not a Cause, GEMS and GENTS, Sonship School of the Firstborn, Prophetic Impartation.
- Your own photography and films throughout – the sanctuary preaching, the church family, the worship band, the Sonship class, both leadership portraits and the crest at full resolution.
- Giving on your real rails: Realm, Cash App and text-to-give, exactly as the church already receives.
- A prayer presence on every page, delivered straight to the ministry inbox.
- Live-stream and sermon integration ready for keys, performance engineering and a search foundation, so Florence finds the tower.

**The commission**

The Custom Website Build for Strong Tower is **$7,500**.

Included with the commission: two full rounds of revisions, thirty days of post-launch support, a training session so the ministry team edits the site themselves and your first three months of hosting and care included – valued at $1,500.

**The return**

A church website earns its keep quietly. The giving rails stay open every day of the week – Realm, Cash App and text-to-give, one tap from any page – so faithfulness is never waiting on a Sunday. The first visit now begins before anyone reaches the door: a family searching Florence finds the tower, reads the welcome, watches the word and arrives already half at home. The ministries stop being a secret known only to those inside – SCORE, Sonship, GEMS and GENTS stand visible to every seeker who did not know to ask. One family finding home, one giver moving to the rails, one prayer reaching the inbox that would not have reached the door – the commission returns itself in the life of the house, and keeps returning long after the invoice is forgotten.

We build and refine until the site serves exactly that, and the payment terms below hold us to it.

**Payment**

Half commissions the work – $3,750. The final half falls due at the handoff demonstration, once you have watched it work. We keep building and refining at no charge until the site does what it promised, or that final half is never owed.

**What remains before launch**

- Your confirmation on the brand direction and the wordmark
- The content confirmations flagged through the site – history, leadership roster, visit details and pastoral approval of the doctrine pages
- The integration keys for live-stream, forms and newsletter
- The editing layer for your staff, and the cutover from Wix when you are ready

**The other price**

The old site keeps greeting every first-time seeker with a dated welcome while the real life of the house – the ministries, the word, the family – stays invisible until someone walks in. What this studio builds, the church owns outright: the site, the content and the keys. No platform holds it hostage.

**Where to start**

Say the word and the remaining confirmations go in order. Anything that reads wrong in this document, tell me plainly – the house should recognise itself in every line.
