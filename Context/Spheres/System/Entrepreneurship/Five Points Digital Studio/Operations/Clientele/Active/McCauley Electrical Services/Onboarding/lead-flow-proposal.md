# McCauley Electrical Services – A Proposal
### Five Points Digital Studio · More Than One Man

**What this is for**

You keep the business small, but you do not want it small forever. You told me
the truth of it plainly: you want the phone ringing more than one man can
answer – because the day the work outgrows you is the day hiring stops being a
gamble and becomes the obvious next move. Not a busier week. A business that
has outgrown one man.

That is the only outcome this proposal serves.

**How demand actually gets built**

Demand like that does not arrive by accident – it is engineered, and it has
two halves: creating the demand, and catching it so none of it leaks. We build
the first and secure the second, in that order, so every new call has
somewhere to land. Right now the demand is out there searching – "electrician
near me," "Buckhead electrician" – and it is not finding you. You are absent
from the map pack and from every "best electrician" round-up we checked. Your
reviews are flawless and nearly invisible: a handful against 2,239 for the
private-equity brand in your metro. Reviews are not vanity here – they are how
the search decides who to show.

Three ways in. Enter through the Foundation, add builds as you grow, or
commission the whole engine and own it.

---

### 1 – The Foundation · $1,970

The front door, and a number set for you alone. Three things, coupled:

- **The Audit – valued at $1,000, included.** Where your demand hides, where it
  leaks and what to build first – your whole pipeline read the way a new
  customer sees it.
- **The Command Session.** Your workflow mapped one-to-one, and a written plan
  you keep whatever you decide next.
- **Your First Build.** Commissioned on the call and delivered – the first
  piece of the engine, live.

Half commissions the Foundation, half at the demonstration once your first
build is shown working.

### 2 – A La Carte Builds · priced per project

Fixed-price projects, yours to own outright – an asset on your books, never a
rented seat on ours. Where a build runs on a tool, the account is in your name
and your keys.

- **The Booking Line – $1,000.** Booking live on your site, Google profile
  claimed and finished, every listing corrected so you are found and bookable
  everywhere a customer looks.
- **Missed-Call Rescue and Reviews – $1,500.** Every missed call texted back
  inside a minute; every finished job asked for the review you have earned a
  thousand times and rarely collected. Your ranking fuel, finally compounding.
- **The Local Search Build – $2,000.** Your neighborhood pages built to rank
  for "electrician near me," so new work finds you in the map pack instead of
  the conglomerate. This is new demand, not just caught demand.

**Payment.** Half commissions any build; the final half falls due at the
handoff demonstration, once you have watched it work. We keep building and
refining at no charge until the machinery does what it promised – or that final
half is never owed.

**The Care Plan – $500 a month, optional.** Monitoring, monthly optimisation
and a readout, review management, local-search upkeep, priority support and
small improvements. Start, pause or cancel it whenever you like; the builds are
yours either way.

### 3 – The Growth Engine · from $7,500, owned

Every piece above, engineered as one integrated system and handed to you as an
asset – the machine that makes you more than one man can serve. Owned, not
rented; like a good van, you buy it once and come back only for service. Half
commissions it, half at the demonstration. The optional Care Plan – $500 a
month – keeps it sharp and cancels whenever you like. Scoped and structured in
your Foundation session.

**The other price**

Doing nothing has a price too. The demand you want is being taken right now by
the brand that out-reviewed you 2,239 to a handful – not the better
electrician, just the one the search shows. Every month without an engine of
your own is another month of that. What this studio builds, you own. What a
retainer rents you is gone the day you stop paying.

**Where to start**

Book the working call and we will walk your day together – it doubles as your
command session. If a piece reads right, we build it. If none of it does, tell
