# McCauley Electrical Services – Client Brief

## Current State

Listed on Yelp, Angi, Houzz, Nextdoor and BBB. No online booking anywhere. No reviews surfaced on his own site. Absent from every independent "best electrician" round-up checked, including Midtown where he holds a dedicated landing page.

- **What has been tried:** Roughly $2,000 spent on an agency or website with no return. What it bought is undiagnosed – open question. He is a burned, sceptical buyer.

- **Success metrics:** His own definition was not captured on the call – open question. Working proxies: overflow calls captured rather than lost, review velocity, booked-calendar consistency.
