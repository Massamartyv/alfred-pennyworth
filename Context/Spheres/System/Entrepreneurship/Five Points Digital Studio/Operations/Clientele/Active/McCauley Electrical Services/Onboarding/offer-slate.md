# McCauley Electrical Services – Approved Offer Slate

Three client-scoped offers derived from catalogue components (Rapid Site Build
5.2-S, Automation Build 2.1-G, Site Care 1.4-S) under the operator's directive
of 2026-07-10: budget band $1,000-$3,000, ease into a cold relationship, prove
the standard, lose no money. Built through the offer-creator framework;
approved at the revised Direction gate. This file is the pricing source of
truth for every client-facing artefact in the mission. Stripe registration
deferred until commitment, per operator ruling.

## The Three Doors

### 1. The Open Door – $1,000, five business days

- Online booking live on the existing WordPress site via the field-service
  platform he subscribes to directly ($59-79/month, his keys, named plainly)
- Google Business Profile claimed, verified and completed, booking link attached
- NAP repair on his own properties: one phone number sitewide, hours
  reconciled, licence number displayed, www canonical fix
- Third-party listing corrections delivered as a documented checklist
- **Guarantee (make-good):** booking taking real appointments within five
  business days or the work continues free until it is
- Labour estimate ~6 hours at the $100/hr benchmark; ~40% margin

### 2. The Capture Engine – $2,000, ten business days

- Everything in The Open Door, plus:
- Missed-call text-back configured and demonstrated live
- Automated post-job review request wired to Google
- Third-party listing corrections executed (Yelp, Nextdoor, Houzz, BBB)
- **Guarantee (make-good):** missed calls texted back inside a minute and
  completed jobs triggering review asks, shown working at handoff, or the
  building continues free
- Labour estimate ~12 hours; ~40% margin

### 3. The Full Calendar – $3,000, ten business days plus 30-day care window

- Everything in The Capture Engine, plus:
- 30 days of post-launch care (monitoring and adjustments)
- Community layer executed: OUT Georgia Business Alliance solopreneur
  enrolment ($150/yr membership fee is his) with directory profile built,
  LGBTBE certification application prepared ($899 documented value), Nextdoor
  business profile optimised
- Identity made visible: tasteful rainbow detail on the site with his approval
- **Guarantee:** as The Capture Engine, plus the care window
- Labour estimate ~19 hours; ~37% margin

## Scope Constraints – client-expressed

- No full website rebuild. Patrick said so on the call; every door works with
  the existing WordPress site. The Site Rebuild was removed from the
  proposal's later-chapters table on 2026-07-10 accordingly.
- Hosting provider and platform tier unknown. Identified at the working call
  before commissioning. If the plan tier blocks on-page embeds, the booking
  path delivers as a platform-hosted booking page linked prominently from the
  site and the Google profile - the make-good guarantee covers either route.

## Payment Terms – operator ruling 2026-07-10

Pay-at-demonstration, every door. Nothing is charged to commission: the door
is chosen on the working call and confirmed in writing, the studio builds at
its own risk, and payment collects in full at the handoff demonstration once
the machinery is shown working. Ruled by the operator at the art-direction
session in answer to the behavioural finding that an open-ended make-good
with money already taken invites the "is there a stopping point" objection.
The studio's exposure is bounded by the labour estimates below; the platform
subscription is client-direct from day one, so no third-party cost is fronted.

## Doctrine Compliance

- Suite guarantee doctrine (2026-07-09): all three doors price above $497, so
  guarantees are make-good service, never monetary refunds. Pay-at-
  demonstration strengthens compliance - no money is held that could ever
  need refunding before the machinery works. Compliant.
- Two Prices rule: the inaction ledger rides in the proposal - missed calls
  lost for good (roughly a third per the cited CallSource study), each a
  $1,000-4,500 lifetime relationship, against a $2,500-monthly rented
  alternative.
- Later chapters named transparently, commissioned only if earned: Deeper
  Build from $5,000 (2.1-G floor), Care Plan $500/month (1.4-S). The Site
  Rebuild (5.2-S, $2,500) is deliberately absent - client-expressed no-rebuild
  constraint.

Booking capability competitors carry on five-figure sites; reputation
mechanics agencies rent at $300-500 monthly; an $899 certification unlocked by
a $150 membership; a platform he owns outright. Perceived stack $6,000-10,000
against a $1,000-3,000 ask - clears the 10x bar at every door against the
value equation.
