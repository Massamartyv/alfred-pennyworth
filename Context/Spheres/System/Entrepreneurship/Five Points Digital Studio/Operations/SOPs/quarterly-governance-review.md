# OPS-006 – Quarterly Governance Review

Executes the governance review defined in `Foundation/asset-governance-doctrine.md`. One review per Evolution principal per quarter.

## Trigger

- Quarterly, per principal, anchored to the commissioning date – not the calendar quarter – so each principal's review falls on their own anniversary rhythm.
- The quarterly heartbeat surfaces which principals are due in the coming month.

## Inputs

- The commissioning document for the engagement – the named expectations are the yardstick.
- Live system data: analytics, CRM, automation logs, whatever instruments the commissioned asset carries.
- Competitor set as named at commissioning or in the discovery dossier; refresh if the market has moved.

## Steps

1. **Cut the data.** Pull the quarter's numbers from the asset's own instruments. Note the pull date and source for every figure.
2. **Reconcile before reporting.** Verify each headline number against a second source – platform analytics against raw logs, CRM counts against inbox reality, revenue attribution against Stripe. Any figure that cannot be reconciled is flagged, not reported as fact. This is the data integrity pillar in practice.
3. **Run the success analysis.** Take the expectations named in the commissioning document one by one. Mark each satisfied or not, with the evidence beside it. No hedged middle column.
4. **Run the competitive benchmark.** Place the principal's position against the named competitor set – search visibility, response speed, conversion posture, whatever dimensions the engagement was commissioned to move.
5. **Assemble the review document.** A4, house register, commissioning-document treatment. Structure: the quarter in one page, the success analysis, the integrity notes, the benchmark, the recommended calibrations for the coming quarter.
6. **Reviewer pass.** The document clears the brand quality gate and a scrutiny pass before it reaches the principal – every number traced, every claim evidenced.
7. **Deliver as a reading.** Schedule the review call. The document is walked through live, not emailed cold. The recommended calibrations become the working agenda for the next quarter.
8. **File the record.** Review document filed under the principal's folder in `Operations/Clientele/Active/{Principal}/Governance Reviews/`. Outcomes and follow-on work logged as Tasks in the Five Points Notion workspace against the engagement's project record.

## Decision points

- **An expectation is not satisfied.** The review says so plainly and names the calibration. Composure is the reassurance – no apology theatre, no burying the miss.
- **Data cannot be reconciled.** The discrepancy is itself a finding and leads the integrity section. Never present an unreconciled number as fact.
- **The benchmark reveals a competitor pulling ahead.** Surface it with a recommended response. Decision Default 13 governs the register – describe what the studio will build, never editorialise about the competitor.
- **The review surfaces work beyond governance scope.** Quote it separately under the private menu discipline. The review is never a sales document; the recommendation earns the quote conversation.

## Escalation

- Any finding that threatens the asset itself – security exposure, platform deprecation, data loss risk – goes to the founder same-day, ahead of the review cycle.
- A second consecutive quarter with the same expectation unsatisfied escalates to the founder for a scope or architecture decision before the review is delivered.

## Success criteria

- Every Evolution principal receives four reviews per year, none skipped.
- Every reported number carries a verified source.
- Every expectation from the commissioning document is dispositioned – satisfied or not – with evidence.
- The principal leaves the reading knowing exactly what the governance fee governed this quarter.
