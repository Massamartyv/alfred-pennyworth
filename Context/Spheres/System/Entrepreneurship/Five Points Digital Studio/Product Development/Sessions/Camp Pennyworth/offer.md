## Ethos

Ratified by the operator on 8 July 2026. This is the governing idea of the open cohort; all collateral derives from it.

> The car replaced no one's mind – it asked only that you learn to drive. Camp Pennyworth exists for the distance between passenger and driver: three hours, and the technology stops happening to you and starts answering to you.

Ratified alongside it on 8 July 2026, the outcome line – the masthead claim of every selling surface:

> Artificial intelligence. Real freedom.

The governing line states the mechanism; the outcome line states what the mechanism buys. Commercial surfaces lead with the outcome; the room leads with the mechanism. A surface carries the governing line at most once, as its depth beat, never beside the outcome line at equal volume.

### Lineage

The fingerprint essence reads "An instrument tuned to one mind. Drafted by hand. Calibrated until it sings." (Foundation/brand-fingerprint.md, Layer 1). The camp is that sentence taught rather than delivered: one instance, tuned to one life – the attendee learns to draft their own. The construction "exists for the distance between" is deliberately shared with the Command Session ethos; the two rooms speak as siblings under one masthead. Where the team room speaks ensemble, the open room speaks the road.

The outcome line works the hinge inside the technology's own name: the thing everyone calls artificial produces the thing no one would call fake. "Freedom" names the third of the Life-Force 8 desires – freedom from fear and pain – which is the same desire the Foundation block serves pedagogically. The page and the room make one claim in two registers.

### Usage rules

1. **The idea governs; the image stays disciplined.** Two touches maximum per artifact: one planted passage, one echo. The Foundation block is the one place the car runs at full length.
2. **The fear is named universally, never diagnosed in the reader.** "Every revolution reads as a threat" is a fact about revolutions, not an observation about the prospect.
3. **The umbrella word is Foundation-block and spoken material.** In print it lives on Card Zero and nowhere else.
4. **Banned adjacent vocabulary:** future-proof, superpower, 10x, robots, magic, and the cliche "AI won't take your job – someone using AI will." The room expects the cliche; the camp never says it.
5. **Native vocabulary:** tool, drive, wheel, destination, edges (for limitations), tuned (family term, always available), freedom (outcome word, governed by rule 6).
6. **"Freedom" is governed like the car.** The outcome line carries it; beyond that, at most one echo per surface. Overuse converts a claim into a poster.
7. **The selling register: Whitman psychology in fingerprint voice.** Pain named before promise. Extreme specificity – real tasks, real hours, named moments. The mental movie runs in second person: the reader sees themselves using the tool, not admiring it. Never urgency theatre – no countdown clocks, no fear bullets, no hype typography. Precision is the pressure.
8. **The Socratic open.** Every selling surface opens with the room's own question – "What do you wish something else did for you?" – or a pain question in its lineage. The reader answers before the price appears; the page mirrors the room, and the buyer talks themselves toward the seat.
9. **Aspirin, not vitamin.** Sell relief from present pain – the fear itself, the falling-behind, the hours bleeding into admin – never enrichment for someday. On every offer surface the cost of no stands beside the cost of yes, per the house Two Prices rule.
10. **The consumer register (added 11 July 2026).** Selling copy carries zero technical vocabulary: no "knowledge base", no "context architecture", no "instruction files", no "agents". The capability is named by what it does for the reader – it learns who you are, work that runs without you – never by what it is called in the trade.
