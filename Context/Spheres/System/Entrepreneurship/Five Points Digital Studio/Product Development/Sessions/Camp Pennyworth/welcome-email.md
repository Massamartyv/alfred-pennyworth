# Camp Pennyworth – Welcome email

Sent to each registrant after a seat is bought. It confirms the seat and
carries the link to the arrival intake form (`/arrival`). Voice: the camp
register – British spelling, no contractions, warm and plain.

The link carries a short session code so the session is recorded on the form
automatically:

- Saturday 8 August 2026 → `https://workshop.fivepoints.studio/arrival?s=0808`
- Saturday 15 August 2026 → `https://workshop.fivepoints.studio/arrival?s=0815`
- Saturday 22 August 2026 → `https://workshop.fivepoints.studio/arrival?s=0822`

Merge fields: `{{first_name}}`, `{{session_date}}`, `{{arrival_link}}`.

---

**Subject:** Your seat at Camp Pennyworth – one thing before Saturday

**Preheader:** Two minutes that shape the session around your week.

---

{{first_name}},

Your seat is held. {{session_date}} – 2:00 to 5:00 pm ET, live over video.

Camp Pennyworth does not open with a slide. It opens with you – the thing you
wish something else did for you. So before you arrive, tell me where you are
coming from and what you are hoping to walk out with. Two or three minutes, and
it is the difference between a session built on a generic room and one built on
your actual week.

Bring exactly that → {{arrival_link}}

The joining link reaches you the day before. The recording is yours to keep, and
the Campground – the alumni room – opens to you after.

See you Saturday.

Martavious
Five Points · hello@fivepoints.studio
