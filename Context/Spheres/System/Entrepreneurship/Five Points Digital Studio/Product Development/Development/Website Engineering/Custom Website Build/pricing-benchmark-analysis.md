## Recommendation

Commit the Gold build at **$11,500, one number, range retired**. Flex band $9,500 to $12,500. Revisit trigger: proposal win rate across the next six to eight Gold proposals – below the historical close floor, step to $9,500 before touching anything else; at or above it, probe $12,500 and upward.

Scope variability, which the old range existed to absorb, moves out of the price and into named additions – priced explicitly per the Asset Principle. The base commission is one number; additions are their own line items.

## The numbers – benchmark proxies

| Proxy | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| PMC* – credibility floor | ~$6,000 | Below this, published agency floors give way to the freelancer band; a studio commission claim stops being believable |
| IPP* – market normal | ~$9,000–$10,000 | Midpoint of the published $5,000–$15,000 band for custom 10–20 page agency builds |
| PME* – structural ceiling | ~$15,000 | Top of the standard SMB custom band before e-commerce or application complexity justifies more |
| Boutique extension | to $35,000+ | Boutique studios publish $6,000–$35,000+; the marque path runs here, not in the mid-market band |

Current price sits $7,500–$12,000: the floor enters at 25% below market normal – a mid-market signal in conflict with the marque position and with the stated intent to move upmarket.

## Structural readings

- **The floor is the defect, not the ceiling.** $7,500 buys entry below IPP* for a scope that includes 15 pages, CMS, AI features, a performance guarantee and $2,000 of stated bonus value. The market reads price as quality signal in exactly this band.
- **Cost floor supports the move.** BLS puts the web developer median at roughly $44 per hour (~$91,000 per year). A four-to-eight week engagement at 80–140 loaded production hours implies a $5,000–$9,000 internal economic cost at market labour rates. At $7,500 the engagement flirts with market-rate break-even; at $11,500 every dollar above the old floor is contribution.
- **Capacity to pay clears comfortably above $1M revenue.** Marketing budget norms run 7–8% of gross revenue (SBA guidance) to 7.7–9.4% (Gartner, Deloitte/Duke CMO surveys). A $1M–$5M firm carries a $70,000–$400,000 annual marketing budget; an $11,500 one-time build, amortised over a three-to-four year site life, is 1–4% of one year's budget. Census-derived counts put over 90% of US small businesses under $1M revenue – the screener's $250,000 floor and the ICP's gravity toward $1M+ firms are what make this price structurally affordable to the market actually served.
- **Segment separation is plausible but unmeasured.** The boutique band running to $35,000 suggests the over-$5M segment prices in a different register entirely. Only the fielded study settles whether Gold is one market or two.

## By sales motion

Sales-led – discovery calls and proposals. The lens is revenue-max toward the ceiling, not the volume plateau: a human on the call absorbs the hesitation between IPP* and PME*. $11,500 sits in the upper half of the market band, below the structural ceiling, with negotiation give built in above the $9,500 flex floor. The four upmarket returns all point the same way: better clients, better margin, less headache and the price itself signalling which businesses the studio is for.

## Caveats

- **Benchmarks are a prior, not a measurement.** Nothing here observed a buyer's stated threshold. The proxies substitute market publication data for respondent curves; the fielded study replaces them the day responses land.
- **Tier 3 dominance.** The going-rate picture rests on commercial price guides. Tier 1 grounds only the wage floor, budget norms and firm counts.
- **The receipts gap.** Census SUSB receipts data publish only in Economic Census years; the firm-count claim rides an aggregator. Pull SUSB directly before this analysis supports anything higher-stakes than a price commit with a win-rate revisit trigger.
- **No contribution optimum.** Internal delivery cost under the AI-assisted build model was not formally supplied; the cost-floor logic uses market labour rates, which overstate the studio's true marginal cost. The real contribution case for $11,500 is stronger than stated here.
