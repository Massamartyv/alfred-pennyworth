# Human Construct Evolution — Ongoing Offer Architecture

**Offer Classification:** Add-on to Offer 2.4 (Human Construct) within the Consulting and Strategy pillar of Five Points Digital Studio

**Positioning:** The system you built should never stop growing. Human Construct Evolution ensures the Construct gets smarter, more integrated and more attuned to the client over time. This is not maintenance. This is not support. This is the continued engineering of a system that evolves at the same rate the client's life evolves.

**Eligibility:** Available to any Human Construct client at Silver tier and above. Bronze clients receive an architecture document, not a built system, and therefore have nothing to evolve.

**Core Framing:** "You just invested $7,500 to $50,000 in a personal AI operating system. Without Evolution, that system starts drifting from your reality within 90 days. Platforms update. Your life changes. New tools emerge. Evolution ensures your Construct never depreciates."

---

## The Decay Problem

AI platforms ship updates constantly. Claude releases new capabilities. MCP connections change. Notion evolves. The client's ventures pivot, their team grows, their priorities shift, their communication patterns change. Without active evolution, the system the client paid for begins to feel stale within a quarter. Within six months, it feels like last year's phone. Within a year, it feels like legacy software.

The alternative to Evolution is paying for another full build engagement every 12 to 18 months to catch up. Evolution is a fraction of that cost and keeps the system current in real time.

**The anchor:** A full-time chief of staff or executive assistant costs $80,000 to $150,000 per year. A technical AI consultant bills $200 to $400 per hour. Evolution delivers elements of both at a fraction of the cost.

---

## What Is Included

### Monthly Deliverables

- **System health check and platform update sweep:** Five Points reviews the entire Construct for breaking changes, deprecated integrations, new platform capabilities worth incorporating, and general system hygiene. Delivered as a brief written summary.
- **Two new or refined skills per month:** New skills built to address emerging needs in the client's life and work, or existing skills refined based on observed usage patterns and client feedback.
- **One recalibration session (30 minutes, virtual):** A live conversation to realign the system with how the client's life has shifted. New ventures, new priorities, new tools, new patterns. This is the session that keeps the Construct feeling current.
- **Priority async support:** 24-hour response time on business days for any system questions, issues, or requests.

### Quarterly Deliverables

- **Full system audit with written report:** A comprehensive review of the entire Construct. What is working, what is underused, what needs attention, what new opportunities exist. Delivered as a documented report.
- **Integration expansion:** One new tool or connection added to the system per quarter. As the client's stack evolves, the Construct evolves with it.
- **Wrapper identity refinement:** The wrapper personality is recalibrated based on observed usage patterns. The way the client interacts with the system three months in is different from how they interacted at launch. The wrapper should reflect that growth.

## Guarantee

If at any point during the engagement the client feels the system is not meaningfully better than it was the previous month, Five Points will conduct an additional recalibration session and deliver two bonus skills at no charge to course correct. The commitment is simple: every month, the Construct gets better. If it does not, Five Points makes it right.

## Signing Bonus

Clients who commit to Human Construct Evolution at the time of project handoff (before the post-handoff support window expires) receive one free wrapper identity refresh per year for the duration of the Evolution agreement. This is a full re-engineering of the wrapper personality, not a minor tweak. It allows the client to evolve their Construct's identity as they evolve. New chapter, new energy, same infrastructure.

**Value of the bonus:** A standalone wrapper identity build is valued at $2,500 based on the engineering hours involved. Annual clients receive this every year they remain active.

## Upsell Path

Evolution clients are natural candidates for:

- **Additional Human Construct builds:** A client who loves their personal Construct may want a dedicated system for a specific venture, a family office, or a creative project. Each is scoped as a new project engagement.
- **AI Fluency Workshop (Pillar 1):** Deepens the client's understanding of the tools under the hood. Recommended annually as the AI landscape shifts.
- **Automation Build Out (Offer 2.1 Gold):** The client's business operations may benefit from the same engineering philosophy applied at the organizational level.
- **Content Engine (Pillar 3):** The Construct's skill library can be extended to power content creation workflows, making Five Points content services more integrated.

## Operational Notes

**Time budget per client per month:** Approximately four to six hours of founder time. This includes the health check (30 minutes), two skill builds (60 to 90 minutes each), the recalibration session (30 minutes), async support (variable), and quarterly deliverables amortized across the quarter.

**Scalability:** The monthly health check and platform sweep can be partially systematized as the client base grows. Skill builds get faster as the template library expands. The recalibration session remains high-touch and founder-delivered. At 10 active Evolution clients, the retainer book consumes approximately 50 to 60 hours per month of founder time. That is roughly 30 to 35% of a 10-to-5 workday schedule.

**Capacity design:** The solo-delivery configuration serves 12 to 15 active Evolution clients. The book scales past that by design – contractor pods absorb health checks and skill builds, and the template library compresses delivery hours every quarter. Capacity is a hiring decision, not a limit of the model.

**Profitability:** At $3,000 per month and four to six hours of delivery, the effective hourly rate is $500 to $750. At the annual rate of $2,250 per month, the effective rate is $375 to $562. Both exceed the $100/hour labor rate and clear the 75% gross margin floor comfortably.

**Retention target:** 80% annual retention. If 10 clients sign Evolution in year one, eight should renew into year two. That is $216,000 to $288,000 in recurring revenue entering year two before any new clients are acquired.
