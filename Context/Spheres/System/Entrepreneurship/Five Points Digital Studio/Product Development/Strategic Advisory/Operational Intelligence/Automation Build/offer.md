## Pricing

**Price:** $5,000 to $10,000 per engagement depending on complexity (project based)

## Format

Five Points builds and deploys two to five automation workflows over four to six weeks

## What Is Included

- Discovery and scoping (included, not billed separately): $1,500 to $2,500 value
- Full build of agreed upon automations using Claude scheduled tasks and native LLM orchestration, with third party platforms (Make.com or equivalent) deployed only where no native option exists or the client requests it
- Testing, quality assurance, and deployment
- Team training session (60 minutes virtual) on how to monitor and maintain the automations (valued at 750 dollars)
- 30 day post launch support window (valued at 1,000 dollars)
- Documentation: Standard Operating Procedures for each automation

**Total Stated Value:** 10,750 to 15,750 dollars for a 5,000 to 10,000 dollar engagement

## Bonus

One emergency rebuild within 90 days if any automation breaks due to platform changes or integration issues (valued at 1,500 dollars).

## Guarantee

Each automation will perform as specified in the scope document. If it does not within 14 days of deployment, we fix it at no charge. If we cannot fix it, we refund the proportional cost of that specific automation.

## Upsell Path

Monthly Automation Oversight Retainer (Offer 1.3) or Platinum ecosystem engagement.
