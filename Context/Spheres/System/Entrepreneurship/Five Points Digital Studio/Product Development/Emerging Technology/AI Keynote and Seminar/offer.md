## Pricing

**Price:** $3,500 minimum for a keynote or seminar (60 to 90 minutes)

This is not a tiered offer. It is a flat rate engagement with variable pricing based on audience size, customization required, and travel requirements. The floor is $3,500.

## Format

In person or virtual, conferences, corporate events, industry associations

## What Is Included at Base Rate

- 60 to 90 minute keynote or seminar on AI fluency for business leaders
- Customized slide deck
- Attendee takeaway: digital AI Starter Kit

## Upsell Path

**Conference Pipeline Package:** For an additional $2,000 on top of the keynote fee, Five Points sets up a registration page for a Bronze workshop targeted to the audience, runs it within 30 days of the event, and shares revenue on any Silver or Gold conversions. The event promoter gets additional value for their audience. Five Points gets a pipeline event with zero customer acquisition cost.
