# 1.1-S — AI Ascent Lab

**Pillar:** 01 Emerging Technology
**Parent Offer:** AI Workshop Series
**Tier:** Silver (Done With You)
**Stripe Name:** 1.1 — AI Ascent Lab — Silver

---

## Pricing

**Price:** $1,500 (one time) or $2,500 for teams of up to five

## Format

Four virtual sessions over two weeks, 90 minutes each, small group (max eight businesses)

## What Is Included

- Session 1: AI Fluency Framework deep dive plus personal workflow mapping
- Session 2: Identifying and prioritizing the highest impact AI workflows in each participant's business
- Session 3: Hands on build session where participants begin constructing their first automation using Claude scheduled tasks and native LLM tooling as the intelligence and orchestration layer.
- Session 4: Review, troubleshoot, and optimize what was built. Roadmap for next 90 days.
- Private group chat access for duration of the sprint (Slack or similar)
- AI Implementation Playbook (digital workbook with templates and decision frameworks)

## Bonus

Each participant receives a custom AI Opportunity Map. A visual document showing the top 10 workflows in their business ranked by impact, feasibility, and AI readiness. Built during sessions and refined by the Five Points team between sessions. This bonus is valued at 1,500 dollars based on the consulting hours involved.

Additionally, participants receive a First 48 Hours checklist: five things they can do with their new workflow immediately after Session 1 to compress time to value.

## Guarantee

If you complete all four sessions and do not have at least one functional AI powered workflow running in your business, Five Points conducts a private one on one session to get you there at no charge.

## Total Value

Total stated value: $3,000+ for $1,500

## Upsell Path

Gold tier (the team builds out the remaining workflows identified on the AI Opportunity Map).
