# Product Development – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Product Development department owns offers, tiers, bundles, offer strategy and audits, and the venture's own products and builds. This is the source of truth for what Five Points sells. Carries forward the offer-architecture ground of the former Growth department.

See venture-level `Agents/department-heads.md` for the Head of Product Development role definition and specialist seats.

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Product Development** | Department head. Owns the offer suite and its integrity across the ladder. | All | – |
| **Offer Steward** | Offer catalogue maintenance, pricing integrity, tier evolution. | Critique, Release | Researcher, Reviewer:Scrutiny |

### Role activation

Offer reference lookups need no dispatch. Offer catalogue changes activate Head of Product Development + Offer Steward.
