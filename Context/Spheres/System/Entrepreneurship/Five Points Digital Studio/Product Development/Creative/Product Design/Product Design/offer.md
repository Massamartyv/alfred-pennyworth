## Pricing

**Price:** $8,000 to $15,000 (scoped per project)

## Format

Six to 10 week engagement

## What Is Included

- User research and persona development
- User flow mapping
- Wireframes and prototyping
- Full UI design (design system, components, all screens)
- Usability testing coordination
- Developer handoff package (Figma with specs)

## Bonus

Developer handoff QA session. A live call with your development team to walk through the handoff package and answer implementation questions, reducing the gap between design intent and built product (valued at 1,000 dollars).

## Guarantee

If the usability testing does not validate at least 80% of the design decisions, Five Points redesigns the flagged components at no charge.
