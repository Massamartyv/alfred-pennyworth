# Growth

Sales, partnerships, venue sourcing, distribution pipeline and guest acquisition. Growth absorbs the former Business Development and Marketing & Sales departments into a single revenue-generating studio. It owns who finds Lillie and Lynette, who Lillie and Lynette partners with and where the venture opens its doors.

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Venue sourcing – property scouting, real estate relationships, location due diligence
- Partnership pipeline – brand collaborations, co-branded events, complementary hospitality brands
- Event and corporate sales – private events, buyouts, buyout season planning
- Guest acquisition – paid media, PR, referral programmes, loyalty
- Distribution surfaces – OpenTable, Resy, Google Business, directories and aggregators
- Investor and funding relationships

### What does not belong here

- Day-to-day service delivery – Operations
- Service and experience design – Creative
- Revenue tracking and financial reporting – Finance
- Legal terms of partnership agreements – Administration
