# Business Development -- Lillie and Lynette

Partnerships, venue sourcing, vendor relationships and pipeline development. The engine that opens doors and builds the network the company needs to launch and grow.

### What Belongs Here

- Partnership and collaboration pipeline
- Venue scouting and real estate evaluation
- Vendor qualification and relationship management
- Strategic alliances -- co-branding, joint ventures
- Investor and funding relationships
- Market expansion strategy

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Day-to-day service delivery -- that goes in Operations
- Service design and menu development -- that goes in Product Development
- Revenue tracking -- that goes in Finances

### Key Relationships to Develop

| Type | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Venue partners | Landlords, property developers, real estate agents | Pipeline to be built |
| Vendors | Food suppliers, equipment, design firms | Pipeline to be built |
| Strategic partners | Complementary hospitality brands, local businesses | Pipeline to be built |
| Investors | Capital partners for growth phases | Pipeline to be built |

### Current State

No active business development pipeline. Concept definition comes first, then targeted outreach begins.
