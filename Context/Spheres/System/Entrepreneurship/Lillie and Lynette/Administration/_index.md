# Administration -- Lillie and Lynette

Legal, compliance, licensing, policies and brand protection. The structural backbone that keeps the venture legally sound and operationally clean.

### What Belongs Here

- Business entity documents and formation records
- Trademark and intellectual property filings
- Hospitality licensing -- food service, liquor, health permits
- Insurance policies and liability coverage
- Zoning and regulatory compliance
- Privacy policies and guest data handling

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Financial records -- those go in Finances
- Service design documents -- those go in Product Development
- Partnership agreements with revenue terms -- those go in Business Development

### Subdirectories

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| Compliance/ | Health and safety regulations, hospitality-specific compliance |
| Identity/ | Entity documents, formation records |
| Legal/ | Contracts, licensing, IP filings |
| Policies/ | Internal policies, guest policies, data handling |

### Current State

Pre-formation. Entity structure, licensing requirements and compliance framework to be defined as the concept solidifies.
