# Administration

Legal, licensing, food and beverage compliance, insurance and HR. The structural backbone. In hospitality, the regulatory surface is unusually wide – food safety, alcohol, health, zoning, signage, labour, accessibility – and Administration is the studio that keeps all of it clean. Administration absorbs the former Human Resources department under `HR/`.

## Structure

```
Administration/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Legal/                 -- Contracts, leases, IP filings, NDAs, service agreements
  Licensing/             -- Food service, liquor, health, entertainment, zoning, signage
  Compliance/            -- Health code, ADA, labour law, alcohol regulation, data protection
  Insurance/             -- General liability, liquor liability, property, workers compensation
  Identity/              -- Entity documents, formation records
  Policies/              -- Internal policies, guest policies, data handling
  HR/                    -- Team, contractors, hiring plans, culture
  Agents/                -- Studio-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Business entity documents and formation records
- Trademark and intellectual property filings
- Hospitality licensing – food service, liquor, health permits, entertainment, zoning, signage
- Insurance policies and liability coverage specific to hospitality
- Regulatory compliance (health code, labour law, alcohol regulation, ADA, data protection)
- Privacy policies and guest data handling
- HR – team structure, hiring plans, contractor agreements, culture documentation

### What does not belong here

- Financial records – Finance
- Service and menu design – Creative
- Partnership revenue terms – Growth
- Vendor and procurement workflow – Operations

## Key Context

- No legal entity filed yet. Structure to be determined as the concept and venue strategy solidify.
- Hospitality licensing is jurisdictional – requirements shift by city and state and must be evaluated per venue.
- Liquor licensing in particular can be a long lead time. It must be flagged in venue due diligence.
- HR is a solo operation at present – future roles tracked in `HR/_index.md`.

## Agent Instructions

- All legal document creation requires human review before filing or execution.
- Licensing applications require approval before submission (Tier 3).
- Compliance checks against health code, labour law and alcohol regulation are Tier 2 (execute then notify).
- HR decisions (hires, contractor terms) are Tier 3 (approval required).
- Never modify or submit anything that affects regulatory standing without approval.
