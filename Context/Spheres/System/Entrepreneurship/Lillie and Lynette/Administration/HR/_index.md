# HR – Lillie and Lynette

Team, contractors, hiring plans and company culture. The people who will bring the hospitality vision to life. HR sits under Administration in the seven-studio model.

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Organisational structure and role definitions
- Hiring plans and timelines
- Contractor and freelancer agreements
- Training programmes, onboarding and service standards documentation for staff
- Company culture and values documentation
- Compensation and benefits planning

### What does not belong here

- Guest management – Operations/Clientele
- Vendor relationships – Operations
- Payroll financials and tax – Finance
- Legal terms of employment contracts – Administration/Legal

Solo operation. All concept development and planning handled personally with Alfred as the AI operating layer.
