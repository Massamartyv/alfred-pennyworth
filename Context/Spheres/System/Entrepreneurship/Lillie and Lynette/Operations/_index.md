# Operations -- Lillie and Lynette

Service delivery, vendor management, standard operating procedures and the systems that keep the hospitality experience consistent and excellent.

### What Belongs Here

- Standard operating procedures for all service areas
- Vendor management and procurement
- Client and guest management
- Quality assurance and service standards
- Tool configurations and integrations
- AI agent guidelines and department head definitions
- Health and safety protocols

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Service design and menu creation -- that goes in Product Development
- Guest acquisition strategy -- that goes in Marketing & Sales
- Financial tracking -- that goes in Finances

### Subdirectories

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| AI/ | Agent guidelines, department heads |
| Clientele/ | Client registry, guest profiles, VIP management |
| Delivery Playbooks/ | Step-by-step service delivery guides |
| SOPs/ | Standard operating procedures |
| Templates/ | Reusable templates for recurring operations |
| Tools/ | Tool documentation and configuration |

### Current State

Shell structure in place. SOPs and playbooks to be written as the hospitality concept takes shape and service standards are defined.
