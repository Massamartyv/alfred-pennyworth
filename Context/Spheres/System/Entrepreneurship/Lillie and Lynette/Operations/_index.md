# Operations – Lillie and Lynette

The engine room of Lillie and Lynette. Day-to-day service delivery, standard operating procedures, event and experience production, tool stack and the Clientele directory for guest and vendor relationships. Operations absorbed the service-delivery ground of the former Production studio at The Restoration, 2026-09-10 – shift choreography, event production and experience fabrication now live here alongside daily service management.

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Service delivery management – daily operations, shift oversight, guest flow
- Standard operating procedures for all service areas
- Service production workflows – how a shift is planned, run and closed
- Event production workflows for private events, curated experiences and pop-ups
- Experience fabrication – signature tactile moments, installations, guest touchpoints
- Vendor management and procurement workflow
- Guest and vendor Clientele management – profiles, VIP recognition, relationship tracking
- Tool configurations and integrations (reservation, POS, inventory)
- Quality assurance and service standards
- Health and safety protocols at the operational level

### What does not belong here

- Agent guidelines and department heads – those now live at `Agents/` (venture root)
- Service and experience design, content production – Marketing & Sales
- Guest acquisition strategy – Business Development and Marketing & Sales
- Financial tracking – Finances
- Regulatory licensing and compliance – Administration

## Clientele Scope (Hospitality)

For Lillie and Lynette, the Clientele directory holds two relationship types:

1. **Guests** – Repeat guests, VIPs, and notable booking relationships that benefit from recognition and continuity
2. **Vendor partners** – Suppliers, service providers and operational partners critical to daily service

Both types follow the same registry structure and lifecycle (Active > Archived > Churned).
