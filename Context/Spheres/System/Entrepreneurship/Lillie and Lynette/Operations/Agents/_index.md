# Operations – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Operations department is the engine room. Daily service, SOPs, event and experience production, tools, vendor procurement, guest and vendor clientele management. Primary phases span Execution, Critique and Release. The former Production studio's delivery roles folded in here at The Restoration, 2026-09-10.
