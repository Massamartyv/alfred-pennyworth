# Production

Service delivery production, event production, experience fabrication and content production. The execution engine that turns creative direction into the service, space and collateral a guest actually experiences.

## Structure

```
Production/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Service Delivery/      -- Shift choreography, floor flow, pacing templates
  Events/                -- Private events, curated experiences, pop-ups, takeovers
  Experience Fabrication/ -- Signature moments, installations, tactile elements
  Content/               -- Photography, video, printed menus and collateral production
  Agents/                -- Studio-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Service production workflows – how a shift is planned, run and closed
- Event production workflows for private events, curated experiences and pop-ups
- Experience fabrication – signature tactile moments, installations, guest touchpoints
- Content production – photography shoots, video, printed menus, signage, collateral
- Production stack documentation – tooling for service, events and content

### What does not belong here

- Service design and guest journey concepts – Creative
- Day-to-day service management and SOPs – Operations
- Distribution and guest acquisition – Growth
- Menu and offering concepts – Creative

## Agent Instructions

- Production work is primarily Execution and Critique phases
- Creator and Evaluator crews operate here most frequently
- Template-based production is Tier 1. Draft to finished work is Tier 2.
- Anything that reaches a guest or ships externally is Tier 3 (approval required).

### Token Budget Defaults

| Task | Tier |
|---|---|
| Template population, shift sheets, basic content briefs | Light |
| Event production plans, shoot briefs, service flow drafts | Standard |
| Venue launch production plan, flagship event production, full content campaigns | Heavy |
