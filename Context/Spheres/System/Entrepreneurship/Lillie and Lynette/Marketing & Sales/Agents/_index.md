# Marketing & Sales – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Marketing & Sales department holds the aesthetic, narrative and experiential standard for Lillie and Lynette, and turns that standard into bookings. Every piece of work that enters or exits this department is held to the quality rubric (to be written in `Criteria/quality-rubric.md`). Created at The Restoration, 2026-09-10, folding in the former Creative studio roster in full, the sales-and-conversion roles of the former Growth studio, and the Content Producer role of the former Production studio.

---

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of Marketing & Sales** | Department head. Holds the standard across all creative and guest-facing conversion output. | All | – |
| **Brand Storyteller** | Naming, narrative, written voice across menus, signage and guest copy | Direction, Execution | Researcher, Creator |
| **Experience Designer** | Guest journey maps, service choreography, signature moments | Direction, Execution | Creator |
| **Interior and Environment Director** | Spatial direction, material palette, lighting, scent, sound | Execution | Creator |
| **Visual Director** | Photography, print collateral, social aesthetics, typography | Execution | Creator |
| **Culinary and Beverage Stylist** | Menu concept, plating standards, beverage programme narrative | Direction, Execution | Creator |
| **Content Producer** | Photography, video, menus, printed collateral | Execution | Creator |
| **Sales Lead** | Private event sales, booking conversion, corporate accounts | Execution | Creator, Broadcaster |
| **Guest Acquisition Lead** | Paid channels, referral programmes, loyalty, PR | Direction, Execution | Researcher, Creator |
| **Distribution and Platform Lead** | Reservation platforms, directories, aggregators | Execution | Broadcaster |
| **Editor** | Quality eye. Holds output against the rubric and the brief | Critique | Reviewer:Scrutiny |

### Role activation

Not every task activates every role. A menu description pass may only need the Brand Storyteller and Editor. A venue launch activates all of them. Match the roles to the complexity of the work.
