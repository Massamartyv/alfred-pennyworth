## Scope

### What belongs here

- Brand identity – name origin, narrative, voice, visual system
- Guest experience design – journey mapping, signature moments, service choreography
- Interior and environmental direction – material palette, lighting, scent, soundscape
- Menu and offering narrative – how the culinary and beverage programme tells the story
- Visual direction across photography, print and digital collateral
- Naming conventions for offerings, events, spaces and signature experiences
- Content production – photography shoots, video, printed menus, signage, collateral
- Event and corporate sales – private events, buyouts, booking conversion
- Guest acquisition – paid media, PR, referral programmes, loyalty
- Distribution surfaces – reservation platforms, directories and aggregators

### What does not belong here

- Service delivery SOPs and shift execution – Operations
- Venue sourcing and partnership pipeline – Business Development
- Pricing architecture and offer tiers – Product Development, jointly with Finances
- Revenue tracking – Finances
