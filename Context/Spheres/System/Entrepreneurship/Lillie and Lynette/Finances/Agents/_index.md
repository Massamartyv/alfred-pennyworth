# Finances – Agent Roster and Workflow Registry

The Finances department holds the numbers. Revenue, cost of service, expenses, projections and tax. Hospitality margins are unforgiving, and Finances keeps the discipline that turns vision into a viable enterprise.

| Role | Function | Primary Phase | Primary Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
