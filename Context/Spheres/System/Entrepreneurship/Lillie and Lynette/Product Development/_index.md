# Product Development – Lillie and Lynette

Offers, tiers, bundles, offer strategy and audits – and the venture's own products and builds. For a hospitality-grade home-care and cleaning company, the written operating standard is the intellectual property; Product Development owns the offer ladder that sells access to that standard and the Objects retail line, the venture's product in the literal sense. Created as a full department at The Restoration, 2026-09-10 – none of the four retired studios carried an explicit offer or product scope for this venture, so this index is written fresh against the nine-department taxonomy rather than folded from prior material.

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Offer architecture – service tiers, pricing rungs, bundles
- Offer strategy and audits – testing and refining what is sold and at what price
- Objects – the retail bath, body and decor line; formulation, sourcing and amenity-to-retail sequencing

### What does not belong here

- Sales process and booking conversion – Marketing & Sales
- Revenue tracking and margin reporting – Finances
- Service delivery of the offer once sold – Operations
- Partnership and venue pipeline – Business Development
