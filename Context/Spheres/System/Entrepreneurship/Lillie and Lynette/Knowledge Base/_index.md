# Knowledge Base – Lillie and Lynette

Industry research, competitive analysis, case studies and the intellectual fuel that powers the hospitality vision. Knowledge Base is a department, not a shared resource. It feeds every other department with the material they need to work from informed ground. It absorbed the research and competitive-intelligence ground of the former Strategy studio at The Restoration, 2026-09-10.

## Structure

```
Knowledge Base/
  _index.md              -- This file
  AI Learnings/          -- Insights from AI-assisted hospitality planning
  Case Studies/          -- Breakdowns of exceptional hospitality brands and experiences
  Methodologies/         -- Documented approaches to service design, guest experience, operations
  Reference Library/     -- Creators, brands, works and curators for inspiration and creative direction
  Research/              -- Market analysis, location research, industry trends, guest behaviour, competitive intelligence
  Agents/                -- Department-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Hospitality industry research and trends, emerging formats and category shifts
- Competitive landscape analysis and reference brand breakdowns
- Case studies of outstanding hospitality brands and experiences
- Guest behaviour research – segment maps, booking patterns, spend behaviour, loyalty drivers
- Guest experience design methodologies
- Culinary research and food trend analysis
- Real estate and location research
- AI learnings specific to hospitality operations

### What does not belong here

- Personal sphere research – that lives in the personal Notion workspace under Sphere Manager
- Other venture research – each venture is entirely separate
- Market and venue economics, brand positioning – Business Development
- Active service design documents – Marketing & Sales
