# The Cleaning Authority

**Who** – A residential cleaning franchise co-listed with Molly Maid as the explicit anti-reference for Lillie and Lynette's Stewardship pillar. Operates in the same commodity-tier market.

**Why aligned** – Same structural case as Molly Maid: this card exists to name the boundary. The Cleaning Authority represents the market position and operational model Lillie and Lynette is built in opposition to. Volume acquisition, franchise replication, commodity pricing. The name itself is instructive: authority claimed in the brand language, earned only by repetition of a minimum standard. Lillie and Lynette stakes authority on the felt quality of genuine welcome, not on scale or frequency of transaction.

**The proposition** – Franchise cleaning at scale. Systemised but not elevated. The client relationship is transactional and the brand language mirrors the category conventions it competes within. Nothing hospitality-grade in the model – no amenity, no guest relationship, no standard that elevates the home.

**Canon** – Named alongside Molly Maid in the revenue battle plan anti-reference grouping: "Anti-reference: Molly Maid, The Cleaning Authority." The pairing is intentional – both represent the franchise-at-commodity-tier category as a whole.

**Pull for** – positioning, proposition, offer-design, restraint. Summon this card when defining what the offer is not, setting the $250 floor rationale, or writing competitive contrast language.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. Category position unchanged from source signal.
