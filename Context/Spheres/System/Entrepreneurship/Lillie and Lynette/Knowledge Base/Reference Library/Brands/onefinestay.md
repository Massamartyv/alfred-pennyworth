# Onefinestay

**Who** – A London-founded luxury short-let and property management company that curates and manages private homes to a hospitality-grade standard, placing guests in properties it does not own. Named in the Lillie and Lynette revenue battle plan as "the standard on homes the company does not own."

**Why aligned** – Onefinestay solves the exact structural problem at the heart of Lillie and Lynette's Stewardship pillar: how to build a brand defined by a standard of care across property you do not control. The answer is selection, the standard written down and the inspection enforced. The brand does not try to own the homes it manages; it earns the right to place its name on them by applying a single, documented quality gate to every property in the portfolio. The homeowner is a partner, not a client. The standard travels because it is the brand, not the building.

For L&L, this is the aspirational architecture for the Estate Care and short-let portfolio tiers: curated homes, the L&L Standard applied to each, the brand name on the welcome note rather than on the door of a company-owned property.

**The proposition** – A hospitality experience in a private home, managed to hotel-grade standard. Onefinestay handled pre-arrival provisioning, in-stay support, keyless entry, linen service and on-call assistance – all the invisible infrastructure that turns a private home into a hospitality product. The brand promise to guests was: a home, but someone has genuinely prepared it for you. The brand promise to homeowners was: your property, managed to a standard worthy of your investment.

**Canon** – The original Onefinestay model in London and subsequently Paris, Los Angeles and New York. The AccorHotels acquisition in 2016 and the subsequent integration into the Accor Homes division (later discontinued as a standalone brand after 2019) – the latter relevant as a cautionary note on what happens to boutique stewardship brands inside hotel-group acquisition logic.

**Pull for** – positioning, offer-design, hospitality, ritual. Summon this card when designing the Estate Care and portfolio management offer architecture, writing the homeowner pitch, defining what the L&L Standard must cover to govern a home the company does not own, or drafting the curation criteria for short-let hosts.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. The standalone Onefinestay brand no longer operates independently post-Accor integration. The model reference remains fully valid; the structural lesson holds regardless of the brand's current status.
