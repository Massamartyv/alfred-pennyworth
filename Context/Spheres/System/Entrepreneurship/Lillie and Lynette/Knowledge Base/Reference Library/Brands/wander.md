# Wander

**Who** – A US-based tech-enabled luxury short-let company that manages a curated portfolio of smart homes to a consistent hospitality standard, without owning the underlying property. Named alongside Onefinestay in the Lillie and Lynette revenue battle plan as "the standard on homes the company does not own."

**Why aligned** – Wander is the modern execution of the same principle Onefinestay established: the brand defines and delivers the standard; the property is someone else's asset. Where Onefinestay built the model on bespoke city homes with concierge-style management, Wander represents the tech-instrumented, short-let-native evolution – smart-home infrastructure, app-managed guest experience, a curated network of high-design properties in drive-to destinations. Together with Onefinestay, the pairing in the battle plan confirms the model: the standard can travel across unowned property when the selection is rigorous and the delivery is systematised.

For Lillie and Lynette's Phase 3 portfolio management offering, Wander's model is instructive: curation rules are the defence against brand dilution, the Standard is the asset, and the technology layer (even if lighter at L&L's current stage) governs the consistency of delivery.

**The proposition** – Curated smart homes in destination markets, managed by Wander to a consistent luxury standard. The guest experience is standardised across every property in the portfolio – similar amenities, similar infrastructure, similar service level – despite each home being architecturally distinct and privately owned. The brand promise rests on the curation gate, not on ownership of the asset.

**Canon** – The Wander portfolio of smart-home destination properties across US markets. The tech-enabled guest experience layer. The curation standard and the brand's selection criteria for which homes earn a place in the network.

**Pull for** – offer-design, positioning, hospitality, restraint. Summon this card alongside the Onefinestay card when designing the short-let portfolio management offer, writing curation criteria, or building the argument that Lillie and Lynette's standard can govern homes it does not own.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. Wander was active and expanding at the time of the Direction session. The structural model is the reference; current funding and market position require fresh research.
