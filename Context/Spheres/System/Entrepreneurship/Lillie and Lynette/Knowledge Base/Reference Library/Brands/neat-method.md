# NEAT Method

**Who** – A North American premium home organisation brand that delivers a branded, luxury-positioned organising service and has scaled from a local practice to a national franchise model. Named in the Lillie and Lynette revenue battle plan as proof that "premium branded home service scales."

**Why aligned** – NEAT Method answers the structural scepticism that premium home service is inherently local and person-dependent. The brand scaled a service that is tactile, time-intensive and deeply personalised – exactly the profile of Lillie and Lynette's Stewardship offer – without collapsing the premium positioning into a commodity franchise. The extraction is the proof of concept: if a home-organisation service can become a scalable brand at the premium tier, so can a premium hospitality-grade cleaning and stewardship service.

The mechanism NEAT Method used is instructive: a documented standard, a trained delivery team, a brand identity that signals the premium tier visually and verbally, and a price point held above the market. The brand does not compete on price or convenience – it competes on the quality of the result and the feeling of the experience.

**The proposition** – Home organisation as an experience, not a task. NEAT Method's brand language is editorial and lifestyle-adjacent – the photography is styled, the aesthetic is restrained and contemporary, the copy treats the organised home as a form of personal wellness. The service itself is thorough and systematic, but the brand communicates the transformation rather than the labour. Price point is premium – the anti-reference to the hourly rate model.

**Canon** – The NEAT Method national franchise expansion. The brand's visual language and editorial photography. The premium price positioning in the home-organisation market.

**Pull for** – positioning, offer-design, proposition. Summon this card when building the scalability argument for L&L's Stewardship offer, designing the brand language for the recurring keeping tier, or writing investor or partner materials that need proof the premium home-service model scales.

**Trend read** – Pending enrichment. NEAT Method was active and growing at the time of the Direction session. The scale-proof argument is structural and does not depend on the brand's current state.
