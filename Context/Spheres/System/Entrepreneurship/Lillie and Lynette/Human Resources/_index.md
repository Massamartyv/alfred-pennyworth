# Human Resources -- Lillie and Lynette

Team, contractors, hiring plans and company culture. The people who will bring the hospitality vision to life.

### What Belongs Here

- Organisational structure and role definitions
- Hiring plans and timelines
- Contractor and freelancer agreements
- Training programmes and onboarding
- Company culture and values documentation
- Compensation and benefits planning

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Guest management -- that goes in Operations/Clientele
- Vendor relationships -- that goes in Operations or Business Development
- Payroll financials -- those go in Finances

### Current Structure

Solo operation. All concept development and planning handled personally with Alfred as the AI operating layer.
