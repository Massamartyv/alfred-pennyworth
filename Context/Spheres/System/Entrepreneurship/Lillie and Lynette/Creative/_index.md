# Creative

Brand identity, guest experience design, visual language, interior aesthetic direction, menu and offering narrative. Creative holds the standard for what Lillie and Lynette looks like, feels like, tastes like and sounds like. The guest never sees the studio, but they feel every decision made inside it.

## Structure

```
Creative/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Brand/                 -- Identity system, naming, voice, visual language
  Experience Design/     -- Guest journey maps, signature moments, service choreography
  Environment/           -- Interior direction, material palette, lighting, scent, sound
  Menu and Offerings/    -- Menu narrative, beverage programme concept, offering tiers
  Visual/                -- Photography direction, collateral, social aesthetics
  Agents/                -- Studio-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Brand identity – name origin, narrative, voice, visual system
- Guest experience design – journey mapping, signature moments, service choreography
- Interior and environmental direction – material palette, lighting, scent, soundscape
- Menu and offering narrative – how the culinary and beverage programme tells the story
- Visual direction across photography, print and digital collateral
- Naming conventions for offerings, events, spaces and signature experiences

### What does not belong here

- Service delivery SOPs – Operations
- Menu production and kitchen workflows – Production
- Guest acquisition channels – Growth
- Pricing decisions – Growth and Finance jointly
