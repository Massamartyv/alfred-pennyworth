# Finance

Revenue, cost of service, expenses, projections, tax and metrics. The numbers. Hospitality is an unforgiving margin business – Finance holds the discipline that turns vision into a viable enterprise.

## Structure

```
Finance/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Revenue/               -- Revenue tracking by stream (service, events, retail, partnerships)
  Cost of Service/       -- Cost of goods sold, labour cost, prime cost monitoring
  Expenses/              -- Operating expenses, overhead, tool subscriptions, rent
  Projections/           -- Forecasts, budgets, financial models, capital strategy
  Metrics/               -- Prime cost, RevPASH, check average, cover counts, guest lifetime value
  Tax/                   -- Sales tax, liquor tax, payroll tax, income tax, filings
  Agents/                -- Studio-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Revenue stream definitions and tracking
- Cost of goods sold and labour cost (prime cost) management
- Operating expense categorisation and budgeting
- Financial projections, scenario models and capital requirements
- Hospitality-specific metrics – prime cost, RevPASH, cover counts, check average
- Tax preparation and compliance across all tax types

### What does not belong here

- Personal finances – those live in the personal Notion workspace
- Other venture financial data – each venture is entirely separate
- Partnership negotiation terms – Growth
- Regulatory compliance – Administration

## Agent Instructions

- All financial commitments are Tier 3 (approval required).
- Routine expense logging and invoice processing is Tier 1.
- Monthly and quarterly reports are Tier 2.
- Capital strategy and funding decisions require approval.

### Token Budget Defaults

| Task | Tier |
|---|---|
| Expense logging, invoice runs, daily sales imports | Light |
| Monthly P&L, prime cost reports, cash flow statements | Standard |
| Annual forecasting, capital raise materials, revenue model design | Heavy |

Pre-revenue. All financial planning is forward-looking. Capital requirements and funding strategy to be developed alongside the concept and venue strategy.
