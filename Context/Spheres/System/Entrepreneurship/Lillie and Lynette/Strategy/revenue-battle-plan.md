# Revenue Battle Plan – Zero to Leveraged

Approved at Direction on 2026-06-11. Three operator decisions govern this plan:

1. **Stewardship leads revenue.** Objects enter inside the service as the amenity and go retail in Phase 4. Sequence amendment logged in the brand fingerprint, version 0.2.
2. **Founder R&D period.** The founder delivers Phase 1 jobs as field research with a hard exit when the first crew is trained. Founder-on-tools ends at Phase 2, no exceptions.
3. **Homeowners are the wedge.** Affluent Atlanta homeowners via warm network first. Short-let turnover work is added in Phase 2 under curation rules.

## The Thesis

A solo cleaner grosses $5,000 to $6,000 a month at full capacity, builds zero enterprise value and owns a job, not a company. The escape from that trap is structural, not motivational. The company owns three assets that scale without the founder's hands:

- **The Standard** – the written, trainable, inspectable operating standard. The IP.
- **The brand** – hospitality-grade positioning that commands premium pricing in a category priced as a commodity.
- **The client book** – recurring doors and retainers that compound.

Price at the brand tier. Pay crews well beneath it. The founder sells, inspects and directs. Recurring revenue plus a written standard plus a brand is a sellable company.

## The Ascension Ladder

Each rung earns trust for the next. Products ride every rung as amenity, gift and eventually purchase.

| Rung | Offer | Price guide | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signature deep clean | From $350 | Entry. Buyable on sight, proves the Standard in one visit |
| 2 | Recurring keeping – biweekly or weekly | $180–$280 per visit | The revenue spine. Converts trust into MRR |
| 3 | Estate Care retainer | $900–$2,500 per month | Keeping plus vendor coordination, provisioning, seasonal calendar |
| 4 | Portfolio management – short-let and second homes | Per-door fee plus turnovers | Asset-light scale on property the house does not own |

Live state: Notion Tasks, personal workspace (Lillie and Lynette – Venture Operations project, Phases 0 to 4).

## Guardrails

- **$250 minimum engagement.** The house does not compete at the commodity tier.
- **55% gross margin floor** on service after direct labor.
- **Curated portfolio.** Decline misfit homes and hosts. Growth never dilutes the boutique promise.
- **Every kept door is stocked.** House product in every home, cost built into price.
- **Formulation integrity is existential.** Non-toxic, safety-tested, documented. The Laundress recall is the cautionary tale on record.
- **Founder hours on tools: zero after Phase 1.**

## Risks on Record

- **Founder gravity.** The trap re-forms quietly every time a crew member cancels. The hard line holds or the company does not exist.
- **Hiring to the Standard.** The first crew defines whether the Standard is real or decorative. Slow hiring beats fast regret.
- **Margin erosion via amenity costs.** Amenity spend is capped within the 55% floor.
- **Brand drift in short-let work.** Turnover volume pulls toward utility pricing. Curation rules are the defense.
