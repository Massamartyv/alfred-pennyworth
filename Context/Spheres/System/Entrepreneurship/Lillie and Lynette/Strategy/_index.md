# Strategy

Hospitality industry research, competitive intelligence, venue and market positioning, guest behaviour analysis. The thinking behind the work – what Creative designs, what Growth pursues and what Operations runs is informed here first.

## Structure

```
Strategy/
  _index.md              -- This file
  Industry/              -- Hospitality trends, emerging formats, category shifts
  Market and Venue/      -- Geographic analysis, neighbourhood dynamics, venue economics
  Guest Behaviour/       -- Segment maps, booking patterns, spend behaviour, loyalty drivers
  Competitive/           -- Adjacent concepts, local competitive set, reference brands
  Positioning/           -- Brand positioning frameworks, differentiation strategy
  Agents/                -- Studio-specific agents, workflows, criteria
```

## Scope

### What belongs here

- Hospitality industry research and trend analysis
- Market and neighbourhood reconnaissance for potential venues
- Guest behaviour research – who they are, how they book, what they spend, why they return
- Competitive intelligence on adjacent concepts and reference hospitality brands
- Strategic positioning decisions for the brand and its offerings
- Unit economics models and scenario planning

### What does not belong here

- Creative direction and brand identity – Creative
- Venue acquisition and partnership execution – Growth
- Financial tracking and reporting – Finance
