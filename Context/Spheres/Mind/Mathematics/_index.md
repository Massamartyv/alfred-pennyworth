# Mathematics

Founded 4 August 2026 under the worldly-wisdom adoption. The sphere exists because Munger names mathematics as one of the disciplines a working latticework cannot do without, and because it was the largest genuine gap across the 42 spheres – everything held sat on the hard-science side of his list and the arithmetic of uncertainty was missing entirely.

Live state: Notion Sphere Manager, personal workspace.

---

## Scope

Mathematics is the parent. Statistics, probability and accounting live inside it rather than as separate spheres, on the operator's ruling of 4 August 2026 – mathematics is the highest expression of statistics and probability, and splitting them would bleed the sphere system without adding tracking value.

| Track | What it covers | Why it earns the place |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic of consequence | Compound interest, orders of magnitude, rates of change | The single most under-applied idea in ordinary decisions. Compounding is the reason cadence, catalogue and capital all behave the way they do |
| Combinatorics | Permutations and combinations, the Fermat and Pascal system | Munger's minimum. The ability to enumerate outcomes before estimating them |
| Probability | Base rates, conditional probability, Bayesian updating, expected value | How to hold uncertainty without collapsing it into a guess or a certainty |
| Statistics | Distributions, the normal curve and where it lies, variance, regression to the mean, sampling error | The discipline that turns evidence into a claim, and the one most often misused to launder a conclusion |
| Accounting | Double entry, the three statements, cash against accrual, and the limits of each | The language of business. Held here rather than in Personal Finance because the reasoning is mathematical and the application is everywhere |

## Domain constants

**The purpose is judgement, not calculation.** The aim is not to compute but to recognise – to see that a situation is a base-rate problem, a compounding problem or a regression-to-the-mean problem before reaching for anything. Fluency at the level of recognition beats rigour at the level of arithmetic.

**Numbers that are not felt are not held.** A model that produces a figure the operator cannot sanity-check by instinct has not been learned. Every track is worked until the order of magnitude is intuitive.

**The normal distribution is the most dangerous idea in the sphere.** It is the default assumption in almost every tool and it is wrong wherever the interesting outcomes live. Treat it as a convenience with a known failure mode.

**Accounting is a map, not the territory.** Munger is emphatic on this. Learn it thoroughly and hold its limits just as firmly – the figures are a compressed model of a business, and every compression discards something.
