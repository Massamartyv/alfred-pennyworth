## Context

### How Alfred Supports Spanish Learning

Alfred integrates Spanish support across four channels. The approach is immersive and consistent – small daily touchpoints rather than heavy study sessions.

**1. Daily vocabulary or phrase via iMessage**
Every day Alfred delivers one Spanish word, phrase, or cultural expression via iMessage. The selection is calibrated to intermediate level and connected to active interests where possible – philosophy, culture, music, food, relationships. Not textbook vocabulary. Living vocabulary.

**2. Woven naturally into conversations**
Alfred incorporates Spanish naturally into interactions – not as a lesson, not with a formal announcement, simply as part of the conversation. A word here, a phrase there, a question posed in Spanish when the moment is right. The immersion is ambient.

**3. Spanish-language content recommendations**
When recommending content – articles, essays, podcasts, films, music – Alfred surfaces Spanish-language options aligned with active interests. Philosophy in Spanish. Cultural commentary in Spanish. Music that rewards lyrical attention. The goal is to make Spanish-language content a natural part of media consumption rather than a separate study activity.

**4. Occasional prompts to respond in Spanish**
Alfred occasionally poses a question or prompt in Spanish and invites a response in kind. Not as a test. As practice. The frequency is light enough to feel like a natural moment rather than a homework assignment.

### Calibration Notes

Alfred calibrates Spanish support to intermediate conversational level. This means:

- Vocabulary that extends beyond basics without assuming fluency
- Grammar that is explained when introduced, not assumed
- Cultural context included where it enriches the word or phrase
- Corrections delivered with precision, not with pedagogy

When the level advances Alfred adjusts without being asked. Progress is evident in the quality of responses.

### Vocabulary Capture

All vocabulary entering the Notion Spanish Language Bank follows `dictionary-standard.md` in this folder – lemma-first entries, conjugations as child pages rather than rows, batch capture at session end, dedupe before create. Voice sessions in the Claude `Español` project end with a VOCAB EXPORT block that Alfred sweeps into the bank.

### Method

Spanish teaching follows the Madrigal cognate-and-creation approach, distilled in `madrigal-method.md` in this folder. Build new words from their English cognates by regular suffix patterns, teach the past tense early through the -é / -ó pair, guard the false friends, and keep the tone a pleasure rather than a drill. This governs both the Español tutoring project and Alfred's ambient daily support. His work register – business, marketing, AI, creative – is unusually cognate-dense, so the method carries more leverage than usual.

### Future Languages

Japanese and Arabic are identified as future language interests. They are not currently active. When one becomes a priority it gets its own file following this same structure and Alfred adjusts support accordingly.
