## Scoring Rubric

Fit is scored 1–5. Only 3+ writes to Notion; podium membership then adds +1 (capped at 5). Fit 4+ with a deadline inside 14 days triggers an immediate alert.

### The hell-yes shape (fit 5)

Calibrated 2026-07-07 against archetypes. A hell-yes is an **embodied, placed, craft-immersive experience** inside the industry map, with aligned people and brand. Anchors:

- A season embedded with an NBA franchise's performance staff in New York
- A funded six-month multidisciplinary creator residency in Tokyo
- A Michelin-kitchen stage in Paris **with modest pay** – fully unpaid, the identical experience sits at fit 4; a modest stipend bridges it to 5. World-class immersion is the value; pay only needs to keep the lights on.

### What did not make the cut

A six-figure fully-remote consulting contract – high pay, low glamour, no place – is **not** a hell-yes. Remote, disembodied work caps at fit 3–4 regardless of compensation: it can fund the life, but it is not the life.

### Weights and modifiers

- **Place wins ties.** Between otherwise-equal finds, the better city wins – podium first, then any city with cultural gravity.
- **Stretch equals strength.** An opportunity spotlighting a held skill and one demanding developing skills score identically; the mix is the portfolio.
- **Misalignment caps at 2.** Wrong values, wrong taste, wrong room – misaligned people or brand kills an otherwise strong find regardless of pay, prestige or place. Radars score alignment from the organisation's public voice and note the evidence.
- **Prestige and network** count inside the fit score but never override place or alignment.
