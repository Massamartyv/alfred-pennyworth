# The Louvre

**What** – The world's most visited art museum, opened to the public in 1793 in a former royal palace dating to the 12th century. Paris. Holdings run from antiquity to the mid-19th century.

**Why aligned** – The canon at its most absolute, and a study in how a palace becomes a repository for what empires acquire – every work carrying the politics of its collection alongside its beauty. You cannot look with total innocence, and that tension is part of the experience.

**The filter** – The default Western canon. If a work hangs in the Louvre it has passed every gate history has. Read it for the established masterpieces and for the weight of the institution itself, and for I. M. Pei's pyramid, the modern intervention on the old palace.

**Canon** – *Mona Lisa* by Leonardo da Vinci. the *Venus de Milo* (c. 130-100 BC). the *Winged Victory of Samothrace* (c. 190 BC). I. M. Pei's glass pyramid (inaugurated 1988).
