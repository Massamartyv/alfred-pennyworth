# The Metropolitan Museum of Art

**What** – The encyclopedic museum, founded in New York in 1870, spanning 5,000 years of human making across every medium and geography. One of the largest art museums in the world.

**Why aligned** – A named bucket-list visit, and the museum that refuses specialization – everything beside everything, where context becomes meaning. Its Costume Institute and the Met Gala also put it on the fashion line, the rare institution that holds art and dress as equals.

**The filter** – Breadth as argument. Ancient Egyptian temples, European masters and contemporary fashion under one roof, each speaking differently for the company it keeps. Read it for range and juxtaposition rather than a single canon.

**Canon** – the encyclopedic collection across all eras. the Costume Institute (35,000-plus costumes) and the Met Gala, the most-watched annual event at the seam of fashion, art and celebrity. the Fifth Avenue building (from 1880).

**Pull for** – breadth, juxtaposition, costume-institute, editorial, art-direction.

**Trend read** – The Met Gala makes it the most culturally visible museum on earth each May, fashion and art fused for a night. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
