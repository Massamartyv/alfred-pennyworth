# The Hermitage Museum

**What** – One of the largest and oldest museums in the world, founded in 1764 by Catherine the Great and housed across the Winter Palace and adjoining buildings in St. Petersburg.

**Why aligned** – A bucket-list visit and the museum as imperial spectacle – the collection and the palace inseparable, opulence at a scale almost no institution can match. The reference for when the container is as much the experience as the contents.

**The filter** – Imperial encyclopedic. Old Masters, antiquities and decorative arts inside a tsar's residence, so the gilt rooms compete with the canvases. Read it for grandeur and for the museum-as-palace, where architecture and collection are one performance.

**Canon** – the Winter Palace state rooms. a vast holding of Rembrandt and European masters, including two Leonardo Madonnas. the founding collection of Catherine the Great (1764).

**Pull for** – scale, opulence, spatial-direction, imperial, art-direction.

**Trend read** – An enduring symbol of imperial collecting and one of the great museum interiors on earth. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
