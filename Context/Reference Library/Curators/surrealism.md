# Surrealism

**What** – The movement consecrated in Paris in 1924 by André Breton's *Manifesto of Surrealism*, devoted to bypassing rational control to reach the unconscious directly. Built on Freud's theory of dreams and a politics drawn from Marx.

**Why aligned** – Named directly in creative-director.md – "Surrealism as a movement, its dreamlike expression in a way that feels shockingly realistic." The dream rendered with the conviction of the real, which is also the throughline to the screenwriting pull toward the dystopian and the surreal.

**The filter** – Trust the unconscious over reason. Dream imagery, automatism, the uncanny and the impossible juxtaposition as valid creative instruments. Read it for the logic of the dream made concrete – not random strangeness, but the irrational executed with total conviction.

**Canon** – Breton's 1924 manifesto. Dalí's paranoid-critical method. Magritte's enigmatic juxtapositions. Ernst's collage and frottage. Miró's biomorphic abstraction.

**Pull for** – dream-logic, the-uncanny, juxtaposition, conviction, art-direction.
