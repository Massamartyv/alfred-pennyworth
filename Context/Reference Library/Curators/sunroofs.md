# Sunroofs

**What** – The residential operable roof, held here under its vernacular name with the industry terms on record: "sunroof" belongs to cars, and the building trades use skylight or roof window at the small scale and retractable roof or operable roof for the full systems that roll a roof section open to the sky. The category is real – Roll-A-Cover and OpenAire design custom residential retractable systems for great rooms, patios, pools and courtyards, with Dynadome and BTX alongside – and its famous technological precedent is Toronto's SkyDome, now Rogers Centre, which debuted its fully retractable roof in 1989.

**Why aligned** – The ceiling as an aperture. Alongside the solarium and sunroom in the operator's programme, this is the most theatrical of the light-capture family – the room that converts to open air on command, weather as a feature the house can invite in or shut out.

**The filter** – Scale decides the term and the engineering: skylight and roof window for glazed openings within the roof plane; retractable and operable systems for the true transformation, motorized sections that remove the boundary entirely. Pull it when a space should have two natures – enclosed and open – rather than a compromise between them. Search the trade under the industry terms, not the vernacular.

**Canon** – the SkyDome retractable roof (1989) as the technology's landmark; Roll-A-Cover and OpenAire as the residential practice; the skylight and roof window as the everyday scale.

**Pull for** – open-sky, transformation, light-capture, indoor-outdoor, engineering-of-delight.
