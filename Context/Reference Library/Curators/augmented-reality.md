# Augmented Reality

**What** – The medium that overlays digital content onto the physical world rather than replacing it. The term was coined by Boeing researcher Tom Caudell (1990), who built a heads-up display to guide aircraft-assembly workers; its mass-market breakthrough was Pokémon GO (Niantic, 2016), which placed digital creatures into real streets.

**Why aligned** – The reference for the real world as the canvas, and context as the content. Where virtual reality replaces the room, AR annotates it – the digital layer earns its meaning from the physical place it sits on top of. It is the case study in designing for a surface you do not control, where location and context carry the experience.

**The filter** – Treat the real world as the substrate, not the backdrop. Read AR for the discipline of the overlay – the digital must respect and respond to the physical, registering to real space and real light. The content is the relationship between the layer and the place; design the join, not just the object.

**Canon** – Tom Caudell's coining of the term at Boeing (1990); the head-mounted-display lineage shared with VR; Pokémon GO (2016) as the mass-market proof; the smartphone as the everyday AR device.
