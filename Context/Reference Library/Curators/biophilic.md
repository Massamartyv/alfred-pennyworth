# Biophilic

**What** – A design philosophy that treats contact with nature as a human need, building greenery, natural light, water, natural materials and views into the structure rather than around it. Rooted in the biophilia hypothesis, carried into design by Stephen Kellert.

**Why aligned** – The closest match to the spatial creed of any style on the list – "no such thing as too much green, plants are co-inhabitants, not decorations." It runs straight through the ideal creative environment – greenery, rain outside, natural light flooding the work – and connects Kalach's botanical machine to the Einstein atrium.

**The filter** – Bring living matter, daylight and natural material into the core, not just the edges. Automated care so the green actually thrives. Real views, real light, real plants, water where it can be heard. The test is whether nature organizes the space or merely decorates it.

**Canon** – Bosco Verticale, Milan (Stefano Boeri); the Amazon Spheres, Seattle; Parkroyal Collection Pickering, Singapore (WOHA); Gardens by the Bay. Kalach and Wright as the architectural ancestors.

**Pull for** – biophilic, nature-integration, color-and-light, greenery, wellbeing.
