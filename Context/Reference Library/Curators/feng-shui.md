# Feng Shui

**What** – The ancient Chinese practice of arranging environments to optimize the flow of energy – chi. Roots reported as far back as roughly 960 BC in astrology and site alignment, grounded in Taoist philosophy and the balance of yin and yang. The name itself is the method: feng (wind) and shui (water), the flow of natural forces as the model for how life should move through a space.

**Why aligned** – Named in creative-director.md as an operating condition of the workspace – technology chosen to stay out of the way and "facilitate feng shui and flow." The alignment is practical, not mystical: the conviction that a room's arrangement either moves energy or stagnates it, and that clutter is a cost paid in attention.

**The filter** – Chi as the diagnostic: energy should move, never stagnate. The Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) held in equilibrium; the Form School reading landscape, the Compass School reading direction; the bagua map dividing a space into nine life domains. The contemporary extractions are the durable ones – clutter read as stagnant energy, the commanding position (bed, desk and stove facing the door with a solid wall behind – security made spatial), natural elements integrated as material fact. Pull it as a discipline for arrangement decisions, not decoration.

**Canon** – the Form and Compass schools; the bagua map; the commanding-position principle; the Five Elements framework.
