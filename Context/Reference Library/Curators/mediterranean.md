# Mediterranean

**What** – The architectural language of the warm Mediterranean coast – Spanish, Italian, Greek and their colonial revivals. Stucco or lime-washed walls, terracotta roof tiles, arches, courtyards, thick walls that hold cool, and a constant blur between inside and outside.

**Why aligned** – Named directly in the spatial vision as Traditional Mediterranean, and again in the duality principle – "a warm Mediterranean room with concrete and steel accents." It is warmth as a default and indoor-outdoor living as a value, the soft 80% the raw materials get set against.

**The filter** – Warm earth surfaces, natural light, the courtyard as the organizing room, the threshold between indoor and outdoor kept soft. Read it through warmth and patina, not through theme-park "Tuscan" pastiche – hand-finished plaster, aged wood, real stone, olive and clay.

**Canon** – Whitewashed Cycladic island houses; Andalusian courtyards; Spanish Colonial Revival; terracotta, lime plaster, wrought iron, weathered timber, internal courtyards and loggias.

**Pull for** – materiality, indoor-outdoor, warmth, color-and-light, spatial-direction.
