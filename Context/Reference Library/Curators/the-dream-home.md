# The Dream Home

**What** – The operator's own dream-home programme, held as a single composite reference: the feature list that defines what dream-home grade means in this library. Named by the operator 2026-07-10. Not an external reference – a personal standard, queryable as one unit.

**Why aligned** – This is the alignment itself: the programme every residential reference in the library is ultimately measured against.

**The filter** – Sixteen features, grouped by the instincts they encode:

- **Nature drawn inside** – floating stairs over an indoor pond; indoor greenery; indoor trees; a kitchen that opens to nature; sunroom; garden.
- **Water and bathing as event** – a private courtyard for the primary bathtub; a shower with a commanding view; rain chains turning drainage into ornament.
- **Engineering of ease** – central vacuum system with a modern built-in dustpan; built-in chopping board; soft-close cabinets.
- **Theatre and secrecy** – massive front doors; secret rooms.
- **Landscape as room** – private tennis courtyard; clover lawn.

Read the groupings as the standard: nature integrated structurally rather than decoratively, ordinary rituals elevated into events, convenience engineered invisibly into the architecture, arrival and discovery treated as drama, and the grounds designed with the same intent as the rooms.

**Canon** – the sixteen features above, as named. Adjacent cards elaborate individual instincts: Solariums, Sunroofs and Outdoor Showers for light and bathing; Speakeasy for secrecy; Smart Home for invisible ease; Verdant, Biophilic and Native Plant Gardens for the green thread; Feng Shui for flow.
