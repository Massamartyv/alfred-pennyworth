# Dover Street Market

**What** – The Comme des Garçons multi-brand store, founded by Rei Kawakubo and Adrian Joffe. Named in creative-director.md alongside Mr Porter as an editorial buyer whose filter already matches the taste.

**Why aligned** – A curator used as a proxy for judgement. When a specific brand is not carried directly, the store's selection is a reliable secondary filter – the buying is the taste. The space itself argues that merchandising is an art form, not logistics.

**The filter** – Trust a selector whose criteria already match yours and let them run the first pass. High and low on the same floor with no apology. The environment is part of the edit, not a backdrop to it.

**Canon** – the London and Ginza flagships; the seasonal in-store installations.

**Pull for** – merchandising, spatial-direction, high-low, curation strategy, retail editorial.

**Trend read** – pending enrichment.
