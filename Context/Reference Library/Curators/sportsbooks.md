# Sportsbooks

**What** – The venues and platforms that set odds on sporting events and accept wagers against them, pricing risk through the line and taking a margin known as the vig. Long centered on Las Vegas in the United States, sportsbooks expanded nationally after the Supreme Court struck down the federal ban on state-authorized sports betting (2018), driving the online sportsbook boom.

**Why aligned** – The reference for pricing engagement and building a habit loop around a live event. A sportsbook turns the act of watching into the act of participating, and its real product is the line – odds calibrated to balance the book and hold a margin regardless of outcome. It is a study in monetizing attention through risk, incentive and the pull of the live moment.

**The filter** – Sell participation in the moment, and price the risk so the house holds on the spread, not on the single bet. Read the sportsbook for the mechanics of the engagement loop – the line as a designed product, the margin built into the odds, the live event as the trigger. The discipline is the math of incentive and the architecture of habit. Hold a clear eye on the ethics of a model engineered to keep people wagering.

**Canon** – Las Vegas as the historic American home; the line, the odds and the vig as the core product; the 2018 fall of the federal ban as the inflection that opened United States online betting.
