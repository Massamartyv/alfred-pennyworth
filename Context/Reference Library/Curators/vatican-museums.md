# Vatican Museums

**What** – The papal collections in Vatican City, founded in the early 16th century by Pope Julius II, culminating in the Sistine Chapel and the Raphael Rooms. Among the most visited museum complexes in the world.

**Why aligned** – A bucket-list visit and the ceiling the body-as-gallery vision already reveres – Michelangelo, whose *David* and *Creation of Adam* are named as tattoo references, painted his masterpiece here. The reference for art as the highest sacred act, scale and devotion fused.

**The filter** – The Renaissance summit. Read it for Michelangelo and Raphael at the peak of the High Renaissance, and for art deployed as faith and power at once – the most concentrated rooms of genius in Western art.

**Canon** – Michelangelo's Sistine Chapel ceiling and *The Last Judgment*. the Raphael Rooms. classical antiquities including the *Laocoön*. the celebrated spiral staircase.
