# Museo del Prado

**What** – Spain's national art museum, opened in Madrid in 1819, holding the world's definitive collection of Spanish painting and one of the finest of European Old Masters.

**Why aligned** – A bucket-list visit and the temple of the Spanish masters – Velázquez and Goya at their fullest. Velázquez is the very source Kehinde Wiley reworks, so the Prado is where one thread of this list runs back to its origin. Darkness, drama and dignity in paint.

**The filter** – The Spanish-master authority. Read it for Velázquez's composure, Goya's descent into the Black Paintings, El Greco's elongation and Bosch's fever – the canon of European painting at its most psychologically charged.

**Canon** – Velázquez's *Las Meninas*. Goya's *The Third of May 1808* and the Black Paintings. Bosch's *The Garden of Earthly Delights*. El Greco holdings.

**Pull for** – old-master, spanish-masters, art-direction, drama, portraiture.
