# Architectural Digest

**Who** – The international authority on architecture and interior design, founded in the United States in 1920 and published by Condé Nast. A century-old editorial brand that sets the popular standard for what a beautifully designed home looks like, across print, web and a vast video presence.

**Why aligned** – The hotel-room three-check and the lived-in test in the spatial operating system are exactly the eye AD trains. It is the editorial filter for interiors the way Mr Porter is for menswear – a taste proxy whose pages and lists already do a first pass on what is worth looking at.

**The filter** – Editorial curation at scale. The home as the unit of study, shot and storied. The AD100 names the designers and architects the establishment considers the best working today; the Open Door video series turns the private home tour into mass culture. Read it for breadth and for who is being anointed, not for an underground edge – AD is the canon, not the frontier.

**Canon** – Founded 1920; Condé Nast title. The annual AD100 list of leading interior designers and architects. The Open Door celebrity home-tour series. International editions across Spain, Italy, France, Germany, India and the Middle East.
