# The Guggenheim

**What** – The Solomon R. Guggenheim Foundation and its constellation of museums – Frank Lloyd Wright's spiral on Fifth Avenue in New York (1959), Frank Gehry's titanium Bilbao (1997), the Peggy Guggenheim Collection in Venice, and Guggenheim Abu Dhabi in development. The logged goal is to visit every one.

**Why aligned** – A bucket-list visit, all of them, and the place where this list folds back on itself: the New York building is Frank Lloyd Wright, just carded in Architecture, and Bilbao is the museum that proved a building could regenerate a city. Architecture as the artwork, the container as destination.

**The filter** – Modern and contemporary art inside architecture that competes with it. Read it for the union of collection and building – Wright's continuous spiral ramp, Gehry's titanium bloom – and for the "Bilbao effect," the idea that a museum can put a city on the map.

**Canon** – Wright's spiral rotunda, New York (1959). Gehry's Guggenheim Bilbao (1997) and the Bilbao effect. the Peggy Guggenheim Collection, Venice. a deep modern and contemporary holding.
