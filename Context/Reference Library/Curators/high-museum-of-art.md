# High Museum of Art

**What** – The leading art museum of the American Southeast, established under its current name in 1926 (from the 1905 Atlanta Art Association) and anchoring the Woodruff Arts Center in Midtown Atlanta.

**Why aligned** – The home-city institution. It is the museum on the doorstep, and it builds its own identity rather than inheriting one – Richard Meier then Renzo Piano, two architects across two generations. It also gave Kim Chong Hak his first US retrospective, so it is already wired into this very list.

**The filter** – The Southeast's flagship, with an eye that reaches well beyond the region. Read it as the local anchor and as an architecture reference in its own right – the white Meier wing and the Piano village around it. Closest of the ten, and the easiest to make a habit.

**Canon** – the Richard Meier building (1983), named among the best American works of its decade. the Renzo Piano expansion (2005), "a village for the arts" that more than doubled the footprint. the 2025 Kim Chong Hak retrospective.
