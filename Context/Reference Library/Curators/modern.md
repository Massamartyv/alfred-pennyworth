# Modern

**What** – Architectural Modernism – the early-to-mid-20th-century break with ornament toward form following function. Open plans, flat roofs, clean lines, glass, steel and concrete, the International Style of Mies, Le Corbusier and Gropius, and its warmer American cousin, Midcentury Modern.

**Why aligned** – The design principles read like a Modernist manifesto already – clean over cluttered, intentional over accidental, the right amount of meaningful weight. It lives in the spatial vision as organic Japanese modern and in the interior quizzes as Midcentury Modern. Modern is the structural grammar the warmth and rawness get composed over.

**The filter** – Clarity and function as the rule, ornament earning its absence. Open plan, honest structure, glass to landscape, restraint. The taste reading is warm modern, not sterile – Midcentury and organic Japanese modern over white-box minimalism, which the taste explicitly rejects as performative.

**Canon** – Mies' Farnsworth House and Barcelona Pavilion; Le Corbusier's Villa Savoye; the Eames House; Philip Johnson's Glass House; Adolf Loos and "Ornament and Crime" as the founding argument.

**Pull for** – clean-line, open-plan, form-follows-function, spatial-direction, restraint.
