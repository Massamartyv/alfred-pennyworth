# Paraboot

**What** – The French shoemaker (1908), maker of the Michael derby on a Norwegian welt, with its own Para rubber sole, made in France.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. Heritage French footwear built to outlast trends, the considered shoe that grounds a wardrobe. Craft and durability over fashion churn, which sits with the operator's buy-well, buy-once instinct and his preference for objects that age into character.

**The proposition** – Build a shoe to outlive trends. The Norwegian welt, the in-house rubber sole, French manufacture: buy once and wear for decades.

**Canon** – the Michael derby; the Norwegian welt construction; the in-house Para rubber sole; over a century of making in Izeaux.

**Pull for** – french, heritage, footwear, craft.
