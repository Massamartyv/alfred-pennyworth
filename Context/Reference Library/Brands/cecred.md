# Cécred

**What** – The prestige haircare brand founded by Beyoncé Knowles-Carter, launched February 2024 after six years in development; the name fuses the "Cé" of Beyoncé with "sacred." Self-funded and wholly owned.

**Why aligned** – Star power converted into a genuine product house. Six years of development, all hair textures from straight to coily, a prestige tier alongside Olaplex and Kérastase, self-funded with 100% ownership retained – the reference for a celebrity brand built as an owned asset, not a licensing deal. The name itself treats haircare as ritual.

**The proposition** – Build the celebrity brand as equity, not a name-rental. Develop slowly, price prestige, serve every texture, keep the ownership – the asset is the point.

**Canon** – the February 2024 launch after six years' development; the all-textures positioning; the 2025 Ulta expansion, Ulta's largest haircare launch; 100% founder ownership.

**Pull for** – haircare, prestige, ownership-as-strategy.
