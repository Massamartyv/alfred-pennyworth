# Duke and Dexter

**What** – The London footwear house founded by Archie Hewlett (2015), handmade in Sheffield, known for brush-velvet and statement loafers. Recorded in creative-director.md as "Duke and Webster," corrected here.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Mid tier ($80-$300) of creative-director.md. The dress-up loafer with nerve, velvet, pony hair and bold leathers, sits in the operator's seductive-detail register where the shoe makes the statement. It pairs with Blackstock and Weber as the loafer reimagined, carrying high-low credibility from Eddie Redmayne to Snoop Dogg.

**The proposition** – Take the most traditional English shoe and give it nerve. Velvet, colour and limited runs turn the loafer into the statement piece, made by hand in England.

**Canon** – the brush-velvet loafer; the Sheffield "Handmade in England" make; the founding story (Hewlett, £5,000 and a love of vintage slippers, 2015).

**Pull for** – loafer, velvet, british, statement.
