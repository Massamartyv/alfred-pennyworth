# Erin McKenna's Bakery

**What** – Vegan and gluten-free bakery, founded 2005 in New York as BabyCakes by Erin McKenna, who has food allergies and pivoted from fashion. Rebranded under her own name in 2015, with locations in New York, Los Angeles and Orlando.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster as a gluten-free pick. The proof that allergen-free baking need not sacrifice flavor or texture – delicious and considerate of health at once, the exact creed of the kitchen.

**The proposition** – Remove the allergens, keep the joy. Vegan and gluten-free baking that competes on taste, not on dietary apology. Constraint treated as a design problem, not a limit.

**Canon** – the BabyCakes origin on the Lower East Side. the 2015 rebrand. the New York, Los Angeles and Orlando shops.

**Pull for** – vegan, gluten-free, allergen-free, no-compromise, craft.
