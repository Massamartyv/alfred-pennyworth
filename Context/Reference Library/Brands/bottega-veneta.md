# Bottega Veneta

**What** – The fragrance line of the Italian leather house Bottega Veneta (fragrance launched 2011), tethered to the Intrecciato weave and the house's invisible, private-luxury ethos.

**Why aligned** – The reference for fragrance as an extension of quiet-luxury craft. Bottega's scent identity leans on leather, the Intrecciato signature and discretion rather than logo – premium designer fragrance doing its differentiation through restraint.

**The proposition** – Let the craft scent the fragrance. Tie the scent to the house's material signature, leather and the weave, and let quiet opulence carry it.

**Canon** – the 2011 debut leathery floral chypre; the Intrecciato-coded identity; the later Venetian-heritage collection.

**Pull for** – designer-fragrance, quiet-luxury, leather, restraint.

**Trend read** – pending enrichment.
