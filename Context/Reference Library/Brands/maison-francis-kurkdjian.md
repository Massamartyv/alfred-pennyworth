# Maison Francis Kurkdjian

**What** – The Parisian house (2009, founded by Francis Kurkdjian and Marc Chaya), where every fragrance is composed by Kurkdjian himself; acquired by LVMH in 2017.

**Why aligned** – The reference for haute parfumerie where couture precision meets technical rigour. One nose composes everything, a structural rarity, so the house carries a single legible signature, and Baccarat Rouge 540 became the most-referenced niche scent of the era. Parisian, exacting, modern.

**The proposition** – One master nose, total control. Compose every scent yourself so the house has a single signature; precision is the luxury.

**Canon** – Baccarat Rouge 540 (2015); Grand Soir; Aqua Celestia; the LVMH era and Kurkdjian's parallel role as Dior's perfume director.

**Pull for** – haute-parfumerie, technical-precision, signature-scent, parisian.
