# Prada

**What** – The Milanese house (founded 1913 as a leather-goods shop), reinvented by Miuccia Prada into the standard-bearer of intellectual, "ugly-chic" fashion.

**Why aligned** – Prada thinks before it pleases. The deliberate refusal of easy prettiness in favour of ideas, proportion and unexpected material, industrial nylon treated as luxury, is the operator's "rewards closer attention" stated as a brand philosophy: intention over display, the interesting choice over the obvious one.

**The proposition** – Make people think, not just look. Beauty that comes too easily is forgettable, while the considered and slightly difficult choice endures. Intellect is the differentiator.

**Canon** – the Pocono nylon backpack (1984); ugly-chic as a stance; Miu Miu as the younger line; the Raf Simons co-creative era; the Fondazione Prada.

**Pull for** – intellectual-fashion, restraint, materiality, anti-pretty.
