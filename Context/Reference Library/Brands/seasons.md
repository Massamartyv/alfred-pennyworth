# Seasons

**What** – The home-fragrance brand (seasonslife.com) making waterless aroma diffusers positioned explicitly as design objects rather than appliances – patented nebulizing technology, scents developed with perfumers in France and the UK, offices on Fifth Avenue in New York and at Trafalgar Square in London. Four co-founders – Leander, Ning, Waikeung and Yeeman per the brand's own pages – each born in a different season, the origin of the name. Founding year unstated by the brand and left unrecorded here. Carried by Saks Fifth Avenue, Nordstrom and SSENSE, with an iF Design Award entry to its name.

**Why aligned** – Owned and loved. The Seasons diffuser is one of the operator's favourite decor items – the card exists on that verdict. It earns the Aesop principle for the scent-technology category: the functional object designed well enough to sit in the room as decor, wellness hardware passing as sculpture.

**The proposition** – Engineer the appliance out of the object. Waterless nebulizing removes the humidifier clutter; the industrial design carries stone and matte finishes rather than plastics; distribution through fashion and department retail (Saks, SSENSE) rather than gadget channels states the category intent. Scent delivered as an interior material, with the device worthy of the shelf it scents.

**Canon** – the Soni SM in its stone-like resin finish; the Aero SM and Aero Mini Pro portables in colorways like Matcha Green and Linen; the Saks-Nordstrom-SSENSE shelf.

**Pull for** – diffuser-as-object, design-conscious, ritual, scent, wellness-design.
