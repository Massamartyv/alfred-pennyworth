# Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams

**What** – Artisan ice cream company, founded 2002 in Columbus, Ohio by James Beard Award winner Jeni Britton Bauer. Known for inventive flavors built on fine culinary technique.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster as the ice-cream pick. Unusual flavor combinations grounded in real technique – the Flavor Bible instinct in a scoop, evidence of care over novelty for its own sake.

**The proposition** – Treat ice cream like cuisine. A signature cream-cheese base for density and clean finish, flavors composed rather than thrown together – salty caramel, brambleberry crisp, wildberry lavender.

**Canon** – the cream-cheese base. salty caramel and brambleberry crisp. the New York Times-bestselling cookbook (2011).

**Pull for** – flavor-invention, technique, artisan, dessert, craft.
