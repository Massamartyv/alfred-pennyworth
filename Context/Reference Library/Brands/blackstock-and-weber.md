# Blackstock and Weber

**What** – The American footwear label founded by Chris Echevarria, reimagining the penny loafer for a younger set in colour and attitude.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. The loafer made modern and expressive, a heritage shape given colour and a pulse. It sits in the operator's seductive-detail register, the considered shoe that catches the eye and lifts an otherwise restrained fit.

**The proposition** – Take a classic shoe and give it a pulse. The loafer reframed for a generation that wants heritage with personality rather than nostalgia.

**Canon** – the Ellis loafer; the colour-forward penny loafer revival.

**Pull for** – loafer, americana-reimagined, statement, footwear.
