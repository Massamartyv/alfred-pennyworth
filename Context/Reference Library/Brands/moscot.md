# Moscot

**What** – The New York eyewear house, family-run since 1915, known for the Lemtosh and Miltzen frames worn from the Lower East Side to the front row.

**Why aligned** – Eyewear is the operator's seductive-detail register, the thing that catches the light and finishes a look. Moscot is the considered frame that does this without shouting: a heritage object recognised by those who know, carried on craft rather than hype.

**The proposition** – A single object, perfected over generations, becomes a signature. Keep the frame, keep the family, let longevity be the luxury. The right detail dates a face to no era.

**Canon** – the Lemtosh and Miltzen frames; the Orchard Street origin; five generations of family ownership.

**Pull for** – eyewear, heritage, detail, craft.

**Trend read** – pending enrichment.
