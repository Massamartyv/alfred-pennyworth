# Drake's

**What** – The British house (London, 1977), founded in ties and scarves and now a full menswear label of relaxed English elegance.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. Relaxed English tailoring with sprezzatura, the soft-shouldered, slightly-rumpled elegance the operator names directly: a slight natural rumple is the point, not a flaw. Pattern mixed with ease, the clothes worn as if lived in.

**The proposition** – Tailoring with the starch taken out. Soft construction, mixed patterns and a studied ease that says the clothes are worn, not staged.

**Canon** – the origin in ties and scarves (1977); the soft-tailored menswear; the easy-elegance editorial world.
