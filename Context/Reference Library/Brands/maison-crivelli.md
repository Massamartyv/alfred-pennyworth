# Maison Crivelli

**What** – The Parisian independent niche house (2018, founder Thibaud Crivelli), built around "olfactive shocks" – sensory experiences from the founder's travels rendered in unexpected, nature-forward materials.

**Why aligned** – The reference for fragrance as a captured sensory shock. Crivelli turns specific travel moments – volcanic, tropical, humid – into compositions with collaborating perfumers, the conceptual, experience-led edge of new niche.

**The proposition** – Bottle the shock. Anchor each scent in a precise sensory memory and reach for the unexpected raw material; concept leads, convention follows.

**Canon** – Rose Saltifolia; Tolu Aromatique; the olfactive-shock concept and its invented-name compositions.

**Pull for** – niche-fragrance, olfactive-shock, nature-forward, concept-led.
