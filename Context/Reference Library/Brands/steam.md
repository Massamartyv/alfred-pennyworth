# Steam

**What** – Valve's digital storefront and distribution platform for PC games, launched in 2003 under Gabe Newell's Valve. Built first to patch and deliver Valve's own games, it became the dominant marketplace for PC gaming.

**Why aligned** – The reference for owning the channel and letting the community do the merchandising. Steam turned distribution into the business – storefront, library, automatic updates, user reviews, mod hosting through the Workshop, and the seasonal sale as a cultural event. The platform compounds through network effects: more players draw more developers, which draw more players.

**The proposition** – Own the rails, then hand the community the keys. Build the channel that sits between maker and buyer, and let user reviews, wishlists and the sale calendar carry the merchandising you would otherwise pay for. Convenience and selection become the moat; the storefront becomes the habit.

**Canon** – the Steam seasonal sale; user reviews and wishlists; the Steam Workshop for mods; the Steam Deck (2022) as the platform made portable hardware.

**Pull for** – platform-ecosystem, distribution, community, merchandising, positioning.
