# ARKET

**What** – The H&M Group's Nordic line (2017), a modern market hall of considered basics across fashion and home, complete with an in-store café.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Foundation tier of creative-director.md. Considered Nordic basics: quality everyday pieces in a restrained palette, the accessible foundation layer carried with a design point of view. The calm, curated end of the affordable wardrobe, aligned to the operator's restraint and earth-tone base.

**The proposition** – A modern market hall. Assemble essentials across fashion, home and a café under one calm Nordic aesthetic. Accessible quality held together by curation.

**Canon** – the market-hall retail concept; the in-store café; the muted Scandinavian essentials.

**Pull for** – scandinavian, basics, market-hall, accessible.
