# Olive and Sinclair

**What** – Southern artisan chocolate, founded 2007 in Nashville by Scott Witherow, the first bean-to-bar maker in Tennessee and the American South. Signature Southern flavors – buttermilk white chocolate, bourbon-nib brittle.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for chocolate, and it pulls on the Southern-American thread in the cooking. Place-rooted craft – the chocolate tastes like where it is from, heritage worn as flavor.

**The proposition** – Make chocolate that could only come from the South. Stone-ground, slow-conched, flavored with bourbon, buttermilk and brown butter. Regional identity as the differentiator.

**Canon** – "Southern Artisan Chocolate," Nashville. buttermilk white chocolate. bourbon-nib brittle. the Fatherland Street factory.

**Pull for** – southern, place-rooted, craft, heritage, bean-to-bar.

**Trend read** – A reference for regionally rooted American craft chocolate. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
