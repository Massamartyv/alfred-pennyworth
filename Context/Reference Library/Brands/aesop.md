# Aesop

**Who** – The grooming house that earns alignment across two domains – named in the grooming brands of creative-director.md and again in the Ideal Bathroom, "products like Aesop integrated into the design."

**Why aligned** – The reference for a product that is also a piece of the room. Aesop is held up because the object carries the same restraint as the space around it – amber glass, considered type, a muted apothecary austerity that reads as part of the architecture rather than clutter on the counter. It proves a principle the whole compound runs on: the everyday object can be designed with as much rigour as the room it sits in.

**The proposition** – Design the product to belong to the space. A grooming object earns its place when it can sit on the shelf as decor – restraint in the bottle, restraint on the label, no shouting. The packaging is part of the interior, not a thing to hide. Make the daily ritual something the eye is glad to meet. Evidence of care over evidence of cost, rendered in amber glass.

**Canon** – the amber-glass apothecary bottle; the considered store interiors; the bathroom-integrated range.

**Pull for** – design-conscious products, ritual, restraint, interior integration, grooming.
