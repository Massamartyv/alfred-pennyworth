# Baserange

**What** – The French-Danish label (2012), undyed and natural-fibre essentials in soft tonal palettes, with sustainability at the centre.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Mid tier of creative-director.md. Natural-fibre essentials in muted tones, the undyed base layer that fits the operator's fabric-first standard and his cream-and-earth-tone foundation. The most basic garment treated as the most considered one.

**The proposition** – Strip the colour and the synthetic out. Undyed natural fibres, tonal neutrals and a sustainability ethic turn the base layer into the considered layer.

**Canon** – the bamboo and natural-fibre basics; the undyed tonal palette; the soft-essentials catalogue.

**Pull for** – natural-fibre, sustainability, basics, restraint.
