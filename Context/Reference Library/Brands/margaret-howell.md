# Margaret Howell

**What** – The British designer and house (1970), understated British utility built on fine fabrics, with the MHL line as its workwear arm.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. Quiet British utility: impeccable cloth, functional shapes, nothing surplus. Restraint rendered as a national accent, aligned to the operator's fabric-first standard and his preference for pieces that reward closer attention rather than demand it.

**The proposition** – Function, fabric and nothing extra. Perfect the shirt, the mac and the knit, then leave them alone. Understatement as the whole signature.

**Canon** – the shirting; the MHL utility line; the mid-century British sensibility.

**Pull for** – british, utility, restraint, fabric-first.
