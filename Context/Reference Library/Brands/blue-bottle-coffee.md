# Blue Bottle Coffee

**What** – Third-wave specialty coffee roaster, founded 2002 in Oakland by clarinetist-turned-roaster James Freeman, who began in a potting shed with a pledge to sell beans within forty-eight hours of roasting.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for coffee. Freshness-first, minimalist, craft-led – the disciplined, evidence-of-care ethos applied to the cup, with a clean Apple-adjacent aesthetic that fits the wider taste.

**The proposition** – Freshness as the founding constraint. Roast small, sell fast, brew with precision, strip the branding to near-nothing. Let the coffee and the ritual carry it.

**Canon** – the Temescal potting-shed origin. the forty-eight-hour freshness pledge. the New Orleans-style iced coffee. the later Nestlé majority acquisition.

**Pull for** – third-wave, freshness-first, minimalism, craft, ritual.
