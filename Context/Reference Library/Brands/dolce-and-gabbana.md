# Dolce and Gabbana

**What** – The fragrance line of the Italian house Dolce and Gabbana (line launched 1992), mainstream luxury designer scent with a Mediterranean, Southern-Italian sensibility.

**Why aligned** – The reference for warm, sensory, Italian-coded designer fragrance done at scale. Light Blue became one of the best-selling scents in the world, and The One's tobacco-amber is a masculine benchmark. Accessible luxury with a clear regional identity.

**The proposition** – Bottle a place and a feeling. Mediterranean warmth, citrus and florals, broadly wearable and unmistakably Italian – designer scent with a sense of home.

**Canon** – Light Blue (2001); The One (2006 women, 2008 men); the Velvet Collection as the rarified tier.

**Pull for** – designer-fragrance, mediterranean, italian, accessible-luxury.
