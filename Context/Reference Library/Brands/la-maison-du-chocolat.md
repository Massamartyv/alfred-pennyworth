# La Maison du Chocolat

**What** – French luxury chocolate house, founded 1977 on Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré in Paris by Robert Linxe, among the first to position chocolate as high gastronomy rather than confectionery.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for chocolate. Linxe gave chocolate the rigor of fine wine, and the house ganache remains the signature – refinement and restraint over spectacle, the quiet-luxury instinct in a praline.

**The proposition** – The ganache as the whole argument. Treat chocolate with the seriousness of a sommelier, balance and restraint over sweetness, the Paris house as the standard.

**Canon** – the Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré house (1977). Robert Linxe, the master of ganache. the first US boutique, New York (1990).

**Pull for** – ganache, french-luxury, restraint, heritage, chocolate-as-gastronomy.
