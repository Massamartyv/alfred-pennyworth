# Isabel Marant

**What** – The French house (Paris, 1994), shorthand for Parisian off-duty cool: effortless, slightly bohemian, lived-in.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. The literal embodiment of sprezzatura, the studied nonchalance the style thesis is built on. The Parisienne who looks thrown-together is in fact precisely engineered, which is the operator's effortless-over-effort principle stated as a whole wardrobe.

**The proposition** – Effort concealed is the entire craft. Engineer nonchalance so the result looks accidental. The French off-duty uniform is a designed illusion, not a lucky one.

**Canon** – the Étoile diffusion line; the concealed-wedge sneaker; the bohemian Parisian tailoring.

**Pull for** – parisian, effortless, bohemian, sprezzatura.
