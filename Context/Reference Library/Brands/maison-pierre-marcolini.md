# Maison Pierre Marcolini

**What** – Belgian luxury chocolate house, founded 1995 in Brussels by Pierre Marcolini, the only Belgian to win the World Pastry Championship. Direct bean-to-bar sourcing since 2001.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for chocolate. Marcolini treats chocolate with the precision and seasonality of haute cuisine – evidence of care over evidence of cost, the maker as a name rather than a factory.

**The proposition** – Chocolate as fine gastronomy. Source the bean directly, work it like a pastry chef works a season, present it like a jeweler. Restraint and rigor over volume.

**Canon** – the flagship boutique at Place du Grand Sablon, Brussels. seasonal chocolate collections. bean-to-bar since 2001.

**Pull for** – haute-chocolate, craft-led, seasonality, provenance, restraint.
