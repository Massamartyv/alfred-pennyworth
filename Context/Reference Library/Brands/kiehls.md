# Kiehl's

**What** – The prestige skincare house with an apothecary lineage, founded 1851 as a pharmacy at 13th and Third in New York's East Village; family-run for over a century before L'Oréal acquired it in 2000.

**Why aligned** – The apothecary register applied to hygiene – pharmaceutical-grade formulation, a try-before-you-buy sampling ethos that predated modern beauty sampling, and the East Village flagship as a destination. The reference for science-led skincare wrapped in heritage rather than hype.

**The proposition** – Lead with formulation and let the apothecary heritage do the marketing. Sample generously, keep the labels plain and clinical, let the product earn the loyalty.

**Canon** – the Creme de Corps and Ultra Facial Cream; the 1851 East Village apothecary; the plain clinical packaging and sampling ethos.

**Pull for** – apothecary, heritage, formulation.
