# Levain Bakery

**What** – New York bakery, founded 1995 on the Upper West Side by Constance McDonald and Pamela Weekes, friends who met training for an Ironman. Famous for one thing above all – the six-ounce chocolate-chip walnut cookie.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster as a shippable bakery. The lesson is the single perfect object – one cookie so good it built a destination, evidence of care concentrated into a single item. Bakery over confectionery, exactly the documented preference.

**The proposition** – Make one thing untouchable. The dense, underbaked, six-ounce cookie as a cult object, the rest of the case in its orbit. Depth over breadth.

**Canon** – the six-ounce chocolate-chip walnut cookie. the 1997 New York Times rave. the original Upper West Side shop.

**Pull for** – signature-product, cult-object, indulgence, craft, focus.
