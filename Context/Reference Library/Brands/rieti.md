# Rieti

**What** – A Seoul eyewear brand out of the Seongsu design district, pitching "a modern European mood harmonised with Korean sensibility" across sleek classics, Y2K wraparounds and transparent frames, worn by Korean recording artists.

**Why aligned** – The accessible end of the operator's detail register, eyewear staged like a gallery object. Frames presented on trays beside Kinfolk books, priced as a friend rather than a flex, current Korean cool as a live reference. The finishing detail made attainable without cheapening it.

**The proposition** – Stage the accessory like art and price it like a friend. A showroom in the gallery register plus a sub-40,000-won entry point makes taste accessible without diluting it.

**Canon** – the Kinfolk Seongsu flagship; the artist following across Korean music and screen; the transparent and pastel frame range.

**Pull for** – eyewear, korean, accessible-cool, detail.
