# Lululemon

**What** – The Canadian athletic label (Vancouver, 1998), technical performance and yoga apparel, carried by the Align legging and its proprietary fabrics.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Repositioned tier of creative-director.md, scoped explicitly to gym and activewear only. It earns its place by being best-in-class inside one lane and by enforcing the operator's own discipline: athletic fabrics belong in the gym, not the everyday wardrobe. The reference for owning a category completely and staying in it.

**The proposition** – Own the lane completely. Engineer the fabric for movement and become the category standard, then keep the category to where performance actually matters.

**Canon** – the Align legging; the technical fabrics (Luon, Nulu); the yoga-origin Vancouver brand.

**Pull for** – activewear, technical, performance, repositioned.
