# Mast Brothers

**What** – American craft chocolate brand, founded 2007 in Brooklyn by Rick and Michael Mast, widely credited with popularizing bean-to-bar chocolate and its artisan aesthetics in the US.

**Why aligned** – The reference value is the design – their patterned paper wrappers reset what craft-chocolate packaging could look like and seeded a whole movement. Held with a caveat, not as a clean ideal.

**The proposition** – Sell the story and the surface as much as the bar. Beautifully patterned packaging, the bearded-maker narrative, bean-to-bar as a lifestyle. The aesthetic was the innovation.

**Canon** – the patterned-paper wrappers. the Brooklyn craft-chocolate aesthetic. **The caveat** – a 2015 controversy alleged the brothers remelted industrial couverture in their early years while marketing as bean-to-bar; they said the practice ended by 2009 and denied mislabeling. A study in how a story can outrun the substance.

**Pull for** – packaging-design, craft-aesthetics, brand-storytelling, cautionary.

**Trend read** – Cited now as much for the authenticity controversy as for the design – a reminder that the narrative has to be true. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
