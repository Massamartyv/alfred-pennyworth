# Creed

**What** – The Anglo-French niche house in Paris, ultra-premium and handcrafted in small batches. The house dates itself to 1760, though that heritage is contested; the modern house was built by Olivier Creed from the 1970s.

**Why aligned** – The reference for aristocratic, heritage-coded scent sold without advertising. Creed trades on a 250-year story and small-batch craft, and its signatures, Green Irish Tweed and Aventus, became the template for the modern masculine status fragrance. A study in heritage as positioning, whether inherited or constructed.

**The proposition** – Sell the legacy and the craft, never the ad. Handcraft in small batches, price at the ceiling, and let the story and the signature scent carry it.

**Canon** – Green Irish Tweed (1985); Aventus (2010); the contested 1760 origin claim; the 2023 acquisition.

**Pull for** – niche-fragrance, heritage-coded, status-scent, masculine.
