# Clare Paint

**What** – The direct-to-consumer paint brand founded 2018 by interior designer Nicole Gibbons, built against the big-box paint wall: a tightly curated, designer-selected color system in place of a thousand chips, in premium Zero-VOC, GREENGUARD Gold-certified formulation. One of the most visible early Black-founded DTC home brands, backed by $11M-plus from First Round Capital, Bullish and Pendulum.

**Why aligned** – Decision design. Clare's proposition is that curation is the product – the expert edit that removes the paralysis of infinite choice – which is the Reference Library's own operating theory applied to paint. The designer's taste sold as the shortcut.

**The proposition** – Edit ruthlessly, then stand behind the edit. A constrained palette chosen by a professional eye beats an exhaustive one chosen by nobody; health-grade formulation as table stakes, not upsell. The brand note worth tracking: Gibbons has exited (per her own LinkedIn, around 2024) while the company operates on under the Clare Paint name – the acquirer is undisclosed in every source checked. A founder-story brand now running past its founder.

**Canon** – the curated color system itself; the Zero-VOC, GREENGUARD Gold formulation; the 2018 launch as a DTC category marker.

**Pull for** – curated-palette, color-and-light, decision-design, restraint, positioning.
