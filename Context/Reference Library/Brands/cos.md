# COS

**What** – The elevated arm of the H&M Group (2007), architectural minimalism at an accessible price.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Mid tier of creative-director.md. Architectural minimalism made democratic: clean lines, considered proportion, a gallery-toned palette, the everyday pieces that read like quiet luxury for a fraction of the cost. The base layer with a point of view, aligned to the operator's restraint and earth-tone foundation.

**The proposition** – Minimalism democratised. Sculptural, restrained design at a mid price, the daily staples that carry a quiet-luxury read without the quiet-luxury invoice.

**Canon** – the architectural knitwear and trousers; the gallery-toned palette; the H&M Group's elevated line.

**Pull for** – minimalism, architectural, accessible, restraint.
