# Innersense

**What** – The clean professional-grade haircare brand (founded 2006 by salon hairstylists Greg and Joanne Starkman in California), built salon-first and free of parabens, silicones, sulfates and synthetic fragrance.

**Why aligned** – Clean haircare with trade credibility, not influencer hype. The Starkmans launched well ahead of the clean-beauty wave and built through salon professionals first, earning credibility inside the trade before the consumer. The reference for channel as strategy: prove it with the pros, then scale.

**The proposition** – Earn the trade before the timeline. Build a clean brand through salon professionals so the credibility is real, and let consumer demand follow the pros.

**Canon** – the salon-first distribution; the paraben, silicone, sulfate and synthetic-fragrance-free formulation; the 2006 founding ahead of the clean-beauty curve.

**Pull for** – clean-haircare, salon-led, professional-grade.
