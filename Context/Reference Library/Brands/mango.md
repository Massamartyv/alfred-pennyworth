# Mango

**What** – The Barcelona-founded high-street house (1984), a Mediterranean counterpoint to fast fashion's churn, increasingly pitched at elevated, on-trend wardrobe staples at an accessible price.

**Why aligned** – Foundation-tier fashion that reads above its price, which is the operator's principle that fabric and proportion matter more than the label and that base layers can come cheap when cut and styled with intention. Mediterranean ease sits in the DNA, aligned to the warm-weather and earth-tone direction of the wardrobe.

**The proposition** – Elevate the high street with a point of view. Mediterranean warmth, of-the-moment tailoring and a consistent aesthetic let an accessible brand punch above its tier.

**Canon** – the Barcelona origin; the women's tailoring and linen anchoring the recent elevation; the Mango Man line.

**Pull for** – high-street, mediterranean, accessible, foundation-fashion.
