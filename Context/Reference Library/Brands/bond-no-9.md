# Bond No. 9

**What** – The American niche house (2003, founder Laurice Rahmé), named for its boutique at 9 Bond Street, where each fragrance maps a New York neighbourhood, landmark or moment.

**Why aligned** – The reference for place as the organizing concept. Bond No. 9 turns New York into a fragrance atlas, a scent per neighbourhood, in bold, collectible, pop-art bottles. Concept and city as the whole brand.

**The proposition** – Map a city in scent. Give every fragrance a place and a story, make the bottle collectible, and let the cartography be the brand.

**Canon** – Chinatown; New Haarlem; the neighbourhood-atlas concept; the star-shaped collectible bottles.

**Pull for** – niche-fragrance, new-york, place-as-concept, collectible.
