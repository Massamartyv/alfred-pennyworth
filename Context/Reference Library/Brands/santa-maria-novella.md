# Santa Maria Novella

**Who** – The Florentine apothecary that appears in both the fragrance and the grooming lists of creative-director.md – a heritage house earning alignment across two categories at once.

**Why aligned** – The reference for provenance as the proposition. Santa Maria Novella is one of the oldest pharmacies in the world, and the alignment is exactly that depth of lineage – formulas and rituals carried forward for centuries, the opposite of a brand invented for a launch. It sits at the heritage end of both fragrance and grooming because it embodies a value the operator holds across every domain: real history cannot be performed, only inherited.

**The proposition** – Let centuries of lineage do the talking. A heritage apothecary earns trust because the formula and the ritual predate the marketing by a very long way – authenticity that cannot be manufactured. Carry the same craft across fragrance and grooming alike. The depth is the product, worn with the ease of something that never needed to announce itself.

**Canon** – the centuries-old Florentine apothecary; the heritage fragrance formulas; the cross-category grooming range.

**Pull for** – heritage craft, apothecary, cross-category alignment, ritual, fragrance.
