# Burlap and Barrel

**What** – Single-origin spice company, founded 2016 in New York by former Michelin-kitchen chef and aid worker Ethan Frisch with Ori Zohar. Direct-trade sourcing from small farmers worldwide.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for spices. It repositions spices as a specialty ingredient on par with single-origin coffee or chocolate – the best ingredients that do the least harm, provenance and ethics built into the supply chain.

**The proposition** – Source spice the way third-wave coffee sources beans. Direct from the farmer, single origin, traceable, fresher and more potent than the commodity jar. Quality and equity in the same act.

**Canon** – the Afghan cumin origin story. the direct-trade model. the Shark Tank appearance.

**Pull for** – single-origin, provenance, direct-trade, ethics, specialty-pantry.
