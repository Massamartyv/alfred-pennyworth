# PlayStation

**What** – Sony's video game platform, launched in Japan (1994) and the West (1995), born from a collapsed Sony–Nintendo CD-ROM partnership and shepherded by Ken Kutaragi, "the father of the PlayStation." The PlayStation 2 (2000) became the best-selling home console ever made.

**Why aligned** – The reference for a platform that wins on world and identity, not specifications. PlayStation built a brand language a generation reads on sight – the four-symbol controller buttons circle, cross, triangle and square, the startup chime, the austere wordmark – and anchored each generation on cinematic, exclusive worlds rather than raw horsepower. It is the case study in how an ecosystem becomes a cultural identity people belong to.

**The proposition** – Win with the world, not the hardware. Treat the platform as a curated universe – a small set of unmistakable signature properties, one consistent sensory signature across every touchpoint, an identity the owner wears. The moat is exclusivity and feeling, not the spec sheet.

**Canon** – the four-symbol controller language; the PS2 as best-selling console of all time; the DualShock controller; the cinematic first-party exclusive as the platform's signature.

**Pull for** – world-building, platform-ecosystem, brand-identity, immersive-atmosphere, high-low.
