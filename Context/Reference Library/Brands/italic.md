# Italic

**What** – The marketplace founded 2018 by Jeremy Cai, selling unbranded goods made in the same factories that supply established luxury houses – apparel, accessories and a confirmed-active home line spanning bedding, towels, candles, kitchenware and knives – sold near cost, historically through a paid membership. Roughly $18M in 2025 sales on $86.9M raised from Index Ventures, Ludlow Ventures and Comcast Ventures.

**Why aligned** – The anti-logo thesis run to its logical extreme. Where the fashion shelf of this library holds quiet luxury and logo avoidance as aesthetic postures, Italic strips the brand off entirely and sells the manufacturing itself – the claim that quality lives in the factory and the material, not the mark. A retail expression of craft-over-brand.

**The proposition** – Same factory, no logo. Pay for the making, not the marque; let the object stand without its badge. The home categories are where the model reads most naturally – bedding, towels and kitchen tools are bought for hand-feel and performance, and reviewers consistently single out exactly those (sateen and percale bedding, plush towels, candles, knives). The membership mechanic is the trade: access economics in place of brand premium.

**Canon** – the logoless model itself; the November 2018 launch; the home line as the model's most natural fit.

**Pull for** – anti-logo, quality-over-brand, materiality, proposition, merchandising.
