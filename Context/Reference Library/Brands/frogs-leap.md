# Frog's Leap

**What** – Organic Napa Valley winery, founded 1981 in St. Helena by John Williams and Larry Turley, now at the Red Barn Ranch in Rutherford. In 1989 it became the first Napa winery to earn CCOF organic certification. Dry-farmed.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for wine. Organic, dry-farmed and vocally sustainable – the natural-materials, least-harm ethos that governs the wider taste, applied to the vineyard. New World, principled, and quietly witty about it.

**The proposition** – Farm right and let it show. Dry-farming and certified-organic viticulture as the whole philosophy, restraint in the cellar, a sense of humor on the label – the name a pun on Stag's Leap.

**Canon** – the Rutherford Red Barn Ranch. the first CCOF organic certification in Napa (1989). dry-farmed Sauvignon Blanc and Cabernet Sauvignon.

**Pull for** – organic, dry-farmed, sustainability, new-world, principled-craft.

**Trend read** – A long-standing reference for organic, dry-farmed sustainable winemaking in Napa. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
