# FanDuel

**What** – The daily fantasy sports company turned sportsbook, founded in Edinburgh (2009) by a five-person team led by Nigel Eccles, pivoting out of a prior prediction-market site, Hubdub. After the United States Supreme Court struck down the federal ban on sports betting (2018), FanDuel became one of the dominant American online sportsbooks, controlled by Flutter Entertainment.

**Why aligned** – The reference for riding a category-defining regulatory shift with a product already in motion. FanDuel spent a decade building a sports-betting-adjacent audience and brand through daily fantasy; when the law opened, it converted that head start into market leadership rather than starting cold. It is the case study in being positioned before the door opens.

**The proposition** – Build the audience before the market is legal to serve, then convert the moment it opens. Own a defensible adjacent product, accumulate the users and the brand trust, and treat the regulatory unlock as a conversion event you are already staged for. Distribution and timing beat the better mousetrap.

**Canon** – the daily fantasy sports format; the Hubdub prediction-market origin; the 2018 pivot to sportsbook after the federal ban fell; the Flutter Entertainment parentage.

**Pull for** – positioning, product-pivot, conversion, category-leadership, offer-design.
