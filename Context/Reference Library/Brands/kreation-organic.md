# Kreation Organic

**What** – Organic juicery and café, founded 2007 in Santa Monica by Tehran-born Marjan Sarshar, grown from cold-pressing juice in her garage to more than a dozen Los Angeles locations. Persian-Mediterranean wellness sensibility.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster as the juice pick. Organic, cold-pressed, health-forward and culturally rooted – the morning nutritional ritual the perfect-day routine reaches for, delicious and considerate at once.

**The proposition** – Wellness without the clinical chill. Cold-pressed organic juice and Persian-Mediterranean food, the cafe as a daily health ritual rather than a cleanse gimmick.

**Canon** – cold-pressed juices and tonics. the Montana Avenue origin. the Persian-Mediterranean organic menu.

**Pull for** – organic, cold-pressed, wellness, juice, ritual.
