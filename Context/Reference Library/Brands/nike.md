# Nike

**Who** – The American athletic giant carried in the mid tier of creative-director.md with a tight constraint – "ACG, Tech Fleece, Dunks, AF1s – selectively." Alignment by specific sub-line, not by swoosh.

**Why aligned** – The reference for engaging a mass brand on the operator's terms. The pull is the handful of lines that hold proportion and craft – the technical ACG outerwear, the clean Dunk and AF1 silhouettes – used as the street-and-architectural leg of an outfit, not as a logo statement. It earns its place by being the low end of a deliberate high-low mix, chosen surgically rather than worn whole.

**The proposition** – Take the line, not the brand. Even a ubiquitous label holds a few pieces with real design integrity – isolate them and ignore the catalogue. Selectivity is the whole discipline: the value is in restraint, the willingness to wear a $90 sneaker beside investment-tier cloth and let the mix do the talking. High-low works only when the low end is chosen with the same rigour as the high.

**Canon** – ACG technical outerwear; Tech Fleece; the Dunk; the Air Force 1.

**Pull for** – selective alignment, sub-line curation, sneaker, high-low, fashion.
