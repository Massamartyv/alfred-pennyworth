# Louis Vuitton

**What** – The LVMH flagship (founded 1854 as a Parisian trunk-maker), the most valuable luxury brand in the world, and named in the fragrance tier of creative-director.md. Trunk heritage carried into a global fashion and leather house.

**Why aligned** – The case study in high-low done at the largest possible scale. Virgil Abloh's menswear tenure (2018-2021) brought streetwear architecture into the most establishment house alive, the exact seam the style thesis is built on, and Pharrell Williams continues it as menswear creative director. The Monogram works as heritage and street signal at once.

**The proposition** – Heritage is a platform for reinvention, not a museum. Hand the codes to the most credible cultural directors and let them translate a 170-year trunk house into the present without breaking the craft. Scale and subculture can share one logo.

**Canon** – the trunk and the Monogram canvas; the Virgil Abloh menswear era (2018-2021); Pharrell Williams as menswear creative director (2023-); the fragrance line.

**Pull for** – investment-luxury, high-low, world-building, craft-led.
