# Stüssy

**What** – The American streetwear original (early 1980s), born from California surf and the hand-scrawled signature of Shawn Stussy, the brand that effectively wrote the streetwear playbook.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Mid tier of creative-director.md. The foundational streetwear house and the base of the operator's streetwear-architecture pole. Its currency is decades of subcultural credibility rather than price, the original community brand whose logo still reads as cool because the lineage is real.

**The proposition** – Build the network, own the origin. Streetwear's first community brand, where authenticity is the accumulated decades of credibility, not the markup.

**Canon** – the Shawn Stussy hand-scrawled signature; the surf-to-streetwear lineage; the International Stussy Tribe.

**Pull for** – streetwear, surf, heritage, high-low.
