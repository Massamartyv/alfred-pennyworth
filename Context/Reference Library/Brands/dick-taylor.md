# Dick Taylor Craft Chocolate

**What** – Small-batch bean-to-bar maker, founded 2010 in Eureka, California by former carpenters and boat-builders Adam Dick and Dustin Taylor. Single-origin, two-ingredient dark chocolate.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for chocolate. The woodworkers' eye carried into chocolate – process and materiality treated with craftsman's rigor, evidence of care in every step. Honest, restrained, made by hand.

**The proposition** – Two ingredients, single origin, nothing to hide behind. Bring boat-builder precision to the bean, let the craft of the process be the product. Restraint as the flex.

**Canon** – single-origin bars. the Eureka, California workshop. the carpenter-to-chocolatier maker story.

**Pull for** – single-origin, maker-craft, materiality, restraint, two-ingredient.
