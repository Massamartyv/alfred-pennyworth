# Diaspora Co.

**What** – Single-origin Indian spice company, founded 2017 by Mumbai-born Sana Javeri Kadri, sourcing some thirty spices from roughly 150 farms across India and Sri Lanka. Bay Area based.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for spices, and the closest to the values in the work – an anti-colonial reframing of the spice trade, equitable farmer pricing and a vivid brand identity. Ethics, provenance and design in one move.

**The proposition** – Rewrite the spice trade. Pay farmers many times the commodity rate, name the origin and the grower, and dress it in a brand identity as bold as the turmeric. Justice as a product feature, not a footnote.

**Canon** – the single-origin Pragati turmeric. the equitable-sourcing model. the vivid, color-saturated brand identity.

**Pull for** – single-origin, equity, anti-colonial, brand-identity, provenance.
