# Dr. Bronner's

**What** – The castile-soap company (founded 1948 by Emanuel Bronner, based in Vista, California), best known for the multi-use Pure-Castile Peppermint soap and the manifesto-covered label.

**Why aligned** – The reference for a hygiene product that is also a belief system. The 18-in-1 castile soap is genuinely best-in-class, and the dense "All-One!" label turns a bottle into a manifesto, so the ethics are inseparable from the product. Family-owned across five generations, profits reinvested in fair trade and social causes.

**The proposition** – Make the everyday object carry the whole worldview. A soap can top its category on quality and stand as a statement of values at once; the label is the conscience worn on the outside.

**Canon** – the Pure-Castile Peppermint liquid soap; the "All-One!" manifesto label; the 18-in-1 multi-use claim; five generations of family ownership.

**Pull for** – values-led, heritage, ritual.
