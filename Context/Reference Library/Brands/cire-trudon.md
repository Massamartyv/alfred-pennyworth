# Cire Trudon

**What** – The oldest candle maker still operating (Paris, 1643; named Cire Trudon in 2007), which entered personal fragrance as Maison Trudon in 2017 with a five-scent collection.

**Why aligned** – The reference for deep heritage authority extended carefully into scent. Cire Trudon's candle lineage runs to 1643 and the court of Versailles; the move into eau de parfum is recent and deliberately narrow, the heritage lending the fragrance instant credibility.

**The proposition** – Let centuries of authority carry a careful expansion. Build the fragrance line slowly off a candle heritage no one can fake; scarcity and lineage do the talking.

**Canon** – the 1643 candle house; the 2017 Maison Trudon debut (Bruma, Mortel, Révolution among the five); the beeswax heritage.

**Pull for** – heritage, candle-house, lineage, restraint.
