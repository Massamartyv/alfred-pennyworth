# Salt and Stone

**What** – The premium body-care brand (founded 2017 by Nima Jalali, a former professional snowboarder), anchored by natural aluminium-free deodorant and expanded into skincare and SPF.

**Why aligned** – Clean personal care that refuses the clean-beauty look. Salt and Stone leads on elevated fragrance and chic minimalist packaging rather than the aluminium-free claim – performance and sensory pleasure together, described internally as the Apple of personal care. The reference for taking a utilitarian category and making it aspirational through design and scent.

**The proposition** – Do not lead with the virtue, lead with the pleasure. Make the non-toxic product the most beautiful and best-smelling on the shelf, and let clean be the quiet baseline, not the headline.

**Canon** – the aluminium-free deodorant; the elevated fragrance and pastel-neutral minimalist packaging; the skincare and SPF extensions.

**Pull for** – deodorant, elevated-design, fragrance-led.
