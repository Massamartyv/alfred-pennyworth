# Hermès

**Who** – The French house that earns alignment across more than one domain – named in creative-director.md for accessories, scarves and small leather in fashion, and again for homeware, tableware and blankets in the home.

**Why aligned** – Proof that a single standard of craft can travel across categories without dilution. The pull is not the brand as status but the brand as a guarantee that the object was made with care – the scarf, the small leather, the blanket held to the same bar. It is alignment chosen by category, not wholesale: the parts that carry the craft, not the parts that carry the flex.

**The proposition** – Let craft be the through-line, not the logo. A house earns cross-category trust by holding one standard everywhere it operates – the tableware made with the same rigour as the leather. Buy the category where the craft is densest and ignore the rest. Evidence of care over evidence of cost, even at the top of the price ladder.

**Canon** – the silk scarves; the small leather goods; the homeware, tableware and blankets.

**Pull for** – investment-luxury, craft-led direction, cross-category alignment, accessories, homeware.
