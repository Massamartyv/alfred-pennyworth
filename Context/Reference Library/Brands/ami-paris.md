# Ami Paris

**What** – The French label founded by Alexandre Mattiussi (2011), accessible Parisian chic carried by the Ami de Coeur heart logo.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. Everyday Parisian wardrobe with warmth: friendly minimalism, the heart used as a small signal rather than a billboard. The proof that elevated does not have to mean cold, aligned to the restraint and earth-tone foundation of the style thesis.

**The proposition** – Make luxury feel like a friend. Approachable Parisian staples, elevated in fabric and cut, never aloof. Ami de Coeur as warmth made into a mark.

**Canon** – the Ami de Coeur heart logo; the relaxed Parisian tailoring; Mattiussi's everyday-wardrobe thesis.

**Pull for** – parisian, accessible-luxury, everyday-chic, restraint.
