# Loewe

**Who** – The Spanish house of leather and ready-to-wear, named first in the investment tier of creative-director.md, the $300-and-above shelf where primary fashion alignment sits.

**Why aligned** – The clearest argument that heritage craft and contemporary art direction are not in tension. Loewe leads with leather and the hand of the maker, then lets a clear creative vision carry it forward without sliding into logo noise. It sits at the front of the investment tier because it holds the line the whole wardrobe is built on – fabric and craft as the centrepiece, the mark as an afterthought.

**The proposition** – Build the object around the material and the craft, then let a singular creative vision do the editorialising. Heritage is a foundation to push against, not a costume to wear. The result reads as luxury to those who know and as quiet good taste to those who do not – the depth-worn-with-ease principle in leather.

**Canon** – the Spanish leather craft heritage; the artistically directed ready-to-wear; the small leather goods.

**Pull for** – investment-luxury, craft-led direction, materiality, artistic-direction, fashion.
