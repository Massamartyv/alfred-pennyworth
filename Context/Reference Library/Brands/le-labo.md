# Le Labo

**What** – The American niche house (New York, 2006, Fabrice Penot and Edouard Roschi), where fragrances are blended to order in-store, hand-labelled with the buyer's name, and named by principal ingredient and ingredient count.

**Why aligned** – The reference for ritual and anti-luxury-luxury. Le Labo's raw, industrial, analogue packaging and made-to-order ceremony turn buying a scent into an experience, and Santal 33 became a defining smell of the 2010s. Premium through ritual, not gloss.

**The proposition** – Make the purchase a ritual. Blend to order, hand-label, name by ingredient, and let the anti-luxury packaging and the ceremony be the luxury.

**Canon** – Santal 33; Rose 31; Another 13; the hand-labelled made-to-order ritual.

**Pull for** – niche-fragrance, ritual, anti-luxury-luxury, experiential.
