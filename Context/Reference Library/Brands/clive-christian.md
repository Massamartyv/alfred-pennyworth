# Clive Christian

**What** – The British ultra-luxury house, founded 1999 when Clive Christian bought the Crown Perfumery (established 1872 and granted use of the royal crown by Queen Victoria).

**Why aligned** – The reference for maximalist British-aristocratic opulence: 20%-plus perfume-oil concentration, 150 to 250 ingredients per formula, the Guinness record for the world's most expensive perfume. Decadence as the entire proposition.

**The proposition** – Out-luxury everyone. Lead with rare ingredients, the highest concentrations and a royal-heritage story; let opulence be unapologetic.

**Canon** – No. 1 (the Original Collection, 2000); Imperial Majesty; the Crown Perfumery royal-crown lineage.

**Pull for** – ultra-luxury, rare-ingredient, opulence, heritage.

**Trend read** – pending enrichment.
