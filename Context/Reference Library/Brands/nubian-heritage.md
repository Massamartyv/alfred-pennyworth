# Nubian Heritage

**What** – The African black soap and shea-butter bath-and-body brand, co-founded in the early 1990s by Richelieu Dennis, Nyema Tubman and Mary Dennis, built from Harlem street-vending into the Sundial Brands house (alongside SheaMoisture; acquired by Unilever in 2017).

**Why aligned** – Ancestral formulation as the whole proposition. Black soap, shea and indigenous African beauty traditions made accessible for melanin-rich skin and natural hair – heritage wisdom turned into a modern hygiene range. The Harlem-vendor-to-Unilever arc is a study in diaspora entrepreneurship.

**The proposition** – Root the product in ancestral wisdom and the formulation tells a story no synthetic can. Heritage ingredients, fair-trade sourcing and accessibility carry the pitch.

**Canon** – the African black soap bar; the shea-butter range; the Sundial Brands lineage with SheaMoisture; the 2017 Unilever acquisition.

**Pull for** – heritage, african-black-soap, diaspora.
