# Brooklyn Circus

**What** – The American label founded by Ouigi Theodore, known as The BKc, retelling heritage Americana through a Black cultural lens.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Mid tier ($80-$300) of creative-director.md. Heritage Americana carried through a specific cultural lens, where varsity, denim and workwear hold narrative rather than nostalgia. It sits on the operator's high-low and cultural-credibility seam, the clothes as an entry point into a story.

**The proposition** – Dress a story, not a season. Reclaim American heritage style through a specific cultural lens and let the garments carry history forward.

**Canon** – the varsity and workwear pieces; Ouigi Theodore's dress-code ethos; the brownstone-rooted brand world.
