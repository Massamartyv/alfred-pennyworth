# Harney and Sons

**What** – American fine tea company, founded 1983 in Salisbury, Connecticut by John Harney, now run by his sons. More than three hundred blends, known for upmarket tin packaging and broad reach.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster for tea. Family craft scaled without losing the artisan reputation – refined packaging, deep blending range, accessible luxury that still feels considered.

**The proposition** – Master the blend and dress it well. Hundreds of blends with the depth of a specialist and the polish of a luxury brand, the tin as much a part of the appeal as the leaf.

**Canon** – more than three hundred blends. the signature tins. the family lineage through Michael and Paul Harney.
