# Puebco

**What** – The Japanese home goods brand, born 2007 (per its own About page), built on repurposed, found and recycled materials – retro-industrial objects filtered through Japanese restraint. Press consistently attributes the founding to Tokyo designer Hirotaka Tanaka, said to source reclaimed materials personally while travelling, though the brand's own channels name no founder – the attribution is corroborated but not self-confirmed. Its stated creed: material first, design second; "there are no imperfections but details meant by design." Entered Canada in 2019 through Drake General Store and opened its first North American flagship in Toronto in September 2025 – Puebco Canada is the brand's own regional arm.

**Why aligned** – Patina as a design position. Puebco makes the case that a history-bearing surface outranks a perfect one – the wabi-sabi thread of this library rendered as shoppable objects, and the textural counterweight to the minimalist shelf.

**The proposition** – Let the material lead. Source what already exists, design around its marks, and sell the imperfection as the point rather than the flaw. Distribution is its own proof of cachet – Puebco travels through the world's curated concept stores (La Garçonne, Drake General Store, UP THERE) rather than mass retail, the object vouched for by the company it keeps.

**Canon** – the recycled-material vases, bookends and tool-derived decor; the concept-store distribution network; the 2025 Toronto flagship.

**Pull for** – found-materials, patina, imperfection-as-design, materiality, high-low.
