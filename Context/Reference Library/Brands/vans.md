# Vans

**What** – The American skate institution (California, 1966), the side stripe and the checkerboard, named in creative-director.md for Old Skools and Authentics only and worked into the summer-heat outfit formula directly.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Foundation tier (under $80) of creative-director.md and called up by name in a go-to warm-weather fit. The heritage skate shoe as the high-low anchor: a cheap, credible foundation piece that grounds an otherwise considered outfit. Total credibility at the bottom of the price ladder.

**The proposition** – The democratic classic. A decades-old skate shoe that costs little and carries complete credibility. The great equaliser at the base of a look.

**Canon** – the Old Skool side stripe; the Authentic; the checkerboard slip-on; the Off the Wall skate heritage.
