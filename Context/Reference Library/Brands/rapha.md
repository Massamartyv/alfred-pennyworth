# Rapha

**What** – The British label (London, 2004), premium road-cycling apparel where performance meets genuine design taste, anchored by the Rapha Cycling Club.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Mid tier of creative-director.md, scoped to cycling. The proof that technical apparel can carry real design and a strong brand world: muted palettes, editorial restraint, performance made beautiful. The operator's athletic-with-intention register, kept to where the performance belongs.

**The proposition** – Performance gear deserves design and community. Make the technical beautiful and build a club around the ride. The kit as belonging, not just function.

**Canon** – the premium road kit; the Rapha Cycling Club (RCC); the muted editorial brand world.

**Pull for** – cycling, performance, premium, community.
