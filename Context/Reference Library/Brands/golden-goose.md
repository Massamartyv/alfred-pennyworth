# Golden Goose

**What** – The Italian label (Venice, 2000), maker of the hand-distressed Superstar sneaker, named in creative-director.md specifically for sneakers only.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Mid tier of creative-director.md, scoped to sneakers only. The lived-in luxury sneaker, pre-worn by design, the relaxed footwear that undercuts a polished fit with sprezzatura. The deliberate imperfection is the operator's natural-rumple principle applied to a shoe.

**The proposition** – Sell the patina, not the newness. A hand-distressed sneaker that arrives looking loved. Imperfection as the luxury cue.

**Canon** – the Superstar low-top; the hand-applied distressing and star motif; the Venice origin.

**Pull for** – sneaker, distressed, statement, high-low.
