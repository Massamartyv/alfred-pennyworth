# Kettl

**What** – Japanese tea importer and retailer, founded 2011 by Zach Mangan, with bases in New York and Fukuoka. Single-origin, seasonally harvested Japanese teas – matcha, gyokuro, sencha.

**Why aligned** – On the aligned-brands roster, noted specifically for genuine provenance. With tea over coffee at an eighty-twenty split, Kettl is the reference for the leaf – Japan's finest teas, sourced with a rigor analogous to fine wine.

**The proposition** – Bring the teas Japan keeps for itself. Build direct producer relationships, honor the harvest season, and treat single-origin tea with the seriousness of single-vineyard wine.

**Canon** – single-origin matcha, gyokuro and sencha. the Fukuoka producer relationships. *Stories of Japanese Tea* (2022).

**Pull for** – japanese-tea, single-origin, provenance, seasonality, connoisseurship.
