# Diptyque

**What** – The Parisian heritage house (1961, three founders, 34 Boulevard Saint-Germain), among the first to treat the scented candle as a luxury object; its first eau de toilette followed in 1968.

**Why aligned** – The reference for heritage scent with literary, botanical restraint. Diptyque's hand-drawn labels, naturalistic palette and candle-first lineage make it the credible, slightly austere end of accessible luxury – where the scented object became art.

**The proposition** – Make the everyday scent an object of taste. Treat the candle as seriously as the perfume, dress it in literary, hand-drawn restraint, and let heritage do the rest.

**Canon** – the Baies and Feu de Bois candles; Philosykos; Do Son; the 1961 Saint-Germain origin.

**Pull for** – heritage, candle-and-scent, botanical, restraint.
