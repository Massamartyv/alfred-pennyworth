# Yves Saint Laurent

**What** – The French house founded in 1961 by Yves Saint Laurent and Pierre Bergé, which put women in tailoring and redrew the line between masculine and feminine; now Saint Laurent under Anthony Vaccarello.

**Why aligned** – Le Smoking is the original argument that power lives in restraint and a perfect cut. The tuxedo as seduction through tailoring rather than exposure is the operator's "withhold, do not display" made into a garment, and the blueprint for borrowing menswear codes and making them magnetic through fit and attitude.

**The proposition** – Tailoring is the most powerful seduction. A perfectly cut line says more than any reveal. Take the codes of menswear and make them charged through precision.

**Canon** – Le Smoking (1966); the Mondrian dress (1965); the safari jacket; the Rive Gauche ready-to-wear; the lean modern Saint Laurent silhouette.

**Pull for** – tailoring, androgyny, parisian, statement.
