# Our Legacy

**What** – The Swedish label (2005), elevated wardrobe built on unusual textures and a slightly-off palette, fabric leading every decision.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. Fabric-first elevated basics, which is the operator's instruction to prioritise fabric quality over brand name made into a house style. The shapes stay wearable while the cloth does the talking: boucle, brushed wool, treated cotton, muted tones a half-step from expected.

**The proposition** – Let the cloth be the concept. Source unexpected fabrics, keep the silhouettes calm, and the wardrobe reads considered without a single logo.

**Canon** – the textured overshirts and knitwear; the boxy-but-refined silhouette; the muted off-palette.

**Pull for** – scandinavian, fabric-first, textured, restraint.
