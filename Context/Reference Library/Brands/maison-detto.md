# Maison d'Etto

**What** – The niche fragrance and candle house founded 2019 by Brianna Lipovsky, built around her equestrian life – handcrafted, genderless, deliberately animalistic scents carried by Neiman Marcus, SSENSE and FWRD. Lipovsky came to it from a decade running creative agencies for top-tier brands, and the design fluency shows in the object as much as the juice. Candles run a proprietary plant-derived wax blend with cotton wicks and roughly 70-hour burns.

**Why aligned** – A world-building fragrance house at interior scale. The equestrian thread is a genuine differentiator in a crowded niche field – a single lived narrative carried through every scent rather than a mood board – and the candle line is how the world enters the room. It sits in this sphere for the same reason Aesop does: the scent object as part of the interior.

**The proposition** – One narrative, worn deep. Build the house on a life actually lived – stable, turf, leather, animal warmth – and let the specificity do the marketing. The hero product proves it: Macanudo reads vetiver, hay absolute, cut grass and neroli as "freshly turned earth from galloping hooves." Scent as place-memory, sold by the burn hour.

**Canon** – the Macanudo candle as the hero; the wider candle line – Verdades, Rotano, Karat EG, Canaan; the Neiman Marcus, SSENSE and FWRD shelf as positioning proof.

**Pull for** – narrative, world-building, ritual, materiality, niche.
