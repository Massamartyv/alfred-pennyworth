# Alden

**What** – The American shoemaker (Massachusetts, 1884), master of shell cordovan and the Indy boot, built on the Goodyear welt.

**Why aligned** – Named in the Investment tier of creative-director.md. American heritage footwear at the level of a standard, the shell cordovan that ages into character rather than out of style. The durable, considered shoe that anchors a wardrobe built to last, which is the operator's permanence-over-trend instinct.

**The proposition** – Master one material and one method. Shell cordovan on a Goodyear welt, made in New England, sold for generations. Permanence as the luxury.

**Canon** – the Indy boot; the shell cordovan loafers and boots; the Massachusetts factory running since 1884.
