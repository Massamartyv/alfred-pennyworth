# House of Nangman

**What** – A South Korean lifestyle label whose name, 낭만 (nangman), translates loosely as romance or warmth, which the brand defines as "a moment that brings a warm smile to your face just by thinking about it." Headwear-led, with products named after emotional moments.

**Why aligned** – Naming as world-building, taken down to the smallest object. Each cap is titled after a feeling, "A warm heart," "The day before a picnic," rather than a product code, which is the operator's "build a world, not a logo" applied to a single accessory. The sentiment is the product.

**The proposition** – Sell the feeling, not the feature. Name a product after the moment it belongs to and the object becomes a keepsake. Warmth scales when it is made specific.

**Canon** – the Valentine's Day 2024 four-cap capsule, each carrying an uplifting Korean phrase; distribution through Asian fashion aggregators.

**Pull for** – naming, narrative, headwear, sentiment-led.
