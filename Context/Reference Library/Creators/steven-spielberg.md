# Steven Spielberg

**Who** – American filmmaker (born 1946), the most commercially dominant director in history and the architect of the modern blockbuster, equally fluent in spectacle and in conscience.

**Why aligned** – Named in creative-director.md among the core film references. Spielberg is the master of wonder engineered with total craft – the camera that finds awe in a child's face, the texture of feeling over the recycled image. Populist reach without surrendering the eye.

**The formula** – Stage the emotion through the camera, not the dialogue. Build awe in the reaction shot, withhold the monster, let the score carry the lump in the throat. Then alternate the crowd-pleaser with the conscience picture so neither muscle wastes.

**Canon** – *Jaws* (1975). *Raiders of the Lost Ark* (1981). *E.T.* (1982). *Jurassic Park* (1993). *Schindler's List* (1993). *Saving Private Ryan* (1998).

**Pull for** – wonder, populist-craft, world-building, spectacle, narrative.
