# Martin Scorsese

**Who** – American filmmaker (born 1942), the great chronicler of guilt, ambition and the American underworld, and cinema's most devoted living student of its own history.

**Why aligned** – Named in creative-director.md among the core film references. Scorsese marries a kinetic, music-driven camera to genuine moral weight – style that means something, the punk energy beneath the craft. The needle-drop as structure speaks straight to the former DJ.

**The formula** – Move the camera like a pulse and cut to the music. Follow the rise, sit in the seduction of the sin, then let the reckoning land. Devotion to film history worn openly, every frame in conversation with what came before.

**Canon** – *Taxi Driver* (1976). *Raging Bull* (1980). *Goodfellas* (1990). *The Departed* (2006). *The Irishman* (2019). *Killers of the Flower Moon* (2023).

**Pull for** – kinetic-camera, music-as-structure, moral-weight, auteur, narrative.

**Trend read** – Still actively making major work and the most vocal advocate for cinema as an art form against the content tide. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
