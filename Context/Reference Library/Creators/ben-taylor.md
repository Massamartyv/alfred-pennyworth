# Ben Taylor

**Who** – A New York creator covering luxury goods, menswear accessories, home and tech, at roughly 796,500 TikTok followers and 24.3M likes, with a YouTube channel and an LTK storefront. Identity and current activity are confirmed, which carries the card at active rather than watch. The reading rests on six of his videos in the operator's saved corpus plus published profile data.

**Why aligned** – Price transparency is the whole mechanism. He puts the number next to the object, every time, and lets the two sit together without resolving the tension for you. That is a rarer posture than it sounds: most luxury content either hides the price to preserve the fantasy or leads with it to sell outrage. He does neither, which turns a haul into an appraisal. For an operator working out where cost and taste genuinely diverge, that is the useful format.

**The formula** – State the price, always, and state it plainly. Compare across tiers rather than crowning a winner, so the viewer performs the judgement. Ask the room a direct question — is this bottle design worth $450 — instead of supplying the verdict. Run the same appraisal logic across categories, from wallets to watches to closets to fragrance, so the method rather than the product line is the through-line.

The Bottega Veneta bottle-design question at $450. The Cartier Santos and Love bracelet piece. The luxury wallet comparison across Saint Laurent and Louis Vuitton. A niche fragrance shortlist spanning Byredo, Celine and Louis Vuitton. The custom-closet hidden-features piece, where the same appraisal method turns on interiors.
