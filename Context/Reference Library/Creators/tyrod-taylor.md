# Tyrod Taylor

**Who** – Named in creative-director.md as a style reference for "clean athletic tailoring, fitted pieces that respect the physique without shouting." NFL quarterback who co-founded the menswear label Diallo (2021) with stylist Dex Robinson.

**Why aligned** – The athletic frame dressed with restraint, which is the operator's own build-and-silhouette principle: broad shoulders, narrow waist, fitted pieces doing the heavy lifting without tipping into skinny. Taylor reads the V-taper and tailors to it. Diallo extends the idea into a thesis, rooting new American luxury in workwear and Black labour.

**The formula** – Tailor to the athletic build: fitted, never skinny, so the physique reads through the cut rather than the logo. Anchor luxury in American workwear heritage. Self-style with conviction and let restraint signal the confidence.

**Canon** – Diallo (launched in New York, February 2021); the reimagined mesh practice jersey; Paris Men's Fashion Week appearances; repeated NFL best-dressed listings.
