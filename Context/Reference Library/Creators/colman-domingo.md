# Colman Domingo

**Who** – Actor and a named style reference in creative-director.md: "rich quiet elegance, luxurious fabrics, statement pieces worn with restraint".

**Why aligned** – The reference for the statement held under control. He wears the bold piece without letting it wear him – restraint is the frame that makes the richness land. Luxurious fabric and quiet elegance carry the depth-worn-with-ease principle into formalwear, where most men either shout or vanish.

**The formula** – Make the statement, then restrain everything around it. Anchor the look in luxurious fabric and a quiet, rich palette, then let a single bold piece carry the drama. Control is the styling – the boldness reads as taste because nothing competes with it.

**Canon** – the controlled-statement formalwear silhouette as a recurring template.

**Pull for** – art-direction, restraint, materiality, statement-with-control direction, fashion.
