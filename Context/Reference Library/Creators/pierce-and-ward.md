# Pierce and Ward

**Who** – Interior design partnership, the only studio named under the Interior Design References in creative-director.md. The thesis is exact: "constrained maximalism – they achieve sprezzatura at a high object count".

**Why aligned** – The reference that resolves the maximalism-versus-minimalism false choice. The principle is not a low object count, it is intention per square foot – when the intention density stays high, the count can rise without tripping the gaudiness wire. They are the proof that a full room can still read as taste, the deliberate-density principle of the operating system made physical.

**The formula** – Raise the object count only as fast as the intention density. The discipline is intention per square foot, not subtraction – every piece earns its place, nothing sits by default. Hold sprezzatura at scale: the room reads effortless even when it is full. The gaudiness wire is tripped by indifference, not abundance.

**Canon** – the constrained-maximalist interiors; the high-object-count rooms that still read as sprezzatura.

**Pull for** – spatial-direction, materiality, constrained-maximalism framing, intention-density, maximalism.
