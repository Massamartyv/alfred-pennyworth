# Takashi Murakami

**Who** – Japanese artist (born 1962), founder of the Superflat movement and the most fluent operator at the seam between fine art, anime and luxury fashion.

**Why aligned** – Murakami proved that anime eyes and cartoon flowers could carry full art-historical weight – he weaponized cuteness. He sits on two of the taste's own lines at once: the Japanese-modern, anime-as-sacred-gesture thread in the tattoo vision, and the cross-disciplinary force named beside Kanye, whose *Graduation* he designed.

**The formula** – Flatten the hierarchy between high art and pop. Pull from manga, anime and traditional Japanese painting into one bright, glossy surface, then run it across canvas, sculpture, album art and handbags without changing the language.

**Canon** – the Superflat manifesto. Kanye West's *Graduation* cover (2007). the Louis Vuitton collaboration with Marc Jacobs (from 2003). the DOB character and the smiling flowers.

**Pull for** – high-low, art-fashion, collaboration, anime-iconography, art-direction.
