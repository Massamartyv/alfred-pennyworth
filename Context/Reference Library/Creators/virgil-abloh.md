# Virgil Abloh

**Who** – Designer and architect, named in creative-director.md as the articulator of a governing spatial principle: "pay attention to the space a thing occupies with as much rigor as the thing itself – arguably more".

**Why aligned** – The reference for negative space as a primary material. The principle that bears his name governs the spatial philosophy directly – the room around the object matters more than the object – and it is the discipline behind "intention per square foot". Cross-disciplinary by default: the same rigour applied to cloth, space and the gap between things.

**The formula** – Design the space around the thing before the thing. Treat negative space as a material with as much rigour as the object inside it. Apply one discipline across every medium – fashion, room, architecture answer to the same logic. The restraint is active: what you leave empty is a decision, not a remainder.

**Canon** – the spatial-rigour principle itself, applied across design disciplines.

**Pull for** – spatial-direction, art-direction, restraint, negative-space framing, cross-disciplinary direction.
