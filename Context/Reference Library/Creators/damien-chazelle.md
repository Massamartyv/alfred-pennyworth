# Damien Chazelle

**Who** – American filmmaker (born 1985), the youngest winner of the Best Director Oscar, fixated on the price of artistic greatness and the rhythm of performance.

**Why aligned** – Director of *Whiplash*, already carded here, so promoting him connects the cost-of-greatness study to its maker. His whole body of work asks what excellence actually costs – the same question the taste holds, the obsession with mastery and what it takes.

**The formula** – Cut to the tempo, shoot performance as combat. Put a character in pursuit of greatness and refuse to tell you whether the price was worth it. Music and motion as the engine, ambivalence as the theme.

**Canon** – *Whiplash* (2014). *La La Land* (2016). *First Man* (2018). *Babylon* (2022).

**Pull for** – obsession, cost-of-greatness, music-and-rhythm, kinetic, narrative.
