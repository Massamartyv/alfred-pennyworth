# Bad Bunny

**Who** – Musician and a named style reference in creative-director.md: "sensual fabrics, relaxed flowing pieces, colour confidence, gender-fluid energy".

**Why aligned** – The reference for fabric and colour worn without hesitation. The relaxed, flowing silhouette and the sensual material answer the wardrobe thesis directly – clothes that move, that invite closer attention, that read poem more than uniform. The gender-fluid ease and the colour confidence give the otherwise neutral palette permission to risk a statement.

**The formula** – Let the fabric move. Choose sensual, flowing material over structure and let the silhouette relax. Carry colour without apology – confidence is the styling. Treat the codes as fluid rather than fixed, so the look reads free rather than rule-bound.

**Canon** – the relaxed, sensual, colour-forward silhouette as a recurring template.

**Pull for** – materiality, color-and-light, colour-confidence direction, relaxed-silhouette, fashion.
