# Adele

**Who** – Adele Laurie Blue Adkins, London, BRIT School class of 2006, signed to XL Recordings straight after. The age-titled album arc – 19, 21, 25, 30 – built on a formula she has never broken: the voice, the heartbreak and as little else as possible. Sixteen Grammys from 25 nominations.

**Why aligned** – Anti-trend as a career strategy. Every Adele record is deliberately less produced than its pop moment, foregrounding vocal performance and confessional lyric while peers chase the sound of the year – and the restraint wins every time. 21 (2011) is the proof at historic scale: 24 weeks at number one on the Billboard 200, tying Purple Rain and standing as the longest run ever by a female solo artist, Diamond-certified, over 31 million sold worldwide and commonly cited as the best-selling album of this century, with a record-tying six Grammys.

**The formula** – Strip everything that is not the song. One instrument-forward arrangement, one devastating lyric, one voice mixed like the room went quiet for it – then disappear until the next era has something true to say. The exits are as deliberate as the records: the Weekends with Adele residency closed November 23, 2024 and she announced an open-ended break in her own words – "I will not see you for an incredibly long time." No return confirmed as of 2026. Pull her for voice-first production values, era pacing and the power of leaving.

**Canon** – 21 and its historic run; "Rolling in the Deep," "Someone Like You," "Set Fire to the Rain"; the six-Grammy night; the residency close and the declared hiatus.

**Pull for** – restraint, voice-and-tone, anti-trend, era-pacing, walk-away-power.
