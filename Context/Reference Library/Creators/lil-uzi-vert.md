# Lil Uzi Vert

**Who** – Symere Bysil Woods (they/them), from Francisville, North Philadelphia – the SoundCloud-era original who fused rap, emo and pop-punk sensibility into the lineage rage production descends from. Luv Is Rage (2015) brought the Generation Now/Atlantic deal that has held through the whole run: Eternal Atake (2020), Pink Tape (2023) and Eternal Atake 2 (November 2024).

**Why aligned** – Genre-blur with total commitment. "XO Tour Llif3" (2017) put nihilistic emo lyricism over trap production and crossed over without apologizing to either audience – the high-low fusion this library keeps returning to, executed at radio scale. And no one commits to persona harder: the 10-carat pink diamond, reported at $24 million and paid off over years, surgically implanted in their forehead in February 2021 – reasoning that a stone set in flesh cannot be lost like a ring – ripped out crowd-surfing at Rolling Loud Miami, and as of 2026 slated for reinstallation with modifications.

**The formula** – Make the persona load-bearing. The sound blurs categories; the image work makes the artist the genre. The delay-heavy release cadence (Pink Tape arrived after a famously long tease cycle) works because the persona holds attention between drops. Eternal Atake 3 does not exist as an announced project – the record stops at EA2. Pull them for genre-fusion, persona-as-asset and the rage lineage's source code.

**Canon** – "XO Tour Llif3" (2017); Eternal Atake (2020); Pink Tape (2023); Eternal Atake 2 (2024); the pink diamond as commitment made literal.

**Pull for** – genre-blur, persona-as-asset, high-low, world-building, conviction.
