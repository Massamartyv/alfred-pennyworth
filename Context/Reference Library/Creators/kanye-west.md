# Kanye West

**Who** – Producer, designer and cross-disciplinary creative force. He appears in creative-director.md four times over – as a producer with a recognisable fingerprint, as a fashion reference for brutalist minimalism, as the lighting reference of the Sunday Service era and as one of two named cross-disciplinary forces alongside Donald Glover. *My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy* anchors the culture.md music canon.

**Why aligned** – The widest demonstration in the canon that one sensibility can govern sound, cloth, space and light at once. The work is held for the discipline, not the persona – architectural silhouettes, earth-tone restraint and light treated as a building material. The reference is the method, separated cleanly from the man.

**The formula** – Treat every medium as the same problem. Brutalist minimalism – architectural shape, neutral earth-tone palette, ornament stripped to the structure. Light is a material, not a utility, the way Sunday Service staged it. Maximalist ambition with a minimalist surface: scale the intent, discipline the finish.

**Canon** – *My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy* (2010); the Sunday Service staging; the brutalist earth-tone wardrobe.
