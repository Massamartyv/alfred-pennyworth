# Miguel

**Who** – Miguel Jontel Pimentel (b. 1985, San Pedro, California), the psychedelic-funk auteur of alternative R&B: Prince and Hendrix-school guitar texture and falsetto set against contemporary production, positioned as an album artist rather than a hooks-first radio act.

**Why aligned** – The auteur posture held for a decade-plus. Kaleidoscope Dream (2012) earned the Grammy for Best R&B Song with "Adorn" (2013) and set the lane; Wildheart (2015) and War & Leisure (2017, carrying "Sky Walker" with Travis Scott) deepened it without chasing the chart's weather. Then the boldest move: eight years of silence, ended on his own terms.

**The formula** – Destroy to rebuild, and say so. CAOS arrived October 23, 2025 – his 40th birthday – via ByStorm Entertainment and RCA, his first album since 2017, framed in his own words: "To rebuild, I had to destroy myself... CAOS is the sonic iteration of me bending that violence into something universally felt." The extraction is the auteur's pact: guard the sonic identity absolutely, release only when the work carries a life chapter, and let the gap build the meaning. Pull him for texture-led R&B, falsetto-and-guitar palettes and era pacing measured in transformations rather than quarters.

**Canon** – Kaleidoscope Dream (2012) and the "Adorn" Grammy; Wildheart (2015); War & Leisure (2017) and "Sky Walker"; CAOS (2025), the return.

**Pull for** – auteur, texture, sound-design, era-pacing, reinvention.
