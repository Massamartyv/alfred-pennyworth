# Vince Gilligan

**Who** – American writer and showrunner (born 1967), creator of *Breaking Bad* and *Better Call Saul*, the master of long-form moral transformation on television.

**Why aligned** – Creator of *Breaking Bad*, already carded here, so promoting him connects the transformation epic to its architect. Gilligan's whole thesis – turn the protagonist into the antagonist, one earned choice at a time – is the long-form discipline the taste admires, the system behind the thing built across sixty hours.

**The formula** – Make every consequence land. Plant a detail and pay it off five seasons later. Let the slow burn do the work, trusting patience over shock, until the transformation is total and the audience is complicit.

**Canon** – *Breaking Bad* (2008-2013). *Better Call Saul* (2015-2022). early work writing on *The X-Files*.

**Pull for** – long-form-structure, moral-transformation, slow-burn, consequence, narrative.
