# Quentin Tarantino

**Who** – Director, named in the creative-director.md film section for "cinematography, outrageousness, surrealism" and present in the culture.md canon as the director of *Django Unchained*: "reclaimed dignity, violent justice, Blackness as heroic force, style as resistance".

**Why aligned** – The reference for style pushed past the comfortable limit and held there on purpose. The outrageousness is not excess for its own sake – it is the surrealism and the cinematography doing the arguing. *Django* makes the case directly: style as resistance, the aesthetic itself carrying the dignity. The punk-beneath-the-composure engine, turned all the way up.

**The formula** – Earn the right to be outrageous, then commit fully. Let the cinematography and the surreal register carry meaning the plot does not state. Use style as the argument – the aesthetic is the resistance, not a coat of paint over it. Defy the genre while operating inside its grammar.

**Canon** – *Reservoir Dogs*, 1992; *Pulp Fiction*, 1994; *Django Unchained*, 2012 – each carded in Works; the surreal, cinematographic, outrageous body of direction.

**Pull for** – cinematography, visual-direction, style-as-resistance framing, maximalism, film-direction.
