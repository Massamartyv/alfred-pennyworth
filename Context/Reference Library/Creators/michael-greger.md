# Dr. Michael Greger

**Who** – American physician and nutrition author, founder of NutritionFacts.org and author of *How Not to Die*, *How Not to Diet* and *How Not to Age*. A leading public voice for evidence-based, whole-food plant-based eating.

**Why aligned** – Named in creative-director.md as one of the two parent forces of culinary creation – the nutritional science foundation under the cooking. He is the proof that delicious and health-conscious are not in tension, the discipline that keeps the laboratory of the kitchen honest. The patterns his work assembles – whole-food plant-forward eating, daily frequency over portion arithmetic, processing as the axis that matters – are written directly into the Medicine sphere charter and consultation schema as house doctrine, unattributed and standing on their own evidence. That is the correct relationship to him: take the pattern, grade each claim inside it independently, and hold no dietary position further than the evidence carries it. The man is a reference, not an authority, which is how his own method asks to be read.

**The formula** – Start from the peer-reviewed evidence, not the trend. Build the plate around what extends life – whole plants, the daily dozen – then let flavor do its work inside those rails. Science as the floor, never the ceiling.

**Canon** – *How Not to Die* (2015). *How Not to Diet* (2019). *How Not to Age* (2023). NutritionFacts.org. The Daily Dozen framework.
