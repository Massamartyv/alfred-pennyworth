# Dr. Michael Greger

**Who** – American physician and nutrition author, founder of NutritionFacts.org and author of *How Not to Die* and *How Not to Diet*. A leading public voice for evidence-based, whole-food plant-based eating.

**Why aligned** – Named in creative-director.md as one of the two parent forces of culinary creation – the nutritional science foundation under the cooking. He is the proof that delicious and health-conscious are not in tension, the discipline that keeps the laboratory of the kitchen honest.

**The formula** – Start from the peer-reviewed evidence, not the trend. Build the plate around what extends life – whole plants, the daily dozen – then let flavor do its work inside those rails. Science as the floor, never the ceiling.

**Canon** – *How Not to Die* (2015). *How Not to Diet*. NutritionFacts.org. the Daily Dozen framework.

**Pull for** – evidence-based-nutrition, health-as-foundation, plant-forward, longevity, food-science.
