# Logan Sylve

**Who** – American painter and illustrator (born 1995, New Orleans), based in New York and self-taught. Works in paint, ink, pencil and sgraffito, and built an international collector base before 30. Shows at Chase Contemporary in SoHo.

**Why aligned** – Self-taught and already international, working the exact seam the taste prizes – street illustration, fine art and animation in one body of work, high and low with no apology. New Orleans roots in a city that already shaped the cultural compass.

**The formula** – Let figures hover between states – human, animal, spectral, caught between motion and stillness, life and death. Recurring teeth, eyes and grinning faces. Comic-book and animation DNA carried into gallery painting, incised and layered.

**Canon** – solo and live-painting shows at Chase Contemporary, SoHo. *Speaking in Two Tongues*, the duo exhibition with Cristiano Mangovo at Allouche Gallery.

**Pull for** – street-to-gallery, high-low, surreal-expressionism, emerging-artist, illustration.

**Trend read** – An emerging name with commercial traction and critical credibility at once – the rare zone where both hold. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
