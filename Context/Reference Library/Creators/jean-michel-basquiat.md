# Jean-Michel Basquiat

**Who** – Painter, named first in the artists list in creative-director.md alongside Warhol, Dali and Picasso. Held here for canonical weight – the thin text signal is elevated by his position in the list and his cultural standing.

**Why aligned** – The reference for raw force that earns the gallery without sanding off the street. The work carries the punk sensibility the system names as its engine – seductive, unrefined, charged – and proves that rawness is a discipline, not an absence of one. He moved from the wall to the museum without trading the energy for polish.

**The formula** – Keep the rawness when the room gets refined. Carry the energy of the street into the gallery rather than cleaning it off at the door. Let the mark stay urgent and unfinished – the charge lives in what looks unresolved. Make the commentary part of the surface, not a caption beneath it.

**Canon** – the neo-expressionist body of work; the street-to-gallery arc itself.
