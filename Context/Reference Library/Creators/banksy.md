# Banksy

**Who** – Anonymous English street artist of Bristol origin, the most famous artist alive whose face no one knows.

**Why aligned** – Banksy is U.K. culture in its purest reference form – sharpness, brevity, layered meaning packed into a small space, the punk sensibility beneath the composure. He understands that the medium is the moment: the shredding was not vandalism, it was the completion of the work. Context is content.

**The formula** – Say the most with the least, in the most charged place. Stencil so it reads in a glance, site it where it detonates, then let the act – the auction, the wall, the hotel – become the artwork. Anonymity as the ultimate brand.

**Canon** – *Girl with Balloon* (2002) and its self-shredding into *Love Is in the Bin* at Sotheby's (2018). *Dismaland* (2015). the Walled Off Hotel, Bethlehem (2017).

**Pull for** – subversion, context-as-content, wit, anti-establishment, street-art.
