# Claude Monet

**Who** – French painter (1840-1926), the central figure of Impressionism and the artist whose canvas gave the movement its name.

**Why aligned** – Light and water as the whole subject, the garden built to be painted – it sits exactly where the taste lives, with biophilic instinct, light treated as a material and rain as the best weather. And the late work, done half-blind through cataracts, is the proof that limitation can force transcendence.

**The formula** – Paint the light, not the thing. Chase the same motif across hours and seasons until atmosphere becomes the subject. Build the garden, then spend decades looking at the same pond until it turns abstract.

**Canon** – *Impression, Sunrise* (1872), the painting that named the movement. The *Water Lilies* cycle at the Musée de l'Orangerie. Giverny, the garden as studio.

**Pull for** – light, atmosphere, water-and-garden, color-and-light, biophilic.
