# Jet Tila

**Who** – American chef of Thai-Chinese heritage, the inaugural Culinary Ambassador of Thai Cuisine, and a Food Network regular. His family opened the first Thai market and one of the first Thai restaurants in Los Angeles.

**Why aligned** – Tila did not discover Thai food, he inherited the American Thai-food lineage – a custodian of culture, not a translator of it. That is the cultural-facilitator instinct in the kitchen, and the global-awareness fusion the cooking already prizes.

**The formula** – Carry the heritage forward with authority, then bridge it outward into pan-Asian range. Honor the source while making it legible to a wider table. Lineage as license to riff.

**Canon** – inaugural Culinary Ambassador of Thai Cuisine (2013). *101 Asian Dishes You Need to Cook Before You Die* (2017). Dragon Tiger Noodle Co. the family's Bangkok Market, Los Angeles.

**Pull for** – thai-cuisine, pan-asian, heritage, fusion, cultural-custodian.

**Trend read** – A leading public face for Thai and pan-Asian cooking in American media. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
