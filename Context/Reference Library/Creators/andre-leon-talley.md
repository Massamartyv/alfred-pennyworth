# André Leon Talley

**Who** – The towering Vogue editor-at-large and former creative director (1948-2022), the first African American man to hold real power in fashion media, known as "the Bishop of Fashion."

**Why aligned** – The proof that taste itself is a form of authority. Talley wielded erudition, presence and a Black Southern lineage as the instruments of his eye, turning getting dressed into cultural text. The caped grandeur is maximalist where the style thesis leans to restraint, but the underlying lesson is the same: a singular point of view, held with total conviction, is what commands the room.

**The formula** – Treat taste as authority and wield it with erudition, not apology. Read fashion as autobiography and culture, never as mere clothing. Let personal history be the lens that makes the read singular, and let presence do the talking.

**Canon** – the Vogue editor-at-large tenure; the memoirs "The Chiffon Trenches" (2020) and "A.L.T." (2003); the Met Gala red-carpet stewardship; the capes and caftans.
