# Gunna

**Who** – Sergio Giavanni Kitchens (b. 1993, Atlanta), the melodic-trap stylist who rose through Young Thug's YSL Records from 2016: the Drip Season mixtapes, Drip Harder (2018, with Lil Baby), Drip or Drown 2 (2019, number three debut) and Wunna (2020, number one).

**Why aligned** – Atlanta's melodic school, and a case study in language as brand. "Pushin P" (2022) turned two characters of slang into a values-coded cultural cycle – proof that a lexicon, deployed with total consistency, is an asset class. The drip vocabulary built the persona the same way.

**The formula** – Sing the flow, code the values, survive the storm. The Auto-Tune-inflected melodic delivery over luxury imagery is the sound; the proprietary slang is the moat. The record also carries the hard chapter, held precisely: named in the 2022 YSL RICO indictment, he entered an Alford plea to a single racketeering count on December 14, 2022 – a plea form that accepts the strength of the state's evidence without admitting the underlying facts – with a court statement on the record that he had not cooperated, been interviewed or agreed to testify against any party. The controversy that followed did not dent the commerce: One of Wun (2024) debuted at number two and The Last Wun (August 2025) closed the YSL chapter as his stated final album on the imprint. Next label unconfirmed.

**Canon** – the Drip Season-to-Wunna arc; "pushin P" as slang-as-brand; One of Wun and The Last Wun as the post-2022 proof of resilience.

**Pull for** – melodic-trap, slang-as-brand, cadence, resilience, atlanta.
