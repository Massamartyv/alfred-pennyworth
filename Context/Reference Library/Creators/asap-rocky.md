# A$AP Rocky

**Who** – Musician and a primary style reference in creative-director.md: "layering precision, mixing high and low, oversized proportions that still feel intentional".

**Why aligned** – The cleanest demonstration that high and low belong on the same body when the intention reads. He oversizes without drowning – the volume is controlled, the proportion deliberate – which is precisely the silhouette principle the wardrobe runs on. Streetwear architecture worn like it was composed, not assembled.

**The formula** – Mix high and low until the price points stop mattering and only the intention shows. Oversize on purpose: the shoulder near the shoulder, the body draping, controlled volume rather than excess. Layer with precision – each piece earns its place in the stack. Make the considered read as effortless.

**Canon** – the high-low layered streetwear silhouette as a recurring template.

**Pull for** – art-direction, high-low, layering direction, oversized-silhouette, streetwear.

**Trend read** – pending enrichment.
