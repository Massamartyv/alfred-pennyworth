# Travis Scott

**Who** – Producer and architect of immersive musical worlds, named in creative-director.md among the five producers with a recognisable sonic fingerprint. *Astroworld* sits in the culture.md five-star canon: "immersive world-building, nostalgia weaponized as euphoria, Houston as mythology".

**Why aligned** – The clearest case of an album built as a place to live inside rather than a playlist to scroll. It turns a personal mythology – a demolished Houston theme park – into a world the listener can inhabit. The same "ear candy from Astroworld" creative-director.md cites as the standard for what makes a song skip-proof: evidence that someone cared, made audible.

**The formula** – Build a world, then score it. Take a specific personal mythology and make it the whole environment. Layer the detail – the ad-libs, the beat switches, the texture – so the ear keeps finding new things. Turn nostalgia into euphoria rather than sentiment: the past as a launchpad, not a museum.

**Canon** – *Astroworld* (2018).

**Pull for** – world-building, sound-design, immersive-atmosphere, narrative, ear-candy framing.
