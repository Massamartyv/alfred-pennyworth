# Salvador Dalí

**Who** – Spanish artist (1904-1989), Surrealism's most theatrical practitioner. Named in creative-director.md among the core visual references, and the embodiment of the line "Surrealism as a movement – its dreamlike expression in a way that feels shockingly realistic."

**Why aligned** – Dalí understood that the grotesque and the beautiful share one psychic space, and that nothing is too strange if it arrives with total conviction. It is the André 3000 principle in paint – earn the right to break the rule, then break it completely. Avant-garde is welcome; the line is taste.

**The formula** – Render the impossible with the precision of the real. Hand-paint a dream until the eye believes it. The paranoid-critical method – court the irrational on purpose, then execute it with old-master craft.

**Canon** – *The Persistence of Memory* (1931). *Un Chien Andalou* (1929) and *L'Âge d'or* (1930) with Luis Buñuel. The melting watches, the lobster telephone.

**Pull for** – surrealism, dream-logic, conviction, art-direction, theatrical.
