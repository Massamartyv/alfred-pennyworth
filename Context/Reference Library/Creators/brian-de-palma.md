# Brian De Palma

**Who** – American filmmaker (born 1940), the great stylist of suspense and operatic excess, heir to Hitchcock and master of the bravura set piece.

**Why aligned** – Director of *Scarface*, already carded here, so promoting him connects the ambition-and-hubris classic to its author. De Palma is style as the statement – the split screen, the long take, the slow build – maximalism executed with surgical control, never decoration.

**The formula** – Build the sequence like an aria. Split the screen, stretch the take, choreograph the camera until technique becomes the suspense. Heightened, operatic, unafraid of excess when the excess is earned.

**Canon** – *Carrie* (1976). *Blow Out* (1981). *Scarface* (1983). *The Untouchables* (1987). *Mission: Impossible* (1996).

**Pull for** – visual-bravura, suspense, operatic-excess, stylist, cinematography.
