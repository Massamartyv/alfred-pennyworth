# Frank Lloyd Wright

**Who** – American architect (1867-1959), the defining figure of 20th-century American architecture and author of "organic architecture" – the doctrine that a building must grow from its site and program rather than be imposed on them. Originator of the Prairie School and the Usonian house.

**Why aligned** – The horizontal line as calm, the hearth as center, nature carried inside until the boundary disappears. It is the same instinct behind "organic Japanese modern" and dark-warm sanctuary over bright-and-airy. Wright designed the building, the furniture and the light as one work – the system behind the thing mattering as much as the thing.

**The formula** – Take the earth line, not the sky line. Low roofs, deep overhanging eaves, open plans that flow outward into landscape. Native materials, passive orientation, total design from structure down to the chair. Nature is not a view to frame – it is the medium the building sits inside.

**Canon** – Robie House, Chicago (1910). Unity Temple, Oak Park (1909). Fallingwater, Mill Run (1935-1939). The Guggenheim, New York (1959). Taliesin and Taliesin West. The Jacobs House (1936), the first Usonian. Eight works inscribed as UNESCO World Heritage in 2019.
