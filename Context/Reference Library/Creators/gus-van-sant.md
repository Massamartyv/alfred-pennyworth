# Gus Van Sant

**Who** – American filmmaker (born 1952), a defining voice of American independent cinema who moves between intimate studio dramas and austere experimental work.

**Why aligned** – Director of *Good Will Hunting*, already carded here, so promoting him connects a favorite work to its maker. His best films hold emotion under restraint until it breaks – the suppressed feeling, the cost of wasted potential, the exact register the taste responds to.

**The formula** – Find the outsider and stay close, patient, unforced. Let silence and landscape carry what dialogue will not. Toggle between the accessible studio film and the formally radical one without losing the naturalist's eye.

**Canon** – *Drugstore Cowboy* (1989). *My Own Private Idaho* (1991). *Good Will Hunting* (1997). *Elephant* (2003). *Milk* (2008).

**Pull for** – emotional-restraint, outsiders, naturalism, character-study, narrative.
