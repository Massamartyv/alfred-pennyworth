# Dan Harmon

**Who** – American writer and showrunner (born 1973), creator of *Community* and co-creator of *Rick and Morty*, and author of the Story Circle, a working distillation of the hero's journey.

**Why aligned** – Named in creative-director.md as the narrative-structure framework of choice – the Story Circle is the storytelling tool already in the kit. Harmon is the bridge from consumption to production, the writer-director ambition to build worlds rather than only watch them.

**The formula** – Eight steps drawn from Campbell's monomyth: a character is comfortable, wants something, enters an unfamiliar world, adapts, gets it, pays a price, returns changed. Run every episode and every arc through the circle. Character defined by flaw, comedy that bends its own form.

**Canon** – the Story Circle. *Community* (2009). *Rick and Morty* (2013), co-created with Justin Roiland.

**Pull for** – narrative-structure, story-circle, world-building, character-flaw, voice-and-tone.

**Trend read** – The Story Circle is among the most widely used practical screenwriting frameworks working today. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
