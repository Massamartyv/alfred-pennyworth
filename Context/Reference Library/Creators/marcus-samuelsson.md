# Marcus Samuelsson

**Who** – Ethiopian-born, Swedish-raised, American chef. Founder of Red Rooster Harlem, former executive chef of Aquavit, multiple James Beard Award winner and author of the memoir *Yes, Chef*.

**Why aligned** – No major American chef has a backstory spanning Africa, Scandinavia and Harlem, which makes his food genuinely autobiographical – the biography is the brand. That is the cultural-facilitator ideal made literal, three culinary cultures fused into one identity, and Harlem championed as a destination.

**The formula** – Cook the autobiography. Fuse Ethiopian, Scandinavian and American influences into a single voice, then anchor it in a place and a community – Red Rooster as a gathering table framed around Harlem's history. Identity as the cuisine.

**Canon** – Red Rooster Harlem (2010). Aquavit, the youngest chef to earn a three-star New York Times review. *Yes, Chef* (2012). multiple James Beard Awards, including Best Chef NYC (2003).

**Pull for** – diaspora, three-continent-fusion, hospitality, cultural-bridge, autobiography-as-cuisine.
