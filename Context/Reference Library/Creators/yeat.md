# Yeat

**Who** – Noah Olivier Smith (b. 2000, Irvine, California; raised in Fullerton – the commonly repeated Portland origin is an overstatement, a few late-teen years only). The rage era's most complete world-builder: the distorted, sub-heavy "bell-and-tonka" sound – his own term for the bass and bell-tone signatures – under a processed, near-robotic melodic delivery.

**Why aligned** – A proprietary language operated as a brand system. The lexicon – twizzy, krank, luh geeky – is not incidental slang; it is deliberate world-building that makes the audience bilingual in the artist. Paired with the owner-operator structure: his own label, Twizzy Rich (2021), rebranded Lyfestyle Corporation by 2023, running through Field Trip Recordings in joint venture with the majors – sovereignty inside the distribution machine.

**The formula** – Invent the sound, name the sound, own the pipe. The onomatopoeic branding of his own production style means nobody describes Yeat without using Yeat's words. The release cadence is relentless and fully verified: 2 Alivë (February 2022, number six debut), AftërLyfe (2023, number four), 2093 (2024, with Lil Wayne, Future and Donald Glover), the DANGEROUS SUMMER EP (2025, with Don Toliver and FKA twigs) and ADL (March 2026, via Lyfestyle, Field Trip and Capitol) – the most currently active artist on this shelf. Pull him for sonic-signature branding, lexicon-as-moat and the independent-label-major-distribution model.

**Canon** – 2 Alivë (2022); AftërLyfe (2023); 2093 (2024); ADL (2026); the Lyfestyle Corporation structure.
