# Caroline Shaw

**Who** – Composer, vocalist and violinist; the youngest-ever winner of the Pulitzer Prize for Music, at 30 in 2013, for Partita for 8 Voices, written for the vocal ensemble Roomful of Teeth. At least four Grammy wins across three different configurations – with Attacca Quartet (2020, 2023), in the solo contemporary-classical category (2022) and with Sō Percussion (2025, for Rectangles and Circumstance) – and still touring with Attacca into 2026.

**Why aligned** – The high-low bridge at its most rigorous. A Pulitzer composer who co-produced and sang on Kanye West's 2015 "Say You Will" remix and carries writing and vocal credits on The Life of Pablo (2016) – "Wolves," vocals on "Father Stretch My Hands Pt. 2." The same person, the same ear, moving between the concert hall and the culture without condescension in either direction. That porousness is the cultural-facilitator move this whole library exists to serve.

**The formula** – The voice as a full orchestra. Partita treats human voices as a complete sound world – speech, whisper, breath and belted tone as compositional materials – and her wider work keeps that instinct: form invented per piece rather than inherited, collaboration as a first-class practice rather than a detour. Pull her when a project needs formal invention with warmth, or proof that credentialed craft and popular culture strengthen each other.

**Canon** – Partita for 8 Voices and the 2013 Pulitzer; Roomful of Teeth; The Life of Pablo credits (2016); the Grammy wins across Attacca Quartet, solo and Sō Percussion configurations.
