# Rebecca Maria

**Who** – Painter and sculptor working in the iconography of hip-hop album-cover art. Self-taught, expanding from gestural painting into clay sculpture. Shows at Stems Gallery (Paris and Brussels) and Volery Gallery (Dubai). Nationality is not publicly disclosed by any gallery source, so it is left unstated here rather than guessed.

**Why aligned** – She paints the visual memory of hip-hop itself – the shapes and colors of the album covers that scored the culture. That is the music DNA of the whole operating system rendered as fine art, the former DJ's reference library hung on a wall. Nostalgia as texture, not recycling.

**The formula** – Take the emblematic hip-hop album cover and repaint it in thick gestural strokes, sometimes hung suspended the way CDs hang in a record shop. Translate the flat sleeve into paint and clay, so a cover becomes an object you walk around.

**Canon** – the nine-foot clay sculpture of Sade from the *Love Deluxe* cover, commissioned by Drake and completed April 2026. *Some Things I Can't Forget*, Stems Gallery (2022).
