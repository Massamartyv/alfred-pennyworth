# Dries Van Noten

**Who** – Belgian designer, one of the Antwerp Six, founder of his eponymous house (1986), revered for print, colour and textile mastery held together by a wearable cut.

**Why aligned** – Fabric and print as the centrepiece, which is the operator's "fabric is the centerpiece, not a detail" stated at the level of a whole house. The silhouette stays calm so the surface can riot: clashing florals, hand-treated textiles, a colourist's nerve. Restraint in cut, conviction in cloth.

**The formula** – Lead with textile and print and let pattern and fabric carry the garment while the shape stays wearable. Clash references with conviction rather than caution. Build slowly, stay independent, ignore the hype cycle.

**Canon** – the Antwerp Six London showing (1986); decades of print-led runway collections; the move to Puig backing while keeping creative independence; the 2024 step back from the creative lead.

**Pull for** – print, textile, fabric-first, restraint-in-cut.
