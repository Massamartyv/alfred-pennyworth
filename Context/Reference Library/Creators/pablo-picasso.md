# Pablo Picasso

**Who** – Spanish artist (1881-1973), co-founder of Cubism with Georges Braque and the most restlessly inventive painter of the 20th century. Named in creative-director.md among the core visual references.

**Why aligned** – Picasso showed that the goal of representation is not accuracy, it is truth – multiple perspectives held at once. And he reinvented himself by period rather than repeating a signature, the same refusal to settle that runs through the whole taste.

**The formula** – Break the single viewpoint. Show every angle at once and let the mind reassemble it. Move through periods on purpose – Blue, Rose, Cubist – treating reinvention as the method, not a crisis.

**Canon** – *Les Demoiselles d'Avignon* (1907), the proto-Cubist rupture. *Guernica* (1937), painting as political force. The Blue and Rose periods.

**Pull for** – reinvention, multiple-perspective, art-direction, creative-risk, truth-over-accuracy.
