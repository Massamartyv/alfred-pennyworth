# Kim Chong Hak

**Who** – Korean painter (born 1937), known as the "Painter of Seoraksan." He settled by the mountain in the late 1970s and has painted its flowers, forests and seasons in saturated color ever since. The High Museum of Atlanta gave him his first US retrospective in 2025.

**Why aligned** – Kim chose one mountain and stayed – forty years of looking at the same landscape until he found what no one else had seen. Depth of attention as a creative strategy, intention per square foot, depth over surface. He rejected the monochrome school his peers made famous and went the other way, into color and abundance.

**The formula** – Commit to one subject for a lifetime. Reject the prevailing fashion – here, Dansaekhwa monochrome – and answer it with saturated, gestural abundance. Blend the energy of Abstract Expressionism with Korean folk-color tradition, rooted in the labor of daily observation.

**Canon** – the Seoraksan and Sokcho landscape and flower paintings. *Summer Gaewoon* (2004). the 2025 High Museum retrospective *Painter of Seoraksan*, which traveled to the Phoenix Art Museum.

**Pull for** – color-and-light, nature, depth-of-attention, materiality, anti-trend.
