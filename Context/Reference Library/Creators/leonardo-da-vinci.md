# Leonardo da Vinci

**Who** – Italian polymath of the High Renaissance (1452-1519), painter of the most famous picture on earth and a mind that refused the wall between art and science.

**Why aligned** – Leonardo did not separate science from art – both were instruments of understanding. It is the multi-hyphenate ideal in its origin form, the creative director and the engineer in one body, curiosity with no disciplinary walls. The system behind the thing studied as closely as the thing.

**The formula** – Observe relentlessly, draw to think, dissolve the line between disciplines. Anatomy feeds the figure, optics feed the light, engineering feeds the composition. Mastery as the by-product of total curiosity.

**Canon** – *Mona Lisa* (c. 1503-1519), the Louvre. *The Last Supper* (c. 1495-1498), Milan. *Vitruvian Man* (c. 1490). The notebooks.

**Pull for** – polymath, art-and-science, mastery, curiosity, craft.
