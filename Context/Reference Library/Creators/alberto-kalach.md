# Alberto Kalach

**Who** – Mexican architect, born 1960, working from Mexico City through his practice Taller de Arquitectura X (TAX), founded 1981. Designer of the Biblioteca Vasconcelos and the decades-long "return to the lake city" vision for the Valley of Mexico.

**Why aligned** – Architecture as infrastructure for nature, not shelter set against it. Kalach treats greenery as a co-inhabitant the way the spatial operating system does – "no such thing as too much green, plants are co-inhabitants, not decorations." His concrete is left tactile, raw and warm at once, the 80/20 duality at building scale.

**The formula** – The botanical machine. Build a structural scaffold, then let living matter penetrate and run it. Material honesty as the rule – concrete, steel and glass used structurally and left to show their nature. Scale moves freely from a single house to an 8,000-square-kilometer basin without changing the logic.

**Canon** – Biblioteca Vasconcelos, Mexico City (competition won 2004, opened 2006). Casa GGG (1999). México Ciudad Futura / Return to the City of Lakes, the Lake Texcoco regeneration vision, special prize at the 2002 Venice Biennale. Reforma 27 Tower (2007-2010).

**Pull for** – biophilic, materiality, spatial-direction, nature-integration, world-building.
