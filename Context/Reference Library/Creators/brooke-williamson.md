# Brooke Williamson

**Who** – American chef, winner of *Top Chef* season 14, and chef-owner of Playa Provisions in Playa del Rey, Los Angeles. A leading voice for vegetable-forward California cooking.

**Why aligned** – Her produce-first lens matches the conviction that food can be delicious and health-conscious at once – vegetables as the main event, rooted in Southern California terroir and farmers-market culture. The experimental, ingredient-led instinct the home kitchen already runs on.

**The formula** – Put the vegetable at the center of the plate, not the side. Build from what the market gives that week, technique in service of the produce. Range across formats – the four concepts of Playa Provisions under one roof – without losing the through-line.

**Canon** – *Top Chef* season 14 win (2017). Playa Provisions, Los Angeles. Food Network's Tournament of Champions. *Sun-Kissed Cooking: Vegetables Front and Center* (2024).

**Pull for** – vegetable-forward, california-cuisine, produce-first, technique, hospitality.
