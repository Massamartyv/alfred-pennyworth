# Anthony Bourdain

**Who** – Chef, writer and traveller. creative-director.md names him as "the compass for what taste looks like in food and dining" – a single line, but load-bearing: he sets the dining-taste standard across every context.

**Why aligned** – The reference that severs quality from prestige. A street vendor, a family recipe, a Michelin room and a fast-food spot blossoming into a gem are weighed on the same scale – the output, never the address. He treats food as a way into a place and its people, which is the same instinct that drives the travel philosophy: walk in the local community, develop respect for the space.

**The formula** – Judge the plate, not the postcode. Let the food be the door into a culture rather than the destination. Refuse pretension – knowledge worn lightly, curiosity over credential. Tell the story around the meal, because the context is half the taste.

**Canon** – the body of food-and-travel work as a single compass for taste without snobbery.

**Pull for** – narrative, taste-compass framing, hospitality, food-culture direction, travel.
