# Eddie Jackson

**Who** – American chef and former NFL cornerback, born Eddie Paul Jackson Jr. in Americus, Georgia. Winner of *Food Network Star* season 11 and host across the network. Southern roots with global influences.

**Why aligned** – His brand is built on the line between competitive sport and competitive cooking – the discipline-to-kitchen narrative. That athlete's discipline applied to craft is the same standard the work holds itself to, and the physical-health and culinary domains meeting in one person is the operating model in miniature.

**The formula** – Carry the athlete's discipline into the kitchen and make it the story. Southern foundations opened up with global influence, competition as the proving ground. Train, compete, repeat.

**Canon** – *Food Network Star* season 11 win (2015). an NFL cornerback career across four teams. *Kids BBQ Championship* and Food Network hosting. the Chefs United Foundation.

**Pull for** – athlete-to-chef, southern, bbq, discipline, competition.
