# Page and Dornenburg

**Who** – Karen Page and Andrew Dornenburg, the married food-writing partnership behind *The Flavor Bible*, *Culinary Artistry* and *What to Drink with What You Eat*. The reference shelf for how flavors combine.

**Why aligned** – Named in creative-director.md as the other parent force of culinary creation – the creative flavor-pairing engine opposite Greger's science. Where he sets the rails, they open the possibilities. The Flavor Bible is the improviser's chord chart for the kitchen.

**The formula** – Map the affinities. Take an ingredient and chart everything it loves, then compose from the matches rather than the recipe. Pairing as a generative system, not a lookup – the tool that turns technique into invention.

**Canon** – *The Flavor Bible* (2008). *Culinary Artistry* (1996). *What to Drink with What You Eat* (2006).

**Pull for** – flavor-pairing, culinary-reference, creative-engine, technique, improvisation.
