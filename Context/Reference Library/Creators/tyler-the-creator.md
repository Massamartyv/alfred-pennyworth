# Tyler, the Creator

**Who** – Musician and producer carried in creative-director.md as the lesson in growth through resistance: "initially resisted – the teenage angst brand did not land. The musicality and level of craftsmanship became undeniable over time."

**Why aligned** – The canon entry for how taste itself evolves. The reference is not only the work but the arc of being wrong about it – the recognition that the talent was always present, even during the period of resistance. He proves that craft outlasts the brand it arrives wrapped in, the reminder that a first impression is a poor judge of a long career.

**The formula** – Outlast the resistance with craft. Let the early reception be hostile and keep deepening the musicality until the work becomes undeniable on its own terms. Trust that quality compounds past the initial brand. Refuse the genre the audience has assigned you.
