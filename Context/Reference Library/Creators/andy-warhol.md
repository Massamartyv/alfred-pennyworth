# Andy Warhol

**Who** – American artist (1928-1987), the defining figure of Pop Art and the maker of the Factory. Named in creative-director.md among the core visual references alongside Basquiat, Dalí and Picasso.

**Why aligned** – The man who proved a soup can could be an icon. He dissolved the wall between commerce and art, which is the exact terrain a creative director who is also an entrepreneur lives on. High and low in the same frame, the brand made into culture.

**The formula** – Take the most ordinary object or the most famous face, repeat it until repetition becomes the meaning, and let the surface do the talking. Mechanical reproduction as fine art. The artist as a studio, a brand and a scene, not just a hand.

**Canon** – *Campbell's Soup Cans* (1962). *Marilyn Diptych* (1962). *Brillo Boxes* (1964). The Factory and the films. Cross-disciplinary into music with the Velvet Underground.
