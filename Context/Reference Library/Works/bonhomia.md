# Bonhomía

**What** – A salumería-restaurant on Vía Primavera in the El Poblado district of Medellín, Colombia. A 400-square-meter restored building holding a cured-meat-and-cheese deli counter, a bakery, a small market and wine cellar, and an open-air terrace. Wood-fired pizza, charcuterie, carpaccios, wine and live music. One of the five-star restaurant references.

**Why aligned** – On the five-star restaurant list, and a model of the convivial, indoor-outdoor table the social philosophy gravitates to – guards down, conversation genuine, individuality welcome. The name itself, from the Spanish *bonhomía*, means warmth and goodness of character.

**Why it lands** – Conviviality built into the architecture. Four zones – deli, bakery, market, terrace – under one roof keep the place social and unhurried, the cured boards and wood-fired pizza made for sharing. Medellín, a city already on the food compass.

**Canon** – the cured-meat-and-cheese boards. the wood-fired pizza. the El Poblado terrace on Vía Primavera. the name meaning affability and goodness of character.

**Pull for** – salumeria, convivial, indoor-outdoor, charcuterie, hospitality.
