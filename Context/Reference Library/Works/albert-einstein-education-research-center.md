# Albert Einstein Education and Research Center

**What** – A medical education and research campus in Morumbi, São Paulo, by Safdie Architects (Moshe Safdie) with Perkins and Will as executive architect, completed 2022. Roughly 23,300 square meters of teaching, laboratory and simulation space organized around a vaulted, skylit garden atrium.

**Why aligned** – Garden as the heart of an institution, light brought deep into the section, greenery as the primary organizer rather than the afterthought. It is the spatial palette at civic scale – plants as co-inhabitants, light treated as a material, warmth engineered into a building type that usually forgets it.

**Why it lands** – The "urban oasis" move. A vaulted skylit atrium garden pulls sky, planting and daylight into the core of a dense program, so a research building reads as a landscape. Nature is the social armature, not the decoration around it.

**Canon** – The vaulted garden atrium; planted terraces threaded through labs and 40 classrooms; a 400-seat auditorium; clinical simulation suites. Landscape by Isabel Duprat. Safdie's section-as-social-landscape signature applied to medicine.

**Pull for** – biophilic, spatial-direction, color-and-light, institutional-warmth, atrium.

**Trend read** – Healthcare and research architecture is moving decisively toward daylight, planting and wellbeing as measurable design goals, and this sits at the front of that shift. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
