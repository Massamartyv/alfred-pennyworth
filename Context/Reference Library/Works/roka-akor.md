# Roka Akor

**What** – A modern Japanese robatayaki fine-dining group – food cooked over bincho-tan charcoal on a multi-tiered grill, alongside sushi, wagyu and seafood. Locations in Scottsdale, Chicago, San Francisco and Oak Brook. One of the five-star restaurant references.

**Why aligned** – On the five-star restaurant list. It is the moody, highbrow, seductive evening-social register the dining philosophy names – delicious food, soothing ambiance, the charcoal grill as quiet theater. Japanese precision with a contemporary, high-end-casual ease.

**Why it lands** – The robata at the center. Cooking over bincho-tan charcoal makes fire the focal craft, the multi-tiered grill the room's heartbeat. Quality of output over location, exactly the stated value.

**Canon** – the bincho-tan robata grill. Scottsdale, Chicago, San Francisco and Oak Brook. the sushi, wagyu and seafood program.
