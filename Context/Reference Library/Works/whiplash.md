# Whiplash

**What** – Damien Chazelle, 2014. A five-star entry in the culture.md media canon. A young drummer and his conductor locked in a duel over what greatness costs.

**Why aligned** – It refuses the comfortable lie that excellence is gentle. The film holds its question without resolving it – is the cruelty the price of the mastery, or a separate evil wearing mastery as cover. The tension is the meaning.

**Why it lands** – Build the whole piece around a single unresolved question and let the audience carry it out of the room. Tempo as emotional architecture. Discomfort earned, never decorative.

**Canon** – the final nine-minute drum sequence; the line "not quite my tempo".

**Pull for** – narrative, pacing, cost-of-greatness, mentor dynamics, dramatic structure.
