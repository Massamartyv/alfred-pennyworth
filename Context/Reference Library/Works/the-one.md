# The One

**What** – Dolce and Gabbana's paired franchise: The One for Women (2006, Christine Nagel), a warm fruity-oriental, and The One for Men (2008, Olivier Polge), an oriental-spicy whose tobacco-amber drydown is the cultural reference when "The One" is named without qualification.

**Why it lands** – The men's tobacco-amber base became a benchmark for the warm-spicy masculine category – about as close to critical consensus as mass-prestige gets. Approachable, versatile, and the standard others are measured against. Two noses who went on to Hermès and Chanel.

**Canon** – The One for Men (2008) and its tobacco-amber drydown; The One for Women (2006); the perfumers' later pedigree.

**Pull for** – oriental-spicy, tobacco-amber, masculine-benchmark, accessible.
