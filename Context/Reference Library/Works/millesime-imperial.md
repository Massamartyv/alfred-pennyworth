# Millésime Impérial

**What** – Creed's 1995 marine-aquatic fragrance, made for the house's 150th anniversary – a salted citrus built on sea-salt accords, Sicilian lemon, bergamot and iris over a soft woody-musk base.

**Why it lands** – One of the defining marine compositions of the modern era. The salinity reads as effortless luxury rather than sport, which is why it became a warm-weather signature for people who want fresh without generic. Versatile, expensive-smelling, unmistakably Creed.

**Canon** – the sea-salt and citrus opening; the marine-woody drydown; the 1995 anniversary release.

**Pull for** – marine, aquatic-citrus, warm-weather, fresh-luxury.

**Trend read** – pending enrichment.
