# Bal d'Afrique

**What** – Byredo's 2009 woody floral musk by Jérôme Epinette, inspired by 1920s Paris and its fascination with African aesthetics – bergamot, neroli and marigold over violet and jasmine on a warm amber-cedar-musk base.

**Why it lands** – A niche curiosity that became a modern classic without softening for the mainstream. The bright-to-warm arc is endlessly wearable, and it functions as the gateway into higher-priced niche – the scent that converts people. Fifteen years of relevance is the proof.

**Canon** – the citrus-and-marigold opening; the warm musk-amber drydown; its gateway-niche status; the 2025 Absolu.

**Pull for** – woody-floral-musk, modern-classic, warm, gateway-niche.
