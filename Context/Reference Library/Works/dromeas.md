# Dromeas (The Runner)

**What** – Costas Varotsos's monumental glass sculpture of a running figure in Athens – the glass runner – known as Dromeas (The Runner) and originally titled O Xenos (The Stranger). Twelve metres of stacked green glass shards over an iron frame.

**Why aligned** – Motion made from the most static, fragile material there is. Thousands of glass shards stack into a figure mid-stride that dematerialises into blur as you move past – the work reads one way at walking pace and another at driving speed, so the city becomes part of it. The reference for a material used against its own nature, and for a form that only resolves in motion.

**Why it lands** – One counter-intuitive choice, glass for speed, carries the whole idea. The fragility against the kinetic subject holds a tension a literal bronze runner could never reach. The blur is the meaning.

**Canon** – the twelve-metre green-glass figure near the Hilton Athens; the original 1988 Omonia Square installation; the 1994 permanent relocation.
