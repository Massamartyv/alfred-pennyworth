# Forrest Gump

**What** – Robert Zemeckis' 1994 film, with Tom Hanks as a guileless Alabama man who drifts through three decades of American history. Won Best Picture, Best Director and Best Actor.

**Why aligned** – Already on the five-star film list in culture.md – innocence as wisdom, history as the backdrop to personal experience, love as the organizing force of a life. The texture of feeling over the recycled image, sentiment earned rather than manufactured.

**Why it lands** – The holy fool as a lens on a turbulent era. By refusing cynicism it lets history land as lived feeling rather than lecture, and the love story underneath gives the picaresque its spine. Sentiment held just short of saccharine by Hanks' restraint.

**Canon** – Tom Hanks as Forrest. the bench and the box of chocolates. the run across America. adapted from Winston Groom's novel.

**Pull for** – innocence-as-wisdom, americana, sentiment, narrative, history.

**Trend read** – A permanent fixture of the American popular canon, equally beloved and debated. Pending enrichment for current-cycle signal.
