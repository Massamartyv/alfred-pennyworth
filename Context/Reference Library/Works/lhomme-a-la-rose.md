# L'Homme à la Rose

**What** – Maison Francis Kurkdjian's 2020 masculine rose by Francis Kurkdjian, built on two rose varieties – the full-bodied Bulgarian Damask and the fresher Grasse Centifolia – with grapefruit, sage and amberwood.

**Why it lands** – A rose fragrance built unambiguously for men, at a time when masculine florals were still commercially unusual at the prestige tier. The two-rose construction gives it dimension, and it offers an elegant alternative to the amber-tobacco masculine template.

**Canon** – the two-rose construction; the grapefruit-and-amberwood frame; its standing as a masculine-floral standard-bearer.

**Pull for** – masculine-floral, rose, elegant, prestige.

**Trend read** – pending enrichment.
