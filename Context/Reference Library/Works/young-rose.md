# Young Rose

**What** – Byredo's 2021 floral woody musk, a restrained modern rose – ambrette and Sichuan pepper over Damask rose and iris on an ambroxan-musk base.

**Why it lands** – The argument for rose that does not read as conventional floral. The pepper and ambroxan keep it sharp and unisex, which is exactly why it reaches people who would never pick up a rose-forward scent. Modern, restrained, a little cool.

**Canon** – the peppered-rose signature; the ambroxan base; the "restless youth" concept.

**Pull for** – floral-woody-musk, modern-rose, unisex, restrained.
