# Oud Ispahan

**What** – Dior's 2012 oriental-woody oud from La Collection Privée Christian Dior, by François Demachy – a Damascus rose woven with Indonesian agarwood over patchouli, saffron, sandalwood and cedar.

**Why it lands** – A classical rose-oud pairing made legible and wearable. Demachy keeps the oud from turning medicinal, letting the rose-oud symbiosis breathe – the refined, accessible entry into a category that often overwhelms. A permanent Collection Privée pillar.

**Canon** – the rose-and-agarwood core; the saffron-patchouli frame; the New Look limited editions and the 2024 Esprit de Parfum.

**Pull for** – oud, rose-oud, refined, collection-privee.
