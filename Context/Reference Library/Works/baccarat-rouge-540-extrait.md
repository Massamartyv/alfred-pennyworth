# Baccarat Rouge 540 Extrait

**What** – The intensified extrait of Maison Francis Kurkdjian's Baccarat Rouge 540 (extrait 2017, original 2015, by Francis Kurkdjian) – bitter almond and saffron over jasmine and cedar on an amber-ambroxan-cashmeran base, darker and longer-lasting than the eau de parfum.

**Why it lands** – The most imitated luxury fragrance of the era. The amber-and-almond glow is instantly recognizable, the extrait deepens it, and an entire global dupe industry plus a roster of famous wearers is the clearest possible signal of cultural reach. It redefined the status scent for the social-media age.

**Canon** – the saffron-and-almond signature; the amber-ambroxan drydown; the 2015 eau de parfum and the contained 2014 Baccarat-crystal commission; the dupe phenomenon.

**Pull for** – oriental-floral, status-scent, amber, cultural-reach.
