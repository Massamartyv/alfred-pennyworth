# Dallas Buyers Club

**What** – Jean-Marc Vallée's 2013 drama, with Matthew McConaughey as Ron Woodroof, a Texas electrician who, given thirty days to live with AIDS in 1985, builds a smuggling operation to get unapproved treatment to fellow patients. McConaughey and Jared Leto both won Academy Awards.

**Why aligned** – Total commitment as the whole story, on screen and off – McConaughey's radical transformation the centerpiece of his reinvention, the same earn-the-right-then-go-all-the-way conviction the taste prizes. An outsider building his own system against an institution that wrote him off.

**Why it lands** – The hustler turned reluctant lifeline. A bigot's worldview cracked open by the people he is forced to depend on, played without sentiment. Defiance against mortality and bureaucracy at once, carried by two performances stripped to the bone.

**Canon** – McConaughey as Ron Woodroof and Jared Leto as Rayon, both Oscar-winning. the 1980s Texas AIDS-crisis setting. the McConaissance turning point.

**Pull for** – transformation, defiance, total-commitment, against-the-system, character-study.
