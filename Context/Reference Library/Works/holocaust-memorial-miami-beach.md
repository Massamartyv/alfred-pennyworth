# Holocaust Memorial Miami Beach

**What** – Kenneth Treister's Holocaust memorial in Miami Beach (dedicated 1990): a 42-foot bronze arm rising from the earth, tattooed with an Auschwitz number, with nearly 100 life-sized figures clinging to it, set within a Jerusalem-stone enclosure.

**Why aligned** – Architecture as sequence and feeling. Treister designed the whole site as a path – the Memorial Wall of names, the photographic history, the Jerusalem-stone enclosure – building to the central arm. The reference for a memorial that walks the body through a narrative and ends not in dignified repose but in the last kinetic desperation of the dying.

**Why it lands** – The skyward-reaching arm inverts the usual memorial grammar, and the tattooed number grounds the abstraction in one historical fact, making the universal particular. Treister called it "a scene from hell, frozen in bronze."

**Canon** – the 42-foot bronze arm and its roughly 100 figures; the Memorial Wall and Jerusalem-stone enclosure; the 1933-1945 Meridian Avenue address; dedicated 1990.

**Pull for** – memorial, monumental, emotional-weight, spatial-direction.
