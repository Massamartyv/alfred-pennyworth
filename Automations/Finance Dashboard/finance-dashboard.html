The Business Account Problem

The Five Points business account is operating in reverse. It opened in January 2025 and has been subsidized by the personal account every month since. 70% of all deposits into the business account are Zelle transfers from personal -- meaning the personal account is funding business operations rather than the business funding personal income.

The business subscription overhead (~$550/mo) exceeds what the account generates. And the $15/mo service fee hits every period because the balance never reaches the $2,000 minimum to waive it.

Meanwhile, business tools (Instantly at $159/mo across multiple plans, Webflow at $84/mo for two sites, Google Workspace at $135/mo) are escalating while the account has no independent revenue stream feeding it.
